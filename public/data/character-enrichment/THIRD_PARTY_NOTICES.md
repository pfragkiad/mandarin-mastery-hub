# Character enrichment sources

These optional, unreviewed annotations were supplied in the character-enrichment
handoff. The original canonical catalogue remains separate and unchanged.

- Make Me a Hanzi dictionary, commit bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92:
  LGPL-3.0-or-later. Corresponding dictionary source and licence notices are in
  `notices/`. Source: https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/tree/bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92
- Hanzi Writer Data 2.0.1: Arphic Public License. Geometry is fetched on demand
  from the version-pinned package, not replaced by standalone components.
  Notice: `notices/STROKE_DATA_ARPHICPL.txt`.
- BabelStone IDS, 2025-06-27 / Unicode 16.0: source comparisons retain their
  regional qualifiers. The supplied manifest records permission evidence, not
  an independently established licence for all underlying characters. No full
  BabelStone snapshot is redistributed here. Source: https://www.babelstone.co.uk/CJK/IDS.TXT
- GF 0014-2009: eight bounded textual rule-example annotations only. No PDF,
  font artwork, table artwork or complete standard component inventory is
  redistributed. Source: https://www.unicode.org/irg/docs/n2739-GF0014-2009.pdf

`manifest.json` retains the supplied source versions, hashes, consumer policy,
licence-notice references and coverage. Original generator/audit utilities and
all their inputs were not provided; the local split adapter is not a substitute
for rerunning that upstream audit. Exact source agreement and complete stroke
coverage do not establish editorial approval, etymology or verified stroke names.
