# Private real-source Chinese examples

Local research examples found through public search, separate from Tatoeba and
from the published application. `sources.json`, `examples.jsonl` and `raw-*.html`
are intentionally excluded from Git. Nothing from this directory is imported by
browser modules or shipped in public runtime data.

Each collected example retains its exact Chinese sentence and surrounding
paragraph, publisher, author, article title/date/URL, search query, acquisition
timestamp, raw-page SHA-256 and licence/permission evidence. Pinyin, English and
linguistic review remain explicitly absent until authored/reviewed separately.
Preserve written forms; never convert scripts or assign target readings to fill
gaps. A source occurrence verifies where wording appeared, not its teaching
suitability or a historical claim.

Private storage is not a blanket reuse licence. Source-specific restrictions,
including third-party exceptions, noncommercial terms and adaptation limits,
remain attached. Any future public import needs its own compatible rights and
language review. Do not add these local files to Git or publish them as examples.
