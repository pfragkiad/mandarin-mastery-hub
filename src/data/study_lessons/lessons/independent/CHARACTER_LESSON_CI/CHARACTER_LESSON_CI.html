Talking About Times & Occasions

Once, last time, this time, next time, every time, and again

numbers-quantities · time-calendar · #frequency · #repetition · #classifiers · #tone-sandhi

At a glance

次 (cì)

次 (cì) counts occurrences of actions; 一次 (yí cì) can count one occurrence, while 上一次 (shàng yí cì), 这一次 (zhè yí cì), and 下一次 (xià yí cì) identify particular occasions.

Meaning and use

After a verb, 一次 (yí cì) answers “how many times?” In expressions like 上一次 (shàng yí cì), 这一次 (zhè yí cì), or 下一次 (xià yí cì), it answers “which occasion?” 再一次 (zài yí cì) generally means “once again.” In all these expressions, 一 (yī) is pronounced yí before the fourth-tone word 次 (cì).

Count occurrences of an action

去一次 (qù yí cì) — go once

Refer to a particular occasion

上一次 (shàng yí cì) — last time

Contribute an occurrence count inside a repetition phrase

再一次 (zài yí cì) — once again

Common constructions

动词 + 一次 (dòngcí + yí cì)

Count one occurrence of a verb

去一次 (qù yí cì) — Go once

一次 (yí cì) follows the verb and answers “How many times?”; also possible with experience marker 过 (guo).

上一次 (shàng yí cì)

Identify a previous occasion

上一次我没听懂 (Shàng yí cì wǒ méi tīng dǒng) — Last time I did not understand what I heard

Often appears at the start of a sentence as a time expression.

这一次 (zhè yí cì)

Identify this occasion

这一次我明白了 (Zhè yí cì wǒ míngbai le) — This time I understood

A sentence-initial temporal phrase; 这次 (zhè cì) is a common shortening.

下一次 (xià yí cì)

Identify the following occasion

下一次我们一起去吧 (Xià yí cì wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba) — Let’s go together next time

A sentence-initial temporal phrase; 下次 (xià cì) is common in speech.

每一次 + 动作, ... 都 ... (měi yí cì + dòngzuò, ... dōu ...)

Apply a statement to each occurrence

每一次见到你，我都很开心 (Měi yí cì jiàn dào nǐ, wǒ dōu hěn kāixīn) — Every time I see you, I am happy

每一次 (měi yí cì) and 都 (dōu) are conventionally paired, but 都 is not universally obligatory.

再一次 + 动词 (zài yí cì + dòngcí)

Express one more repetition before a verb

我想再一次感谢你 (Wǒ xiǎng zài yí cì gǎnxiè nǐ) — I want to thank you once again

再一次 (zài yí cì) modifies the verb 感谢 (gǎnxiè).

再 + 动词 + 一次 (zài + dòngcí + yí cì)

Ask for or describe one more occurrence

再试一次 (zài shì yí cì) — Try one more time

Compare the postverbal count in 再试一次 (zài shì yí cì) with the preverbal adverbial 再一次 (zài yí cì).

Natural examples

Once: count an experience

我去过北京一次。 (Wǒ qù guo Běijīng yí cì.)

I’ve been to Beijing once.

过 (guo) marks experiential aspect, while 一次 (yí cì) counts the occurrence.

Last time: did not understand

上一次我没听懂。 (Shàng yí cì wǒ méi tīng dǒng.)

Last time, I didn’t understand what I heard.

没听懂 (méi tīng dǒng) indicates failure to achieve understanding by listening.

This time: understood

这一次我明白了。 (Zhè yí cì wǒ míngbai le.)

This time, I understood.

Sentence-final 了 (le) presents a newly attained state of understanding.

Next time: a suggestion

下一次我们一起去吧。 (Xià yí cì wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba.)

Let’s go together next time.

吧 (ba) softens the statement into a suggestion.

Every time: repeated experience

每一次见到你，我都很开心。 (Měi yí cì jiàn dào nǐ, wǒ dōu hěn kāixīn.)

Every time I see you, I’m very happy.

每一次 (měi yí cì) and 都 (dōu) form a conventional distributive pattern.

Once again: gratitude

我想再一次感谢你。 (Wǒ xiǎng zài yí cì gǎnxiè nǐ.)

I want to thank you once again.

再一次 (zài yí cì) modifies 感谢 (gǎnxiè) and expresses repetition.

Extra illustration: a particular occasion

上一次我去了北京。 (Shàng yí cì wǒ qù le Běijīng.)

Last time, I went to Beijing.

上一次 (shàng yí cì) identifies the occasion; compare 我去过北京一次 (Wǒ qù guo Běijīng yí cì), which counts occurrences.

Meaning contrasts

上次 (shàng cì) — last time

上次 (shàng cì)

Shorter form of 上一次 (shàng yí cì), often conversational.

Longer form can give emphasis to a specific occasion, not a different grammatical meaning.

这次 (zhè cì) — this time

这次 (zhè cì)

Shorter form of 这一次 (zhè yí cì), often conversational.

Shortened expressions are not obligatory; full forms are correct.

下次 (xià cì) — next time

下次 (xià cì)

Shorter form of 下一次 (xià yí cì), often conversational.

Both are natural; longer expression can be emphatic.

每次 (měi cì) — every time

每次 (měi cì)

Shorter form of 每一次 (měi yí cì), often conversational.

This is not a one-occurrence frequency complement when used as a temporal phrase.

再 (zài) — again; one more time

再试一次 (zài shì yí cì)

Adverb indicating repetition, often before a verb.

Not identical to 在 (zài), despite identical pronunciation.

在 (zài) — at; in; progressive marker

我在学习 (Wǒ zài xuéxí)

Marks location or an ongoing action in this example.

Not interchangeable with 再 (zài).

Common mistakes

Treat 去一次 (qù yí cì) and 上一次 (shàng yí cì) as the same syntactic structure.

Identify the slot: after a verb, 一次 (yí cì) counts how many times; before a clause, 上一次 (shàng yí cì) can identify when.

One is a frequency complement; the other is a temporal noun phrase.

Write the contextual pronunciation of 一次 as yī cì in tone-marked learning examples.

Write 一次 (yí cì) when teaching its ordinary spoken pronunciation; retain 一 (yī) as the dictionary reading of the individual character.

一 (yī) becomes yí before fourth tone 次 (cì).

Confuse 再 (zài, again) and 在 (zài, at/in or ongoing action).

Use 再试一次 (zài shì yí cì) for “try again” and 我在学习 (Wǒ zài xuéxí) for “I am studying”.

They are homophones with different grammatical functions.

Assume 上一次 (shàng yí cì) is incorrect because speakers often say 上次 (shàng cì).

Both are correct; the shorter alternative is especially common in conversation.

The longer variant can emphasize one particular occasion.

Treat 都 (dōu) as mandatory after every use of 每一次 (měi yí cì).

Learn 每……都…… (měi ... dōu ...) as a highly conventional pattern, not an absolute requirement.

Other syntactic configurations are also possible.

Describe 再来一次 (zài lái yí cì) as a shortened form of 再一次 (zài yí cì).

Teach it as a related verb phrase with 来 (lái), not a shortening.

The form and syntactic structure are different.

Practice

Comprehension

In 我去过北京一次 (Wǒ qù guo Běijīng yí cì), does 一次 (yí cì) answer “how many times” or “which occasion”?

Show answer
How many times: once.

一次 (yí cì) is a postverbal frequency complement.

Selection

Choose the common shorter form of 下一次 (xià yí cì).

a. 下次 (xià cì) — next time

b. 上次 (shàng cì) — last time

c. 这次 (zhè cì) — this time

Show answer
a

The initial 下 (xià) marks the following occasion.

Comprehension

Why is 一次 (yí cì) written with yí rather than yī in contextual pinyin?

Show answer
Because 次 (cì) has fourth tone, which normally changes 一 (yī) to yí.

Distinguish dictionary pronunciation from contextual tone sandhi.

Selection

Which expression means “try one more time”?

a. 再试一次 (zài shì yí cì) — try one more time

b. 我在学习 (Wǒ zài xuéxí) — I am studying

Show answer
a

Use 再 (zài) for repetition rather than 在 (zài).

Production

Say “Every time I see you, I’m very happy” using 每一次 (měi yí cì) and 都 (dōu).

Show answer
每一次见到你，我都很开心。(Měi yí cì jiàn dào nǐ, wǒ dōu hěn kāixīn.)

A temporal every-time phrase and a main clause with 都 (dōu) express recurrence.
