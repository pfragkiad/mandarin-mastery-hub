# 以 (yǐ) — Common Words & Grammatical Uses

Learn established words containing 以 (yǐ), including 可以 (kěyǐ), 所以 (suǒyǐ), and 以为 (yǐwéi), then explore formal grammatical constructions, lexicalization, and register differences from 用 (yòng).

**Topics:** grammar-functions

## Lesson focus

Learn common complete words containing 以 (yǐ), then recognize formal patterns expressing means or taking one thing as another.

## At a glance

Occurs frequently in established words such as 可以 (kěyǐ), 所以 (suǒyǐ), 以前 (yǐqián), and 以后 (yǐhòu).

The character does not map to one English word in every context. It participates in many complete words and some reusable constructions; learn the complete modern words before trying to interpret the character in isolation.

## Essential words and expressions

### 可以 (kěyǐ) — Can; may; be permitted to

Lexicalized modal verb; the historical components are not a productive modern rule.

### 所以 (suǒyǐ) — So; therefore

An established connective expressing a result or conclusion.

### 以后 (yǐhòu) — After; later; in the future

Often follows a time phrase or an event.

### 以前 (yǐqián) — Before; previously; in the past

Often used before or after a temporal reference.

### 以为 (yǐwéi) — Think; assume

Often implies that an assumption was wrong, but this is a tendency, not a rule.

### 以上 (yǐshàng) — Above; at least; or more

Boundary inclusion can depend on the construction and context.

### 以下 (yǐxià) — Below; at most; or less

Boundary interpretation depends on context.

### 以内 (yǐnèi) — Within; inside a limit

Time, space, or quantity.

### 以外 (yǐwài) — Outside; apart from; except

Location or exclusion depends on context.

### 以及 (yǐjí) — As well as; and

Connects parallel terms or groups.

### 以便 (yǐbiàn) — So that; in order to

Expresses a desirable purpose or convenience.

### 以免 (yǐmiǎn) — So as to avoid; lest

Introduces an undesirable outcome to prevent.

## Common constructions

### 以 + 名词短语 (yǐ + míngcí duǎnyǔ)

Using or by a particular means

以书面形式提交 (yǐ shūmiàn xíngshì tíjiāo) — Submit in writing

Abstract means and manner are frequent; it is not restricted to abstract nouns.

### 以 X 为 Y (yǐ X wéi Y)

Take or treat X as Y

以他为榜样 (yǐ tā wéi bǎngyàng) — Take him as a role model

X and Y denote variable constituents, not necessarily single characters.

### 以 X 为主 (yǐ X wéi zhǔ)

Make X the principal focus

以学习为主 (yǐ xuéxí wéi zhǔ) — Focus mainly on studying

Keep the fixed element 为主 (wéi zhǔ) distinct from the general slots.

### 以 X 为例 (yǐ X wéi lì)

Use X as an example

以中国为例 (yǐ Zhōngguó wéi lì) — Take China as an example

Keep the fixed element 为例 (wéi lì) distinct from the general slots.

## Natural examples

### Asking permission

**我可以进去吗？**
*Wǒ kěyǐ jìnqù ma?*
May I come in?
**Usage:** Treat the two-character modal as a single word.

### Explaining a result

**今天下雨，所以我不出去。**
*Jīntiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ bù chūqù.*
It's raining today, so I'm not going out.
**Usage:** A connective linking reason and result.

### After a time

**我三点以后有时间。**
*Wǒ sān diǎn yǐhòu yǒu shíjiān.*
I'm free after three o'clock.
**Usage:** The temporal word follows a time expression.

### Talking about the past

**我以前住在北京。**
*Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng.*
I used to live in Beijing.
**Usage:** Marks an earlier period; the present location remains unspecified.

### A potentially wrong assumption

**我以为你不来了。**
*Wǒ yǐwéi nǐ bù lái le.*
I thought you weren't coming.
**Usage:** The assumption commonly turns out wrong in such contexts, but this is not mandatory.

### Formal instructions

**请以书面形式提交申请。**
*Qǐng yǐ shūmiàn xíngshì tíjiāo shēnqǐng.*
Please submit your application in writing.
**Usage:** A means or manner prepositional phrase.

### Main focus of a course

**这门课以口语练习为主。**
*Zhè mén kè yǐ kǒuyǔ liànxí wéi zhǔ.*
This course focuses mainly on speaking practice.
**Usage:** Recognize the established subpattern as a unit.

## Meaning contrasts

### 用 (yòng) — Use; with

Natural with concrete instruments in ordinary speech.

Frequently conversational; not freely interchangeable with formal means-constructions.

### 以 (yǐ) — By; by means of

Often describes abstract means or manners.

Especially written/formal in instrumental use; concrete nouns are not categorically excluded.

### 以为 (yǐwéi) — Think; assume

Often used when an expectation is contradicted.

That implication is common but not obligatory.

### 认为 (rènwéi) — Consider; hold the opinion

Often for an assessment or opinion.

Does not inherently carry the same mistaken-expectation tendency.

### 觉得 (juéde) — Feel; think

Common for subjective impressions.

Different usage emphasis from holding an argued opinion.

## Common mistakes

**Misconception:** Translate every occurrence of 以 (yǐ) as “using”.

**Correct:** Identify the complete word or grammatical construction first.

For instance, 所以 (suǒyǐ) is an established conjunction meaning “so; therefore”.

**Misconception:** Apply 可 (kě) + 以 (yǐ) as a productive rule to form new modal verbs.

**Correct:** Learn 可以 (kěyǐ) as one word.

Historical lexicalization does not create a general productive beginner rule.

**Misconception:** Treat 以为 (yǐwéi) as an unfilled 以 X 为 Y (yǐ X wéi Y) pattern.

**Correct:** Differentiate lexicalized words and grammatical constructions.

The modern verb is a lexical unit, whereas the pattern is a construction.

**Misconception:** Assume 以为 (yǐwéi) *always* means a mistaken assumption.

**Correct:** Interpret the discourse context.

Often—but not invariably—suggests a contradicted assumption.

**Misconception:** Swap 以 (yǐ) and 用 (yòng) in every sentence.

**Correct:** Check register and noun collocations.

Comparable English glosses do not imply unrestricted interchangeability.

## Historical context

秦亦不以城予赵。 (Qín yì bù yǐ chéng yǔ Zhào.) — Qin likewise did not give the cities to Zhao.

The phrase 以城 (yǐ chéng) identifies what would be given. The comparison with 把 (bǎ) is restricted to this kind of construction.

## Practice

**Comprehension:** In 我以前住在北京 (Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng), does the speaker refer to present or earlier residence?
**Answer:** Earlier residence; current location is unspecified.. The temporal expression 以前 (yǐqián) points to an earlier period.

**Selection:** Which sounds natural for casual “eat with chopsticks”?
- a. 用筷子吃饭 (yòng kuàizi chīfàn) — Eat with chopsticks
- b. 以筷子吃饭 (yǐ kuàizi chīfàn) — Unnatural as an everyday conversational phrasing
**Answer:** a. 用 (yòng) is the ordinary choice with a concrete tool in this casual context.

**Production:** Translate “May I come in?” using 可以 (kěyǐ).
**Answer:** 我可以进去吗？(Wǒ kěyǐ jìnqù ma?). The complete modal verb expresses permission.
