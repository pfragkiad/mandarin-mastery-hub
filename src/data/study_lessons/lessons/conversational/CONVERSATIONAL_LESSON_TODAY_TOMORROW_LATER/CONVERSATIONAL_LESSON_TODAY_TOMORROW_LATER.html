Conversational Mandarin 15 min

Today, Tomorrow, and Later

Moving plans from now to later or tomorrow

Learn to postpone an activity until later today or suggest meeting tomorrow instead.

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish now, later, tonight, and tomorrow.
  • Move an invitation to another day.
  • Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

Study guidance

Learn to postpone an activity until later today or suggest meeting tomorrow instead. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud. Pronunciation note: This course uses some northern-style 儿化 (érhuà) forms. The final -r belongs to the preceding syllable, not a separate syllable. In 一会儿 (yíhuìr), the -r is attached to 会 (huì); do not read 儿 (ér) as an extra syllable.

Model conversation

A

你想现在吃吗？

Nǐ xiǎng xiànzài chī ma?

Do you want to eat now?

B

现在不行。

Xiànzài bù xíng.

Now does not work.

A

那么明天晚上，好吗？

Nàme míngtiān wǎnshang, hǎo ma?

Then tomorrow evening, okay?

B

好，明天见！

Hǎo, míngtiān jiàn!

Okay, see you tomorrow!

Grammar structure and spoken example

时间词 + 主语 + 动词 (shíjiāncí + zhǔyǔ + dòngcí)

<time word or phrase> + <who or what> + <action word>

Time words often precede the main verb or follow the topic.

明天我想去。 (míngtiān wǒ xiǎng qù.) — I want to go tomorrow.

Mentioning tomorrow first highlights when the planned action will happen.

Grammar structure and spoken example

过 + 一会儿 + 动词 (guò + yíhuìr + dòngcí)

过 + 一会儿 + <action word>

This phrase means after a short while; 一 (yī) is pronounced yí before fourth-tone 会 (huì).

过一会儿见。 (guò yíhuìr jiàn.) — See you in a little while.

Use this when proposing to meet shortly rather than immediately.

Key expressions and vocabulary

现在 (xiànzài) — Now.
什么时候 (shénme shíhou) — When?
过一会儿Northern-style -r (guò yíhuìr) — In a little while; later.
今天 (jīntiān) — Today.
今天晚上 (jīntiān wǎnshang) — Tonight.
明天 (míngtiān) — Tomorrow.
明天晚上 (míngtiān wǎnshang) — Tomorrow evening.
现在还是过一会儿Northern-style -r (xiànzài háishi guò yíhuìr) — Now or a little later?
明天好吗 (míngtiān hǎo ma) — Would tomorrow be okay?
明天见 (míngtiān jiàn) — See you tomorrow.
明天呢 (míngtiān ne) — What about tomorrow?
现在吗 (xiànzài ma) — Now?
过一会儿好吗Northern-style -r (guò yíhuìr hǎo ma) — Would a little later be okay?
过一会儿不好Northern-style -r (guò yíhuìr bù hǎo) — Later is not a good time.
Usage note · Context
In the source scheduling exchange, this means that a slightly later time is not good. A more explicit way to say that the time is inconvenient is 过一会儿不方便 (guò yíhuìr bù fāngbiàn). The 儿化 (érhuà) ending in 一会儿 (yíhuìr) reflects northern-leaning spoken usage.
我想现在喝 (wǒ xiǎng xiànzài hē) — I want to drink now.
今天晚上我不想吃东西 (jīntiān wǎnshang wǒ bù xiǎng chī dōngxi) — I do not want to eat anything tonight.
明天晚上不行 (míngtiān wǎnshang bù xíng) — Tomorrow evening doesn't work.
太晚 (tài wǎn) — too late
现在太晚了 (xiànzài tài wǎn le) — it is too late now
十点钟 (shí diǎn zhōng) — ten o’clock

Study guidance

Speaking task: Postpone plans from tonight to tomorrow, or from now to later.

Dinner Tonight or Tomorrow

A

今天晚上一起吃晚饭，好吗？

Jīntiān wǎnshang yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn, hǎo ma?

Shall we have dinner together tonight?

B

对不起，我今天晚上没时间。

Duìbuqǐ, wǒ jīntiān wǎnshang méi shíjiān.

Sorry, I do not have time tonight.

A

那么明天晚上七点呢？

Nàme míngtiān wǎnshang qī diǎn ne?

Then how about seven tomorrow evening?

B

七点很好，十点就太晚了。

Qī diǎn hěn hǎo, shí diǎn jiù tài wǎn le.

Seven is great; ten would be too late.
