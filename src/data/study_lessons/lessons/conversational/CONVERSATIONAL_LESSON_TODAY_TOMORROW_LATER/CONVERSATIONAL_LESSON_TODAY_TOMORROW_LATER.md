# Today, Tomorrow, and Later

Practice distinguish now, later, tonight, and tomorrow. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish now, later, tonight, and tomorrow.

- Move an invitation to another day.

## Explanation

Practice distinguish now, later, tonight, and tomorrow. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想现在吃吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng xiànzài chī ma?) — Do you want to eat now?

**B:** 现在不行。 (Xiànzài bù xíng.) — Now does not work.

**A:** 那么明天晚上，好吗？ (Nàme míngtiān wǎnshang, hǎo ma?) — Then tomorrow evening, okay?

**B:** 好，明天见！ (Hǎo, míngtiān jiàn!) — Okay, see you tomorrow!

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 今天 / 明天 (jīntiān / míngtiān)

Time words often precede the main verb or follow the topic.

**明天我想去。**
*míngtiān wǒ xiǎng qù.*
I want to go tomorrow.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 过一会儿 (guò yíhuìr)

This phrase means after a short while; 一 (yī) is pronounced yí before fourth-tone 会 (huì).

**过一会儿见。**
*guò yíhuìr jiàn.*
See you in a little while.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

什么时候 (shénme shíhou) — When?

今天 (jīntiān) — Today.

今天晚上 (jīntiān wǎnshang) — Tonight.

明天 (míngtiān) — Tomorrow.

明天晚上 (míngtiān wǎnshang) — Tomorrow evening.

现在还是过一会儿 (xiànzài háishi guò yíhuìr) — Now or a little later?

明天好吗 (míngtiān hǎo ma) — Would tomorrow be okay?

明天见 (míngtiān jiàn) — See you tomorrow.

明天呢 (míngtiān ne) — What about tomorrow?

现在吗 (xiànzài ma) — Now?

过一会儿好吗 (guò yíhuìr hǎo ma) — Would a little later be okay?

过一会儿不好 (guò yíhuìr bù hǎo) — Later is not a good time.

我想现在喝 (wǒ xiǎng xiànzài hē) — I want to drink now.

今天晚上我不想吃东西 (jīntiān wǎnshang wǒ bù xiǎng chī dōngxi) — I do not want to eat anything tonight.

明天晚上不行 (míngtiān wǎnshang bù xíng) — Tomorrow evening doesn't work.

## Explanation

Speaking task: Postpone plans from tonight to tomorrow, or from now to later.
