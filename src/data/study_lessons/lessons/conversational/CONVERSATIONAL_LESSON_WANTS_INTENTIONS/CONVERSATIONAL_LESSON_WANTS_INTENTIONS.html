Expressing Wants and Intentions

Offering food, declining, and accepting a drink

grammar-functions · daily-actions · ↳ everyday-routines · #wants · #intentions · #offers

Learning objectives

  • Use 想 and 要 for wants and intentions.
  • Decline an offer naturally.

Explanation

Learn to say what you want, decline food politely, and accept an offered drink. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A: 你想吃一点东西吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi ma?) — Would you like something to eat?

B: 我不想吃。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī.) — I do not want to eat.

A: 你想喝一点茶吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá ma?) — Would you like some tea?

B: 好，我想喝一点茶。 (Hǎo, wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — Yes, I would like a little tea.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 我想吃午饭。 (wǒ xiǎng chī wǔfàn.)

想 (xiǎng) expresses a wish or desire followed by a verb.

我想吃午饭。 (wǒ xiǎng chī wǔfàn.)

I want to eat lunch.

想 (xiǎng) expresses a wish or desire followed by a verb.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 你想不想喝茶？ (nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng hē chá?)

An A-not-A question asks whether someone wants to do something.

你想不想喝茶？ (nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng hē chá?)

Do you want to drink tea?

An A-not-A question asks whether someone wants to do something.

Explanation

Speaking task: Offer food and drink, accept one offer and politely decline another.

Practice

Comprehension

How does speaker B decline the offer of food?

Show answer
我不想吃。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī.) — I do not want to eat.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

Production

Translate into Mandarin: I want to eat.

Show answer
我想吃 (wǒ xiǎng chī)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

Production

Ask someone whether they would like tea, using 想不想 (xiǎng bu xiǎng).

Show answer
你想不想喝茶？ (Nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng hē chá?) — Would you like to drink tea?

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

Production

Offer food and drink, accept one offer and politely decline another.

Show answer
A: 你想吃一点东西吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi ma?) — Would you like something to eat? B: 我不想吃，谢谢。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī, xièxie.) — I do not want to eat, thank you. A: 你想喝一点茶吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá ma?) — Would you like some tea? B: 好，我想喝一点茶。 (Hǎo, wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — Yes, I would like some tea.
