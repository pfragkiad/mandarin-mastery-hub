# Expressing Wants and Intentions

Learn to say what you want, decline food politely, and accept an offered drink.

*Offering food, declining, and accepting a drink*

## Learning objectives

- Use 想 and 要 for wants and intentions.
- Decline an offer naturally.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to say what you want, decline food politely, and accept an offered drink. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想吃一点东西吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi ma?) — Would you like something to eat?

**B:** 我不想吃。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī.) — I do not want to eat.

**A:** 你想喝一点茶吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá ma?) — Would you like some tea?

**B:** 好，我想喝一点茶。 (Hǎo, wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — Yes, I would like a little tea.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 想 + 动作** (zhǔyǔ + xiǎng + dòngzuò)

想 (xiǎng) expresses a wish or desire followed by a verb.

我想吃午饭。 (wǒ xiǎng chī wǔfàn.) — I want to eat lunch.
A useful opening when suggesting what to eat or do next.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**想不想 + 动作？** (xiǎng bu xiǎng + dòngzuò?)

An A-not-A question asks whether someone wants to do something.

你想不想喝茶？ (nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng hē chá?) — Do you want to drink tea?
The listener can answer 想 (xiǎng) or 不想 (bù xiǎng).

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 我想 (wǒ xiǎng) — I would like to; I want to.
- 你想 (nǐ xiǎng) — You would like to; you want to.
- 我不想 (wǒ bù xiǎng) — I do not want to.
- 我想吃 (wǒ xiǎng chī) — I want to eat.
- 我想喝一点东西 (wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn dōngxi) — I would like something to drink.
- 你想买什么 (nǐ xiǎng mǎi shénme) — What do you want to buy?
- 我想要一点茶 (wǒ xiǎng yào yìdiǎn chá) — I would like a little tea.
- 你想不想 (nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng) — Do you want to (or not)?
- 我不要 (wǒ bú yào) — I do not want it.
- 你想吃一点东西吗 (nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi ma) — Would you like something to eat?
- 我想吃一点东西 (wǒ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi) — I would like something to eat.
- 我不想现在吃 (wǒ bù xiǎng xiànzài chī) — I do not want to eat now.
- 我想现在喝 (wǒ xiǎng xiànzài hē) — I would like to drink now.
- 我现在要吃午饭 (wǒ xiànzài yào chī wǔfàn) — I am going to have lunch now.
- 我不要吃 (wǒ bú yào chī) — I do not want to eat.
- 你想什么时候吃 (nǐ xiǎng shénme shíhou chī) — When would you like to eat?
- 一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) — a little (optional -r pronunciation)  [Northern-style -r]
- 可是 (kěshì) — but; however
- 你想做什么 (nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme) — what would you like to do?
- 你想喝一点东西吗 (nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn dōngxi ma) — would you like something to drink?
- 我现在想吃午饭 (wǒ xiànzài xiǎng chī wǔfàn) — I would like to have lunch now.

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Offer food and drink, accept one offer and politely decline another.

## Eating Now or Drinking Later

**A:** 你现在想吃东西吗？ (Nǐ xiànzài xiǎng chī dōngxi ma?) — Do you want something to eat now?

**B:** 我不想吃，我想喝一点儿水。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī, wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎnr shuǐ.) — I do not want to eat; I would like a little water.

**A:** 一会儿一起吃午饭，好吗？ (Yíhuìr yìqǐ chī wǔfàn, hǎo ma?) — Shall we have lunch together in a little while?

**B:** 好，我一会儿有时间。 (Hǎo, wǒ yíhuìr yǒu shíjiān.) — Sure. I have time a little later.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** How does speaker B decline the offer of food?

**Model answer:**
我不想吃。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī.) — I do not want to eat.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: I want to eat.

**Model answer:**
我想吃 (wǒ xiǎng chī)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Ask someone whether they would like tea, using 想不想 (xiǎng bu xiǎng).

**Model answer:**
你想不想喝茶？ (Nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng hē chá?) — Would you like to drink tea?

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Offer food and drink, accept one offer and politely decline another.

**Model answer:**
A: 你想吃一点东西吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi ma?) — Would you like something to eat?
B: 我不想吃，谢谢。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī, xièxie.) — I do not want to eat, thank you.
A: 你想喝一点茶吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá ma?) — Would you like some tea?
B: 好，我想喝一点茶。 (Hǎo, wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — Yes, I would like some tea.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
