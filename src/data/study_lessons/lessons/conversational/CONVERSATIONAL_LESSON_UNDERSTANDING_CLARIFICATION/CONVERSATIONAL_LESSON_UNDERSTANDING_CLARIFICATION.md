# Asking for Clarification

Learn to say when you do not understand and ask someone to clarify their meaning.

*Understanding, repeating, and asking what was said*

## Learning objectives

- Say that you do or do not understand.
- Ask what somebody said.

## Study guidance

Learn to say when you do not understand and ask someone to clarify their meaning. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你明白吗？ (Nǐ míngbai ma?) — Do you understand?

**B:** 我不明白。 (Wǒ bù míngbai.) — I do not understand.

**B:** 你说什么？ (Nǐ shuō shénme?) — What did you say?

**A:** 我说普通话。 (Wǒ shuō Pǔtōnghuà.) — I am speaking Mandarin.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 不 + 心理动词** (zhǔyǔ + bù + xīnlǐ dòngcí)

Negate a mental state using 不 (bù); the next syllable is not fourth tone here.

我不明白。 (wǒ bù míngbai.) — I do not understand.
This is a natural way to signal difficulty following what someone says.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 说 + 什么？** (zhǔyǔ + shuō + shénme?)

什么 (shénme) occupies the position of the unknown thing in the sentence.

你说什么？ (nǐ shuō shénme?) — What did you say?
In a conversation, this asks the other person to clarify their words.

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 我明白 (wǒ míngbai) — I understand.
- 我不明白 (wǒ bù míngbai) — I do not understand.
- 你明白吗 (nǐ míngbai ma) — Do you understand?
- 我不知道 (wǒ bù zhīdào) — I do not know.
- 你知道吗 (nǐ zhīdào ma) — Do you know?
- 我不明白你说什么 (wǒ bù míngbai nǐ shuō shénme) — I do not understand what you are saying.
- 你说什么 (nǐ shuō shénme) — What did you say?
- 我普通话说得不好 (wǒ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de bù hǎo) — I do not speak Mandarin well.
- 你明白 (nǐ míngbai) — You understand.
- 我知道 (wǒ zhīdào) — I know.
- 你不知道 (nǐ bù zhīdào) — You do not know.
- 你不明白 (nǐ bù míngbai) — You do not understand.
- 说得不好 (shuō de bù hǎo) — To speak poorly.
- 不是很好 (bú shì hěn hǎo) — not very good
- 可是 (kěshì) — but; however

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Tell someone you do not understand and ask what they said.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** How does speaker B say they do not understand?

**Model answer:**
我不明白。 (Wǒ bù míngbai.) — I do not understand.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: I do not know.

**Model answer:**
我不知道 (wǒ bù zhīdào)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Ask someone what they just said, using 什么 (shénme).

**Model answer:**
你说什么？ (Nǐ shuō shénme?) — What did you say?

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Tell someone you do not understand and ask what they said.

**Model answer:**
A: 你明白吗？ (Nǐ míngbai ma?) — Do you understand?
B: 我不明白。你说什么？ (Wǒ bù míngbai. Nǐ shuō shénme?) — I do not understand. What did you say?
A: 我说我们明天见。 (Wǒ shuō wǒmen míngtiān jiàn.) — I said we will see each other tomorrow.
B: 好，我明白了。谢谢！ (Hǎo, wǒ míngbai le. Xièxie!) — Okay, now I understand. Thank you!

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
