# Asking Permission Politely

Learn to ask permission, request an item politely, and respond appropriately.

*Requests, giving things, and permission to act*

## Learning objectives

- Ask whether someone can do something.
- Ask someone to give you an amount or item.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to ask permission, request an item politely, and respond appropriately. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你可不可以给我一点钱？ (Nǐ kě bu kěyǐ gěi wǒ yìdiǎn qián?) — Could you give me some money?

**B:** 可以，我给你十块人民币。 (Kěyǐ, wǒ gěi nǐ shí kuài rénmínbì.) — Yes, I can. I will give you ten yuan.

**A:** 谢谢你！ (Xièxie nǐ!) — Thank you!

**B:** 不客气。 (Bú kèqi.) — You are welcome.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**可不可以 + 动作？** (kě bu kěyǐ + dòngzuò?)

An A-not-A form of 可以 (kěyǐ) asks about possibility or permission.

你可不可以买茶？ (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ mǎi chá?) — Can you buy tea?
Ask whether an action is possible or allowed in the situation.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**给 + 人 + 数量 + 物品** (gěi + rén + shùliàng + wùpǐn)

给 (gěi) can mean to give something to someone.

给我一点茶。 (gěi wǒ yìdiǎn chá.) — Give me a little tea.
In a polite request, consider adding 请 (qǐng) before the verb.

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 我可以 (wǒ kěyǐ) — I can; I am allowed to.
- 你可以 (nǐ kěyǐ) — You can; you are allowed to.
- 你可不可以 (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ) — Can you?; may you?
- 你可不可以现在吃 (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ xiànzài chī) — Can you eat now?
- 我不可以 (wǒ bù kěyǐ) — I cannot; I am not allowed to.
- 给我一点人民币 (gěi wǒ yìdiǎn rénmínbì) — Give me a little RMB.
- 你可不可以给我一点钱 (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ gěi wǒ yìdiǎn qián) — Could you give me some money?
- 我给你十块美金 (wǒ gěi nǐ shí kuài měijīn) — I will give you ten US dollars.
- 不客气 (bú kèqi) — You are welcome.
- 你可不可以买很多啤酒 (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ mǎi hěn duō píjiǔ) — Can you buy a lot of beer?
- 我可以买很多啤酒 (wǒ kěyǐ mǎi hěn duō píjiǔ) — I can buy a lot of beer.
- 你可不可以去买一点啤酒 (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ qù mǎi yìdiǎn píjiǔ) — Can you go buy some beer?
- 我可以去买一点啤酒 (wǒ kěyǐ qù mǎi yìdiǎn píjiǔ) — I can go buy some beer.
- 我不可以给你 (wǒ bù kěyǐ gěi nǐ) — I cannot give it to you.
- 给我三块人民币 (gěi wǒ sān kuài rénmínbì) — Give me three yuan.
- 我给你五块美金 (wǒ gěi nǐ wǔ kuài měijīn) — I will give you five US dollars.
- 可不可以 (kě bu kěyǐ) — may I?; can or cannot?
- 是不是 (shì bú shì) — is it or not?
- 有没有 (yǒu méiyǒu) — do you have?; is there any?
- 给你 (gěi nǐ) — here you are; give to you
- 太多了 (tài duō le) — that is too much

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Ask politely for an item and respond with permission, refusal or thanks.

## Asking Whether You May Pay

**A:** 请问，可不可以用人民币付钱？ (Qǐngwèn, kě bu kěyǐ yòng rénmínbì fù qián?) — Excuse me, may I pay with yuan?

**B:** 可以。你有现金吗？ (Kěyǐ. Nǐ yǒu xiànjīn ma?) — Yes. Do you have cash?

**A:** 我有钱，可是只有十块。 (Wǒ yǒu qián, kěshì zhǐ yǒu shí kuài.) — I have money, but only ten yuan.

**B:** 十块不够，这个要十五块。 (Shí kuài bú gòu, zhège yào shíwǔ kuài.) — Ten yuan is not enough. This costs fifteen yuan.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 可不可以 + 动词 + 物品？** (zhǔyǔ + kě bu kěyǐ + dòngcí + wùpǐn?)

可不可以 (kě bu kěyǐ) asks for permission or possibility as an A-not-A question.

我可不可以用美金？ (Wǒ kě bu kěyǐ yòng měijīn?) — May I use US dollars?
Here the customer is checking whether paying in US dollars is acceptable.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** What does speaker B say when giving permission?

**Model answer:**
可以，我给你十块人民币。 (Kěyǐ, wǒ gěi nǐ shí kuài rénmínbì.) — Yes, I can. I will give you ten yuan.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: Can you eat now?

**Model answer:**
你可不可以现在吃 (nǐ kě bu kěyǐ xiànzài chī)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Politely ask someone to give you some tea, using 给 (gěi).

**Model answer:**
请给我一点茶。 (Qǐng gěi wǒ yìdiǎn chá.) — Please give me some tea.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Ask politely for an item and respond with permission, refusal or thanks.

**Model answer:**
A: 请问，你可不可以给我一点茶？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ kě bu kěyǐ gěi wǒ yìdiǎn chá?) — Excuse me, could you give me some tea?
B: 可以，给你。 (Kěyǐ, gěi nǐ.) — Yes, here you are.
A: 谢谢！我可以在这儿喝吗？ (Xièxie! Wǒ kěyǐ zài zhèr hē ma?) — Thank you! May I drink it here?
B: 可以。 (Kěyǐ.) — Yes, you may.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
