Conversational Mandarin 20 min

Buying a Newspaper

Checking availability, prices, and currencies

Learn to ask for a newspaper, confirm its price, and respond when you lack the right currency.

Learning objectives

  • Ask whether a shop sells a particular kind of newspaper.
  • Confirm prices using positive-negative questions.
  • Explain currency limitations and end the transaction politely.

Study guidance

Learn to ask for a newspaper, confirm its price, and respond when you lack the right currency. practice the new dialogue and the examples, then complete the role-play.

Model conversation

A

您好，请问你们有没有美国报纸？

Nín hǎo, qǐngwèn nǐmen yǒu méiyǒu Měiguó bàozhǐ?

Hello, do you have American newspapers?

B

有，也有英文报纸。

Yǒu, yě yǒu Yīngwén bàozhǐ.

Yes, and we also have English-language newspapers.

A

这份报纸是不是十八块？

Zhè fèn bàozhǐ shì bú shì shíbā kuài?

Does this newspaper cost eighteen yuan?

B

不是十八块，是八块人民币。

Bú shì shíbā kuài, shì bā kuài rénmínbì.

Not eighteen; it is eight yuan.

A

我有美金，可是没有人民币。

Wǒ yǒu měijīn, kěshì méiyǒu rénmínbì.

I have US dollars but no yuan.

B

这里只收人民币。

Zhèlǐ zhǐ shōu rénmínbì.

We only accept yuan here.

A

那么我一会儿再来，谢谢。Northern-style -r

Nàme wǒ yíhuìr zài lái, xièxie.

Then I will come back in a little while, thank you.

B

不客气！

Bú kèqi!

You are welcome!

Grammar structure and spoken example

有没有 + 物品？ (yǒu méiyǒu + wùpǐn?)

有没有 (yǒu méiyǒu) is the A-not-A form of 有 (yǒu), used for availability or possession.

你有没有今天的报纸？ (Nǐ yǒu méiyǒu jīntiān de bàozhǐ?) — Do you have today’s newspaper?

Replace newspaper with another item when asking whether it is in stock.

Grammar structure and spoken example

物品 + 是不是 + 价格？ (wùpǐn + shì bú shì + jiàgé?)

是不是 (shì bú shì) asks for confirmation without a final 吗 (ma).

这个是不是八块？ (Zhège shì bú shì bā kuài?) — Is this eight yuan?

Use this to check the amount you think you heard.

Grammar structure and spoken example

数字 + 不是 + 另一个数字 (shùzì + bú shì + lìng yí ge shùzì)

Check numbers carefully: 八 (bā) is eight, whereas 十八 (shíbā) is eighteen.

八块，不是十八块。 (Bā kuài, bú shì shíbā kuài.) — Eight yuan, not eighteen yuan.

Repeat the corrected number if the buyer misheard the price.

Key expressions and vocabulary

报纸 (bàozhǐ) — newspaper
美国报纸 (Měiguó bàozhǐ) — American newspaper
有没有 (yǒu méiyǒu) — have or not have; do you have?
是不是 (shì bú shì) — is it or not?
八块 (bā kuài) — eight yuan
十八块 (shíbā kuài) — eighteen yuan
人民币 (rénmínbì) — renminbi
美金 (měijīn) — US dollars
给你 (gěi nǐ) — here you are
太多 (tài duō) — too much
不够 (bú gòu) — not enough
没有 (méiyǒu) — do not have

Practice

Question 1 · comprehension

What price does the shopkeeper confirm?

Show model answer
八块人民币。 (Bā kuài rénmínbì.) — Eight yuan.

Do not confuse eight with eighteen.

Question 2 · production

Ask if the shop has American newspapers.

Show model answer
请问，你们有没有美国报纸？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐmen yǒu méiyǒu Měiguó bàozhǐ?) — Excuse me, do you have American newspapers?

Use 有没有 (yǒu méiyǒu) for availability.

Question 3 · production

Ask for confirmation that a newspaper costs eighteen yuan.

Show model answer
这份报纸是不是十八块？ (Zhè fèn bàozhǐ shì bú shì shíbā kuài?) — Is this newspaper eighteen yuan?

The 是不是 (shì bú shì) form asks for confirmation.

Question 4 · production

Ask for a newspaper, verify its price, explain your currency problem, then close politely.

Show model answer
A: 您好，请问有报纸吗？ (Nín hǎo, qǐngwèn yǒu bàozhǐ ma?) — Hello. Do you have newspapers? B: 有，一份八块。 (Yǒu, yí fèn bā kuài.) — Yes, eight yuan each. A: 是不是十八块？ (Shì bú shì shíbā kuài?) — Is it eighteen yuan? B: 不是，是八块。 (Bú shì, shì bā kuài.) — No, it is eight yuan. A: 我只有美金，没有人民币。我一会儿再来，谢谢！ (Wǒ zhǐ yǒu měijīn, méiyǒu rénmínbì. Wǒ yíhuìr zài lái, xièxie!) — I only have dollars, no yuan. I will return shortly, thanks! B: 不客气，再见！ (Bú kèqi, zàijiàn!) — You are welcome. Goodbye!

A complete buying exchange covers availability, correction, payment and farewell.
