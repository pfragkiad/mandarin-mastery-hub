Counting and Simple Arithmetic

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

numbers-quantities · #arithmetic · #counting · #large-numbers · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Recognize numbers from one to forty-plus.
  • Ask and answer simple addition questions.

Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

Model conversation

A: 二加三是多少？ (Èr jiā sān shì duōshao?) — What is two plus three?

B: 五。 (Wǔ.) — Five.

A: 六加六是多少？ (Liù jiā liù shì duōshao?) — What is six plus six?

B: 十二。 (Shí’èr.) — Twelve.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: A 加 B 是多少 (A jiā B shì duōshao)

Use 加 (jiā) for addition; 多少 (duōshao) asks for the result.

六加六是多少？ (liù jiā liù shì duōshao?)

What is six plus six?

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 十 + 数字 (shí shùzì)

Numbers 11–19 use ten plus a digit, while 20–99 use tens plus a digit.

二十五 (èrshíwǔ)

Twenty-five.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.
