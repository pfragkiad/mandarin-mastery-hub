# Counting and Simple Addition

Learn to say two-digit numbers and ask or answer simple addition questions.

*Number combinations and mental arithmetic*

## Learning objectives

- Recognize numbers from one to forty-plus.
- Ask and answer simple addition questions.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to say two-digit numbers and ask or answer simple addition questions. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 二加三是多少？ (Èr jiā sān shì duōshao?) — What is two plus three?

**B:** 五。 (Wǔ.) — Five.

**A:** 六加六是多少？ (Liù jiā liù shì duōshao?) — What is six plus six?

**B:** 十二。 (Shí'èr.) — Twelve.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**数字 + 加 + 数字 + 是多少？** (shùzì + jiā + shùzì + shì duōshao?)

Use 加 (jiā) for addition; 多少 (duōshao) asks for the result.

六加六是多少？ (liù jiā liù shì duōshao?) — What is six plus six?
Use the pattern to ask for a total during a purchase.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**数字 + 十 + 数字** (shùzì + shí + shùzì)

Numbers 11–19 use ten plus a digit, while 20–99 use tens plus a digit.

二十五 (èrshíwǔ) — Twenty-five.
二十五 (èrshíwǔ) consists of two tens plus five.

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Ask and answer three addition questions in Mandarin.

## Checking Prices with Two-Digit Numbers

**A:** 这是十三块还是三十块？ (Zhè shì shísān kuài háishi sānshí kuài?) — Is this thirteen or thirty yuan?

**B:** 十三块，不是三十块。 (Shísān kuài, bú shì sānshí kuài.) — Thirteen yuan, not thirty.

**A:** 那两件一共多少钱？ (Nà liǎng jiàn yígòng duōshao qián?) — Then how much are two items altogether?

**B:** 一共二十六块。 (Yígòng èrshíliù kuài.) — Twenty-six yuan altogether.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** In the model dialogue, what is the result of the first addition?

**Model answer:**
五。 (Wǔ.) — Five.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: What is sixteen plus four?

**Model answer:**
十六加四是多少 (shíliù jiā sì shì duōshao)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Write the number twenty-five in Chinese and give its pinyin.

**Model answer:**
二十五 (èrshíwǔ) — twenty-five.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Ask and answer three addition questions in Mandarin.

**Model answer:**
A: 三加五是多少？ (Sān jiā wǔ shì duōshao?) — What is three plus five?
B: 八。 (Bā.) — Eight.
A: 十六加四是多少？ (Shíliù jiā sì shì duōshao?) — What is sixteen plus four?
B: 二十。 (Èrshí.) — Twenty.
A: 十二加七是多少？ (Shí'èr jiā qī shì duōshao?) — What is twelve plus seven?
B: 十九。 (Shíjiǔ.) — Nineteen.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
