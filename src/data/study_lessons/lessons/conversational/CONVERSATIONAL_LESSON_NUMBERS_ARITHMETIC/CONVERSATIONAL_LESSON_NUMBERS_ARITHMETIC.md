# Counting and Simple Arithmetic

Practice recognize numbers from one to forty-plus. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Recognize numbers from one to forty-plus.

- Ask and answer simple addition questions.

## Explanation

Practice recognize numbers from one to forty-plus. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 二加三是多少？ (Èr jiā sān shì duōshao?) — What is two plus three?

**B:** 五。 (Wǔ.) — Five.

**A:** 六加六是多少？ (Liù jiā liù shì duōshao?) — What is six plus six?

**B:** 十二。 (Shí’èr.) — Twelve.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** A 加 B 是多少 (A jiā B shì duōshao)

Use 加 (jiā) for addition; 多少 (duōshao) asks for the result.

**六加六是多少？**
*liù jiā liù shì duōshao?*
What is six plus six?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 十 + 数字 (shí shùzì)

Numbers 11–19 use ten plus a digit, while 20–99 use tens plus a digit.

**二十五**
*èrshíwǔ*
Twenty-five.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.
