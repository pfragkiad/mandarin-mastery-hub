# Introducing Yourself and Other People

Practice say who you are and ask where another person is from. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Say who you are and ask where another person is from.

- Use polite forms of address.

## Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你是美国人吗？ (Nǐ shì Měiguó rén ma?) — Are you American?

**B:** 不是，我是中国人。 (Bú shì, wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.) — No, I am Chinese.

**B:** 你呢？ (Nǐ ne?) — And you?

**A:** 我是美国人。 (Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.) — I am American.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 我是…… (wǒ shì)

Use 是 (shì) to identify nationality or identity.

**我是中国人。**
*wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.*
I am Chinese.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 不是…… (bú shì)

不 (bù) changes to bú before a fourth-tone syllable, as in 是 (shì).

**我不是美国人。**
*wǒ bú shì Měiguó rén.*
I am not American.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.
