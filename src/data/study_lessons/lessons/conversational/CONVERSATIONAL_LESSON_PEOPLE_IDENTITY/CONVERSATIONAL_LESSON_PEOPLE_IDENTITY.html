Conversational Mandarin 15 min

Introducing Yourself and Others

Nationalities, identity, and follow-up questions

Learn to introduce yourself, ask about someone’s nationality, and respond to a follow-up question.

Learning objectives

  • Say who you are and ask where another person is from.
  • Use polite forms of address.

Study guidance

Learn to introduce yourself, ask about someone’s nationality, and respond to a follow-up question. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A

你是美国人吗？

Nǐ shì Měiguó rén ma?

Are you American?

B

不是，我是中国人。

Bú shì, wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.

No, I am Chinese.

B

你呢？

Nǐ ne?

And you?

A

我是美国人。

Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.

I am American.

Grammar structure and spoken example

主语 + 是 + 身份 (zhǔyǔ + shì + shēnfèn)

Use 是 (shì) to identify nationality or identity.

我是中国人。 (wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.) — I am Chinese.

Replace the nationality with another identity to introduce yourself or someone else.

Grammar structure and spoken example

主语 + 不是 + 身份 (zhǔyǔ + bú shì + shēnfèn)

不 (bù) changes to bú before a fourth-tone syllable, as in 是 (shì).

我不是美国人。 (wǒ bú shì Měiguó rén.) — I am not American.

The spoken reading changes to bú shì before fourth-tone 是 (shì).

Key expressions and vocabulary

你是美国人吗 (nǐ shì Měiguó rén ma) — Are you American?
我不是美国人 (wǒ bú shì Měiguó rén) — I am not American.
我是中国人 (wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén) — I am Chinese.
你是中国人吗 (nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma) — Are you Chinese?
你呢 (nǐ ne) — And you?

王小姐 (Wáng xiǎojiě) — Miss Wang.
吴先生 (Wú xiānsheng) — Mr. Wu.
这位 (zhè wèi) — This person (polite classifier).

中国人 (Zhōngguó rén) — A Chinese person.
你不是美国人吗 (nǐ bú shì Měiguó rén ma) — Aren't you American?
你不是中国人 (nǐ bú shì Zhōngguó rén) — You are not Chinese.
我不是中国人 (wǒ bú shì Zhōngguó rén) — I am not Chinese.
你是美国人 (nǐ shì Měiguó rén) — You are American.

陈小姐 (Chén xiǎojiě) — Miss Chen

Study guidance

Speaking task: Introduce yourself, ask whether someone is Chinese or American, and ask “And you?”

Grammar structure and spoken example

主语 + 是不是 + 身份？ (zhǔyǔ + shì bú shì + shēnfèn?)

是不是 (shì bú shì) is a positive-negative question; normally omit final 吗 (ma) here.

你是不是中国人？ (Nǐ shì bú shì Zhōngguó rén?) — Are you Chinese?

A direct response may begin with 是 (shì) or 不是 (bú shì).
