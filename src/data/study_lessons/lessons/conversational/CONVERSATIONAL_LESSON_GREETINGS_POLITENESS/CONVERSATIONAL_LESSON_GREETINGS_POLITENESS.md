# Greetings and Politeness

Practice start a polite conversation. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Start a polite conversation.

- Greet, thank, and close a short exchange.

## Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!

**B:** 你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!

**A:** 你好吗？ (Nǐ hǎo ma?) — How are you?

**B:** 很好，谢谢！ (Hěn hǎo, xièxie!) — Very well, thank you!

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 你好吗？ (nǐ hǎo ma)

The neutral-tone question particle 吗 (ma) can form a yes–no question.

**你好吗？**
*nǐ hǎo ma?*
How are you?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 请问…… (qǐngwèn)

A polite way to introduce a request for information.

**请问，你会说英文吗？**
*qǐngwèn, nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?*
Excuse me, do you speak English?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Practice

**Answer:** 对不起 (duìbuqǐ). One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.
