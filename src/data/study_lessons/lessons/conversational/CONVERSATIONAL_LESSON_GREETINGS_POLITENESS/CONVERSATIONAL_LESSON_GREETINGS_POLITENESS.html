Greetings and Politeness

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

communication-social · #greetings · #apologies · #courtesy · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Start a polite conversation.
  • Greet, thank, and close a short exchange.

Explanation

Learn to start a polite conversation. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A: 你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!

B: 你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!

A: 你好吗？ (Nǐ hǎo ma?) — How are you?

B: 很好，谢谢！ (Hěn hǎo, xièxie!) — Very well, thank you!

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 你好吗？ (nǐ hǎo ma)

The neutral-tone question particle 吗 (ma) can form a yes–no question.

你好吗？ (nǐ hǎo ma?)

How are you?

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 请问…… (qǐngwèn)

A polite way to introduce a request for information.

请问，你会说英文吗？ (qǐngwèn, nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?)

Excuse me, do you speak English?

Use this construction in your own dialogue.
