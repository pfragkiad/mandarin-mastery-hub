Conversational Mandarin

17 independently authored, practical lessons. Chinese sentences are accompanied by tone-marked pinyin, English translations, and guided speaking practice.

A Northern-style -r marker identifies selected 儿化 (érhuà) forms; the final -r is not a separate syllable.

1. Greetings and Politeness · 15 min

Learn to greet someone, ask a polite question, and thank them for their answer.

Model conversation

A: 你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!
B: 你好！ (Nǐ hǎo!) — Hello!
A: 你好吗？ (Nǐ hǎo ma?) — How are you?
B: 很好，谢谢！ (Hěn hǎo, xièxie!) — Very well, thank you!

Grammar structure · not for audio

陈述句 + 吗？ (chénshùjù + ma?) — The neutral-tone question particle 吗 (ma) can form a yes–no question.

Grammar structure · not for audio

请问 + 问题 (qǐngwèn + wèntí) — A polite way to introduce a request for information.

Key expressions

你好 (nǐ hǎo) — Hello.
你好吗 (nǐ hǎo ma) — How are you?
很好 (hěn hǎo) — Very well.
对不起 (duìbuqǐ) — Excuse me; sorry.
请问 (qǐngwèn) — Excuse me, may I ask
谢谢 (xièxie) — Thank you.
谢谢你 (xièxie nǐ) — Thank you.
不客气 (bú kèqi) — You’re welcome.
再见 (zàijiàn) — Goodbye.
明天见 (míngtiān jiàn) — See you tomorrow.
2. Introducing Yourself and Others · 15 min

Learn to introduce yourself, ask about someone’s nationality, and respond to a follow-up question.

Model conversation

A: 你是美国人吗？ (Nǐ shì Měiguó rén ma?) — Are you American?
B: 不是，我是中国人。 (Bú shì, wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén.) — No, I am Chinese.
B: 你呢？ (Nǐ ne?) — And you?
A: 我是美国人。 (Wǒ shì Měiguó rén.) — I am American.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 是 + 身份 (zhǔyǔ + shì + shēnfèn) — Use 是 (shì) to identify nationality or identity.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 不是 + 身份 (zhǔyǔ + bú shì + shēnfèn) — 不 (bù) changes to bú before a fourth-tone syllable, as in 是 (shì).

Key expressions

我是美国人 (wǒ shì Měiguó rén) — I am American.
你是美国人吗 (nǐ shì Měiguó rén ma) — Are you American?
我不是美国人 (wǒ bú shì Měiguó rén) — I am not American.
我是中国人 (wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén) — I am Chinese.
你是中国人吗 (nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma) — Are you Chinese?
你呢 (nǐ ne) — And you?
李先生 (Lǐ xiānsheng) — Mr. Li.
王小姐 (Wáng xiǎojiě) — Miss Wang.
吴先生 (Wú xiānsheng) — Mr. Wu.
这位 (zhè wèi) — This person (polite classifier).
美国人 (Měiguó rén) — An American person.
中国人 (Zhōngguó rén) — A Chinese person.
你不是美国人吗 (nǐ bú shì Měiguó rén ma) — Aren't you American?
你不是中国人 (nǐ bú shì Zhōngguó rén) — You are not Chinese.
我不是中国人 (wǒ bú shì Zhōngguó rén) — I am not Chinese.
你是美国人 (nǐ shì Měiguó rén) — You are American.
我是 (wǒ shì) — I am; it is me.
是不是 (shì bú shì) — is it?; positive-negative question form
李明 (Lǐ Míng) — Li Ming; personal name
陈小姐 (Chén xiǎojiě) — Miss Chen

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 是不是 + 身份？ (zhǔyǔ + shì bú shì + shēnfèn?) — 是不是 (shì bú shì) is a positive-negative question; normally omit final 吗 (ma) here.

3. Talking About Language Ability · 15 min

Learn to ask which languages someone speaks and describe what you can and cannot say.

Model conversation

A: 请问，你会说英文吗？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?) — Excuse me, can you speak English?
B: 不会，我不会说英文。 (Bú huì, wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén.) — No, I cannot speak English.
B: 我会说一点普通话。 (Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Pǔtōnghuà.) — I can speak a little Mandarin.
A: 你普通话说得很好。 (Nǐ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de hěn hǎo.) — You speak Mandarin very well.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 会 + 说 + 语言 (zhǔyǔ + huì + shuō + yǔyán) — 会 (huì) expresses acquired ability; add a language after 说 (shuō).

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 说 + 得 + 程度 (zhǔyǔ + shuō + de + chéngdù) — 得 (de) introduces how well the action is performed.

Key expressions

你会说英文吗 (nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma) — Can you speak English?
你会说普通话吗 (nǐ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà ma) — Can you speak Mandarin?
会说 (huì shuō) — Can speak; know how to speak.
不会说 (bú huì shuō) — Cannot speak.
我会说英文 (wǒ huì shuō Yīngwén) — I can speak English.
我不会说英文 (wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén) — I cannot speak English.
我会说一点普通话 (wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Pǔtōnghuà) — I can speak a little Mandarin.
我说得不好 (wǒ shuō de bù hǎo) — I do not speak very well.
你普通话说得很好 (nǐ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de hěn hǎo) — You speak Mandarin very well.
你会说 (nǐ huì shuō) — You can speak.
你会说英文 (nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén) — You can speak English.
我会说普通话 (wǒ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà) — I can speak Mandarin.
我不会说普通话 (wǒ bú huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà) — I cannot speak Mandarin.
我会说一点英文 (wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Yīngwén) — I can speak a little English.

我会说一点儿英文Northern-style -r (wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Yīngwén) — I can speak a little English.

Speaking a Little and Encouraging Someone

A: 你会说普通话吗？ (Nǐ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà ma?) — Can you speak Mandarin?
B: 我会说一点儿，可是说得不好。Northern-style -r (Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr, kěshì shuō de bù hǎo.) — A little, but I do not speak it well.
A: 我觉得你说得很好。 (Wǒ juéde nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.) — I think you speak it very well.
B: 谢谢！我还想多练习。 (Xièxie! Wǒ hái xiǎng duō liànxí.) — Thanks! I still want to practice more.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 说得 + 不太好 (zhǔyǔ + shuō de + bú tài hǎo) — The result complement 得 (de) describes how well an action is performed; do not confuse it with 的 (de).

Grammar structure · not for audio

可是 + 对比的句子 (kěshì + duìbǐ de jùzi) — 可是 (kěshì) introduces a contrast; 一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) has optional northern rhotic pronunciation.

4. Asking for Clarification · 15 min

Learn to say when you do not understand and ask someone to clarify their meaning.

Model conversation

A: 你明白吗？ (Nǐ míngbai ma?) — Do you understand?
B: 我不明白。 (Wǒ bù míngbai.) — I do not understand.
B: 你说什么？ (Nǐ shuō shénme?) — What did you say?
A: 我说普通话。 (Wǒ shuō Pǔtōnghuà.) — I am speaking Mandarin.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 不 + 心理动词 (zhǔyǔ + bù + xīnlǐ dòngcí) — Negate a mental state using 不 (bù); the next syllable is not fourth tone here.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 说 + 什么？ (zhǔyǔ + shuō + shénme?) — 什么 (shénme) occupies the position of the unknown thing in the sentence.

Key expressions

我明白 (wǒ míngbai) — I understand.
我不明白 (wǒ bù míngbai) — I do not understand.
你明白吗 (nǐ míngbai ma) — Do you understand?
我不知道 (wǒ bù zhīdào) — I do not know.
你知道吗 (nǐ zhīdào ma) — Do you know?
我不明白你说什么 (wǒ bù míngbai nǐ shuō shénme) — I do not understand what you are saying.
你说什么 (nǐ shuō shénme) — What did you say?
我普通话说得不好 (wǒ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de bù hǎo) — I do not speak Mandarin well.
你明白 (nǐ míngbai) — You understand.
我知道 (wǒ zhīdào) — I know.
你不知道 (nǐ bù zhīdào) — You do not know.
你不明白 (nǐ bù míngbai) — You do not understand.
说得不好 (shuō de bù hǎo) — To speak poorly.
不是很好 (bú shì hěn hǎo) — not very good
可是 (kěshì) — but; however
5. Asking Where Places Are · 15 min

Learn to ask where a street is and explain whether a place is here or over there.

Model conversation

A: 请问，学院路在哪儿？Northern-style -r (Qǐngwèn, Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Excuse me, where is Xueyuan Road?
B: 在那儿。Northern-style -r (Zài nàr.) — It is over there.
A: 长安街也在那儿吗？Northern-style -r (Cháng'ān Jiē yě zài nàr ma?) — Is Chang’an Street also over there?
B: 不，长安街在这儿。Northern-style -r (Bù, Cháng'ān Jiē zài zhèr.) — No, Chang’an Street is here.

Grammar structure · not for audio

地名 + 在 + 哪儿？ (dìmíng + zài + nǎr?) — Ask for a location with 在哪儿 (zài nǎr), without adding 吗 (ma).

Grammar structure · not for audio

不在 + 地点 (bú zài + dìdiǎn) — Negate a location with 不在 (bú zài); the 不 changes tone before fourth-tone 在.

学院路在这儿Northern-style -r (Xuéyuàn Lù zài zhèr) — Xueyuan Road is here.
我不知道 (wǒ bù zhīdào) — I do not know.
学院路在那儿Northern-style -r (Xuéyuàn Lù zài nàr) — Xueyuan Road is over there.

Checking Two Street Names

A: 请问，您知道长安街在哪儿吗？Northern-style -r (Qǐngwèn, nín zhīdào Cháng'ān Jiē zài nǎr ma?) — Excuse me, do you know where Chang’an Avenue is?
B: 对不起，我不知道。 (Duìbuqǐ, wǒ bù zhīdào.) — Sorry, I do not know.
A: 那学院路呢？ (Nà Xuéyuàn Lù ne?) — What about Xueyuan Road?
B: 学院路在前面，可是长安街不在这儿。Northern-style -r (Xuéyuàn Lù zài qiánmiàn, kěshì Cháng'ān Jiē bú zài zhèr.) — Xueyuan Road is ahead, but Chang’an Avenue is not here.
6. Expressing Wants and Intentions · 15 min

Learn to say what you want, decline food politely, and accept an offered drink.

Model conversation

A: 你想吃一点东西吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi ma?) — Would you like something to eat?
B: 我不想吃。 (Wǒ bù xiǎng chī.) — I do not want to eat.
A: 你想喝一点茶吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá ma?) — Would you like some tea?
B: 好，我想喝一点茶。 (Hǎo, wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — Yes, I would like a little tea.

Grammar structure · not for audio

主语 + 想 + 动作 (zhǔyǔ + xiǎng + dòngzuò) — 想 (xiǎng) expresses a wish or desire followed by a verb.

Grammar structure · not for audio

想不想 + 动作？ (xiǎng bu xiǎng + dòngzuò?) — An A-not-A question asks whether someone wants to do something.

Learn to choose a drink, order the number of cups you want, and ask for food.

Model conversation

8. Choosing Where to Meet · 15 min

Learn to invite someone to meet for food, decline one place, and suggest another.

Model conversation

A: 你想去哪儿吃午饭？Northern-style -r (Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr chī wǔfàn?) — Where would you like to have lunch?
B: 我想去饭店。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn.) — I would like to go to a restaurant.
A: 去北京饭店，好吗？ (Qù Běijīng Fàndiàn, hǎo ma?) — How about going to the Beijing Hotel?
B: 好，我们去吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù ba.) — Okay, let us go.

Grammar structure · not for audio

想 + 去 + 地点 + 动作 (xiǎng + qù + dìdiǎn + dòngzuò) — Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

Grammar structure · not for audio

在 + 地点 + 动作 (zài + dìdiǎn + dòngzuò) — Use 在 (zài) before a place to say where an action happens.

去咖啡厅 (qù kāfēitīng) — Go to a café.
去饭店 (qù fàndiàn) — Go to a restaurant.
我想去酒店 (wǒ xiǎng qù jiǔdiàn) — I want to go to the hotel.
我想去北京饭店 (wǒ xiǎng qù Běijīng Fàndiàn) — I want to go to the Beijing Hotel.
我想去你那儿Northern-style -r (wǒ xiǎng qù nǐ nàr) — I want to go to your place.

B: 一共二十六块。 (Yígòng èrshíliù kuài.) — Twenty-six yuan altogether.
15. Asking Permission Politely · 15 min

Learn to ask permission, request an item politely, and respond appropriately.

Model conversation

A: 你可不可以给我一点钱？ (Nǐ kě bu kěyǐ gěi wǒ yìdiǎn qián?) — Could you give me some money?
B: 可以，我给你十块人民币。 (Kěyǐ, wǒ gěi nǐ shí kuài rénmínbì.) — Yes, I can. I will give you ten yuan.
A: 谢谢你！ (Xièxie nǐ!) — Thank you!
B: 不客气。 (Bú kèqi.) — You are welcome.

可不可以 + 动作？ (kě bu kěyǐ + dòngzuò?) — An A-not-A form of 可以 (kěyǐ) asks about possibility or permission.

Grammar structure · not for audio

Asking Whether You May Pay

A: 请问，可不可以用人民币付钱？ (Qǐngwèn, kě bu kěyǐ yòng rénmínbì fù qián?) — Excuse me, may I pay with yuan?
B: 可以。你有现金吗？ (Kěyǐ. Nǐ yǒu xiànjīn ma?) — Yes. Do you have cash?
A: 我有钱，可是只有十块。 (Wǒ yǒu qián, kěshì zhǐ yǒu shí kuài.) — I have money, but only ten yuan.
B: 十块不够，这个要十五块。 (Shí kuài bú gòu, zhège yào shíwǔ kuài.) — Ten yuan is not enough. This costs fifteen yuan.

16. Arranging Plans by Telephone · 20 min

Learn to identify yourself on a call, invite someone to dinner, and negotiate a convenient time.

Model conversation

A: 喂，您好！请问陈小姐在吗？ (Wèi, nín hǎo! Qǐngwèn Chén xiǎojiě zài ma?) — Hello! Excuse me, is Miss Chen there?
B: 我就是。请问您是哪位？ (Wǒ jiù shì. Qǐngwèn nín shì nǎ wèi?) — Speaking. May I ask who is calling?
A: 我是李明。今天晚上可以一起吃饭吗？ (Wǒ shì Lǐ Míng. Jīntiān wǎnshang kěyǐ yìqǐ chīfàn ma?) — This is Li Ming. Could we have dinner together tonight?
B: 今天不行，我有事。明天怎么样？ (Jīntiān bù xíng, wǒ yǒu shì. Míngtiān zěnmeyàng?) — Today does not work; I am busy. How about tomorrow?
A: 明天晚上十点钟可以吗？ (Míngtiān wǎnshang shí diǎn zhōng kěyǐ ma?) — Would ten tomorrow night work?
B: 十点太晚了。七点可以。 (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le. Qī diǎn kěyǐ.) — Ten is too late. Seven works.
A: 好的，那么明天七点见。 (Hǎo de, nàme míngtiān qī diǎn jiàn.) — All right. See you at seven tomorrow.
B: 好，再见！ (Hǎo, zàijiàn!) — Okay, goodbye!

Grammar structure · not for audio

请问 + 称呼 + 在吗？ (qǐngwèn + chēnghu + zài ma?) — Use 请问 (qǐngwèn) plus a name and 在吗 (zài ma) when asking if someone is available on the telephone.

Grammar structure · not for audio

时间 + 不行；另一个时间 + 呢？ (shíjiān + bù xíng; lìng yí ge shíjiān + ne?) — The particle 呢 (ne) offers an elliptical alternative when the topic is already understood.

Grammar structure · not for audio

太晚了 + 早点见 (tài wǎn le + zǎo diǎn jiàn) — Use 太晚了 (tài wǎn le) to object and 早点 (zǎo diǎn) to suggest an earlier time.

17. Buying a Newspaper · 20 min

Learn to ask for a newspaper, confirm its price, and respond when you lack the right currency.

Model conversation

A: 您好，请问你们有没有美国报纸？ (Nín hǎo, qǐngwèn nǐmen yǒu méiyǒu Měiguó bàozhǐ?) — Hello, do you have American newspapers?
B: 有，也有英文报纸。 (Yǒu, yě yǒu Yīngwén bàozhǐ.) — Yes, and we also have English-language newspapers.
A: 这份报纸是不是十八块？ (Zhè fèn bàozhǐ shì bú shì shíbā kuài?) — Does this newspaper cost eighteen yuan?
B: 不是十八块，是八块人民币。 (Bú shì shíbā kuài, shì bā kuài rénmínbì.) — Not eighteen; it is eight yuan.
A: 我有美金，可是没有人民币。 (Wǒ yǒu měijīn, kěshì méiyǒu rénmínbì.) — I have US dollars but no yuan.
B: 这里只收人民币。 (Zhèlǐ zhǐ shōu rénmínbì.) — We only accept yuan here.
A: 那么我一会儿再来，谢谢。Northern-style -r (Nàme wǒ yíhuìr zài lái, xièxie.) — Then I will come back in a little while, thank you.
B: 不客气！ (Bú kèqi!) — You are welcome!

Grammar structure · not for audio

有没有 + 物品？ (yǒu méiyǒu + wùpǐn?) — 有没有 (yǒu méiyǒu) is the A-not-A form of 有 (yǒu), used for availability or possession.

Grammar structure · not for audio

物品 + 是不是 + 价格？ (wùpǐn + shì bú shì + jiàgé?) — 是不是 (shì bú shì) asks for confirmation without a final 吗 (ma).

Grammar structure · not for audio

数字 + 不是 + 另一个数字 (shùzì + bú shì + lìng yí ge shùzì) — Check numbers carefully: 八 (bā) is eight, whereas 十八 (shíbā) is eighteen.

美金 (měijīn) — US dollars
