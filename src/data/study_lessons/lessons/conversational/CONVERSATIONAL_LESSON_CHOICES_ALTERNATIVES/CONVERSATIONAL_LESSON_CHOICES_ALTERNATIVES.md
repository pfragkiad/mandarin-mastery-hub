# Choosing Between Alternatives

Learn to offer two alternatives, ask someone to choose, and give a direct answer.

*Choice questions with 还是 and statements with 或者*

## Learning objectives

- Choose between two options in a question.
- Distinguish question-or from statement-or.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to offer two alternatives, ask someone to choose, and give a direct answer. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想喝咖啡还是茶？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē kāfēi háishi chá?) — Would you like coffee or tea?

**B:** 我想喝咖啡。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē kāfēi.) — I would like coffee.

**A:** 你想现在喝还是过一会儿喝？ (Nǐ xiǎng xiànzài hē háishi guò yíhuìr hē?) — Do you want it now or later?

**B:** 过一会儿吧。 (Guò yíhuìr ba.) — A little later, please.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**喝 + 饮料 + 还是 + 饮料？** (hē + yǐnliào + háishi + yǐnliào?)

**喝** + &lt;first drink&gt; + **还是** + &lt;second drink&gt;？

还是 (háishi) is normal in questions that present a choice.

你喝茶还是啤酒？ (nǐ hē chá háishi píjiǔ?) — Tea or beer?
The speaker is asking the listener to select one of the drinks.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**动词 + 名词 + 或者 + 名词** (dòngcí + míngcí + huòzhě + míngcí)

&lt;action word&gt; + &lt;a thing or option&gt; + **或者** + &lt;a thing or option&gt;

或者 (huòzhě) commonly offers alternatives in statements.

我想买茶或者啤酒。 (wǒ xiǎng mǎi chá huòzhě píjiǔ.) — I want to buy tea or beer.
This describes possibilities instead of directly requesting a choice.

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 你想喝茶还是啤酒 (nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ) — Would you like tea or beer?
- 我想买茶或者啤酒 (wǒ xiǎng mǎi chá huòzhě píjiǔ) — I want to buy tea or beer.
- 一点钟还是两点钟 (yì diǎn zhōng háishi liǎng diǎn zhōng) — One o’clock or two o’clock?
- 四点钟或者五点钟 (sì diǎn zhōng huòzhě wǔ diǎn zhōng) — Four or five o’clock.
- 你要不要 (nǐ yào bu yào) — Do you want it?
- 你想不想 (nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng) — Do you want to?
- 你有没有 (nǐ yǒu méiyǒu) — Do you have any?
- 好不好 (hǎo bu hǎo) — Is that okay?
- 八点钟还是九点钟 (bā diǎn zhōng háishi jiǔ diǎn zhōng) — Eight or nine o'clock?
- 你想要茶还是啤酒 (nǐ xiǎng yào chá háishi píjiǔ) — Would you like tea or beer?
- 你要不要美金 (nǐ yào bu yào měijīn) — Do you want US dollars?
- 你有没有人民币 (nǐ yǒu méiyǒu rénmínbì) — Do you have any RMB?
- 你想不想吃一点东西 (nǐ xiǎng bu xiǎng chī yìdiǎn dōngxi) — Do you want something to eat?
- 现在还是过一会儿 (xiànzài háishi guò yíhuìr) — Now or later?  [Northern-style -r]
- 可是 (kěshì) — but; however
- 是不是 (shì bú shì) — is it or not?
- 太晚 (tài wǎn) — too late
- 那么 (nàme) — then; in that case

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Ask three choice questions: drinks, location, and time. Answer using a complete sentence.

## Polite Disagreement and Alternatives

**A:** 你想喝茶还是咖啡？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi kāfēi?) — Do you want tea or coffee?

**B:** 我想喝茶，可是这里没有茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē chá, kěshì zhèlǐ méiyǒu chá.) — I want tea, but there is none here.

**A:** 那么喝水或者果汁怎么样？ (Nàme hē shuǐ huòzhě guǒzhī zěnmeyàng?) — Then how about water or juice?

**B:** 好，那就喝水吧。 (Hǎo, nà jiù hē shuǐ ba.) — Sure, I will have water then.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** Which drink does speaker B choose in the first exchange?

**Model answer:**
我想喝咖啡。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē kāfēi.) — I would like coffee.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: Four or five o’clock.

**Model answer:**
四点钟或者五点钟 (sì diǎn zhōng huòzhě wǔ diǎn zhōng)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Use 或者 (huòzhě) to say you want to buy tea or beer.

**Model answer:**
我想买茶或者啤酒。 (Wǒ xiǎng mǎi chá huòzhě píjiǔ.) — I want to buy tea or beer.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Ask three choice questions: drinks, location, and time. Answer using a complete sentence.

**Model answer:**
A: 你想喝水还是茶？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē shuǐ háishi chá?) — Would you like water or tea?
B: 我想喝水。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — I would like water.
A: 我们在这儿吃还是在那儿吃？ (Wǒmen zài zhèr chī háishi zài nàr chī?) — Shall we eat here or over there?
B: 我们在这儿吃吧。 (Wǒmen zài zhèr chī ba.) — Let us eat here.
A: 六点钟还是七点钟？ (Liù diǎn zhōng háishi qī diǎn zhōng?) — Six or seven o'clock?
B: 七点钟吧。 (Qī diǎn zhōng ba.) — Seven o'clock.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
