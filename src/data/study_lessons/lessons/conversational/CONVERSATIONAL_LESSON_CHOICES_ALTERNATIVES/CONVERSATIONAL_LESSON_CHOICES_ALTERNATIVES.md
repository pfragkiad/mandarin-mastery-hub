# Choosing: Or, Either, and Not

Practice choose between two options in a question. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Choose between two options in a question.

- Distinguish question-or from statement-or.

## Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想喝茶还是啤酒？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ?) — Would you like tea or beer?

**B:** 我想喝茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē chá.) — I would like tea.

**A:** 你想现在喝还是过一会儿喝？ (Nǐ xiǎng xiànzài hē háishi guò yíhuìr hē?) — Do you want it now or later?

**B:** 过一会儿吧。 (Guò yíhuìr ba.) — A little later, please.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** A 还是 B (A háishi B)

还是 (háishi) is normal in questions that present a choice.

**你喝茶还是啤酒？**
*nǐ hē chá háishi píjiǔ?*
Tea or beer?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** A 或者 B (A huòzhě B)

或者 (huòzhě) commonly offers alternatives in statements.

**我想买茶或者啤酒。**
*wǒ xiǎng mǎi chá huòzhě píjiǔ.*
I want to buy tea or beer.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.
