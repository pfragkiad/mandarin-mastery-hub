Choosing: Or, Either, and Not

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

grammar-functions · communication-social · food-drink · #alternatives · #negation · #a-not-a · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Choose between two options in a question.
  • Distinguish question-or from statement-or.

Explanation

Learn to choose between two options in a question. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A: 你想喝茶还是啤酒？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ?) — Would you like tea or beer?

B: 我想喝茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē chá.) — I would like tea.

A: 你想现在喝还是过一会儿喝？ (Nǐ xiǎng xiànzài hē háishi guò yíhuìr hē?) — Do you want it now or later?

B: 过一会儿吧。 (Guò yíhuìr ba.) — A little later, please.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: A 还是 B (A háishi B)

还是 (háishi) is normal in questions that present a choice.

你喝茶还是啤酒？ (nǐ hē chá háishi píjiǔ?)

Tea or beer?

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: A 或者 B (A huòzhě B)

或者 (huòzhě) commonly offers alternatives in statements.

我想买茶或者啤酒。 (wǒ xiǎng mǎi chá huòzhě píjiǔ.)

I want to buy tea or beer.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.
