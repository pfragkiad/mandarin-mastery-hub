# Doing Things Together

Learn to invite someone for lunch, accept or decline, and agree on a place to eat.

*Inviting someone to lunch and deciding where to go*

## Learning objectives

- Invite another person to eat or drink with you.
- Ask who someone is going with.

## Study guidance

Learn to invite someone for lunch, accept or decline, and agree on a place to eat. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想跟我一起吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn ma?) — Would you like to have lunch with me?

**B:** 好，我也想跟你一起吃。 (Hǎo, wǒ yě xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī.) — Yes, I would like to eat with you too.

**A:** 我们去哪儿吃？ (Wǒmen qù nǎr chī?) — Where shall we eat?

**B:** 去饭店吧。 (Qù fàndiàn ba.) — Let us go to a restaurant.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**跟 + 人 + 一起 + 动作** (gēn + rén + yìqǐ + dòngzuò)

**跟** + &lt;person&gt; + **一起** + &lt;what to do&gt;

跟 (gēn) introduces the person accompanying you; 一起 (yìqǐ) means together.

我想跟你一起吃饭。 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chīfàn.) — I would like to eat with you.
Use this when inviting someone to share a meal or activity.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 也 + 想 + 动作** (zhǔyǔ + yě + xiǎng + dòngzuò)

&lt;who or what&gt; + **也** + **想** + &lt;what to do&gt;

也 (yě) adds agreement or another participant doing the same thing.

我也想去。 (wǒ yě xiǎng qù.) — I want to go too.
A friendly response when you want to join someone else’s plan.

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 跟谁一起 (gēn shéi yìqǐ) — Together with whom?
- 跟你一起 (gēn nǐ yìqǐ) — Together with you.
- 跟我一起 (gēn wǒ yìqǐ) — Together with me.
- 你想跟我一起吃午饭吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn ma) — Would you like to have lunch with me?
- 我想跟你一起吃午饭 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn) — I would like to have lunch with you.
- 我也想跟你一起吃午饭 (wǒ yě xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn) — I would also like to have lunch with you.
- 你想跟我一起吃晚饭吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn ma) — Would you like to have dinner with me?
- 我不想跟你一起喝 (wǒ bù xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ hē) — I do not want to drink with you.
  - **Usage note · Context** (`CMX-B8F4416EDA77`): The object of 喝 (hē) is understood if the speakers already know what drink is being discussed. Without that context, the refusal sounds abrupt or incomplete. A softer, more explicit alternative is 我今天不太想跟你一起喝茶 (wǒ jīntiān bú tài xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ hē chá).
- 你想跟我一起喝一点东西吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ hē yìdiǎn dōngxi ma) — Would you like to have a drink with me?
- 我想跟你一起吃晚饭 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn) — I would like to have dinner with you.
- 你不想跟我一起喝 (nǐ bù xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ hē) — You do not want to drink with me.
  - **Usage note · Context** (`CMX-C9042CF1784B`): The drink after 喝 (hē) is omitted because it is understood from the conversation. As an isolated sentence this can sound abrupt. A more explicit form is 你不想跟我一起喝茶 (nǐ bù xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ hē chá).
- 我想跟你一起喝一点东西 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ hē yìdiǎn dōngxi) — I would like to have a drink with you.
- 一起 (yìqǐ) — together
- 跟 (gēn) — with; follow
- 跟朋友一起 (gēn péngyou yìqǐ) — together with a friend
- 一起吃晚饭 (yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn) — have dinner together

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: Would you like to have lunch with me?

**Model answer:**
你想跟我一起吃午饭吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn ma)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Say that you also want to go, using 也 (yě).

**Model answer:**

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.
