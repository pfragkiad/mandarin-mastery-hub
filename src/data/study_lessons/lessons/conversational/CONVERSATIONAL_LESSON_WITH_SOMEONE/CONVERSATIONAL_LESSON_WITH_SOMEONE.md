## Explanation

Practice invite another person to eat or drink with you. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想跟我一起吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn ma?) — Would you like to have lunch with me?

**B:** 好，我也想跟你一起吃。 (Hǎo, wǒ yě xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī.) — Yes, I would like to eat with you too.

**A:** 我们去哪儿吃？ (Wǒmen qù nǎr chī?) — Where shall we eat?

**B:** 去饭店吧。 (Qù fàndiàn ba.) — Let us go to a restaurant.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 跟 + 人 + 一起 + 动词 (gēn rén yìqǐ dòngcí)

跟 (gēn) introduces the person accompanying you; 一起 (yìqǐ) means together.

**我想跟你一起吃饭。**
*wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chīfàn.*
I would like to eat with you.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 也 + 动词短语 (yě dòngcí duǎnyǔ)

也 (yě) adds agreement or another participant doing the same thing.

**我也想去。**
*wǒ yě xiǎng qù.*
I want to go too.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.
