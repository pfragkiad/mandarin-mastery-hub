# Doing Things Together

Learn to invite someone for lunch, accept or decline, and agree on a place to eat.

## Learning objectives

- Invite another person to eat or drink with you.

- Ask who someone is going with.

## Explanation

Learn to invite someone for lunch, accept or decline, and agree on a place to eat. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想跟我一起吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn ma?) — Would you like to have lunch with me?

**B:** 好，我也想跟你一起吃。 (Hǎo, wǒ yě xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī.) — Yes, I would like to eat with you too.

**A:** 我们去哪儿吃？ (Wǒmen qù nǎr chī?) — Where shall we eat?

**B:** 去饭店吧。 (Qù fàndiàn ba.) — Let us go to a restaurant.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 我想跟你一起吃饭。 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chīfàn.)

跟 (gēn) introduces the person accompanying you; 一起 (yìqǐ) means together.

**我想跟你一起吃饭。**
*wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chīfàn.*
I would like to eat with you.
**Usage:** 跟 (gēn) introduces the person accompanying you; 一起 (yìqǐ) means together.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 我也想去。 (wǒ yě xiǎng qù.)

也 (yě) adds agreement or another participant doing the same thing.

**我也想去。**
*wǒ yě xiǎng qù.*
I want to go too.
**Usage:** 也 (yě) adds agreement or another participant doing the same thing.

## Review Set

跟谁一起 (gēn shéi yìqǐ) — Together with whom?

跟你一起 (gēn nǐ yìqǐ) — Together with you.

跟我一起 (gēn wǒ yìqǐ) — Together with me.

你想跟我一起吃午饭吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn ma) — Would you like to have lunch with me?

我想跟你一起吃午饭 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn) — I would like to have lunch with you.

我也想跟你一起吃午饭 (wǒ yě xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī wǔfàn) — I would also like to have lunch with you.

你想跟我一起吃晚饭吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn ma) — Would you like to have dinner with me?

我不想跟你一起喝 (wǒ bù xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ hē) — I do not want to drink with you.

你想跟我一起喝一点东西吗 (nǐ xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ hē yìdiǎn dōngxi ma) — Would you like to have a drink with me?

我想跟你一起吃晚饭 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn) — I would like to have dinner with you.

你不想跟我一起喝 (nǐ bù xiǎng gēn wǒ yìqǐ hē) — You do not want to drink with me.

我想跟你一起喝一点东西 (wǒ xiǎng gēn nǐ yìqǐ hē yìdiǎn dōngxi) — I would like to have a drink with you.
