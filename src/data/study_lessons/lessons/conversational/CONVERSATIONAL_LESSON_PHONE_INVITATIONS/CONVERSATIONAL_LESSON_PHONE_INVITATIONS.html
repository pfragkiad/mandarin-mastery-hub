Conversational Mandarin 20 min

Arranging Plans by Telephone

Answering calls, invitations, and changing times

Learn to identify yourself on a call, invite someone to dinner, and negotiate a convenient time.

Learning objectives

  • Open a telephone call and ask for someone by name.
  • Invite someone to dinner and propose another time when needed.
  • Explain politely that a proposed time is too late.

Study guidance

Learn to identify yourself on a call, invite someone to dinner, and negotiate a convenient time. practice the new dialogue and the examples, then complete the role-play.

Model conversation

A

喂，您好！请问陈小姐在吗？

Wèi, nín hǎo! Qǐngwèn Chén xiǎojiě zài ma?

Hello! Excuse me, is Miss Chen there?

B

我就是。请问您是哪位？

Wǒ jiù shì. Qǐngwèn nín shì nǎ wèi?

Speaking. May I ask who is calling?

A

我是李明。今天晚上可以一起吃饭吗？

Wǒ shì Lǐ Míng. Jīntiān wǎnshang kěyǐ yìqǐ chīfàn ma?

This is Li Ming. Could we have dinner together tonight?

B

今天不行，我有事。明天怎么样？

Jīntiān bù xíng, wǒ yǒu shì. Míngtiān zěnmeyàng?

Today does not work; I am busy. How about tomorrow?

A

明天晚上十点钟可以吗？

Míngtiān wǎnshang shí diǎn zhōng kěyǐ ma?

Would ten tomorrow night work?

B

十点太晚了。七点可以。

Shí diǎn tài wǎn le. Qī diǎn kěyǐ.

Ten is too late. Seven works.

A

好的，那么明天七点见。

Hǎo de, nàme míngtiān qī diǎn jiàn.

All right. See you at seven tomorrow.

B

好，再见！

Hǎo, zàijiàn!

Okay, goodbye!

Grammar structure and spoken example

请问 + 称呼 + 在吗？ (qǐngwèn + chēnghu + zài ma?)

Use 请问 (qǐngwèn) plus a name and 在吗 (zài ma) when asking if someone is available on the telephone.

请问李先生在吗？ (Qǐngwèn Lǐ xiānsheng zài ma?) — May I speak to Mr Li?

This sounds courteous when another person answers the telephone.

Grammar structure and spoken example

时间 + 不行；另一个时间 + 呢？ (shíjiān + bù xíng; lìng yí ge shíjiān + ne?)

The particle 呢 (ne) offers an elliptical alternative when the topic is already understood.

今晚不行，明天呢？ (Jīnwǎn bù xíng, míngtiān ne?) — Tonight does not work; how about tomorrow?

The follow-up invites the other speaker to consider another time.

Grammar structure and spoken example

太晚了 + 早点见 (tài wǎn le + zǎo diǎn jiàn)

Use 太晚了 (tài wǎn le) to object and 早点 (zǎo diǎn) to suggest an earlier time.

太晚了，我们早点见。 (Tài wǎn le, wǒmen zǎo diǎn jiàn.) — That is too late; let us meet earlier.

Pair your objection with a concrete earlier meeting suggestion.

Study guidance

Speaking task: Call a friend, identify yourself, invite them to dinner, negotiate a time, and end the call.

Practice

Question 1 · comprehension

Why does speaker B refuse the first invitation?

Show model answer
今天不行，我有事。 (Jīntiān bù xíng, wǒ yǒu shì.) — Today does not work because B has something to do.

The response gives a reason and proposes an alternative.

Question 2 · production

Say politely on the telephone: “Is Miss Chen there?”

Show model answer
请问陈小姐在吗？ (Qǐngwèn Chén xiǎojiě zài ma?) — May I speak to Miss Chen?

Use 请问 (qǐngwèn) before asking for someone.

Question 3 · production

Reject ten o’clock as too late, then suggest seven.

Show model answer
十点太晚了，七点可以吗？ (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le, qī diǎn kěyǐ ma?) — Ten is too late; would seven work?

Give both the objection and an alternative.

Question 4 · production

Call a friend, identify yourself, invite them to dinner, negotiate a time, and end the call.

Show model answer
A: 喂，你好！我是小林。今天晚上一起吃晚饭，好吗？ (Wèi, nǐ hǎo! Wǒ shì Xiǎo Lín. Jīntiān wǎnshang yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn, hǎo ma?) — Hello! This is Xiao Lin. Shall we have dinner tonight? B: 今天不行，明天晚上怎么样？ (Jīntiān bù xíng, míngtiān wǎnshang zěnmeyàng?) — Today does not work. How about tomorrow evening? A: 明天七点可以吗？ (Míngtiān qī diǎn kěyǐ ma?) — Would seven tomorrow work? B: 可以，明天见！ (Kěyǐ, míngtiān jiàn!) — Yes, see you tomorrow! A: 好，再见！ (Hǎo, zàijiàn!) — Okay, goodbye!

The answer includes every step: calling, identifying, inviting, negotiating and closing.
