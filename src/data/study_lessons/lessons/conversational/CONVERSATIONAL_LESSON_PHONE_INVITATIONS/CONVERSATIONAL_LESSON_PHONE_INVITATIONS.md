# Arranging Plans by Telephone

Learn to identify yourself on a call, invite someone to dinner, and negotiate a convenient time.

*Answering calls, invitations, and changing times*

## Learning objectives

- Open a telephone call and ask for someone by name.
- Invite someone to dinner and propose another time when needed.
- Explain politely that a proposed time is too late.

## Study guidance

Learn to identify yourself on a call, invite someone to dinner, and negotiate a convenient time. practice the new dialogue and the examples, then complete the role-play.

## Model conversation

**A:** 喂，您好！请问陈小姐在吗？ (Wèi, nín hǎo! Qǐngwèn Chén xiǎojiě zài ma?) — Hello! Excuse me, is Miss Chen there?

**B:** 我就是。请问您是哪位？ (Wǒ jiù shì. Qǐngwèn nín shì nǎ wèi?) — Speaking. May I ask who is calling?

**A:** 我是李明。今天晚上可以一起吃饭吗？ (Wǒ shì Lǐ Míng. Jīntiān wǎnshang kěyǐ yìqǐ chīfàn ma?) — This is Li Ming. Could we have dinner together tonight?

**B:** 今天不行，我有事。明天怎么样？ (Jīntiān bù xíng, wǒ yǒu shì. Míngtiān zěnmeyàng?) — Today does not work; I am busy. How about tomorrow?

**A:** 明天晚上十点钟可以吗？ (Míngtiān wǎnshang shí diǎn zhōng kěyǐ ma?) — Would ten tomorrow night work?

**B:** 十点太晚了。七点可以。 (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le. Qī diǎn kěyǐ.) — Ten is too late. Seven works.

**A:** 好的，那么明天七点见。 (Hǎo de, nàme míngtiān qī diǎn jiàn.) — All right. See you at seven tomorrow.

**B:** 好，再见！ (Hǎo, zàijiàn!) — Okay, goodbye!

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**请问 + 称呼 + 在吗？** (qǐngwèn + chēnghu + zài ma?)

**请问** + &lt;name or title&gt; + **在吗**？

Use 请问 (qǐngwèn) plus a name and 在吗 (zài ma) when asking if someone is available on the telephone.

请问李先生在吗？ (Qǐngwèn Lǐ xiānsheng zài ma?) — May I speak to Mr Li?
This sounds courteous when another person answers the telephone.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**时间 + 不行；另一个时间 + 呢？** (shíjiān + bù xíng; lìng yí ge shíjiān + ne?)

&lt;proposed time&gt; + **不行**；&lt;another time&gt; + **呢**？

The particle 呢 (ne) offers an elliptical alternative when the topic is already understood.

今晚不行，明天呢？ (Jīnwǎn bù xíng, míngtiān ne?) — Tonight does not work; how about tomorrow?
The follow-up invites the other speaker to consider another time.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**太晚了 + 早点见** (tài wǎn le + zǎo diǎn jiàn)

**太晚了** + **早点见**

Use 太晚了 (tài wǎn le) to object and 早点 (zǎo diǎn) to suggest an earlier time.

太晚了，我们早点见。 (Tài wǎn le, wǒmen zǎo diǎn jiàn.) — That is too late; let us meet earlier.
Pair your objection with a concrete earlier meeting suggestion.

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 电话 (diànhuà) — telephone; phone call
- 打电话 (dǎ diànhuà) — make a phone call
- 喂 (wèi) — hello (on the phone)
- 请问 (qǐngwèn) — excuse me, may I ask
- 是吗 (shì ma) — is that so?
- 是不是 (shì bú shì) — is it or not?
- 太晚 (tài wǎn) — too late
- 不行 (bù xíng) — that does not work
- 那么 (nàme) — then; in that case
- 一会儿 (yíhuìr) — in a little while  [Northern-style -r]
- 今天 (jīntiān) — today
- 明天 (míngtiān) — tomorrow
- 晚上 (wǎnshang) — evening
- 陈小姐 (Chén xiǎojiě) — Miss Chen

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Call a friend, identify yourself, invite them to dinner, negotiate a time, and end the call.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** Why does speaker B refuse the first invitation?

**Model answer:**
今天不行，我有事。 (Jīntiān bù xíng, wǒ yǒu shì.) — Today does not work because B has something to do.

The response gives a reason and proposes an alternative.

**Question 2 (production):** Say politely on the telephone: “Is Miss Chen there?”

**Model answer:**
请问陈小姐在吗？ (Qǐngwèn Chén xiǎojiě zài ma?) — May I speak to Miss Chen?

Use 请问 (qǐngwèn) before asking for someone.

**Question 3 (production):** Reject ten o’clock as too late, then suggest seven.

**Model answer:**
十点太晚了，七点可以吗？ (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le, qī diǎn kěyǐ ma?) — Ten is too late; would seven work?

Give both the objection and an alternative.

**Question 4 (production):** Call a friend, identify yourself, invite them to dinner, negotiate a time, and end the call.

**Model answer:**
A: 喂，你好！我是小林。今天晚上一起吃晚饭，好吗？ (Wèi, nǐ hǎo! Wǒ shì Xiǎo Lín. Jīntiān wǎnshang yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn, hǎo ma?) — Hello! This is Xiao Lin. Shall we have dinner tonight?
B: 今天不行，明天晚上怎么样？ (Jīntiān bù xíng, míngtiān wǎnshang zěnmeyàng?) — Today does not work. How about tomorrow evening?
A: 明天七点可以吗？ (Míngtiān qī diǎn kěyǐ ma?) — Would seven tomorrow work?
B: 可以，明天见！ (Kěyǐ, míngtiān jiàn!) — Yes, see you tomorrow!
A: 好，再见！ (Hǎo, zàijiàn!) — Okay, goodbye!

The answer includes every step: calling, identifying, inviting, negotiating and closing.
