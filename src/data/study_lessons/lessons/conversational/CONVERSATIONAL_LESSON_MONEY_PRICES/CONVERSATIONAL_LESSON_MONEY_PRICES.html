Money, Prices, and Currency

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

shopping-money · numbers-quantities · #money · #currency · #prices · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Ask how much something costs.
  • Say amounts in yuan or US dollars.

Explanation

Learn to ask how much something costs. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A: 请问，一杯啤酒多少钱？ (Qǐngwèn, yì bēi píjiǔ duōshao qián?) — Excuse me, how much is a glass of beer?

B: 十块人民币。 (Shí kuài rénmínbì.) — Ten yuan.

A: 你有人民币吗？ (Nǐ yǒu rénmínbì ma?) — Do you have Chinese currency?

B: 有，我有二十块。 (Yǒu, wǒ yǒu èrshí kuài.) — Yes, I have twenty yuan.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 多少钱 (duōshao qián)

多少钱 (duōshao qián) asks for the price.

一杯茶多少钱？ (yì bēi chá duōshao qián?)

How much is a cup of tea?

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 数词 + 块 + 币种 (shùcí kuài bìzhǒng)

块 (kuài) is the colloquial unit used to count yuan or a currency amount.

我有十块人民币。 (wǒ yǒu shí kuài rénmínbì.)

I have ten yuan.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.
