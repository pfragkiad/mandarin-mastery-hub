Talking About Language Ability

Speaking English and Mandarin; ability and fluency

school-language · communication-social · daily-actions · #languages · #ability · #fluency

Learning objectives

  • Ask whether somebody speaks a language.
  • Describe your proficiency accurately and modestly.

Explanation

Learn to ask which languages someone speaks and describe what you can and cannot say. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A: 请问，你会说英文吗？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?) — Excuse me, can you speak English?

B: 不会，我不会说英文。 (Bú huì, wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén.) — No, I cannot speak English.

B: 我会说一点普通话。 (Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Pǔtōnghuà.) — I can speak a little Mandarin.

A: 你普通话说得很好。 (Nǐ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de hěn hǎo.) — You speak Mandarin very well.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 我会说普通话。 (wǒ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà.)

会 (huì) expresses acquired ability; add a language after 说 (shuō).

我会说普通话。 (wǒ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà.)

I can speak Mandarin.

会 (huì) expresses acquired ability; add a language after 说 (shuō).

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 你说得很好。 (nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.)

得 (de) introduces how well the action is performed.

你说得很好。 (nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.)

You speak very well.

得 (de) introduces how well the action is performed.

Practice
