Speaking Languages and Describing Ability

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

school-language · communication-social · daily-actions · #language-learning · #ability · #confidence · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Ask whether somebody speaks a language.
  • Describe your proficiency accurately and modestly.

Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

Model conversation

A: 请问，你会说英文吗？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?) — Excuse me, can you speak English?

B: 不会，我不会说英文。 (Bú huì, wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén.) — No, I cannot speak English.

B: 我会说一点普通话。 (Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Pǔtōnghuà.) — I can speak a little Mandarin.

A: 你普通话说得很好。 (Nǐ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de hěn hǎo.) — You speak Mandarin very well.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 会 + 说 + 语言 (huì shuō)

会 (huì) expresses acquired ability; add a language after 说 (shuō).

我会说普通话。 (wǒ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà.)

I can speak Mandarin.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 动词 + 得 + 评价 (dòngcí de)

得 (de) introduces how well the action is performed.

你说得很好。 (nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.)

You speak very well.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.
