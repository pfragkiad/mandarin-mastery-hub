# Talking About Language Ability

Learn to ask which languages someone speaks and describe what you can and cannot say.

*Speaking English and Mandarin; ability and fluency*

## Learning objectives

- Ask whether somebody speaks a language.
- Describe your proficiency accurately and modestly.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to ask which languages someone speaks and describe what you can and cannot say. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

Pronunciation note: This course uses some northern-style 儿化 (érhuà) forms. The final -r belongs to the preceding syllable, not a separate syllable. For “a little,” 一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) is common in northern-influenced Mandarin; 一点 (yìdiǎn) is a non-erhua alternative.

## Model conversation

**A:** 请问，你会说英文吗？ (Qǐngwèn, nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?) — Excuse me, can you speak English?

**B:** 不会，我不会说英文。 (Bú huì, wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén.) — No, I cannot speak English.

**B:** 我会说一点普通话。 (Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Pǔtōnghuà.) — I can speak a little Mandarin.

**A:** 你普通话说得很好。 (Nǐ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de hěn hǎo.) — You speak Mandarin very well.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 会 + 说 + 语言** (zhǔyǔ + huì + shuō + yǔyán)

&lt;who or what&gt; + **会** + **说** + &lt;language&gt;

会 (huì) expresses acquired ability; add a language after 说 (shuō).

我会说普通话。 (wǒ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà.) — I can speak Mandarin.
Use this to describe a language you can speak, even if your ability is limited.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 说 + 得 + 程度** (zhǔyǔ + shuō + de + chéngdù)

&lt;who or what&gt; + **说** + **得** + &lt;how well&gt;

得 (de) introduces how well the action is performed.

你说得很好。 (nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.) — You speak very well.
This form can compliment someone on how well they speak a language.

## Speaking a Little and Encouraging Someone

**A:** 你会说普通话吗？ (Nǐ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà ma?) — Can you speak Mandarin?

**B:** 我会说一点儿，可是说得不好。 (Wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr, kěshì shuō de bù hǎo.) — A little, but I do not speak it well.

**A:** 我觉得你说得很好。 (Wǒ juéde nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo.) — I think you speak it very well.

**B:** 谢谢！我还想多练习。 (Xièxie! Wǒ hái xiǎng duō liànxí.) — Thanks! I still want to practice more.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**主语 + 说得 + 不太好** (zhǔyǔ + shuō de + bú tài hǎo)

&lt;who or what&gt; + **说得** + **不太好**

The result complement 得 (de) describes how well an action is performed; do not confuse it with 的 (de).

我说得不太好。 (Wǒ shuō de bú tài hǎo.) — I do not speak very well.
A modest reply acknowledges limited ability without claiming you cannot speak at all.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**可是 + 对比的句子** (kěshì + duìbǐ de jùzi)

**可是** + &lt;contrasting idea&gt;

可是 (kěshì) introduces a contrast; 一点儿 (yìdiǎnr) has optional northern rhotic pronunciation.

可是我听得懂一点儿。 (Kěshì wǒ tīng de dǒng yìdiǎnr.) — But I understand a little.
The speaker contrasts one limitation with something they can still do.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** What does speaker B say about their English-speaking ability?

**Model answer:**
不会，我不会说英文。 (Bú huì, wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén.) — No, I cannot speak English.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: Cannot speak.

**Model answer:**
不会说 (bú huì shuō)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Use 得 (de) to compliment someone’s Mandarin-speaking ability.

**Model answer:**
你普通话说得很好。 (Nǐ Pǔtōnghuà shuō de hěn hǎo.) — You speak Mandarin very well.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Ask a partner what language they speak; answer using 会 (huì), 不会 (bú huì), and 一点 (yìdiǎn).

**Model answer:**
A: 你会说英文吗？ (Nǐ huì shuō Yīngwén ma?) — Can you speak English?
B: 不会，我不会说英文。 (Bú huì, wǒ bú huì shuō Yīngwén.) — No, I cannot speak English.
A: 你会说普通话吗？ (Nǐ huì shuō Pǔtōnghuà ma?) — Can you speak Mandarin?
B: 会，我会说一点普通话。 (Huì, wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎn Pǔtōnghuà.) — Yes, I speak a little Mandarin.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
