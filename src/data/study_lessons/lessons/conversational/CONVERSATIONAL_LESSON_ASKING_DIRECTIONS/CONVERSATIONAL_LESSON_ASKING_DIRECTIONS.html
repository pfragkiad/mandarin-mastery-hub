Conversational Mandarin 15 min

Asking Where Places Are

Finding streets and saying here, there, or not here

Learn to ask where a street is and explain whether a place is here or over there.

Learning objectives

  • Ask where a street or place is.
  • Contrast here, there and not here.
  • Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

Study guidance

Learn to ask where a street is and explain whether a place is here or over there. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud. Pronunciation note: This course uses some northern-style 儿化 (érhuà) forms. The final -r belongs to the preceding syllable, not a separate syllable. For location questions, 哪儿 (nǎr), 这儿 (zhèr), and 那儿 (nàr) have common alternatives 哪里 (nǎlǐ), 这里 (zhèlǐ), and 那里 (nàlǐ).

Model conversation

A

请问，学院路在哪儿？

Qǐngwèn, Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?

Excuse me, where is Xueyuan Road?

B

在那儿。

Zài nàr.

It is over there.

A

长安街也在那儿吗？

Cháng'ān Jiē yě zài nàr ma?

Is Chang’an Street also over there?

B

不，长安街在这儿。

Bù, Cháng'ān Jiē zài zhèr.

No, Chang’an Street is here.

Grammar structure and spoken example

地名 + 在 + 哪儿？ (dìmíng + zài + nǎr?)

Ask for a location with 在哪儿 (zài nǎr), without adding 吗 (ma).

学院路在哪儿？ (Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Where is Xueyuan Road?

Replace the place name to ask about a street, building, or meeting point.

Grammar structure and spoken example

不在 + 地点 (bú zài + dìdiǎn)

Negate a location with 不在 (bú zài); the 不 changes tone before fourth-tone 在.

不在这儿。 (bú zài zhèr.) — It is not here.

After correcting a mistaken location, give the right place or direction.

Study guidance

Speaking task: Role-play asking for Xueyuan Road and Chang’an Street; answer here/there/not here.

Checking Two Street Names

A

请问，您知道长安街在哪儿吗？

Qǐngwèn, nín zhīdào Cháng'ān Jiē zài nǎr ma?

Excuse me, do you know where Chang’an Avenue is?

B

对不起，我不知道。

Duìbuqǐ, wǒ bù zhīdào.

Sorry, I do not know.

A

那学院路呢？

Nà Xuéyuàn Lù ne?

What about Xueyuan Road?

B

学院路在前面，可是长安街不在这儿。

Xuéyuàn Lù zài qiánmiàn, kěshì Cháng'ān Jiē bú zài zhèr.

Xueyuan Road is ahead, but Chang’an Avenue is not here.
