Asking Where Places Are

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

places-directions · travel-transport · #directions · #locations · #streets · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Ask where a street or place is.
  • Contrast here, there and not here.

Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

Model conversation

A: 请问，学院路在哪儿？ (Qǐngwèn, Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Excuse me, where is Xueyuan Road?

B: 在那儿。 (Zài nàr.) — It is over there.

A: 长安街也在那儿吗？ (Cháng’ān Jiē yě zài nàr ma?) — Is Chang’an Street also over there?

B: 不，长安街在这儿。 (Bù, Cháng’ān Jiē zài zhèr.) — No, Chang’an Street is here.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 地点 + 在 + 哪儿 (dìdiǎn zài nǎr)

Ask for a location with 在哪儿 (zài nǎr), without adding 吗 (ma).

学院路在哪儿？ (Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?)

Where is Xueyuan Road?

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 不在 + 地点 (bú zài dìdiǎn)

Negate a location with 不在 (bú zài); the 不 changes tone before fourth-tone 在.

不在这儿。 (bú zài zhèr.)

It is not here.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.
