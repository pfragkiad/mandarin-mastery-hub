# Asking Where Places Are

Learn to ask where a street is and explain whether a place is here or over there.

*Finding streets and saying here, there, or not here*

## Learning objectives

- Ask where a street or place is.
- Contrast here, there and not here.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to ask where a street is and explain whether a place is here or over there. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

Pronunciation note: This course uses some northern-style 儿化 (érhuà) forms. The final -r belongs to the preceding syllable, not a separate syllable. For location questions, 哪儿 (nǎr), 这儿 (zhèr), and 那儿 (nàr) have common alternatives 哪里 (nǎlǐ), 这里 (zhèlǐ), and 那里 (nàlǐ).

## Model conversation

**A:** 请问，学院路在哪儿？ (Qǐngwèn, Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Excuse me, where is Xueyuan Road?

**B:** 在那儿。 (Zài nàr.) — It is over there.

**A:** 长安街也在那儿吗？ (Cháng'ān Jiē yě zài nàr ma?) — Is Chang’an Street also over there?

**B:** 不，长安街在这儿。 (Bù, Cháng'ān Jiē zài zhèr.) — No, Chang’an Street is here.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**地名 + 在 + 哪儿？** (dìmíng + zài + nǎr?)

&lt;place you’re looking for&gt; + **在** + **哪儿**？

Ask for a location with 在哪儿 (zài nǎr), without adding 吗 (ma).

学院路在哪儿？ (Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Where is Xueyuan Road?
Replace the place name to ask about a street, building, or meeting point.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**不在 + 地点** (bú zài + dìdiǎn)

**不在** + &lt;place&gt;

Negate a location with 不在 (bú zài); the 不 changes tone before fourth-tone 在.

不在这儿。 (bú zài zhèr.) — It is not here.
After correcting a mistaken location, give the right place or direction.

## Checking Two Street Names

**A:** 请问，您知道长安街在哪儿吗？ (Qǐngwèn, nín zhīdào Cháng'ān Jiē zài nǎr ma?) — Excuse me, do you know where Chang’an Avenue is?

**B:** 对不起，我不知道。 (Duìbuqǐ, wǒ bù zhīdào.) — Sorry, I do not know.

**A:** 那学院路呢？ (Nà Xuéyuàn Lù ne?) — What about Xueyuan Road?

**B:** 学院路在前面，可是长安街不在这儿。 (Xuéyuàn Lù zài qiánmiàn, kěshì Cháng'ān Jiē bú zài zhèr.) — Xueyuan Road is ahead, but Chang’an Avenue is not here.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** Where does speaker B first say Xueyuan Road is?

**Model answer:**
在那儿。 (Zài nàr.) — It is over there.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: It is here; located here.

**Model answer:**
在这儿 (zài zhèr)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Say that Chang’an Street is not here, using 不在 (bú zài).

**Model answer:**
长安街不在这儿。 (Cháng'ān Jiē bú zài zhèr.) — Chang'an Street is not here.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Role-play asking for Xueyuan Road and Chang’an Street; answer here/there/not here.

**Model answer:**
A: 请问，学院路在哪儿？ (Qǐngwèn, Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Excuse me, where is Xueyuan Road?
B: 学院路在那儿。 (Xuéyuàn Lù zài nàr.) — Xueyuan Road is over there.
A: 长安街也在那儿吗？ (Cháng'ān Jiē yě zài nàr ma?) — Is Chang'an Street over there too?
B: 不，长安街不在那儿，在这儿。 (Bù, Cháng'ān Jiē bú zài nàr, zài zhèr.) — No, Chang'an Street is not there. It is here.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
