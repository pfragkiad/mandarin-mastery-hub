# Asking Where Places Are

Practice ask where a street or place is. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Ask where a street or place is.

- Contrast here, there and not here.

## Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

## Model conversation

**A:** 请问，学院路在哪儿？ (Qǐngwèn, Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?) — Excuse me, where is Xueyuan Road?

**B:** 在那儿。 (Zài nàr.) — It is over there.

**A:** 长安街也在那儿吗？ (Cháng’ān Jiē yě zài nàr ma?) — Is Chang’an Street also over there?

**B:** 不，长安街在这儿。 (Bù, Cháng’ān Jiē zài zhèr.) — No, Chang’an Street is here.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 地点 + 在 + 哪儿 (dìdiǎn zài nǎr)

Ask for a location with 在哪儿 (zài nǎr), without adding 吗 (ma).

**学院路在哪儿？**
*Xuéyuàn Lù zài nǎr?*
Where is Xueyuan Road?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 不在 + 地点 (bú zài dìdiǎn)

Negate a location with 不在 (bú zài); the 不 changes tone before fourth-tone 在.

**不在这儿。**
*bú zài zhèr.*
It is not here.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

长安街在哪儿 (Cháng’ān Jiē zài nǎr) — Where is Chang’an Street?

在这儿 (zài zhèr) — It is here; located here.

在那儿 (zài nàr) — It is there; located there.

不在这儿 (bú zài zhèr) — Not here.

我不知道 (wǒ bù zhīdào) — I do not know.

学院路在那儿 (Xuéyuàn Lù zài nàr) — Xueyuan Road is over there.

长安街在这儿 (Cháng'ān Jiē zài zhèr) — Chang'an Street is here.

长安街不在这儿 (Cháng'ān Jiē bú zài zhèr) — Chang'an Street is not here.

学院路呢 (Xuéyuàn Lù ne) — How about Xueyuan Road?
