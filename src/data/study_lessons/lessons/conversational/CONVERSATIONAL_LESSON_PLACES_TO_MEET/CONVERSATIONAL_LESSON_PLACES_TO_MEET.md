# Choosing Where to Meet

Learn to invite someone to meet for food, decline one place, and suggest another.

*Restaurants, cafés, and proposing a place*

## Learning objectives

- Suggest where to go.
- Accept or reject a proposed location.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to invite someone to meet for food, decline one place, and suggest another. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想去哪儿吃午饭？ (Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr chī wǔfàn?) — Where would you like to have lunch?

**B:** 我想去饭店。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn.) — I would like to go to a restaurant.

**A:** 去北京饭店，好吗？ (Qù Běijīng Fàndiàn, hǎo ma?) — How about going to the Beijing Hotel?

**B:** 好，我们去吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù ba.) — Okay, let us go.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**想 + 去 + 地点 + 动作** (xiǎng + qù + dìdiǎn + dòngzuò)

**想** + **去** + &lt;place&gt; + &lt;what to do&gt;

Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

我想去饭店吃。 (wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn chī.) — I want to go to a restaurant to eat.
Link a proposed destination to the activity you want to do there.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**在 + 地点 + 动作** (zài + dìdiǎn + dòngzuò)

**在** + &lt;place&gt; + &lt;what to do&gt;

Use 在 (zài) before a place to say where an action happens.

我想在这儿喝茶。 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.) — I want to drink tea here.
You can use this structure to suggest staying here instead of going elsewhere.

## Key expressions and vocabulary

- 你想去哪儿 (nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr) — Where do you want to go?  [Northern-style -r]
- 你想去哪儿吃 (nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr chī) — Where do you want to eat?  [Northern-style -r]
- 去咖啡厅 (qù kāfēitīng) — Go to a café.
- 去饭店 (qù fàndiàn) — Go to a restaurant.
- 我想去酒店 (wǒ xiǎng qù jiǔdiàn) — I want to go to the hotel.
- 我想去北京饭店 (wǒ xiǎng qù Běijīng Fàndiàn) — I want to go to the Beijing Hotel.
- 我想去你那儿 (wǒ xiǎng qù nǐ nàr) — I want to go to your place.  [Northern-style -r]
- 我不想在这儿吃 (wǒ bù xiǎng zài zhèr chī) — I do not want to eat here.  [Northern-style -r]
- 你想去哪儿喝 (nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr hē) — Where would you like to go for a drink?  [Northern-style -r]
- 北京饭店在哪儿 (Běijīng Fàndiàn zài nǎr) — Where is the Beijing Hotel?  [Northern-style -r]
- 你想去酒店吗 (nǐ xiǎng qù jiǔdiàn ma) — Do you want to go to the hotel?
- 去你那儿 (qù nǐ nàr) — Go to your place.  [Northern-style -r]
- 去我那儿 (qù wǒ nàr) — Go to my place.  [Northern-style -r]
- 在饭店 (zài fàndiàn) — At a restaurant.
- 我想在这儿吃 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr chī) — I want to eat here.  [Northern-style -r]
- 北京饭店 (Běijīng Fàndiàn) — Beijing Hotel; hotel name
- 咖啡厅 (kāfēitīng) — café
- 饭店 (fàndiàn) — restaurant; hotel, context-dependent
- 酒店 (jiǔdiàn) — hotel

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Invite a friend to a café, restaurant or hotel; decline one place and suggest another.

## Meeting at a Café Rather Than a Hotel

**A:** 明天我们在哪儿见面？ (Míngtiān wǒmen zài nǎr jiànmiàn?) — Where shall we meet tomorrow?

**B:** 你想去北京饭店吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng qù Běijīng Fàndiàn ma?) — Would you like to go to the Beijing Hotel?

**A:** 我不想去酒店。咖啡厅怎么样？ (Wǒ bù xiǎng qù jiǔdiàn. Kāfēitīng zěnmeyàng?) — I would rather not go to a hotel. How about a café?

**B:** 好，我们在咖啡厅见。 (Hǎo, wǒmen zài kāfēitīng jiàn.) — Fine, let us meet at the café.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** Where does speaker B first suggest meeting?

**Model answer:**
我想去饭店。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn.) — I would like to go to a restaurant.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: Go to a restaurant.

**Model answer:**
去饭店 (qù fàndiàn)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Say you want to drink tea here, using 在 (zài) before the location.

**Model answer:**
我想在这儿喝茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.) — I want to drink tea here.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Invite a friend to a café, restaurant or hotel; decline one place and suggest another.

**Model answer:**
A: 你想在饭店吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng zài fàndiàn chī wǔfàn ma?) — Would you like to have lunch at a restaurant?
B: 不想，我想去咖啡馆。 (Bù xiǎng, wǒ xiǎng qù kāfēiguǎn.) — No, I would rather go to a café.
A: 好，我们去咖啡馆吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù kāfēiguǎn ba.) — Okay, let us go to the café.
B: 好，下午一点钟见。 (Hǎo, xiàwǔ yì diǎn zhōng jiàn.) — Okay, see you at one in the afternoon.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
