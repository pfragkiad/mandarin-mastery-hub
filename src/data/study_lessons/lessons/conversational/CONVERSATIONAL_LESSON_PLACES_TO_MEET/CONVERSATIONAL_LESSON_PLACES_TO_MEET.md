# Choosing Where to Meet

Learn to invite someone to meet for food, decline one place, and suggest another.

## Learning objectives

- Suggest where to go.

- Accept or reject a proposed location.

## Explanation

Learn to invite someone to meet for food, decline one place, and suggest another. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想去哪儿吃午饭？ (Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr chī wǔfàn?) — Where would you like to have lunch?

**B:** 我想去饭店。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn.) — I would like to go to a restaurant.

**A:** 去北京饭店，好吗？ (Qù Běijīng Fàndiàn, hǎo ma?) — How about going to the Beijing Hotel?

**B:** 好，我们去吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù ba.) — Okay, let us go.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 我想去饭店吃。 (wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn chī.)

Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

**我想去饭店吃。**
*wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn chī.*
I want to go to a restaurant to eat.
**Usage:** Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 我想在这儿喝茶。 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.)

Use 在 (zài) before a place to say where an action happens.

**我想在这儿喝茶。**
*wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.*
I want to drink tea here.
**Usage:** Use 在 (zài) before a place to say where an action happens.

## Review Set

去咖啡厅 (qù kāfēitīng) — Go to a café.

去饭店 (qù fàndiàn) — Go to a restaurant.

我想去酒店 (wǒ xiǎng qù jiǔdiàn) — I want to go to the hotel.

我想去北京饭店 (wǒ xiǎng qù Běijīng Fàndiàn) — I want to go to the Beijing Hotel.

我想去你那儿 (wǒ xiǎng qù nǐ nàr) — I want to go to your place.

我不想在这儿吃 (wǒ bù xiǎng zài zhèr chī) — I do not want to eat here.

你想去哪儿喝 (nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr hē) — Where would you like to go for a drink?

北京饭店在哪儿 (Běijīng Fàndiàn zài nǎr) — Where is the Beijing Hotel?

你想去酒店吗 (nǐ xiǎng qù jiǔdiàn ma) — Do you want to go to the hotel?

去你那儿 (qù nǐ nàr) — Go to your place.

去我那儿 (qù wǒ nàr) — Go to my place.

在饭店 (zài fàndiàn) — At a restaurant.

我想在这儿吃 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr chī) — I want to eat here.

## Explanation

Speaking task: Invite a friend to a café, restaurant or hotel; decline one place and suggest another.
