Choosing Where to Meet and Eat

Topic-based beginner conversation lesson

travel-transport · places-directions · food-drink · #restaurants · #hotel · #meeting-places · #conversational-mandarin · #beginner · #topic-lesson

Learning objectives

  • Suggest where to go.
  • Accept or reject a proposed location.

Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

Model conversation

A: 你想去哪儿吃午饭？ (Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr chī wǔfàn?) — Where would you like to have lunch?

B: 我想去饭店。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn.) — I would like to go to a restaurant.

A: 去北京饭店，好吗？ (Qù Běijīng Fàndiàn, hǎo ma?) — How about going to the Beijing Hotel?

B: 好，我们去吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù ba.) — Okay, let us go.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 去 + 地点 + 动词 (qù dìdiǎn dòngcí)

Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

我想去饭店吃。 (wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn chī.)

I want to go to a restaurant to eat.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 在 + 地点 + 动词 (zài dìdiǎn dòngcí)

Use 在 (zài) before a place to say where an action happens.

我想在这儿喝茶。 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.)

I want to drink tea here.

Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Explanation

Speaking task: Invite a friend to a café, restaurant or hotel; decline one place and suggest another.
