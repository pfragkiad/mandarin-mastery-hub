Choosing Where to Meet

Restaurants, cafés, and proposing a place

travel-transport · places-directions · food-drink · #meeting-places · #invitations · #alternatives

Learning objectives

  • Suggest where to go.
  • Accept or reject a proposed location.

Explanation

Learn to invite someone to meet for food, decline one place, and suggest another. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A: 你想去哪儿吃午饭？ (Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr chī wǔfàn?) — Where would you like to have lunch?

B: 我想去饭店。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn.) — I would like to go to a restaurant.

A: 去北京饭店，好吗？ (Qù Běijīng Fàndiàn, hǎo ma?) — How about going to the Beijing Hotel?

B: 好，我们去吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù ba.) — Okay, let us go.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 我想去饭店吃。 (wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn chī.)

Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

我想去饭店吃。 (wǒ xiǎng qù fàndiàn chī.)

I want to go to a restaurant to eat.

Combine destination and purpose in one sentence.

Grammar Pattern

Pattern: 我想在这儿喝茶。 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.)

Use 在 (zài) before a place to say where an action happens.

我想在这儿喝茶。 (wǒ xiǎng zài zhèr hē chá.)

I want to drink tea here.

Explanation

Speaking task: Invite a friend to a café, restaurant or hotel; decline one place and suggest another.

Production

Invite a friend to a café, restaurant or hotel; decline one place and suggest another.

Show answer
A: 你想在饭店吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng zài fàndiàn chī wǔfàn ma?) — Would you like to have lunch at a restaurant? B: 不想，我想去咖啡馆。 (Bù xiǎng, wǒ xiǎng qù kāfēiguǎn.) — No, I would rather go to a café. A: 好，我们去咖啡馆吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù kāfēiguǎn ba.) — Okay, let us go to the café. B: 好，下午一点钟见。 (Hǎo, xiàwǔ yì diǎn zhōng jiàn.) — Okay, see you at one in the afternoon.

Complete sample role-play. Practise both speakers, then substitute your own details.
