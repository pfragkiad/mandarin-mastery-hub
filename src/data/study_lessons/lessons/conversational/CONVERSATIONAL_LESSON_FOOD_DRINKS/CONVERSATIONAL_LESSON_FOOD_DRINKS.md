# Ordering Food and Drinks

Learn to choose a drink, order the number of cups you want, and ask for food.

*Choosing beverages and ordering by the cup*

## Learning objectives

- Express what you would like to eat or drink.
- Offer a simple choice of drinks.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to choose a drink, order the number of cups you want, and ask for food. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想喝茶还是啤酒？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ?) — Would you like tea or beer?

**B:** 我想喝一点茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — I would like some tea.

**A:** 你想吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī wǔfàn ma?) — Do you want to have lunch?

**B:** 好，我们去饭店吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù fàndiàn ba.) — Yes, let us go to a restaurant.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**想喝 + 饮料 + 还是 + 饮料？** (xiǎng hē + yǐnliào + háishi + yǐnliào?)

Use 还是 (háishi) to present alternatives in a question.

你想喝茶还是啤酒？ (nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ?) — Would you like tea or beer?
Offer two drinks and let the listener choose one.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**数量 + 杯 + 饮料** (shùliàng + bēi + yǐnliào)

杯 (bēi) counts cups or glasses of drinks.

我要两杯茶。 (wǒ yào liǎng bēi chá.) — I want two cups of tea.
Use the classifier when ordering a specific number of cups.

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Order a drink, specify the number of glasses, and choose between two options.

## Ordering Two Drinks

**A:** 您好，两位想喝什么？ (Nín hǎo, liǎng wèi xiǎng hē shénme?) — Hello, what would you two like to drink?

**B:** 我想要一杯茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng yào yì bēi chá.) — I would like a cup of tea.

**C:** 我要两杯啤酒。 (Wǒ yào liǎng bēi píjiǔ.) — I would like two glasses of beer.

**A:** 好的。还想吃点什么吗？ (Hǎo de. Hái xiǎng chī diǎn shénme ma?) — All right. Would you like something to eat too?

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** Which drink does speaker B choose?

**Model answer:**
我想喝一点茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — I would like some tea.

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: I would like a little beer.

**Model answer:**
我想喝一点啤酒 (wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn píjiǔ)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Order two cups of tea in Mandarin, using the classifier 杯 (bēi).

**Model answer:**
我要两杯茶。 (Wǒ yào liǎng bēi chá.) — I want two cups of tea.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Order a drink, specify the number of glasses, and choose between two options.

**Model answer:**
