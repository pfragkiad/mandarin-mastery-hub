# Ordering Food and Drinks

Practice express what you would like to eat or drink. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Express what you would like to eat or drink.

- Offer a simple choice of drinks.

## Explanation

Topic lesson based on Conversational Mandarin, tracks 1–16. Dialogue is an editorial teaching exchange using source-attested expressions; it is not presented as a verbatim audio transcript.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想喝茶还是啤酒？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ?) — Would you like tea or beer?

**B:** 我想喝一点茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē yìdiǎn chá.) — I would like some tea.

**A:** 你想吃午饭吗？ (Nǐ xiǎng chī wǔfàn ma?) — Do you want to have lunch?

**B:** 好，我们去饭店吧。 (Hǎo, wǒmen qù fàndiàn ba.) — Yes, let us go to a restaurant.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** A 还是 B (A háishi B)

Use 还是 (háishi) to present alternatives in a question.

**你想喝茶还是啤酒？**
*nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi píjiǔ?*
Would you like tea or beer?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 数量 + 杯 + 饮料 (shùliàng bēi yǐnliào)

杯 (bēi) counts cups or glasses of drinks.

**我要两杯茶。**
*wǒ yào liǎng bēi chá.*
I want two cups of tea.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

Speaking task: Order a drink, specify the number of glasses, and choose between two options.
