# Agreeing on a Meeting Time

Learn to propose a time for dinner, reject a conflicting time, and agree on another.

*Suggesting, rejecting, and settling on clock times*

## Learning objectives

- Ask and tell the time.
- Suggest alternate meeting times.
- Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

## Study guidance

Learn to propose a time for dinner, reject a conflicting time, and agree on another. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想几点钟吃晚饭？ (Nǐ xiǎng jǐ diǎn zhōng chī wǎnfàn?) — What time do you want to have dinner?

**B:** 六点钟，好吗？ (Liù diǎn zhōng, hǎo ma?) — Is six o’clock okay?

**A:** 六点钟不行。 (Liù diǎn zhōng bù xíng.) — Six o’clock does not work.

**B:** 那么七点钟吧。 (Nàme qī diǎn zhōng ba.) — Then let’s say seven o’clock.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**现在 + 几点钟？** (xiànzài + jǐ diǎn zhōng?)

几点钟 (jǐ diǎn zhōng) asks for a clock time, not a duration.

现在几点钟？ (xiànzài jǐ diǎn zhōng?) — What time is it now?
Ask for the current clock time before arranging a meeting.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**时间 + 不行** (shíjiān + bù xíng)

Reject a proposed time with 不行 (bù xíng), then suggest an alternative.

八点钟不行。 (bā diǎn zhōng bù xíng.) — Eight o’clock does not work.
Give an alternative time after declining the first suggestion.

## Study guidance

Speaking task: Negotiate a dinner time by proposing six, seven or eight o’clock.

## Negotiating Another Time

**A:** 我们九点钟见面，可以吗？ (Wǒmen jiǔ diǎn zhōng jiànmiàn, kěyǐ ma?) — Can we meet at nine?

**B:** 九点不行。十点钟怎么样？ (Jiǔ diǎn bù xíng. Shí diǎn zhōng zěnmeyàng?) — Nine will not work. How about ten?

**A:** 十点太晚了。八点钟呢？ (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le. Bā diǎn zhōng ne?) — Ten is too late. What about eight?

**B:** 八点也不行。我们明天六点见吧。 (Bā diǎn yě bù xíng. Wǒmen míngtiān liù diǎn jiàn ba.) — Eight does not work either. Let us meet at six tomorrow.

**A:** 好，明天六点钟见。 (Hǎo, míngtiān liù diǎn zhōng jiàn.) — All right, see you at six tomorrow.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**时间 + 不行；另一个时间 + 可以** (shíjiān + bù xíng; lìng yí ge shíjiān + kěyǐ)

不行 (bù xíng) rejects a proposal; 可以 (kěyǐ) accepts an alternative.

八点不行，九点可以。 (Bā diǎn bù xíng, jiǔ diǎn kěyǐ.) — Eight does not work; nine works.
This keeps negotiations moving by supplying an acceptable alternative.

## Grammar structure and spoken example

**太 + 形容词 + 了** (tài + xíngróngcí + le)

太 (tài) + adjective + 了 (le) expresses an excessive degree or a strong judgment.

十点太晚了。 (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le.) — Ten o’clock is too late.
In scheduling, it explains why a proposed time is unsuitable.

## Practice

**Question 1 (comprehension):** What time does speaker B propose first?

**Model answer:**
六点钟，好吗？ (Liù diǎn zhōng, hǎo ma?) — Is six o'clock okay?

Check the model dialogue and identify the relevant response.

**Question 2 (production):** Translate into Mandarin: Is one o’clock okay?

**Model answer:**
一点钟好吗 (yì diǎn zhōng hǎo ma)

One model answer; another natural equivalent may be acceptable.

**Question 3 (production):** Say that eight o’clock does not work for you.

**Model answer:**
八点钟不行。 (Bā diǎn zhōng bù xíng.) — Eight o'clock does not work.

One natural model response; alternatives that express the same meaning are possible.

**Question 4 (production):** Negotiate a dinner time by proposing six, seven or eight o’clock.

**Model answer:**
A: 六点钟吃晚饭，好吗？ (Liù diǎn zhōng chī wǎnfàn, hǎo ma?) — Shall we have dinner at six?
B: 六点钟不行。 (Liù diǎn zhōng bù xíng.) — Six does not work.
A: 七点钟呢？ (Qī diǎn zhōng ne?) — How about seven?
B: 七点钟也不行。 (Qī diǎn zhōng yě bù xíng.) — Seven does not work either.
A: 八点钟好吗？ (Bā diǎn zhōng hǎo ma?) — Is eight o'clock okay?
B: 好，八点钟见。 (Hǎo, bā diǎn zhōng jiàn.) — Yes, see you at eight.

Complete sample role-play. practice both speakers, then substitute your own details.
