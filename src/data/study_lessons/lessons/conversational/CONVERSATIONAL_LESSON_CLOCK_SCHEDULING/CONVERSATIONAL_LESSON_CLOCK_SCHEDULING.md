# Clock Time and Negotiating a Meeting

Practice ask and tell the time. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track.

## Learning objectives

- Ask and tell the time.

- Suggest alternate meeting times.

## Explanation

Practice ask and tell the time. Transcript-referenced conversational Mandarin study organized by communicative goal rather than audio track. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practise the patterns and speak aloud.

## Model conversation

**A:** 你想几点钟吃晚饭？ (Nǐ xiǎng jǐ diǎn zhōng chī wǎnfàn?) — What time do you want to have dinner?

**B:** 六点钟，好吗？ (Liù diǎn zhōng, hǎo ma?) — Is six o’clock okay?

**A:** 六点钟不行。 (Liù diǎn zhōng bù xíng.) — Six o’clock does not work.

**B:** 那么七点钟吧。 (Nàme qī diǎn zhōng ba.) — Then let’s say seven o’clock.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 几点钟 (jǐ diǎn zhōng)

几点钟 (jǐ diǎn zhōng) asks for a clock time, not a duration.

**现在几点钟？**
*xiànzài jǐ diǎn zhōng?*
What time is it now?
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.

## Grammar Pattern

**Pattern:** 时间 + 好吗 / 不行 (shíjiān hǎo ma / bù xíng)

Suggest a time with 好吗 (hǎo ma); reject an unsuitable time with 不行 (bù xíng).

**八点钟不行。**
*bā diǎn zhōng bù xíng.*
Eight o’clock does not work.
**Usage:** Use this construction in your own dialogue.
