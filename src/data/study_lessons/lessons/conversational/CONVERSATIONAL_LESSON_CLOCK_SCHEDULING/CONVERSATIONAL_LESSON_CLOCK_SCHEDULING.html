Conversational Mandarin 15 min

Agreeing on a Meeting Time

Suggesting, rejecting, and settling on clock times

Learn to propose a time for dinner, reject a conflicting time, and agree on another.

Learning objectives

  • Ask and tell the time.
  • Suggest alternate meeting times.
  • Use the additional situational conversation to apply the lesson in a longer exchange.

Study guidance

Learn to propose a time for dinner, reject a conflicting time, and agree on another. Use the model conversation to notice the expressions, then practice the patterns and speak aloud.

Model conversation

A

你想几点钟吃晚饭？

Nǐ xiǎng jǐ diǎn zhōng chī wǎnfàn?

What time do you want to have dinner?

B

六点钟，好吗？

Liù diǎn zhōng, hǎo ma?

Is six o’clock okay?

A

六点钟不行。

Liù diǎn zhōng bù xíng.

Six o’clock does not work.

B

那么七点钟吧。

Nàme qī diǎn zhōng ba.

Then let’s say seven o’clock.

Grammar structure and spoken example

现在 + 几点钟？ (xiànzài + jǐ diǎn zhōng?)

现在 + 几点钟？

几点钟 (jǐ diǎn zhōng) asks for a clock time, not a duration.

现在几点钟？ (xiànzài jǐ diǎn zhōng?) — What time is it now?

Ask for the current clock time before arranging a meeting.

Grammar structure and spoken example

时间 + 不行 (shíjiān + bù xíng)

<proposed time> + 不行

Reject a proposed time with 不行 (bù xíng), then suggest an alternative.

八点钟不行。 (bā diǎn zhōng bù xíng.) — Eight o’clock does not work.

Give an alternative time after declining the first suggestion.

Study guidance

Speaking task: Negotiate a dinner time by proposing six, seven or eight o’clock.

Negotiating Another Time

A

我们九点钟见面，可以吗？

Wǒmen jiǔ diǎn zhōng jiànmiàn, kěyǐ ma?

Can we meet at nine?

B

九点不行。十点钟怎么样？

Jiǔ diǎn bù xíng. Shí diǎn zhōng zěnmeyàng?

Nine will not work. How about ten?

A

十点太晚了。八点钟呢？

Shí diǎn tài wǎn le. Bā diǎn zhōng ne?

Ten is too late. What about eight?

B

八点也不行。我们明天六点见吧。

Bā diǎn yě bù xíng. Wǒmen míngtiān liù diǎn jiàn ba.

Eight does not work either. Let us meet at six tomorrow.

A

好，明天六点钟见。

Hǎo, míngtiān liù diǎn zhōng jiàn.

All right, see you at six tomorrow.

Grammar structure and spoken example

时间 + 不行；另一个时间 + 可以 (shíjiān + bù xíng; lìng yí ge shíjiān + kěyǐ)

<proposed time> + 不行；<another time> + 可以

不行 (bù xíng) rejects a proposal; 可以 (kěyǐ) accepts an alternative.

八点不行，九点可以。 (Bā diǎn bù xíng, jiǔ diǎn kěyǐ.) — Eight does not work; nine works.

This keeps negotiations moving by supplying an acceptable alternative.

Grammar structure and spoken example

太 + 形容词 + 了 (tài + xíngróngcí + le)

太 + <describing word> + 了

太 (tài) + adjective + 了 (le) expresses an excessive degree or a strong judgment.

十点太晚了。 (Shí diǎn tài wǎn le.) — Ten o’clock is too late.

In scheduling, it explains why a proposed time is unsuitable.
