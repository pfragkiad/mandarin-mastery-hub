# **Mandarin Learning Report: 次 (cì) — Times and Occurrences**

Source: one_time.mp4 · Duration: 14 seconds · Reviewed and validated

This lesson introduces six common Mandarin expressions built around 次 (cì), a measure word used for counting occurrences of actions.

**Source distinction:** All six main expressions were extracted directly from the video. The example sentences, conversational alternatives, and grammar explanations are supplementary learning material.

## **1. Main vocabulary — Extracted from video**

| Chinese | Pinyin      | English                      |
| ------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| 一次      | yí cì       | Once; one time               |
| 上一次     | shàng yí cì | Last time; previous occasion |
| 这一次     | zhè yí cì   | This time                    |
| 下一次     | xià yí cì   | Next time                    |
| 每一次     | měi yí cì   | Every time                   |
| 再一次     | zài yí cì   | Once again; one more time    |

All six expressions are valid in modern Mandarin. The pinyin reflects the usual pronunciation change of 一 (yī) to yí before 次 (cì).

## **2. Example sentences — Added for learning**

These six sentences are natural Mandarin examples, not quotations from the video.

**1. 一次 (yí cì) — Once**

我去过北京一次。

Wǒ qù guo Běijīng yí cì.

I've been to Beijing once.

Grammar: 过 (guo) marks experience; 一次 (yí cì) counts the occurrence.

**2. 上一次 (shàng yí cì) — Last time**

上一次我没听懂。

Shàng yí cì wǒ méi tīng dǒng.

Last time, I didn't understand what I heard.

Grammar: 没听懂 (méi tīng dǒng) expresses failure to understand through listening.

**3. 这一次 (zhè yí cì) — This time**

这一次我明白了。

Zhè yí cì wǒ míngbai le.

This time, I understood.

Grammar: 了 (le) marks a new state of understanding.

**4. 下一次 (xià yí cì) — Next time**

下一次我们一起去吧。

Xià yí cì wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba.

Let's go together next time.

Grammar: 吧 (ba) softens the statement into a suggestion.

**5. 每一次 (měi yí cì) — Every time**

每一次见到你，我都很开心。

Měi yí cì jiàn dào nǐ, wǒ dōu hěn kāixīn.

Every time I see you, I'm very happy.

Grammar: 每一次 (měi yí cì) and 都 (dōu) form a highly conventional every-time pattern.

**6. 再一次 (zài yí cì) — Once again**

我想再一次感谢你。

Wǒ xiǎng zài yí cì gǎnxiè nǐ.

I want to thank you once again.

Grammar: 再一次 (zài yí cì) indicates repetition and modifies the verb 感谢 (gǎnxiè).

## **3. Additional vocabulary — Components of the expressions**

| Chinese | Pinyin | English             | Role                    |
| ------- | ------ | ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| 一       | yī     | One                 | Numeral                 |
| 次       | cì     | Time; occurrence    | Measure word            |
| 上       | shàng  | Previous; preceding | Contextual modifier     |
| 这       | zhè    | This                | Demonstrative           |
| 下       | xià    | Next; following     | Contextual modifier     |
| 每       | měi    | Every; each         | Distributive determiner |
| 再       | zài    | Again; once more    | Adverb                  |

The meanings of 上 (shàng) and 下 (xià) listed here are contextual. Individually, the characters have additional meanings such as "up/above" and "down/below".

## **4. Useful conversational alternatives**

In everyday Mandarin, several of the video's longer expressions are commonly shortened.

| Video expression  | Common shorter form | Meaning    |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ---------- |
| 上一次 (shàng yí cì) | 上次 (shàng cì)       | Last time  |
| 这一次 (zhè yí cì)   | 这次 (zhè cì)         | This time  |
| 下一次 (xià yí cì)   | 下次 (xià cì)         | Next time  |
| 每一次 (měi yí cì)   | 每次 (měi cì)         | Every time |

Both forms are grammatically correct. The shorter versions are especially natural in everyday speech. The longer versions can add emphasis to a particular occasion.

**Related expression — not a shortened form:**

再来一次 (zài lái yí cì) — Do it one more time; have another go.

This is a verb phrase containing 来 (lái), rather than a direct shortening of 再一次 (zài yí cì).

## **5. Grammar observations**

### **5.1. 次 (cì) as a measure word for occurrences**

次 (cì) counts how many times an action takes place.

| Expression | Pinyin        | English           |
| ---------- | ------------- | ----------------- |
| 去一次        | qù yí cì      | Go once           |
| 看一次        | kàn yí cì     | Watch/see once    |
| 试一次        | shì yí cì     | Try once          |
| 再试一次       | zài shì yí cì | Try one more time |

### **5.2. Frequency complements vs. temporal expressions**

This distinction is important for learners.

| Expression        | Grammatical role            | Function                        |
| ----------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 去一次 (qù yí cì)    | Verb + frequency complement | Counts action occurrences       |
| 上一次 (shàng yí cì) | Temporal noun phrase        | Identifies a previous occasion  |
| 这一次 (zhè yí cì)   | Temporal noun phrase        | Identifies this occasion        |
| 下一次 (xià yí cì)   | Temporal noun phrase        | Identifies a following occasion |
| 每一次 (měi yí cì)   | Quantified temporal phrase  | Refers to every occurrence      |
| 再一次 (zài yí cì)   | Adverbial expression        | Indicates repetition            |

Compare these two sentences:

**Counting occurrences**

我去过北京一次。

Wǒ qù guo Běijīng yí cì.

I've been to Beijing once.

**Identifying an occasion**

上一次我去了北京。

Shàng yí cì wǒ qù le Běijīng.

Last time, I went to Beijing.

The first answers **"How many times?"** The second identifies **"Which occasion?"**

### **5.3. Tone sandhi for 一 (yī)**

The character 一 (yī) changes pronunciation depending on the following syllable.

| Context                                              | Pronunciation | Example      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Before a fourth tone                                 | yí            | 一次 (yí cì)   |
| Before first, second, or third tones                 | yì            | 一天 (yì tiān) |
| Counting or numbering, when pronounced independently | yī            | 一 (yī)       |

The video consistently uses yí cì because 次 (cì) has a fourth tone.

### **5.4. 再 (zài) vs. 在 (zài)**

These two characters have identical pronunciation but different grammatical functions.

| Character | Meaning                       | Example                             |
| --------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 再 (zài)   | Again; once more              | 再试一次 (zài shì yí cì) — Try again    |
| 在 (zài)   | At; in; ongoing action marker | 我在学习 (wǒ zài xuéxí) — I am studying |

### **5.5. The 每…都 (měi…dōu) pattern**

A very common construction uses 每 (měi) in a quantified phrase and 都 (dōu) in the main clause.

每一次见到你，我都很开心。

*Měi yí cì jiàn dào nǐ, wǒ dōu hěn kāixīn.*

Every time I see you, I'm very happy.

This pairing emphasizes that the statement applies to every occurrence. Although highly conventional, 都 (dōu) is not obligatory in every grammatical construction containing 每一次 (měi yí cì).
