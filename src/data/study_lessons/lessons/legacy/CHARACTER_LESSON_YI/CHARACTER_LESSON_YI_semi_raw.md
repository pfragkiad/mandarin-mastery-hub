# 以 (yǐ) — Character Learning Guide

> **Lesson focus:** Learn the very common complete words containing **以 (yǐ)**, then recognize the more formal patterns meaning “by means of” and “taking X as Y.”
>
> **Core principle:** **以 (yǐ)** is a character representing a morpheme; **可以 (kěyǐ)** and **以为 (yǐwéi)** are words; **以……为…… (yǐ…wéi…)** is a construction. These are different linguistic units.
>
> **Learning priority:** Study common modern compounds first. Formal constructions are a second step. Classical Chinese appears only in the historical appendix.

### Reading and meaning map

| Reading | Sense or function | Register | Typical environment | Confidence / source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **yǐ** | Element in complete lexicalized words | Spoken and written | **可以 (kěyǐ)**, **所以 (suǒyǐ)**, **以为 (yǐwéi)** | Conventional modern usage |
| **yǐ** | By; using; by means of | More formal/written than general **用 (yòng)** | **以书面形式 (yǐ shūmiàn xíngshì)** | Sun Dejin's study, see references |
| **yǐ** | Take X as Y | Formal and written; common fixed phrases | **以……为…… (yǐ…wéi…)** | Established construction |
| **yǐ** | Classical grammatical uses | Classical Chinese, not everyday modern speech | See historical appendix | Literary sources |

**One-minute explanation.** **以 (yǐ)** does not have one English translation that works everywhere. It contributes different meanings in established words and constructions. For beginners, learning complete words is much more useful than translating the character in isolation.

## 2. Learn complete modern words first

| Priority | Word + pinyin | POS | Natural English meaning | Register | Lexical status / usage note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **可以 (kěyǐ)** | Modal verb | Can; may; be allowed to | Everyday | Lexicalized modal; not a productive combination of **可 (kě)** + **以 (yǐ)** |
| A | **所以 (suǒyǐ)** | Conjunction | So; therefore | Everyday | Established connective expressing a result or conclusion |
| A | **以后 (yǐhòu)** | Temporal expression | After; later; in the future | Everyday | Frequently after a time or event |
| A | **以前 (yǐqián)** | Temporal expression | Before; previously; in the past | Everyday | Frequently before a time or event |
| A | **以为 (yǐwéi)** | Verb | Think; assume | Everyday | Often suggests an assumption was mistaken, **but not always** |
| B | **以上 (yǐshàng)** | Localizer / range expression | Above; at least; or more | Neutral | Boundary inclusion depends on construction and context |
| B | **以下 (yǐxià)** | Localizer / range expression | Below; at most; or less | Neutral | Boundary interpretation depends on context |
| B | **以内 (yǐnèi)** | Range expression | Within; inside a limit | Neutral | Temporal, spatial, or quantitative limits |
| B | **以外 (yǐwài)** | Range expression | Outside; apart from; except | Neutral | Context determines exclusion or location |
| C | **以及 (yǐjí)** | Conjunction | As well as; and | Written/formal | Often connects parallel items or groups |
| C | **以便 (yǐbiàn)** | Conjunction | So that; in order to | Written/formal | Expresses intended convenience or purpose |
| C | **以免 (yǐmiǎn)** | Conjunction | So as to avoid; lest | Written/formal | Introduces an undesirable outcome to prevent |

**Vocabulary notes:** **以为 (yǐwéi)** is an established two-character verb. Do not infer its modern meaning by simply combining a standalone **以 (yǐ)** with **为 (wéi)**. The same warning applies to the historical internal structure of **可以 (kěyǐ)**.

## 3. Modern constructions

| Type | Pattern + pinyin | Function | Example + pinyin | Register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prepositional use | **以 + noun phrase (yǐ + noun phrase)** | Using or by a particular means | **以书面形式提交 (yǐ shūmiàn xíngshì tíjiā)** — Submit in writing | Formal/written |
| General construction | **以 X 为 Y (yǐ X wéi Y)** | Take or treat X as Y | **以他为榜样 (yǐ tā wéi bǎngyàng)** — Take him as a role model | Mainly written/formal |
| Established subpattern | **以 X 为主 (yǐ X wéi zhǔ)** | Make X the principal focus | **以学习为主 (yǐ xuéxí wéi zhǔ)** — Focus mainly on studying | Neutral-to-formal |
| Established subpattern | **以 X 为例 (yǐ X wéi lì)** | Use X as an example | **以中国为例 (yǐ Zhōngguó wéi lì)** — Take China as an example | Expository/formal |

**Structural note:** The variable **X** and **Y** slots represent constituents, not single characters only. The general **以 X 为 Y (yǐ X wéi Y)** construction has productive uses. The frequently repeated **以 X 为主 (yǐ X wéi zhǔ)** and **以 X 为例 (yǐ X wéi lì)** are recognized subpatterns. By contrast, the verb **以为 (yǐwéi)** is a single lexical item, not an instance of that construction.

## 4. Natural example sentences

> All examples in this section are **constructed teaching sentences**, not quotations from a corpus. They are intended to demonstrate natural contemporary usage.

### Example 1 — Permission with the modal verb

**我可以进去吗？(Wǒ kěyǐ jìnqù ma?)**

**English:** May I come in?  
**Context:** Asking permission to enter a room.  
**Register:** Spoken, everyday.  
**Teaching note:** Learn **可以 (kěyǐ)** as a complete modal verb.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example.

### Example 2 — Cause and result

**今天下雨，所以我不出去。(Jīntiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ bù chūqù.)**

**English:** It's raining today, so I'm not going out.  
**Context:** Explaining a decision.  
**Register:** Spoken, everyday.  
**Teaching note:** **所以 (suǒyǐ)** connects a cause to its result.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example.

### Example 3 — After a time

**我三点以后有时间。(Wǒ sān diǎn yǐhòu yǒu shíjiān.)**

**English:** I'm free after three o'clock.  
**Context:** Arranging a meeting.  
**Register:** Spoken, everyday.  
**Teaching note:** **以后 (yǐhòu)** can follow a time phrase.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example.

### Example 4 — Previously

**我以前住在北京。(Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng.)**

**English:** I used to live in Beijing.  
**Context:** Talking about past residence.  
**Register:** Spoken, everyday.  
**Teaching note:** **以前 (yǐqián)** indicates an earlier period.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example.

### Example 5 — An assumption that may prove wrong

**我以为你不来了。(Wǒ yǐwéi nǐ bù lái le.)**

**English:** I thought you weren't coming.  
**Context:** Addressing someone who unexpectedly appears.  
**Register:** Spoken, everyday.  
**Teaching note:** **以为 (yǐwéi)** often implies the assumption was wrong, but this is a **tendency, not a rule**.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example; semantic nuance discussed by Zhang Qiulin (1999).

### Example 6 — Formal use: written form

**请以书面形式提交申请。(Qǐng yǐ shūmiàn xíngshì tíjiā shēnqǐng.)**

**English:** Please submit your application in writing.  
**Context:** Institutional instructions or correspondence.  
**Register:** Formal/written.  
**Teaching note:** Standalone **以 (yǐ)** is natural here as part of a formal expression of manner or means.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example.

### Example 7 — Established subpattern

**这门课以口语练习为主。(Zhè mén kè yǐ kǒuyǔ liànxí wéi zhǔ.)**

**English:** This course focuses mainly on speaking practice.  
**Context:** A course description.  
**Register:** Neutral-to-formal.  
**Teaching note:** Recognize the complete **以 X 为主 (yǐ X wéi zhǔ)** pattern.  
**Evidence:** Constructed teaching example.

## 5. Similar words and important contrasts

| Item + pinyin | What it means | Where it fits naturally | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **用 (yòng)** | Use; with | **用筷子吃饭 (yòng kuàizi chīfàn)** — Eat with chopsticks | Often more natural with concrete instruments and in ordinary speech |
| **以 (yǐ)** | By; by means of | **以这种方式 (yǐ zhè zhǒng fāngshì)** — In this way | Instrumental use is relatively formal; especially frequent with abstract means |
| **以为 (yǐwéi)** | Think; assume | **我以为你不来了 (Wǒ yǐwéi nǐ bù lái le)** — I thought you weren't coming | Frequently suggests a contradicted expectation, not invariably |
| **认为 (rènwéi)** | Consider; hold the opinion that | **我认为这个办法不错 (Wǒ rènwéi zhège bànfǎ búcuò)** — I think this approach is good | Generally does not carry the same mistaken-assumption tendency; often more evaluative/formal |
| **觉得 (juéde)** | Feel; think | **我觉得很好 (Wǒ juéde hěn hǎo)** — I think it's good | Common for subjective impressions |

**Preference versus rule:** A corpus analyzed by Sun Dejin found that, among selected instrumental-preposition uses, **166/189 (about 88%)** of **以 (yǐ)**-governed nouns were abstract, versus **146/319 (about 46%)** for **用 (yòng)**. Concrete nouns accounted for **23** and **173** instances respectively. This supports a *collocational preference within that corpus*, not a ban on concrete nouns after **以 (yǐ)** or on abstract nouns after **用 (yòng)**. Do not treat these proportions as contemporary language-wide frequencies.

## 6. Common learner mistakes

| Potential mistake | Better interpretation or correction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Translating every **以 (yǐ)** as “using” | First identify the complete word or construction | **所以 (suǒyǐ)**, for example, means “so/therefore” |
| Treating **可以 (kěyǐ)** as a productive modern **可 (kě)** + **以 (yǐ)** rule | Learn the whole modal verb | Its internal historical analysis does not form a beginner grammar rule |
| Treating **以为 (yǐwéi)** as an unfilled version of **以 X 为 Y (yǐ X wéi Y)** | Keep lexicalized word and grammatical construction separate | They have different modern analyses |
| Assuming **以为 (yǐwéi)** *always* means “thought incorrectly” | Interpret the context; the implication is common but not universal | Linguistic analysis identifies both judgment and opposition-related uses |
| Assuming **以 (yǐ)** and **用 (yòng)** can be swapped anywhere | Check register and collocation | Similar English glosses do not imply full interchangeability |

## 8. Optional: historical / Classical Chinese

> Advanced historical note—not a model for ordinary modern conversation. The pinyin below is a **modern Mandarin reading** of Classical Chinese text.

| Historical function | Classical example + modern Mandarin pinyin | Interpretation | Source / confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marks the item transferred or handled; approximate overlap with certain modern **把 (bǎ)** constructions | **秦亦不以城予赵。(Qín yì bù yǐ chéng yǔ Zhào.)** | “Qin likewise did not give the cities to Zhao.” Here **以城 (yǐ chéng)** specifies what was to be given; the analogy to **把 (bǎ)** is limited to this sort of construction. | Classical text in *Records of the Grand Historian*, see source below |

## 9. Practice

**Comprehension:** In **我以前住在北京 (Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng)**, does the speaker live in Beijing now or in an earlier period?

**Choose the natural expression:** To say “eat with chopsticks” in casual speech, choose **用筷子吃饭 (yòng kuàizi chīfàn)** or an invented word-for-word replacement with **以 (yǐ)**.

**Production:** Translate “May I come in?” using **可以 (kěyǐ)**.

<details>
<summary>Suggested answers</summary>

2. **用筷子吃饭 (yòng kuàizi chīfàn)** is the natural conversational choice.
3. **我可以进去吗？(Wǒ kěyǐ jìnqù ma?)**
