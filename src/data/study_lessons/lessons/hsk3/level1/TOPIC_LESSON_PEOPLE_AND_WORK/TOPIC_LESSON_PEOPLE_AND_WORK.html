  • Introduce people by name or title.

Overview

Describe who someone is and talk about workplaces and daily work.

Introduce people by name or title.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 130
名字 (míngzi)

name

noun · introduced

你的名字是什么？ (nǐ de míngzi shì shénme?)

What is your name?

你的名字是什么 is a direct question about someone’s name; 叫什么名字 is another common wording.

请在这里写名字。 (qǐng zài zhèlǐ xiě míngzi.)

Please write your name here.

HSK entry 142
男 (nán)

male

adjective · introduced

那个男学生是我的朋友。 (nàge nán xuéshēng shì wǒ de péngyou.)

That male student is my friend.

男 is an adjective specifying a male student; more formal contexts may use 男性.

这个班有十个男学生。 (zhège bān yǒu shí gè nán xuéshēng.)

There are ten male students in this class.

HSK entry 152
女 (nǚ)

female

adjective · introduced

那个女学生在看书。 (nàge nǚ xuéshēng zài kàn shū.)

That female student is reading a book.

女 modifies 学生 to specify a female student.

这个班有八个女学生。 (zhège bān yǒu bā gè nǚ xuéshēng.)

There are eight female students in this class.

HSK entry 155
女士 (nǚshì)

Ms.; lady

noun · introduced

这位女士是老师。 (zhè wèi nǚshì shì lǎoshī.)

This lady is a teacher.

女士 is a respectful label for an adult woman, more formal than 女人.

那位女士会说汉语。 (nà wèi nǚshì huì shuō Hànyǔ.)

That lady can speak Chinese.

HSK entry 170
人 (rén)

person

noun · introduced

他是一个好人。 (tā shì yí ge hǎo rén.)

He is a good person.

人 means a person; 一个好人 is one good person, with 个 as the classifier.

这家饭店里有很多人。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn lǐ yǒu hěn duō rén.)

There are many people in this restaurant.

HSK entry 231
先生 (xiānsheng)

Mr.; sir

noun · introduced

这位先生是医生。 (zhè wèi xiānsheng shì yīshēng.)

This gentleman is a doctor.

那位先生在学校工作。 (nà wèi xiānsheng zài xuéxiào gōngzuò.)

That gentleman works at the school.

HSK entry 62
公司 (gōngsī)

company

noun · introduced

她在一家公司上班。 (tā zài yì jiā gōngsī shàngbān.)

She works for a company.

家 classifies companies; 一 is pronounced yì before first-tone 家.

公司里有很多人。 (gōngsī lǐ yǒu hěn duō rén.)

There are many people at the company.

HSK entry 63
工作 (gōngzuò)

work; job

verb · introduced

我今天要工作。 (wǒ jīntiān yào gōngzuò.)

I have to work today.

要 can express necessity or a planned action; context determines how strong “have to” is.

妈妈在医院工作。 (māma zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.)

Mom works at a hospital.

Can be the verb work or the noun work/job.

HSK entry 176
上班 (shàngbān)

go to work

verb · introduced

我爸爸每天八点上班。 (wǒ bàba měitiān bā diǎn shàngbān.)

My dad goes to work at eight every day.

上班 means go to work or start work, not simply enter a building.

我的哥哥早上八点上班。 (wǒ de gēge zǎoshang bā diǎn shàngbān.)

My older brother goes to work at eight in the morning.

HSK entry 228
下班 (xiàbān)

finish work

verb · introduced

我爸爸六点下班。 (wǒ bàba liù diǎn xiàbān.)

My father finishes work at six.

下班 means finish the workday or leave work, here at six.

下班后，我们去饭店吃饭。 (xiàbān hòu, wǒmen qù fàndiàn chī fàn.)

After work, we go to a restaurant to eat.

Usage and grammar

Talking about work

Use the common work verb or noun with a person or workplace.

我今天要工作。 (wǒ jīntiān yào gōngzuò.)

I have to work today.

要 can express necessity or a planned action; context determines how strong “have to” is.

Names and identity

Ask for or give a name using a familiar introductory expression.

你的名字是什么？ (nǐ de míngzi shì shénme?)

What is your name?

你的名字是什么 is a direct question about someone’s name; 叫什么名字 is another common wording.
