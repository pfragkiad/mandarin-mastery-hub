1 · Conversation — What I Can and Want to Do

Two classmates are planning what to do after class. Notice how ability and desire lead to different answers.

A

你会唱歌吗？ (Nǐ huì chàng gē ma?)

Can you sing?

B

我会唱，但是我今天不想唱。 (Wǒ huì chàng, dànshì wǒ jīntiān bù xiǎng chàng.)

I can sing, but I don't want to today.

A

那你想看电影吗？ (Nà nǐ xiǎng kàn diànyǐng ma?)

Then would you like to watch a movie?

B

想！我们一起去吧。 (Xiǎng! Wǒmen yìqǐ qù ba.)

Yes! Let's go together.

2 · How the language works

Ability: 会 (huì) versus 能 (néng)

会 (huì) often refers to a learned skill; 能 (néng) refers to capability or what circumstances permit. They can overlap in some situations.

Subject + 会/能 + verb

我也会说一点儿汉语。 (Wǒ yě huì shuō yìdiǎnr Hànyǔ.)

I can also speak a little Chinese.

我今天能去。 (Wǒ jīntiān néng qù.)

I can go today (circumstances allow).

Desire: 想 (xiǎng) versus 要 (yào)

想 (xiǎng) generally makes a wish softer; 要 (yào) can express a firmer desire, intention or need. Neither is an automatic substitute for 会 (huì).

Subject + 想/要 + verb/noun

我想喝茶。 (Wǒ xiǎng hē chá.)

I would like some tea.

我要看这本书。 (Wǒ yào kàn zhè běn shū.)

I want to read this book.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

小明会唱歌，也喜欢看电影。今天他很忙，不能去电影院。明天他想和朋友一起去。 (Xiǎomíng huì chàng gē, yě xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng. Jīntiān tā hěn máng, bù néng qù diànyǐngyuàn. Míngtiān tā xiǎng hé péngyou yìqǐ qù.)

Xiaoming can sing and also likes movies. He is busy today and cannot go to the cinema. Tomorrow he wants to go with a friend.

他今天不去电影院。 (Tā jīntiān bú qù diànyǐngyuàn.)

He is not going to the cinema today.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Ask a friend which activity they would like to do today.
Show one possible response

你今天想做什么？ (Nǐ jīntiān xiǎng zuò shénme?)

What would you like to do today?

2. Explain a learned skill and one thing you cannot do today.
Show one possible response

I can sing, but I cannot go today.

6 · Watch out

Do not assume 会 (huì), 能 (néng) and 想 (xiǎng) all mean “can”. Choose based on learned ability, circumstances, or desire.

Pronunciation clinic

Practise short replies: 会 (huì), 不会 (bú huì), 想 (xiǎng), 不想 (bù xiǎng).
