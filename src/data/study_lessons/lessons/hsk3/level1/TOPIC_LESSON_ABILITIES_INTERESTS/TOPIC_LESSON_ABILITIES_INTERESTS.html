What I Can and Want to Do

Abilities, Preferences and Leisure

daily-actions · leisure-media · #ability · #desire · #preferences · #entertainment

Learning objectives

  • Express ability and intention.
  • Talk about preferences and leisure activities.
  • Recognize movies, singing and entertainment terms.

Overview

Express what you can do, what you want and what you enjoy doing in free time.

Express ability and intention.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 82
会 (huì)

can; know how

modal-verb · introduced

我会说汉语。 (wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ.)

I can speak Chinese.

会 refers to an acquired skill, such as speaking a language.

我妹妹会唱歌。 (wǒ mèimei huì chàng gē.)

My younger sister can sing.

Modal use for learned ability; the full word has other uses.

HSK entry 96
觉得 (juéde)

feel; think

verb · introduced

我觉得这个很好。 (wǒ juéde zhège hěn hǎo.)

I think this is good.

觉得 introduces a personal opinion or impression.

学生觉得这本书很好。 (xuéshēng juéde zhè běn shū hěn hǎo.)

The students think this book is good.

HSK entry 102
可以 (kěyǐ)

may; can

modal-verb · introduced

我可以坐这儿吗？ (wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma?)

May I sit here?

可以…吗 requests permission. 坐这儿 is natural colloquial Mandarin; 坐在这里 is an alternative.

这里可以看书，也可以休息。 (zhèlǐ kěyǐ kàn shū, yě kěyǐ xiūxi.)

You can read books and rest here.

Modal/verb expressing permission or possibility; some lexical sources also label adjectival use.

HSK entry 145
能 (néng)

be able to

modal-verb · introduced

我能进来吗？ (wǒ néng jìnlái ma?)

May I come in?

能…吗 can ask whether something is possible or permitted, here entering a room.

今天下午你能来吗？ (jīntiān xiàwǔ nǐ néng lái ma?)

Can you come this afternoon?

HSK entry 225
喜欢 (xǐhuan)

like

verb · introduced

我喜欢这里。 (wǒ xǐhuan zhèlǐ.)

I like it here.

很多孩子喜欢看电影。 (hěn duō háizi xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.)

Many children like watching movies.

HSK entry 233
想 (xiǎng)

think; want

modal-verb · introduced

我想学汉语。 (wǒ xiǎng xué Hànyǔ.)

I want to learn Chinese.

想 indicates desire or intention; 学汉语 means learn Chinese.

我妈妈想买一件新衣服。 (wǒ māma xiǎng mǎi yí jiàn xīn yīfu.)

My mom wants to buy a new item of clothing.

Modal sense want/would like, also lexical verb think/miss.

HSK entry 252
要 (yào)

want; will

modal-verb · introduced

我要喝水。 (wǒ yào hē shuǐ.)

I want to drink water.

上课的时候要听老师说话。 (shàngkè de shíhou yào tīng lǎoshī shuōhuà.)

You must listen to the teacher during class.

Modal of intention/need, also a lexical verb meaning want.

HSK entry 263
有 (yǒu)

have

verb · introduced

我有两本书。 (wǒ yǒu liǎng běn shū.)

I have two books.

有 expresses possession; 两 is used before the classifier 本.

学校里有很多学生。 (xuéxiào lǐ yǒu hěn duō xuéshēng.)

There are many students at the school.

HSK entry 18
唱 (chàng)

sing

verb · introduced

她会唱中文歌。 (tā huì chàng Zhōngwén gē.)

She can sing songs in Chinese.

会 marks learned ability; 中文歌 means songs sung in Chinese.

学生们在唱歌。 (xuéshēngmen zài chàng gē.)

The students are singing.

HSK entry 37
电视 (diànshì)

TV

noun · introduced

晚上我们看电视。 (wǎnshang wǒmen kàn diànshì.)

We watch TV in the evening.

看电视 is the standard expression for watching television.

这个房间里有电视，也有电脑。 (zhège fángjiān lǐ yǒu diànshì, yě yǒu diànnǎo.)

There is a television and a computer in this room.

Location phrase 房间里 makes the description of equipment in a room explicit.

HSK entry 38
电影 (diànyǐng)

film

noun · introduced

今天晚上看电影吗？ (jīntiān wǎnshang kàn diànyǐng ma?)

Shall we watch a movie tonight?

电影院今天有新电影。 (diànyǐngyuàn jīntiān yǒu xīn diànyǐng.)

There is a new movie at the cinema today.

Natural cinema listing; 新电影 means a new movie, with no advanced grammar.

HSK entry 39
电影院 (diànyǐngyuàn)

cinema

noun · introduced

电影院在学校旁边。 (diànyǐngyuàn zài xuéxiào pángbiān.)

The cinema is next to the school.

旁边 means “beside” and functions as a location word after 学校.

电影院晚上八点有电影。 (diànyǐngyuàn wǎnshang bā diǎn yǒu diànyǐng.)

The cinema has a movie at eight tonight.

HSK entry 58
歌 (gē)

song

noun · introduced

我喜欢唱歌。 (wǒ xǐhuan chàng gē.)

I like singing.

学生们喜欢唱中文歌。 (xuéshēngmen xǐhuan chàng Zhōngwén gē.)

The students like singing Chinese songs.

HSK entry 75
好玩儿 (hǎowánr)

fun

adjective · introduced

这里真好玩儿！ (zhèlǐ zhēn hǎowánr!)

It's really fun here!

这里有很多好玩儿的东西。 (zhèlǐ yǒu hěn duō hǎowánr de dōngxi.)

There are many fun things here.

Usage and grammar

Learned ability

A modal verb can indicate ability acquired through learning.

我会说汉语。 (wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ.)

I can speak Chinese.

会 refers to an acquired skill, such as speaking a language.

Expressing likes

A preference verb can take a noun or a verb phrase.

我喜欢这里。 (wǒ xǐhuan zhèlǐ.)

I like it here.
