Introducing Family and Friends

Relationships, Relatives and Friendship

family-relationships · people-identity · #family · #relationships

Learning objectives

  • Identify common relatives and relationship words.
  • Introduce friends and family members.
  • Understand short descriptions of family life.

Overview

Introduce family members, friends and people close to you.

Identify common relatives and relationship words.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 1
爱 (ài)

love; like

verb · introduced

我爱我的家。 (wǒ ài wǒ de jiā.)

I love my family.

爱 expresses strong affection here; 家 can refer both to the home and to one’s family.

爸爸爱看中文电影。 (bàba ài kàn Zhōngwén diànyǐng.)

Dad loves watching Chinese-language movies.

HSK entry 3
爸爸 (bàba)

father

noun · introduced

我爸爸是老师。 (wǒ bàba shì lǎoshī.)

My father is a teacher.

爸爸 is a familiar kinship noun, and the possessive 的 is often omitted before close family terms.

爸爸在家做饭。 (bàba zài jiā zuò fàn.)

Dad is cooking at home.

HSK entry 32
弟弟 (dìdi)

younger brother

noun · introduced

我弟弟十岁。 (wǒ dìdi shí suì.)

My younger brother is ten.

Age is commonly stated as person + number + 岁, without 是.

弟弟和姐姐一起上学。 (dìdi hé jiějie yìqǐ shàngxué.)

The younger brother and older sister go to school together.

HSK entry 48
儿子 (érzi)

son

noun · introduced

我儿子喜欢猫。 (wǒ érzi xǐhuan māo.)

My son likes cats.

儿子 specifies one’s son; 喜欢 is followed by the thing liked.

他的儿子今年上小学。 (tā de érzi jīnnián shàng xiǎoxué.)

His son is attending primary school this year.

HSK entry 59
哥哥 (gēge)

older brother

noun · introduced

我哥哥在工作。 (wǒ gēge zài gōngzuò.)

My older brother is working.

在 before a verb marks ongoing activity; the brother is working now.

哥哥每天坐火车上班。 (gēge měitiān zuò huǒchē shàngbān.)

The older brother takes the train to work every day.

HSK entry 68
孩子 (háizi)

child

noun · introduced

那个孩子五岁。 (nàge háizi wǔ suì.)

That child is five years old.

五岁 gives the child’s age; Chinese does not need a verb equivalent to “be.”

孩子们在学校学习。 (háizimen zài xuéxiào xuéxí.)

The children study at school.

HSK entry 86
家 (jiā)

home; family

noun · introduced

我现在回家。 (wǒ xiànzài huí jiā.)

I am going home now.

回家 means go back home, with no need for 在 before 家.

我家有四个人。 (wǒ jiā yǒu sì gè rén.)

There are four people in my family.

Home/family noun; also a classifier for businesses/families.

HSK entry 87
家人 (jiārén)

family members

noun · introduced

我的家人都很好。 (wǒ de jiārén dōu hěn hǎo.)

My family members are all well.

家人 refers to family members; 都 confirms that the whole group is doing well.

我的家人都在中国。 (wǒ de jiārén dōu zài Zhōngguó.)

All my family members are in China.

HSK entry 92
姐姐 (jiějie)

older sister

noun · introduced

我姐姐会开车。 (wǒ jiějie huì kāichē.)

My older sister can drive.

会开车 indicates the learned ability to drive.

我的姐姐今年二十岁。 (wǒ de jiějie jīnnián èr shí suì.)

My older sister is twenty years old this year.

HSK entry 114
妈妈 (māma)

mother

noun · introduced

我妈妈是医生。 (wǒ māma shì yīshēng.)

My mother is a doctor.

妈妈 can be used directly before a description without a possessive 的.

妈妈每天晚上和我一起读书。 (māma měitiān wǎnshang hé wǒ yìqǐ dúshū.)

Mom reads with me every evening.

HSK entry 123
妹妹 (mèimei)

younger sister

noun · introduced

我妹妹是学生。 (wǒ mèimei shì xuéshēng.)

My younger sister is a student.

妹妹 refers to one’s younger sister and is a familiar family term.

妹妹每天和姐姐一起上学。 (mèimei měitiān hé jiějie yìqǐ shàngxué.)

The younger sister goes to school with her older sister every day.

HSK entry 143
男朋友 (nánpéngyou)

boyfriend

noun · introduced

她的男朋友是医生。 (tā de nánpéngyou shì yīshēng.)

Her boyfriend is a doctor.

男朋友 ordinarily means boyfriend, not merely a male friend.

她的男朋友在医院上班。 (tā de nánpéngyou zài yīyuàn shàngbān.)

Her boyfriend works at a hospital.

HSK entry 153
女儿 (nǚ'ér)

daughter

noun · introduced

我的女儿很喜欢书。 (wǒ de nǚ'ér hěn xǐhuan shū.)

My daughter likes books very much.

女儿 means daughter; 很喜欢 increases the strength of the preference.

她的女儿今年五岁。 (tā de nǚ'ér jīnnián wǔ suì.)

Her daughter is five years old this year.

HSK entry 154
女朋友 (nǚpéngyou)

girlfriend

noun · introduced

他的女朋友会说中文。 (tā de nǚpéngyou huì shuō Zhōngwén.)

His girlfriend can speak Chinese.

女朋友 normally identifies a romantic partner.

他的女朋友也在这家公司工作。 (tā de nǚpéngyou yě zài zhè jiā gōngsī gōngzuò.)

His girlfriend also works at this company.

HSK entry 156
朋友 (péngyou)

friend

noun · introduced

他是我的好朋友。 (tā shì wǒ de hǎo péngyou.)

He is my good friend.

好朋友 means a good or close friend, not a romantic partner.

我的朋友住在学校旁边。 (wǒ de péngyou zhù zài xuéxiào pángbiān.)

My friend lives next to the school.

HSK entry 235
小朋友 (xiǎopéngyou)

child; little friend

noun · introduced

这些小朋友在玩。 (zhèxiē xiǎopéngyou zài wán.)

These children are playing.

小朋友 is a friendly way to refer to young children.

小朋友们每天上午在学校学习。 (xiǎopéngyoumen měitiān shàngwǔ zài xuéxiào xuéxí.)

The children study at school every morning.

Usage and grammar

Family terms as nouns

A kinship term can follow a possessive pronoun directly.

我爸爸是老师。 (wǒ bàba shì lǎoshī.)

My father is a teacher.

爸爸 is a familiar kinship noun, and the possessive 的 is often omitted before close family terms.

Home or family

The same noun can refer to a home or a family, depending on context.

我现在回家。 (wǒ xiànzài huí jiā.)

I am going home now.

回家 means go back home, with no need for 在 before 家.
