Negation, Questions and Sentence Particles

1 · Conversation — Making Basic Statements

Listen to how small particles change the *function* of a sentence, not its main vocabulary.

A

你是老师吗？ (Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?)

Are you a teacher?

B

不是，我是学生。 (Bú shì, wǒ shì xuéshēng.)

No, I am a student.

A

你有电脑吗？ (Nǐ yǒu diànnǎo ma?)

Do you have a computer?

B

没有。你的呢？ (Méiyǒu. Nǐ de ne?)

No. What about yours?

2 · How the language works

不 (bù) and 没有 (méiyǒu)

不 (bù) typically negates identity, habitual actions, wants or future choices. 没有 (méiyǒu) negates possession and is used in common past-event negation.

不 + 形容词/动词; 没有 + 名词
bù + xíngróngcí/dòngcí; méiyǒu + míngcí
不 + <adjective>/<verb>; 没有 + <noun>

我晚上不喝茶。 (Wǒ wǎnshang bù hē chá.)

I do not drink tea in the evening.

我妹妹没有手机。 (Wǒ mèimei méiyǒu shǒujī.)

My younger sister does not have a mobile phone.

Particles for questions and responses

吗 (ma) turns a statement into a yes/no question; 呢 (ne) often returns the topic to the listener; 吧 (ba) can soften a suggestion.

陈述句 + 吗？; 话题 + 呢？; 建议 + 吧
chénshùjù + ma?; huàtí + ne?; jiànyì + ba
<statement> + 吗？; <topic> + 呢？; <suggestion> + 吧

她是学生吗？ (Tā shì xuéshēng ma?)

Is she a student?

我们现在走吧。 (Wǒmen xiànzài zǒu ba.)

Let’s go now.

3 · Build new sentences

Make the affirmative statements negative with the appropriate word.

Worked model

我是老师。→ 我不是老师。 (Wǒ shì lǎoshī. → Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.)

I am a teacher. → I am not a teacher.

Change 1: possession
Reveal a model response

我有电脑。→ 我没有电脑。 (Wǒ yǒu diànnǎo. → Wǒ méiyǒu diànnǎo.)

I have a computer. → I do not have a computer.

Change 2: habit
Reveal a model response

我喝茶。→ 我不喝茶。 (Wǒ hē chá. → Wǒ bù hē chá.)

I drink tea. → I do not drink tea.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

小王不是老师，他是学生。他没有车，但是他的朋友有车。今天他不去学校，在家学习。 (Xiǎo Wáng bú shì lǎoshī, tā shì xuéshēng. Tā méiyǒu chē, dànshì tā de péngyou yǒu chē. Jīntiān tā bú qù xuéxiào, zài jiā xuéxí.)

Xiao Wang is not a teacher; he is a student. He does not have a car, but his friend does. Today he is not going to school; he is studying at home.

1. Is Xiao Wang a teacher?
Show the answer

No, he is a student.

2. Where is he studying today?
Show the answer

At home.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Ask whether your partner is a student, then answer.
Show one possible response

你是学生吗？我是。 (Nǐ shì xuéshēng ma? Wǒ shì.)

Are you a student? Yes, I am.

2. Ask someone about their computer; respond negatively.
Show one possible response

你有电脑吗？没有。 (Nǐ yǒu diànnǎo ma? Méiyǒu.)

Do you have a computer? No.

6 · Watch out

Do not use 不有 to mean “do not have.” Use 没有 (méiyǒu) for lack of possession.

Pronunciation clinic

Before fourth tone, 不 (bù) changes to bú: 不是 (bú shì).
