Overview

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 4
吧 (ba)

suggestion particle

particle · introduced

我们走吧。 (wǒmen zǒu ba.)

Let's go.

Sentence-final 吧 softens a proposal into a friendly suggestion to go together.

“明天再来吧。”老师说。 (“míngtiān zài lái ba.” lǎoshī shuō.)

"Come back tomorrow," the teacher said.

Written dialogue: 吧 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Sentence-final suggestion or softening particle.

HSK entry 13
不 (bù)

not

adverb · introduced

我不喝茶。 (wǒ bù hē chá.)

I do not drink tea.

不 negates a general action or preference; there is no aspect marker for a habitual statement.

这里不卖茶。 (zhèlǐ bú mài chá.)

Tea is not sold here.

HSK entry 15
不要 (búyào)

do not want; don't

adverb · introduced

我不要这个。 (wǒ bú yào zhège.)

I don't want this one.

不要 directly rejects an offered object; in some contexts it can sound firm.

请不要在这里吃东西。 (qǐng búyào zài zhèlǐ chī dōngxi.)

Please don't eat here.

Negative request/prohibition: 不 + 要; an expression rather than a single lexical category.

HSK entry 30
的 (de)

possessive particle

particle · introduced

这是我的书。 (zhè shì wǒ de shū.)

This is my book.

的 links the possessor 我 to the noun 书.

爸爸的车在那边。 (bàba de chē zài nàbiān.)

Dad's car is over there.

Structural particle marking modification/possession and other constructions.

HSK entry 108
了 (le)

completion particle

particle · introduced

我吃了一个苹果。 (wǒ chī le yí ge píngguǒ.)

I ate an apple.

了 after 吃 marks a completed event, not a past-tense conjugation.

我已经吃了早饭。 (wǒ yǐjīng chī le zǎofàn.)

I have already eaten breakfast.

Aspect particle after verbs and sentence-final particle are related but different uses.

HSK entry 115
吗 (ma)

yes/no particle

particle · introduced

你喜欢茶吗？ (nǐ xǐhuan chá ma?)

Do you like tea?

Sentence-final 吗 turns a statement into a yes–no question.

今天上课吗？ (jīntiān shàngkè ma?)

Do we have class today?

Yes–no question particle.

HSK entry 122
没有 (méiyǒu)

do not have

verb · introduced

我没有手机。 (wǒ méiyǒu shǒujī.)

I don't have a mobile phone.

没有 expresses non-possession; it is not combined with 不.

今天下午没有课。 (jīntiān xiàwǔ méiyǒu kè.)

There is no class this afternoon.

Verb expressing absence or non-possession (我没有手机); negative adverbial 没(有) before a verb expresses nonoccurrence (我没有去). Both roles are kept, but the current examples illustrate non-possession/absence.

HSK entry 124
们 (men)

plural suffix

suffix · introduced

他们都是老师。 (tāmen dōu shì lǎoshī.)

They are all teachers.

们 marks a plural group of people in pronouns such as 他们, but is not freely added to every noun.

老师们正在上课。 (lǎoshīmen zhèngzài shàngkè.)

The teachers are having class.

Plural suffix for personal pronouns and people nouns.

HSK entry 144
呢 (ne)

question particle

particle · introduced

你呢？ (nǐ ne?)

And you?

你呢? is a compact way to return a question to the other person.

“我会说汉语，你呢？”她问。 (“wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ, nǐ ne?” tā wèn.)

"I can speak Chinese. What about you?" she asked.

Written dialogue: 呢 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Sentence-final particle for follow-up, topic continuation, or ongoing action.

HSK entry 188
是 (shì)

be

verb · introduced

我是学生。 (wǒ shì xuéshēng.)

I am a student.

是 links subject and noun predicate; no article is required before 学生.

这里是学校。 (zhèlǐ shì xuéxiào.)

This is the school.

Copular verb, not a general-purpose adjective.

Usage and grammar

Negating an ordinary action

Use the negative adverb before a verb to negate a habitual action.

我不喝茶。 (wǒ bù hē chá.)

I do not drink tea.

不 negates a general action or preference; there is no aspect marker for a habitual statement.

Yes/no questions

A sentence-final question particle changes a statement into a basic yes/no question.

你喜欢茶吗？ (nǐ xǐhuan chá ma?)

Do you like tea?

Sentence-final 吗 turns a statement into a yes–no question.
