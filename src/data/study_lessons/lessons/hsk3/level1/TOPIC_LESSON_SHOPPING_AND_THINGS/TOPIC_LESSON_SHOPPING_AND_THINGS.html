1 · Conversation — Buying Everyday Things

A shopper compares prices and decides what to buy for a room.

A

这个杯子多少钱？ (Zhège bēizi duōshao qián?)

How much is this cup?

B

二十块。 (Èr shí kuài.)

Twenty yuan.

A

有便宜一点儿的吗？ (Yǒu piányi yìdiǎnr de ma?)

Do you have a slightly cheaper one?

B

有，那个十五块。 (Yǒu, nàge shíwǔ kuài.)

Yes, that one is fifteen yuan.

2 · How the language works

Prices: 元 (yuán) and 块 (kuài)

元 (yuán) names the currency unit; 块 (kuài) is a common colloquial word for yuan. Use 多少钱 (duōshao qián) to ask price.

item + 多少钱? / number + 块

这件衣服五十元。 (Zhè jiàn yīfu wǔ shí yuán.)

This item of clothing is fifty yuan.

那张桌子一百块。 (Nà zhāng zhuōzi yì bǎi kuài.)

That table is one hundred yuan.

Buying versus selling

买 (mǎi) means “buy” and 卖 (mài) means “sell”. Their tone difference changes the meaning. 贵 (guì) and 便宜 (piányi) describe price.

shop + 卖 + item; customer + 买 + item

这家商店卖手机。 (Zhè jiā shāngdiàn mài shǒujī.)

This shop sells mobile phones.

我想买新电脑。 (Wǒ xiǎng mǎi xīn diànnǎo.)

I want to buy a new computer.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

妈妈去商店买一把椅子和一张桌子。桌子有点儿贵，但是椅子很便宜。 (Māma qù shāngdiàn mǎi yì bǎ yǐzi hé yì zhāng zhuōzi. Zhuōzi yǒudiǎnr guì, dànshì yǐzi hěn piányi.)

Mom goes to the shop to buy a chair and a table. The table is a little expensive, but the chair is cheap.

1. What two things does Mom want to buy?
Show the answer

A chair and a table.

2. Which object is cheaper?
Show the answer

The chair.

6 · Watch out

买 (mǎi, buy) and 卖 (mài, sell) look and sound similar but differ in tone and meaning.

Pronunciation clinic

Remember the price words 元 (yuán) and 块 (kuài) can both refer to yuan, with 块 being informal.
