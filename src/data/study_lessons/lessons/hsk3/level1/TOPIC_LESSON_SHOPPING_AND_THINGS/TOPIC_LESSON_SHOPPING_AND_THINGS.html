Buying Everyday Things

Prices, Shops and Household Objects

Learning objectives

  • Ask and answer basic price questions.
  • Identify common shops, furniture and devices.
  • Talk about buying and selling everyday items.

Overview

Buy and describe common things, ask prices and talk about personal possessions.

Ask and answer basic price questions.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 19
超市 (chāoshì)

supermarket

noun · introduced

我去超市买水果。 (wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi shuǐguǒ.)

I go to the supermarket to buy fruit.

去 + place + verb expresses going somewhere in order to do something.

超市早上八点开门。 (chāoshì zǎoshang bā diǎn kāimén.)

The supermarket opens at eight in the morning.

HSK entry 34
店 (diàn)

shop

noun · introduced

那家店很小。 (nà jiā diàn hěn xiǎo.)

That shop is small.

家 can classify a shop or commercial establishment; 很小 describes its size.

这家店早上九点开门。 (zhè jiā diàn zǎoshang jiǔ diǎn kāimén.)

This shop opens at nine in the morning.

HSK entry 65
贵 (guì)

expensive

adjective · introduced

这个手机太贵了。 (zhège shǒujī tài guì le.)

This phone is too expensive.

太…了 often indicates an excessive degree, here a phone being too expensive.

这本中文书不贵。 (zhè běn Zhōngwén shū bú guì.)

This Chinese book is not expensive.

HSK entry 105
块 (kuài)

piece; yuan

classifier · introduced

这个苹果五块钱。 (zhège píngguǒ wǔ kuài qián.)

This apple costs five yuan.

块钱 is a common spoken expression for an amount in yuan.

这本书二十块钱。 (zhè běn shū èr shí kuài qián.)

This book costs twenty yuan.

Classifier and colloquial monetary unit; also used for pieces.

HSK entry 116
买 (mǎi)

buy

verb · introduced

我想买一点儿茶。 (wǒ xiǎng mǎi yìdiǎnr chá.)

I want to buy some tea.

学生可以在这里买书。 (xuéshēng kěyǐ zài zhèlǐ mǎi shū.)

Students can buy books here.

HSK entry 117
卖 (mài)

sell

verb · introduced

这家店卖水果。 (zhè jiā diàn mài shuǐguǒ.)

This shop sells fruit.

卖 is “sell,” in contrast to 买 “buy.”

学校外边的商店也卖书。 (xuéxiào wàibian de shāngdiàn yě mài shū.)

The shop outside the school also sells books.

HSK entry 157
便宜 (piányi)

cheap

adjective · introduced

这个苹果很便宜。 (zhège píngguǒ hěn piányi.)

This apple is cheap.

便宜 describes a low price; it is not the same as bad quality.

这家店的衣服很便宜。 (zhè jiā diàn de yīfu hěn piányi.)

The clothes at this shop are very cheap.

HSK entry 164
钱 (qián)

money

noun · introduced

我没有钱。 (wǒ méiyǒu qián.)

I don't have money.

没有钱 expresses the absence of money; 不有 is not grammatical here.

桌子上有二十块钱。 (zhuōzi shàng yǒu èr shí kuài qián.)

There are twenty yuan on the table.

Straightforward statement of where an amount of money is located.

HSK entry 174
商店 (shāngdiàn)

store

noun · introduced

我们去商店买水。 (wǒmen qù shāngdiàn mǎi shuǐ.)

We go to the store to buy water.

去商店买水 expresses purpose: go to the shop to buy water.

商店上午九点开门。 (shāngdiàn shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn kāimén.)

The shop opens at nine in the morning.

HSK entry 268
元 (yuán)

yuan (currency)

classifier · introduced

这个杯子十元。 (zhège bēizi shí yuán.)

This cup costs ten yuan.

元 is the currency unit yuan; price follows the item directly.

这本书二十元。 (zhè běn shū èr shí yuán.)

This book costs twenty yuan.

Currency unit yuan, functioning as a measure word.

HSK entry 9
杯子 (bēizi)

cup

noun · introduced

这个杯子是我的。 (zhège bēizi shì wǒ de.)

This cup is mine.

The final 我的 stands for “my cup,” with the repeated noun left understood.

桌子上有三个杯子。 (zhuōzi shàng yǒu sān gè bēizi.)

There are three cups on the table.

HSK entry 35
电话 (diànhuà)

telephone

noun · introduced

请给我打电话。 (qǐng gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà.)

Please give me a call.

这是学校的电话。 (zhè shì xuéxiào de diànhuà.)

This is the school's phone number.

HSK entry 36
电脑 (diànnǎo)

computer

noun · introduced

我在电脑上学习。 (wǒ zài diànnǎo shàng xuéxí.)

I study on the computer.

学校里有很多电脑。 (xuéxiào lǐ yǒu hěn duō diànnǎo.)

There are many computers at school.

HSK entry 40
东西 (dōngxi)

thing

noun · introduced

这些东西是谁的？ (zhèxiē dōngxi shì shéi de?)

Whose things are these?

谁 asks about the owner; 谁 can be pronounced shéi or shuí, here read shéi.

请拿好你的东西。 (qǐng ná hǎo nǐ de dōngxi.)

Please take care of your belongings.

HSK entry 52
房间 (fángjiān)

room

noun · introduced

我的房间很小。 (wǒ de fángjiān hěn xiǎo.)

My room is small.

房间 is a room; 很小 describes its physical size.

学校里有三个大房间。 (xuéxiào lǐ yǒu sān gè dà fángjiān.)

There are three large rooms at the school.

HSK entry 189
手机 (shǒujī)

mobile phone

noun · introduced

这是我的手机。 (zhè shì wǒ de shǒujī.)

This is my mobile phone.

手机 is a mobile phone; 这 points to the device being identified.

上课的时候请不要玩手机。 (shàngkè de shíhou qǐng búyào wán shǒujī.)

Please don't play with your phone during class.

HSK entry 255
衣服 (yīfu)

clothes

noun · introduced

我今天买了新衣服。 (wǒ jīntiān mǎi le xīn yīfu.)

I bought new clothes today.

衣服 refers broadly to clothes; it commonly takes no classifier in this statement.

这家店卖很多新衣服。 (zhè jiā diàn mài hěn duō xīn yīfu.)

This shop sells many new clothes.

HSK entry 260
椅子 (yǐzi)

chair

noun · introduced

这儿有椅子，请坐。 (zhèr yǒu yǐzi, qǐng zuò.)

There are chairs here. Please sit down.

椅子在桌子旁边。 (yǐzi zài zhuōzi pángbiān.)

The chair is next to the table.

HSK entry 295
桌子 (zhuōzi)

table

noun · introduced

桌子上有一本书。 (zhuōzi shàng yǒu yì běn shū.)

There is a book on the table.

桌子上 means on the table; 有 introduces something located there.

教室里有很多桌子。 (jiàoshì lǐ yǒu hěn duō zhuōzi.)

There are many desks in the classroom.

Usage and grammar

Talking about money

Use money vocabulary when asking about or stating a price.

我没有钱。 (wǒ méiyǒu qián.)

I don't have money.

没有钱 expresses the absence of money; 不有 is not grammatical here.

Buying versus selling

Distinguish the verb for buying from its related counterpart for selling.

我想买一点儿茶。 (wǒ xiǎng mǎi yìdiǎnr chá.)

I want to buy some tea.
