1 · Conversation — School and University

A younger student and an older student compare which schools they attend.

A

你在哪儿上学？ (Nǐ zài nǎr shàngxué?)

Where do you go to school?

B

我在中学上学。 (Wǒ zài zhōngxué shàngxué.)

I go to middle school.

A

你姐姐呢？ (Nǐ jiějie ne?)

What about your older sister?

B

她是大学生，也学汉语。 (Tā shì dàxuéshēng, yě xué Hànyǔ.)

She is a university student and studies Chinese too.

2 · How the language works

School versus student

学校 (xuéxiào) is a school or educational institution; 学生 (xuéshēng) is a learner. Combining 小学/中学/大学 with 生 specifies the student level.

小学/中学/大学 + 生
xiǎoxué/zhōngxué/dàxué + shēng
小学/中学/大学 + 生

Use one of the three school levels shown to form the corresponding student type.

我弟弟是小学生。 (Wǒ dìdi shì xiǎoxuéshēng.)

My younger brother is a primary-school pupil.

她在大学学习。 (Tā zài dàxué xuéxí.)

She studies at university.

Attending school: 上学 (shàngxué)

上学 (shàngxué) describes attending school; 上课 (shàngkè) describes having/attending a class. The two do not mean exactly the same thing.

主语 + 在 + 地点 + 上学
zhǔyǔ + zài + dìdiǎn + shàngxué
<subject> + 在 + <place> + 上学

我在北京上学。 (Wǒ zài Běijīng shàngxué.)

I attend school in Beijing.

我们上午上课。 (Wǒmen shàngwǔ shàngkè.)

We have class in the morning.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

哥哥是中学生，妹妹是小学生。他们每天一起去学校，下午一起回家。 (Gēge shì zhōngxuéshēng, mèimei shì xiǎoxuéshēng. Tāmen měi tiān yìqǐ qù xuéxiào, xiàwǔ yìqǐ huí jiā.)

The older brother is a middle-school student, and the younger sister is in primary school. They go to school together every day and return home together in the afternoon.

1. Which child is in primary school?
Show the answer

The younger sister.

2. Do they travel to school together?
Show the answer

Yes.

6 · Watch out

学校 (xuéxiào) names the institution; 学生 (xuéshēng) names the person. Adding 生 changes the meaning.

Pronunciation clinic

学习 (xuéxí) is a verb “study/learn”; 学校 (xuéxiào) is the place “school”.
