Overview

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 27
大学 (dàxué)

university

noun · introduced

我在大学学习中文。 (wǒ zài dàxué xuéxí Zhōngwén.)

I study Chinese at university.

大学 is the institution; 在 + place gives the location of the study activity.

大学里有很多学生。 (dàxué lǐ yǒu hěn duō xuéshēng.)

There are many students at the university.

HSK entry 28
大学生 (dàxuéshēng)

university student

noun · introduced

她是大学生。 (tā shì dàxuéshēng.)

She is a university student.

大学生 refers to a university-level student, not an elementary-school pupil.

大学生也可以在这里读书。 (dàxuéshēng yě kěyǐ zài zhèlǐ dúshū.)

University students can also study here.

HSK entry 179
上学 (shàngxué)

go to school

verb · introduced

孩子们每天上学。 (háizimen měitiān shàngxué.)

The children go to school every day.

上学 means attend or go to school; 们 makes the group plural.

孩子们早上八点上学。 (háizimen zǎoshang bā diǎn shàngxué.)

The children go to school at eight in the morning.

HSK entry 211
同学 (tóngxué)

classmate

noun · introduced

他是我的同学。 (tā shì wǒ de tóngxué.)

He is my classmate.

同学 is someone studying in the same class or school.

我的同学们喜欢学中文。 (wǒ de tóngxuémen xǐhuan xué Zhōngwén.)

My classmates like learning Chinese.

HSK entry 237
小学 (xiǎoxué)

primary school

noun · introduced

我的孩子上小学。 (wǒ de háizi shàng xiǎoxué.)

My child attends primary school.

上小学 refers to attending elementary or primary school.

小学早上八点上课。 (xiǎoxué zǎoshang bā diǎn shàngkè.)

Primary school classes start at eight in the morning.

HSK entry 238
小学生 (xiǎoxuéshēng)

primary pupil

noun · introduced

他是小学生。 (tā shì xiǎoxuéshēng.)

He is a primary school pupil.

小学生 specifies a primary-school pupil, not a college student.

这些小学生正在上课。 (zhèxiē xiǎoxuéshēng zhèngzài shàngkè.)

These primary school students are having class.

HSK entry 248
学生 (xuéshēng)

student

noun · introduced

他是学生。 (tā shì xuéshēng.)

He is a student.

学生 is student in general; it does not by itself specify the school level.

这里有二十个学生。 (zhèlǐ yǒu èr shí gè xuéshēng.)

There are twenty students here.

HSK entry 250
学校 (xuéxiào)

school

noun · introduced

我们的学校很大。 (wǒmen de xuéxiào hěn dà.)

Our school is big.

学校 can refer to a school institution or its campus/buildings.

这个学校有很多老师。 (zhège xuéxiào yǒu hěn duō lǎoshī.)

This school has many teachers.

HSK entry 292
中学 (zhōngxué)

middle school

noun · introduced

我弟弟上中学。 (wǒ dìdi shàng zhōngxué.)

My younger brother attends middle school.

中学 generally denotes secondary or middle school.

这个中学有很多老师。 (zhège zhōngxué yǒu hěn duō lǎoshī.)

This middle school has many teachers.

HSK entry 293
中学生 (zhōngxuéshēng)

middle school student

noun · introduced

她是中学生。 (tā shì zhōngxuéshēng.)

She is a middle school student.

中学生 is a secondary-school student, distinct from 小学生 and 大学生.

中学生上午八点上课。 (zhōngxuéshēng shàngwǔ bā diǎn shàngkè.)

Middle school students have class at eight in the morning.

Usage and grammar

Talking about schools

Use the name of an institution to say where someone studies.

我在大学学习中文。 (wǒ zài dàxué xuéxí Zhōngwén.)

I study Chinese at university.

大学 is the institution; 在 + place gives the location of the study activity.

Identifying students

A student noun describes the person, not the activity.

他是学生。 (tā shì xuéshēng.)

He is a student.

学生 is student in general; it does not by itself specify the school level.
