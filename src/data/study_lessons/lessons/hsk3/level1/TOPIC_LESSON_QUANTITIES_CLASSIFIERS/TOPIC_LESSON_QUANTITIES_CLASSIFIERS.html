1 · Conversation — Counting Real Things

A shopper chooses books and clothing. Practise different classifiers and small amounts.

A

你要买几本书？ (Nǐ yào mǎi jǐ běn shū?)

How many books do you want to buy?

B

我要买两本。 (Wǒ yào mǎi liǎng běn.)

I want to buy two.

A

你还要衣服吗？ (Nǐ hái yào yīfu ma?)

Do you want clothes too?

B

要，我想买一件。 (Yào, wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí jiàn.)

Yes, I want to buy one item.

2 · How the language works

Classifiers for countable objects

Chinese normally uses a measure word between a numeral and a noun. 本 (běn) counts bound books; 件 (jiàn) counts many items of clothing; 个 (gè) is common but not universal.

数字 + 量词 + 名词
shùzì + liàngcí + míngcí
<number> + <classifier> + <noun>

桌子上有两本书。 (Zhuōzi shàng yǒu liǎng běn shū.)

There are two books on the table.

我买了三件衣服。 (Wǒ mǎi le sān jiàn yīfu.)

I bought three items of clothing.

两 (liǎng), 几 (jǐ), and 多少 (duōshao)

两 (liǎng) generally precedes classifiers for two items; 几 (jǐ) is natural for small expected numbers; 多少 (duōshao) has a broader numerical range.

几 + 量词 + 名词？
jǐ + liàngcí + míngcí?
几 + <classifier> + <noun>？

你有几个好朋友？ (Nǐ yǒu jǐ ge hǎo péngyou?)

How many close friends do you have?

这本书多少钱？ (Zhè běn shū duōshao qián?)

How much does this book cost?

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

桌子上有五本书，两本是我的。姐姐买了三件衣服，妹妹只买了一件。 (Zhuōzi shàng yǒu wǔ běn shū, liǎng běn shì wǒ de. Jiějie mǎi le sān jiàn yīfu, mèimei zhǐ mǎi le yí jiàn.)

There are five books on the table; two are mine. The older sister bought three pieces of clothing; the younger sister bought only one.

1. How many books belong to the speaker?
Show the answer

Two.

2. Who bought more clothing?
Show the answer

The older sister.

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

Show one possible response

I would like a little water.

6 · Watch out

Do not use the same classifier for all nouns. For a book use 本 (běn), not 件 (jiàn).

Pronunciation clinic

The contrast 二 (èr) versus 两 (liǎng) is especially important before classifiers: 两本 (liǎng běn).
