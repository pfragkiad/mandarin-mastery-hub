Getting Around

Vehicles, Destinations and Position

travel-transport · places-directions · #transport · #directions · #locations

Learning objectives

  • Name common modes of transport.
  • Describe going, coming and returning.
  • Use basic spatial position words.

Overview

Talk about travelling, moving between places and locating people and objects.

Name common modes of transport.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 20
车 (chē)

vehicle

noun · introduced

这是我爸爸的车。 (zhè shì wǒ bàba de chē.)

This is my dad's car.

学校门口有很多车。 (xuéxiào ménkǒu yǒu hěn duō chē.)

There are many cars at the school entrance.

HSK entry 22
出租车 (chūzūchē)

taxi

noun · introduced

我坐出租车去学校。 (wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù xuéxiào.)

I take a taxi to school.

坐出租车 means travel by taxi, rather than literally sit in a taxi.

从学校到医院可以坐出租车。 (cóng xuéxiào dào yīyuàn kěyǐ zuò chūzūchē.)

You can take a taxi from the school to the hospital.

HSK entry 29
到 (dào)

arrive

verb · introduced

他九点到学校。 (tā jiǔ diǎn dào xuéxiào.)

He arrives at school at nine.

九点 gives the arrival time; 到 indicates reaching the school.

从家到学校可以坐车。 (cóng jiā dào xuéxiào kěyǐ zuò chē.)

You can go from home to school by car.

HSK entry 54
飞机 (fēijī)

airplane

noun · introduced

他坐飞机去中国。 (tā zuò fēijī qù Zhōngguó.)

He takes a plane to China.

坐飞机 means to travel by plane; 去中国 specifies the destination.

飞机下午三点到机场。 (fēijī xiàwǔ sān diǎn dào jīchǎng.)

The plane arrives at the airport at three in the afternoon.

HSK entry 81
回 (huí)

return

verb · introduced

我明天回家。 (wǒ míngtiān huí jiā.)

I will go home tomorrow.

回家 means return home; 明天 indicates the future without a future-tense ending.

他下午六点回家。 (tā xiàwǔ liù diǎn huí jiā.)

He goes home at six in the afternoon.

HSK entry 83
火车 (huǒchē)

train

noun · introduced

我坐火车去看朋友。 (wǒ zuò huǒchē qù kàn péngyou.)

I take the train to visit a friend.

火车上午九点到。 (huǒchē shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn dào.)

The train arrives at nine in the morning.

HSK entry 98
开车 (kāichē)

drive

verb · introduced

我爸爸会开车。 (wǒ bàba huì kāichē.)

My father can drive.

会 marks the skill of driving rather than permission to drive.

今天爸爸开车去公司。 (jīntiān bàba kāichē qù gōngsī.)

Dad drives to the company today.

HSK entry 106
来 (lái)

come

verb · introduced

你明天来吗？ (nǐ míngtiān lái ma?)

Are you coming tomorrow?

来 indicates movement toward the speaker’s place or point of reference.

学生们明天上午来学校。 (xuéshēngmen míngtiān shàngwǔ lái xuéxiào.)

The students will come to school tomorrow morning.

HSK entry 167
去 (qù)

go

verb · introduced

我们现在去学校。 (wǒmen xiànzài qù xuéxiào.)

We are going to school now.

去 indicates movement away from the speaker’s present location.

学生们明天去电影院。 (xuéshēngmen míngtiān qù diànyǐngyuàn.)

The students will go to the cinema tomorrow.

HSK entry 11
边 (biān)

side; edge

suffix · introduced

你来这边坐吧。 (nǐ lái zhèbiān zuò ba.)

Come sit over here.

学校在那边。 (xuéxiào zài nàbiān.)

The school is over there.

Primarily a bound locative suffix in 这边、那边、前边; also a noun meaning side or edge in other constructions.

HSK entry 66
国 (guó)

country

noun · introduced

中国是一个大国。 (Zhōngguó shì yí ge dàguó.)

China is a large country.

大国 means a large or major country; 个 is a general classifier.

我哥哥在外国工作。 (wǒ gēge zài wàiguó gōngzuò.)

My older brother works abroad.

外国 (“foreign country/abroad”) is a common compound containing 国.

HSK entry 80
后 (hòu)

after; behind

localizer · introduced

我坐在你后边。 (wǒ zuò zài nǐ hòubian.)

I am sitting behind you.

后边 is a positional noun meaning behind; 在 + person + 后边 locates the speaker.

我们下课后去书店。 (wǒmen xiàkè hòu qù shūdiàn.)

We go to the bookstore after class.

After/behind: temporal or spatial localizer; can also be nominal.

HSK entry 110
里 (lǐ)

inside

localizer · introduced

书在包里。 (shū zài bāo lǐ.)

The book is in the bag.

里 is a location word, showing that the book is inside the bag.

学生们在教室里上课。 (xuéshēngmen zài jiàoshì lǐ shàngkè.)

The students are having class in the classroom.

Localizer meaning inside, traditionally also categorized as a location noun.

HSK entry 163
前 (qián)

before; front

localizer · introduced

学校在商店前边。 (xuéxiào zài shāngdiàn qiánbian.)

The school is in front of the shop.

前边 locates something in front of a landmark, here the shop.

上课前请看书。 (shàngkè qián qǐng kàn shū.)

Please read the book before class.

Before/in front of; a time or place localizer.

HSK entry 175
上 (shàng)

above; on

localizer · introduced

书在桌子上。 (shū zài zhuōzi shàng.)

The book is on the table.

上 is a positional word meaning on top of or above something.

桌子上有两个苹果。 (zhuōzi shàng yǒu liǎng ge píngguǒ.)

There are two apples on the table.

On/above as a localizer or verb for ascending/attending, depending on context.

HSK entry 212
外 (wài)

outside

localizer · introduced

他在门外。 (tā zài mén wài.)

He is outside the door.

门外 locates someone outside the door; 外 means outside.

请在门外等一下。 (qǐng zài ménwài děng yíxià.)

Please wait outside the door for a moment.

HSK entry 213
外边 (wàibian)

outside

localizer · introduced

外边下雨了。 (wàibian xià yǔ le.)

It is raining outside.

外边 means outside; 下雨了 describes the onset or current fact of rain.

学校外边有一个超市。 (xuéxiào wàibian yǒu yí ge chāoshì.)

There is a supermarket outside the school.

HSK entry 226
下 (xià)

below; down; next

localizer · introduced

猫在桌子下面。 (māo zài zhuōzi xià miàn.)

The cat is under the table.

下面 is the full location expression below the table; 在 marks location.

书在椅子下。 (shū zài yǐzi xià.)

The book is under the chair.

A short, concrete location sentence: 在 indicates location, and 下 means beneath or below the chair.

Below/under as a localizer, but verb and measure-word uses also exist.

HSK entry 289
中国 (Zhōngguó)

China

noun · introduced

我想去中国。 (wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó.)

I want to go to China.

中国 is the country China; 去 indicates travel to it.

中国有很多大学。 (Zhōngguó yǒu hěn duō dàxué.)

There are many universities in China.

Usage and grammar

Going to a destination

The movement verb precedes the destination in a simple travel phrase.

我们现在去学校。 (wǒmen xiànzài qù xuéxiào.)

We are going to school now.

去 indicates movement away from the speaker’s present location.

Expressing position

Location expressions identify where a person or thing is.

书在包里。 (shū zài bāo lǐ.)

The book is in the bag.

里 is a location word, showing that the book is inside the bag.
