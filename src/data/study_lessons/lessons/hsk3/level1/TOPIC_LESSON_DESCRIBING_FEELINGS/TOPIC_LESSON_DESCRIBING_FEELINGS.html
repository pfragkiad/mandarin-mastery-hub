Overview

Use common adjectives and evaluative language to describe people and objects.

Describe appearance, size and quality.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 25
大 (dà)

big

adjective · introduced

这个房间很大。 (zhège fángjiān hěn dà.)

This room is big.

很 appears naturally before a descriptive adjective such as 大, without necessarily meaning “very.”

这个大学很大。 (zhège dàxué hěn dà.)

This university is very big.

HSK entry 44
对 (duì)

correct; toward

adjective · introduced

我说得对吗？ (wǒ shuō de duì ma?)

Am I right?

老师说：“对，是这样。” (lǎoshī shuō: “duì, shì zhèyàng.”)

The teacher said, "Yes, that's right."

Written dialogue: 对 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Primarily an adjective meaning correct here; also preposition/verb in other usages.

HSK entry 46
多 (duō)

many; much

adjective · introduced

这里有很多人。 (zhèlǐ yǒu hěn duō rén.)

There are many people here.

很多 directly modifies 人 to express a large number of people.

下午来的人很多。 (xiàwǔ lái de rén hěn duō.)

There are many people coming this afternoon.

HSK entry 57
高兴 (gāoxìng)

happy

adjective · introduced

见到你很高兴。 (jiàndào nǐ hěn gāoxìng.)

Nice to meet you.

见到你很高兴 is a conventional greeting on meeting someone.

学生们今天非常高兴。 (xuéshēngmen jīntiān fēicháng gāoxìng.)

The students are very happy today.

HSK entry 71
好 (hǎo)

good

adjective · introduced

这本书很好。 (zhè běn shū hěn hǎo.)

This book is good.

好 is a broad positive adjective; 很好 can describe books, people, plans, or weather.

这家饭店的菜很好。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn de cài hěn hǎo.)

The food at this restaurant is very good.

HSK entry 73
好看 (hǎokàn)

good-looking

adjective · introduced

这件衣服很好看。 (zhè jiàn yīfu hěn hǎokàn.)

This item of clothing looks nice.

件 is a classifier for clothing; 好看 describes how the garment looks.

这本书很好看。 (zhè běn shū hěn hǎokàn.)

This book is very good to read.

HSK entry 74
好听 (hǎotīng)

pleasant to hear

adjective · introduced

我觉得中文歌很好听。 (wǒ juéde Zhōngwén gē hěn hǎotīng.)

I think Chinese songs sound really nice.

她唱的歌很好听。 (tā chàng de gē hěn hǎotīng.)

The songs she sings sound very nice.

HSK entry 118
忙 (máng)

busy

adjective · introduced

我今天很忙。 (wǒ jīntiān hěn máng.)

I am very busy today.

忙 is a state adjective; 很忙 is a natural predicate without 是.

妈妈早上很忙，晚上有时间。 (māma zǎoshang hěn máng, wǎnshang yǒu shíjiān.)

Mom is very busy in the morning but has time in the evening.

HSK entry 158
漂亮 (piàoliang)

beautiful

adjective · introduced

这件衣服很漂亮。 (zhè jiàn yīfu hěn piàoliang.)

This item of clothing is beautiful.

漂亮 describes attractive appearance, here that of clothing.

学校里有很多漂亮的花。 (xuéxiào lǐ yǒu hěn duō piàoliang de huā.)

There are many beautiful flowers at the school.

HSK entry 180
少 (shǎo)

few; little

adjective · introduced

这里的人很少。 (zhèlǐ de rén hěn shǎo.)

There are very few people here.

很少 describes a small number of people at this location.

今天来上课的学生很少。 (jīntiān lái shàngkè de xuéshēng hěn shǎo.)

Very few students came to class today.

HSK entry 234
小 (xiǎo)

small

adjective · introduced

这只猫很小。 (zhè zhī māo hěn xiǎo.)

This cat is very small.

只 is the animal classifier; 小 describes the cat’s size.

这个房间很小。 (zhège fángjiān hěn xiǎo.)

This room is very small.

HSK entry 242
新 (xīn)

new

adjective · introduced

这是我的新书。 (zhè shì wǒ de xīn shū.)

This is my new book.

新 modifies 书 to mean a new book.

这家书店有很多新书。 (zhè jiā shūdiàn yǒu hěn duō xīn shū.)

This bookstore has many new books.

Usage and grammar

Describing size

In simple affirmative descriptive sentences, a degree word can appear naturally before the adjective.

这个房间很大。 (zhège fángjiān hěn dà.)

This room is big.

很 appears naturally before a descriptive adjective such as 大, without necessarily meaning “very.”

Talking about feelings

Adjectives of feeling serve as predicates in simple descriptions.

见到你很高兴。 (jiàndào nǐ hěn gāoxìng.)

Nice to meet you.

见到你很高兴 is a conventional greeting on meeting someone.
