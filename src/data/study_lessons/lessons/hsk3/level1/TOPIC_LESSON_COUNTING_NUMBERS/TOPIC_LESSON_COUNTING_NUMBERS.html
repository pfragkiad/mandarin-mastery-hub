1 · Conversation — Counting from One Upward

Use prices, ages and quantities to practise number structure without memorizing an isolated list.

A

这本书多少钱？ (Zhè běn shū duōshao qián?)

How much is this book?

B

一百二十元。 (Yì bǎi èr shí yuán.)

One hundred and twenty yuan.

A

那本呢？ (Nà běn ne?)

What about that one?

B

九十八元。 (Jiǔ shí bā yuán.)

Ninety-eight yuan.

2 · How the language works

Building larger numbers

Chinese number order follows thousands, hundreds, tens and ones. 零 (líng) marks a missing internal place where needed, as in 一百零五 (yì bǎi líng wǔ).

(千位数字 + 千) + (百位数字 + 百) + (十位数字 + 十) + 个位数字
(qiānwèi shùzì + qiān) + (bǎiwèi shùzì + bǎi) + (shíwèi shùzì + shí) + gèwèi shùzì
(<number of thousands> + 千) + (<number of hundreds> + 百) + (<number of tens> + 十) + <number of ones>

The slots show place values up to the thousands. Use 零 (líng) where required for a gap. For 10–19, the initial one before 十 (shí) is normally omitted.

二百三十六。 (Èr bǎi sān shí liù.)

Two hundred and thirty-six.

一千零二。 (Yì qiān líng èr.)

One thousand and two.

Numbers with nouns

Use a suitable classifier when counting most nouns. Do not put an English-style plural ending on the noun.

数字 + 量词 + 名词
shùzì + liàngcí + míngcí
<number> + <classifier> + <noun>

三本书。 (Sān běn shū.)

Three books.

十个人。 (Shí ge rén.)

Ten people.

3 · Build new sentences

Change the number, retaining the book classifier.

Worked model

我有三本书。 (Wǒ yǒu sān běn shū.)

I have three books.

Change 1: five
Reveal a model response

我有五本书。 (Wǒ yǒu wǔ běn shū.)

I have five books.

Change 2: ten
Reveal a model response

我现在有十本书。 (Wǒ xiànzài yǒu shí běn shū.)

I now have ten books.

There are three hundred students in the school. Our class has twenty people. Eighteen students came today.

6 · Watch out

A missing tens place may need 零 (líng): 一百零五 (yì bǎi líng wǔ), not 一百五 when you specifically mean 105.

Pronunciation clinic

Before a fourth-tone syllable, 一 (yī) normally sounds like yí; before a non-fourth tone, it often becomes yì. Displayed lexical pinyin may follow a different citation convention.
