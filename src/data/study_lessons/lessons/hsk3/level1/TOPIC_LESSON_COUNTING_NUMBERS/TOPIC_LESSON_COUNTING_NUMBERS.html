Basic Numbers and Large Numbers

numbers-quantities · #basic-numbers · #counting

Learning objectives

  • Read the digits and basic round numbers.
  • Understand how tens, hundreds and thousands are formed.
  • Recognize numbers in example sentences.

Overview

Recognize the basic Chinese numerals and combine them to express quantities.

Read the digits and basic round numbers.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 2
八 (bā)

eight

numeral · introduced

我有八本书。 (wǒ yǒu bā běn shū.)

I have eight books.

八 gives the quantity; 本 is the classifier used for bound books.

今天有八个学生上课。 (jīntiān yǒu bā gè xuéshēng shàngkè.)

Eight students have class today.

HSK entry 6
百 (bǎi)

hundred

numeral · introduced

这里有一百个人。 (zhèlǐ yǒu yì bǎi ge rén.)

There are a hundred people here.

一百 means one hundred; 个 is the general classifier for people here.

这家书店有一百本中文书。 (zhè jiā shūdiàn yǒu yì bǎi běn Zhōngwén shū.)

This bookstore has one hundred books in Chinese.

HSK entry 49
二 (èr)

two

numeral · introduced

我有二十本书。 (wǒ yǒu èrshí běn shū.)

I have twenty books.

二十 is the number twenty; 本 counts books.

今天是二月二日。 (jīntiān shì èr yuè èr rì.)

Today is February 2.

HSK entry 95
九 (jiǔ)

nine

numeral · introduced

我有九个苹果。 (wǒ yǒu jiǔ gè píngguǒ.)

I have nine apples.

个 is the general classifier used when counting apples.

学校九点开始上课。 (xuéxiào jiǔ diǎn kāishǐ shàngkè.)

Classes at the school start at nine o'clock.

HSK entry 112
零 (líng)

zero

numeral · introduced

现在是零点。 (xiànzài shì líng diǎn.)

It is midnight.

学校有一百零二个学生。 (xuéxiào yǒu yì bǎi líng èr gè xuéshēng.)

The school has one hundred and two students.

HSK entry 113
六 (liù)

six

numeral · introduced

我有六本书。 (wǒ yǒu liù běn shū.)

I have six books.

六 is a cardinal number used before the book classifier 本.

我们星期六去书店。 (wǒmen xīngqīliù qù shūdiàn.)

We are going to the bookstore on Saturday.

HSK entry 160
七 (qī)

seven

numeral · introduced

我有七个杯子。 (wǒ yǒu qī gè bēizi.)

I have seven cups.

七 is the quantity, followed by the general classifier 个.

七个学生在教室里。 (qī gè xuéshēng zài jiàoshì lǐ.)

Seven students are in the classroom.

HSK entry 162
千 (qiān)

thousand

numeral · introduced

这里有一千本书。 (zhèlǐ yǒu yì qiān běn shū.)

There are one thousand books here.

千 means a thousand; 一 is pronounced yì before qiān.

这个学校有一千个学生。 (zhège xuéxiào yǒu yì qiān gè xuéshēng.)

This school has one thousand students.

HSK entry 173
三 (sān)

three

numeral · introduced

我有三个朋友。 (wǒ yǒu sān gè péngyou.)

I have three friends.

三 gives the count; 个 is the classifier for 朋友.

三个学生在看书。 (sān gè xuéshēng zài kàn shū.)

Three students are reading.

HSK entry 184
十 (shí)

ten

numeral · introduced

我有十本书。 (wǒ yǒu shí běn shū.)

I have ten books.

十 counts the books; 本 is the required classifier.

上午十点我们上课。 (shàngwǔ shí diǎn wǒmen shàngkè.)

We have class at ten in the morning.

HSK entry 198
四 (sì)

four

numeral · introduced

我有四本书。 (wǒ yǒu sì běn shū.)

I have four books.

四 is the number four, followed by 本 for books.

这个房间有四个人。 (zhège fángjiān yǒu sì gè rén.)

There are four people in this room.

HSK entry 223
五 (wǔ)

five

numeral · introduced

我有五个苹果。 (wǒ yǒu wǔ gè píngguǒ.)

I have five apples.

五 gives the number, and 个 is the general classifier for apples.

今天有五个学生没有来。 (jīntiān yǒu wǔ gè xuéshēng méiyǒu lái.)

Five students did not come today.

HSK entry 254
一 (yī)

one

numeral · introduced

我有一个姐姐。 (wǒ yǒu yí ge jiějie.)

I have one older sister.

一 counts one older sister and changes to yí before neutral-tone 个.

这里有一个学生。 (zhèlǐ yǒu yí ge xuéshēng.)

There is one student here.

一 counts a single student with the measure word 个 in the common existential pattern 这里有… .

Usage and grammar

Counting hundreds

Combine numerals to build larger numbers.

这里有一百个人。 (zhèlǐ yǒu yì bǎi ge rén.)

There are a hundred people here.

一百 means one hundred; 个 is the general classifier for people here.

Tens

The numeral for ten participates in larger numbers.

我有十本书。 (wǒ yǒu shí běn shū.)

I have ten books.

十 counts the books; 本 is the required classifier.
