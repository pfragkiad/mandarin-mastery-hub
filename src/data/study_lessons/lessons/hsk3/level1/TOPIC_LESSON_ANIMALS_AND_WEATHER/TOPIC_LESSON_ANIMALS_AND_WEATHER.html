Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 64
狗 (gǒu)

dog

noun · introduced

这只狗很小。 (zhè zhī gǒu hěn xiǎo.)

This dog is very small.

这里有两只狗。 (zhèlǐ yǒu liǎng zhī gǒu.)

There are two dogs here.

HSK entry 109
冷 (lěng)

cold

adjective · introduced

今天很冷。 (jīntiān hěn lěng.)

It is cold today.

很 + 冷 describes cold weather; context supplies how cold it feels.

今天晚上比上午冷。 (jīntiān wǎnshang bǐ shàngwǔ lěng.)

It is colder tonight than it was this morning.

HSK entry 119
猫 (māo)

cat

noun · introduced

我有一只猫。 (wǒ yǒu yì zhī māo.)

I have a cat.

只 is the common animal classifier when counting cats.

这只猫喜欢睡觉。 (zhè zhī māo xǐhuan shuìjiào.)

This cat likes sleeping.

HSK entry 169
热 (rè)

hot

adjective · introduced

今天太热了。 (jīntiān tài rè le.)

It is too hot today.

太热了 expresses more heat than desired or expected.

下午的天气很热。 (xiàwǔ de tiānqì hěn rè.)

The weather is very hot in the afternoon.

HSK entry 208
天气 (tiānqì)

weather

noun · introduced

今天天气很好。 (jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.)

The weather is very good today.

今天天气 is a common weather-topic phrase in everyday Chinese.

今天的天气比昨天好。 (jīntiān de tiānqì bǐ zuótiān hǎo.)

The weather today is better than yesterday's.

HSK entry 227
下雨 (xià yǔ)

rain

verb · introduced

今天下雨了。 (jīntiān xià yǔ le.)

It rained today.

下雨 is the verb expression for rain; 了 can mark a new or completed event.

下雨的时候不要出去。 (xià yǔ de shíhou búyào chūqu.)

Don't go outside when it rains.

HSK entry 251
雪 (xuě)

snow

noun · introduced

地上有雪。 (dìshang yǒu xuě.)

There's snow on the ground.

地上有雪 states that there is snow on the ground, not merely that it is winter.

今天的雪很大。 (jīntiān de xuě hěn dà.)

There's a lot of snow today.

HSK entry 267
雨 (yǔ)

rain

noun · introduced

今天雨很大。 (jīntiān yǔ hěn dà.)

It's raining heavily today.

雨很大 describes heavy rain; 大 can describe rain intensity.

昨天下午下了很多雨。 (zuótiān xiàwǔ xià le hěn duō yǔ.)

It rained a lot yesterday afternoon.

Usage and grammar

Discussing weather

Use a weather noun as the topic of a simple description.

今天天气很好。 (jīntiān tiānqì hěn hǎo.)

The weather is very good today.

今天天气 is a common weather-topic phrase in everyday Chinese.

Rain

The verb-object weather expression describes rainfall.

今天下雨了。 (jīntiān xià yǔ le.)

It rained today.

下雨 is the verb expression for rain; 了 can mark a new or completed event.
