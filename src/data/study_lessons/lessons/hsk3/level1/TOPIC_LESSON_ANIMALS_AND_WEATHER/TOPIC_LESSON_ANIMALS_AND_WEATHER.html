1 · Conversation — Animals and Weather

Two friends decide whether to go outside and talk about the weather and a pet.

A

今天天气怎么样？ (Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?)

How is the weather today?

B

很冷，还下雨。 (Hěn lěng, hái xià yǔ.)

It is cold, and it is raining too.

A

那只猫在外边吗？ (Nà zhī māo zài wàibian ma?)

Is that cat outside?

B

不在，它在家里。 (Bú zài, tā zài jiā lǐ.)

No, it is at home.

2 · How the language works

Describing weather

Use 天气 (tiānqì) for the weather, then an adjective or weather event. 下雨 (xià yǔ) functions as “to rain”; do not translate it word-for-word as “down rain”.

今天 + 天气 + 很 + 形容词 / 今天 + 下雨
jīntiān + tiānqì + hěn + xíngróngcí / jīntiān + xiàyǔ
今天 + 天气 + 很 + <adjective> / 今天 + 下雨

今天天气很热。 (Jīntiān tiānqì hěn rè.)

The weather is hot today.

明天可能下雨。 (Míngtiān kěnéng xià yǔ.)

It may rain tomorrow.

Talking about animal location

在 (zài) locates the subject. The animal classifier 只 (zhī) introduces an individual cat or dog.

[那/这] + 只 + 动物 + 在 + 地点
[nà/zhè] + zhī + dòngwù + zài + dìdiǎn
[那/这] + 只 + <animal> + 在 + <place>

这只狗在房间里。 (Zhè zhī gǒu zài fángjiān lǐ.)

This dog is in the room.

猫在桌子下边。 (Māo zài zhuōzi xiàbian.)

The cat is under the table.

3 · Build new sentences

Change the weather description, not the sentence structure.

Worked model

今天天气很冷。 (Jīntiān tiānqì hěn lěng.)

It is cold today.

Change 1: hot
Reveal a model response

今天天气很热。 (Jīntiān tiānqì hěn rè.)

It is hot today.

Change 2: good
Reveal a model response

今天下午天气很好。 (Jīntiān xiàwǔ tiānqì hěn hǎo.)

The weather is good this afternoon.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

早上很冷，下午天气很好。妹妹想出去玩，但是家里的猫还在睡觉。 (Zǎoshang hěn lěng, xiàwǔ tiānqì hěn hǎo. Mèimei xiǎng chūqù wán, dànshì jiā lǐ de māo hái zài shuìjiào.)

It is cold in the morning, but the weather is good in the afternoon. The younger sister wants to go out and play, but the family cat is still sleeping.

1. When is the weather good?
Show the answer

In the afternoon.

2. What is the cat doing?
Show the answer

Sleeping.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Describe today’s weather to a friend.
Show one possible response

今天下午天气很好。 (Jīntiān xiàwǔ tiānqì hěn hǎo.)

The weather is good this afternoon.

2. Say where a pet is.
Show one possible response

我的猫在家里。 (Wǒ de māo zài jiā lǐ.)

My cat is at home.

6 · Watch out

A weather event such as 下雨 (xià yǔ) is not an adjective. Prefer 今天下雨 (Jīntiān xià yǔ) rather than 今天很下雨.
