Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 42
读 (dú)

read

verb · introduced

我会读汉字。 (wǒ huì dú Hànzì.)

I can read Chinese characters.

读 refers to reading aloud or decoding written characters in this example.

请读这一课。 (qǐng dú zhè yí kè.)

Please read this lesson.

HSK entry 43
读书 (dúshū)

read books

verb · introduced

我每天读书。 (wǒ měitiān dúshū.)

I read books every day.

读书 means read or study, depending on context; here it means read books.

学生们每天在学校读书。 (xuéshēngmen měitiān zài xuéxiào dúshū.)

The students read at school every day.

HSK entry 69
汉语 (Hànyǔ)

Chinese language

noun · introduced

我正在学汉语。 (wǒ zhèngzài xué Hànyǔ.)

I am learning Chinese.

正在 explicitly indicates an action currently in progress.

学校里有汉语课。 (xuéxiào lǐ yǒu Hànyǔ kè.)

The school has Chinese-language classes.

HSK entry 70
汉字 (Hànzì)

Chinese character

noun · introduced

这个汉字怎么读？ (zhège Hànzì zěnme dú?)

How is this Chinese character pronounced?

怎么读 asks for the reading or pronunciation of this written character.

学生会写一百个汉字。 (xuéshēng huì xiě yì bǎi gè Hànzì.)

The student can write one hundred Chinese characters.

HSK entry 103
课 (kè)

lesson

noun · introduced

我们上午有汉语课。 (wǒmen shàngwǔ yǒu Hànyǔ kè.)

We have Chinese class in the morning.

课 is a class or lesson; 有 + noun expresses having a scheduled class.

学校星期天没有课。 (xuéxiào xīngqītiān méiyǒu kè.)

The school has no classes on Sundays.

HSK entry 107
老师 (lǎoshī)

teacher

noun · introduced

我们的老师很好。 (wǒmen de lǎoshī hěn hǎo.)

Our teacher is very good.

老师 denotes a teacher; 我们的 specifies the group’s teacher.

老师在教室里。 (lǎoshī zài jiàoshì lǐ.)

The teacher is in the classroom.

HSK entry 177
上课 (shàngkè)

attend class

verb · introduced

我们九点上课。 (wǒmen jiǔ diǎn shàngkè.)

We start class at nine.

上课 means start or attend class; the time gives when it begins.

学生们每天上午九点上课。 (xuéshēngmen měitiān shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn shàngkè.)

The students have class at nine every morning.

HSK entry 190
书 (shū)

book

noun · introduced

我喜欢看书。 (wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū.)

I like reading books.

看书 means read a book; 看 has a broader range than English “look.”

这家书店有很多中文书。 (zhè jiā shūdiàn yǒu hěn duō Zhōngwén shū.)

This bookstore has many books in Chinese.

HSK entry 191
书店 (shūdiàn)

bookstore

noun · introduced

书店在学校旁边。 (shūdiàn zài xuéxiào pángbiān.)

The bookstore is next to the school.

旁边 expresses the shop’s location beside the school.

书店每天早上九点开门。 (shūdiàn měitiān zǎoshang jiǔ diǎn kāimén.)

The bookstore opens at nine every morning.

HSK entry 196
说 (shuō)

say

verb · introduced

我会说一点儿中文。 (wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Zhōngwén.)

I can speak a little Chinese.

一点儿 expresses a small amount; 会 means an ability to speak the language.

今天老师说汉语。 (jīntiān lǎoshī shuō Hànyǔ.)

The teacher speaks Chinese today.

HSK entry 197
说话 (shuōhuà)

speak; talk

verb · introduced

请不要说话。 (qǐng bú yào shuōhuà.)

Please don't talk.

请不要… is a polite negative request, often heard in public or class settings.

上课的时候请不要说话。 (shàngkè de shíhou qǐng búyào shuōhuà.)

Please don't talk during class.

HSK entry 209
听 (tīng)

listen

verb · introduced

我喜欢听歌。 (wǒ xǐhuan tīng gē.)

I like listening to songs.

听歌 means listen to songs or music; 听 takes sound as its object.

学生们在听老师说话。 (xuéshēngmen zài tīng lǎoshī shuōhuà.)

The students are listening to the teacher speak.

HSK entry 210
听见 (tīngjiàn)

hear

verb · introduced

我听见你说话了。 (wǒ tīngjiàn nǐ shuōhuà le.)

I heard you speaking.

在这里可以听见老师说话。 (zài zhèlǐ kěyǐ tīngjiàn lǎoshī shuōhuà.)

You can hear the teacher speaking here.

HSK entry 229
下课 (xiàkè)

finish class

verb · introduced

我们四点下课。 (wǒmen sì diǎn xiàkè.)

We finish class at four.

下课 means class has ended or is dismissed, here at four.

下课后，我们去超市。 (xiàkè hòu, wǒmen qù chāoshì.)

After class, we go to the supermarket.

HSK entry 240
写 (xiě)

write

verb · introduced

我会写这个字。 (wǒ huì xiě zhège zì.)

I can write this character.

写 refers to writing a character; 会 indicates learned ability.

请写你的名字。 (qǐng xiě nǐ de míngzi.)

Please write your name.

HSK entry 247
学 (xué)

learn

verb · introduced

我正在学中文。 (wǒ zhèngzài xué Zhōngwén.)

I am learning Chinese.

学 focuses on learning a subject; 正在 marks an ongoing process.

学生在学校学汉语。 (xuéshēng zài xuéxiào xué Hànyǔ.)

The students learn Chinese at school.

HSK entry 249
学习 (xuéxí)

study

verb · introduced

我每天学习汉语。 (wǒ měitiān xuéxí Hànyǔ.)

I study Chinese every day.

学习 refers to studying or learning; 每天 makes it a repeated practice.

学生们每天在这里学习。 (xuéshēngmen měitiān zài zhèlǐ xuéxí.)

The students study here every day.

HSK entry 290
中文 (Zhōngwén)

Chinese language

noun · introduced

我会说中文。 (wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén.)

I can speak Chinese.

中文 refers to the Chinese language or writing; 会说 indicates speaking ability.

这里有很多中文书。 (zhèlǐ yǒu hěn duō Zhōngwén shū.)

There are many Chinese-language books here.

HSK entry 296
字 (zì)

character; written word

noun · introduced

这个字怎么读？ (zhège zì zěnme dú?)

How is this character pronounced?

怎么读 asks how to pronounce or read this written character.

今天请写十个字。 (jīntiān qǐng xiě shí gè zì.)

Please write ten characters today.

Usage and grammar

Listening

The listening verb denotes the activity; the perception compound can express hearing.

我喜欢听歌。 (wǒ xǐhuan tīng gē.)

I like listening to songs.

听歌 means listen to songs or music; 听 takes sound as its object.

Writing

Use the writing verb with the thing written, such as characters.

我会写这个字。 (wǒ huì xiě zhège zì.)

I can write this character.

写 refers to writing a character; 会 indicates learned ability.
