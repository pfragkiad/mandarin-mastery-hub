Talking About People

Personal Pronouns and Who Is Who

1 · Conversation — Talking About People

A group of classmates identifies who is speaking, whom they address, and whom they refer to.

A

你们是学生吗？ (Nǐmen shì xuéshēng ma?)

Are you all students?

B

是，我们都是学生。 (Shì, wǒmen dōu shì xuéshēng.)

Yes, we are all students.

A

她也是学生吗？ (Tā yě shì xuéshēng ma?)

Is she a student too?

B

是，她是我同学。 (Shì, tā shì wǒ tóngxué.)

Yes, she is my classmate.

2 · How the language works

Person and number

我 (wǒ) and 我们 (wǒmen) represent the speaker and a group including the speaker. 你 (nǐ) and 你们 (nǐmen) address one or multiple people; 他们 (tāmen) refers to a group.

代词 + 是 + 身份
dàicí + shì + shēnfèn
<pronoun> + 是 + <identity>

他们是老师。 (Tāmen shì lǎoshī.)

They are teachers.

我们是朋友。 (Wǒmen shì péngyou.)

We are friends.

Written 他 (tā), 她 (tā), 它 (tā)

These are pronounced identically in standard Mandarin but use different written characters for typical male, female, and nonhuman referents. Do not invent pronunciation differences.

他/她/它 + 动词
tā/tā/tā + dòngcí
他/她/它 + <verb>

她在看书。 (Tā zài kàn shū.)

She is reading.

它是一只小猫。 (Tā shì yì zhī xiǎo māo.)

It is a small cat.

3 · Build new sentences

Change the pronoun while keeping the identity predicate.

Worked model

我是学生。 (Wǒ shì xuéshēng.)

I am a student.

Change 1: we
Reveal a model response

我们是学生。 (Wǒmen shì xuéshēng.)

We are students.

Change 2: they
Reveal a model response

他们是学生。 (Tāmen shì xuéshēng.)

They are students.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

我和姐姐都是学生，我们在一个学校。她喜欢读书，我喜欢写汉字。我们每天一起去学校。 (Wǒ hé jiějie dōu shì xuéshēng, wǒmen zài yí ge xuéxiào. Tā xǐhuan dúshū, wǒ xǐhuan xiě Hànzì. Wǒmen měi tiān yìqǐ qù xuéxiào.)

My older sister and I are both students at the same school. She likes reading, and I like writing Chinese characters. We go to school together every day.

1. Who does 我们 (wǒmen) refer to in this passage?
Show the answer

The speaker and their older sister.

2. Are they at the same school?
Show the answer

Yes.

6 · Watch out

他 (tā), 她 (tā) and 它 (tā) have the same pronunciation, so written gender/animacy distinctions must not be treated as different sounds.

Pronunciation clinic

Practise third-tone sandhi in 你好 (nǐ hǎo), typically pronounced approximately ní hǎo in connected speech, while lexical pinyin remains nǐ hǎo.
