Talking About People

Personal Pronouns and Who Is Who

reference-questions · people-identity · #pronouns · #personal-reference

Learning objectives

  • Identify first-, second- and third-person pronouns.
  • Distinguish singular and plural forms.
  • Recognize polite forms of address.

Overview

Refer to yourself and other people using Mandarin personal pronouns.

Identify first-, second- and third-person pronouns.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 26
大家 (dàjiā)

everyone

pronoun · introduced

大家都来了。 (dàjiā dōu lái le.)

Everyone has come.

大家 refers to everyone present or in the relevant group; 都 reinforces “all.”

大家下午两点来上课。 (dàjiā xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn lái shàngkè.)

Everyone comes to class at two in the afternoon.

A simple class schedule: 来上课 describes coming to attend a lesson.

HSK entry 146
你 (nǐ)

you

pronoun · introduced

你好，你是新学生吗？ (nǐ hǎo, nǐ shì xīn xuéshēng ma?)

Hello, are you a new student?

你可以在这里看书。 (nǐ kěyǐ zài zhèlǐ kàn shū.)

You can read books here.

HSK entry 148
你们 (nǐmen)

you (plural)

pronoun · introduced

你们今天上课吗？ (nǐmen jīntiān shàngkè ma?)

Do you all have class today?

你们 addresses two or more people; 吗 marks a yes–no question.

你们明天上午九点来。 (nǐmen míngtiān shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn lái.)

You all come tomorrow at nine in the morning.

HSK entry 150
您 (nín)

you (polite)

pronoun · introduced

您好，请问您叫什么名字？ (nín hǎo, qǐngwèn nín jiào shénme míngzi?)

Hello, may I ask your name?

您 is the respectful form of 你, useful when addressing an unfamiliar adult.

您好，请进。 (nín hǎo, qǐng jìn.)

Hello, please come in.

HSK entry 200
他 (tā)

he

pronoun · introduced

他是我的哥哥。 (tā shì wǒ de gēge.)

He is my older brother.

他 is the third-person pronoun for a male person; 哥哥 is older brother.

他每天坐火车上班。 (tā měitiān zuò huǒchē shàngbān.)

He takes the train to work every day.

HSK entry 201
它 (tā)

it

pronoun · introduced

它是一只猫。 (tā shì yì zhī māo.)

It is a cat.

它 generally refers to animals or things in writing; 只 counts the cat.

这只猫很小，它喜欢玩。 (zhè zhī māo hěn xiǎo, tā xǐhuan wán.)

This cat is very small; it likes playing.

HSK entry 202
她 (tā)

she

pronoun · introduced

她是我的姐姐。 (tā shì wǒ de jiějie.)

She is my older sister.

她 is the written third-person pronoun for a female person.

她会说中文，也会唱歌。 (tā huì shuō Zhōngwén, yě huì chàng gē.)

She can speak Chinese and can also sing.

HSK entry 203
他们 (tāmen)

they (male/mixed)

pronoun · introduced

他们在学校。 (tāmen zài xuéxiào.)

They are at school.

他们 is plural “they” for males or mixed groups in ordinary writing.

他们每天上午上课。 (tāmen měitiān shàngwǔ shàngkè.)

They have class every morning.

HSK entry 204
它们 (tāmen)

they (nonhuman)

pronoun · introduced

它们都是小猫。 (tāmen dōu shì xiǎomāo.)

They are all kittens.

它们 is the written plural form for animals or things, here kittens.

这里有三只猫，它们都很小。 (zhèlǐ yǒu sān zhī māo, tāmen dōu hěn xiǎo.)

There are three cats here; they are all small.

HSK entry 205
她们 (tāmen)

they (female)

pronoun · introduced

她们都是学生。 (tāmen dōu shì xuéshēng.)

They are all students.

她们 refers to a group of women or girls in written Chinese.

她们喜欢一起读书。 (tāmen xǐhuan yìqǐ dúshū.)

They [female] like reading together.

HSK entry 221
我 (wǒ)

I; me

pronoun · introduced

我喜欢汉语。 (wǒ xǐhuan Hànyǔ.)

I like Chinese.

我 is the first-person singular pronoun; the sentence states a personal preference.

我每天上午学习汉语。 (wǒ měitiān shàngwǔ xuéxí Hànyǔ.)

I study Chinese every morning.

HSK entry 222
我们 (wǒmen)

we

pronoun · introduced

我们一起学习。 (wǒmen yìqǐ xuéxí.)

We study together.

我们 is “we”; 一起 emphasizes acting as a group.

我们每天一起读书。 (wǒmen měitiān yìqǐ dúshū.)

We read together every day.

Usage and grammar

Direct address

Mandarin uses dedicated second-person pronouns and a polite form.

你好，你是新学生吗？ (nǐ hǎo, nǐ shì xīn xuéshēng ma?)

Hello, are you a new student?

Referring to yourself

Personal pronouns normally appear before the verb in a simple statement.

我喜欢汉语。 (wǒ xǐhuan Hànyǔ.)

I like Chinese.

我 is the first-person singular pronoun; the sentence states a personal preference.
