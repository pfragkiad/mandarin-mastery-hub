Talking About the Clock

Clock Times and Times of Day

1 · Conversation — Talking About the Clock

A classmate asks about a meeting time. Learn to distinguish a clock reading from a duration.

A

现在几点了？ (Xiànzài jǐ diǎn le?)

What time is it now?

B

上午九点半。 (Shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn bàn.)

Half past nine in the morning.

A

我们几点上课？ (Wǒmen jǐ diǎn shàngkè?)

What time does class start?

B

十点，还有半个小时。 (Shí diǎn, hái yǒu bàn ge xiǎoshí.)

At ten; there is another half hour.

2 · How the language works

Asking and stating clock time

几点 (jǐ diǎn) asks for a clock time; put the time before the action to say when something happens. 半 (bàn) gives half past.

time + subject/verb or subject + time + verb

我们下午三点见。 (Wǒmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn jiàn.)

Let’s meet at 3 p.m.

现在八点十五分。 (Xiànzài bā diǎn shíwǔ fēn.)

It is 8:15 now.

Time point versus duration

点 (diǎn) is a clock hour. 小时 (xiǎoshí) expresses hours of duration; 分钟 (fēnzhōng) expresses minutes of duration.

九点 (clock) ≠ 九个小时 (duration)

我九点上班。 (Wǒ jiǔ diǎn shàngbān.)

I start work at nine.

我学习两个小时。 (Wǒ xuéxí liǎng ge xiǎoshí.)

I study for two hours.

3 · Build new sentences

Change the meeting time in a complete invitation.

Worked model

我们三点见。 (Wǒmen sān diǎn jiàn.)

Let us meet at three.

Change 1: four
Reveal a model response

我们四点见。 (Wǒmen sì diǎn jiàn.)

Let us meet at four.

Change 2: half past five
Reveal a model response

我们五点半见。 (Wǒmen wǔ diǎn bàn jiàn.)

Let us meet at five thirty.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

妈妈下午两点下班，三点到家。她晚上七点吃晚饭。 (Māma xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn xiàbān, sān diǎn dào jiā. Tā wǎnshang qī diǎn chī wǎnfàn.)

Mom finishes work at 2 p.m. and arrives home at three. She eats dinner at 7 p.m.

1. At what time does Mom finish work?
Show the answer

2 p.m.

Show the answer

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Ask the current time and answer.
Show one possible response

现在几点？现在十点。 (Xiànzài jǐ diǎn? Xiànzài shí diǎn.)

What time is it? It is ten.

2. Say how many hours you study, not when you study.
Show one possible response

我学习两个小时。 (Wǒ xuéxí liǎng ge xiǎoshí.)

I study for two hours.

6 · Watch out

Do not confuse 三点 (sān diǎn, 3 o’clock) with 三个小时 (sān ge xiǎoshí, three hours).

Pronunciation clinic

Read 点 (diǎn) as third tone; in two third tones, 九点 (jiǔ diǎn) is pronounced with third-tone sandhi in connected speech.
