Talking About the Clock

Clock Times and Times of Day

time-calendar · daily-actions · #clock · #time-of-day · #schedule

Learning objectives

  • Tell the time with hours and minutes.
  • Recognize morning, afternoon and evening.
  • Describe when daily actions happen.

Overview

Say when activities happen, ask the time and identify parts of the day.

Tell the time with hours and minutes.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 5
白天 (báitiān)

daytime

noun · introduced

我白天上班。 (wǒ báitiān shàngbān.)

I work during the day.

白天 contrasts with 晚上 and places the work in daytime hours.

白天我在学校学习。 (báitiān wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí.)

I study at school during the day.

HSK entry 33
点 (diǎn)

o'clock; point

classifier · introduced

现在几点？ (xiànzài jǐ diǎn?)

What time is it now?

几点 asks for the hour, while 现在 anchors the question to the present.

下午三点下课。 (xiàwǔ sān diǎn xiàkè.)

Class ends at three in the afternoon.

Classifier and time unit in clock readings; also a noun for a point.

HSK entry 55
分 (fēn)

minute; divide

classifier · introduced

现在是八点十分。 (xiànzài shì bā diǎn shí fēn.)

It is 8:10 now.

八点十分 is 8:10, with 分 marking minutes after the hour.

下午三点二十分上课。 (xiàwǔ sān diǎn èr shí fēn shàngkè.)

Class starts at 3:20 p.m.

HSK 1 time measure word/minute marker in 八点十分; the official grammar syllabus groups 分 with time measure words. Also has separate verbal and nominal readings in other contexts.

HSK entry 56
分钟 (fēnzhōng)

minute

classifier · introduced

请等五分钟。 (qǐng děng wǔ fēnzhōng.)

Please wait five minutes.

请 makes the request courteous; 五分钟 is a five-minute duration.

请十分钟后再来。 (qǐng shí fēnzhōng hòu zài lái.)

Please come back in ten minutes.

Time measure word specifying duration, e.g. 十分钟 (“ten minutes”); unlike 分, it is not the minute marker of clock time.

HSK entry 178
上午 (shàngwǔ)

morning

noun · introduced

我上午有时间。 (wǒ shàngwǔ yǒu shíjiān.)

I have time in the morning.

有时间 means have time or be available during the period mentioned.

上午我们有汉语课。 (shàngwǔ wǒmen yǒu Hànyǔ kè.)

We have Chinese-language class in the morning.

HSK entry 185
时候 (shíhou)

time; moment

noun · introduced

你什么时候来？ (nǐ shénme shíhou lái?)

When will you come?

什么时候 asks when an event will happen, not merely for the clock time.

上课的时候不要说话。 (shàngkè de shíhou búyào shuōhuà.)

Please don't talk during class.

HSK entry 186
时间 (shíjiān)

time

noun · introduced

我现在没有时间。 (wǒ xiànzài méiyǒu shíjiān.)

I don't have time now.

没有时间 means lack time or be unavailable now.

上课时间是上午九点。 (shàngkè shíjiān shì shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn.)

Class starts at nine in the morning.

HSK entry 215
晚 (wǎn)

late

adjective · introduced

我今天来晚了。 (wǒ jīntiān lái wǎn le.)

I arrived late today.

来晚了 means arrive late; 晚 here is an adverbial description.

今天爸爸下班很晚。 (jīntiān bàba xiàbān hěn wǎn.)

Dad gets off work very late today.

HSK entry 217
晚上 (wǎnshang)

evening

noun · introduced

晚上我在家看书。 (wǎnshang wǒ zài jiā kàn shū.)

I read at home in the evening.

晚上 is evening or night; 在家 gives the location of reading.

晚上八点我们在家看电视。 (wǎnshang bā diǎn wǒmen zài jiā kàn diànshì.)

We watch TV at home at eight in the evening.

HSK entry 230
下午 (xiàwǔ)

afternoon

noun · introduced

我下午去商店。 (wǒ xiàwǔ qù shāngdiàn.)

I will go to the store this afternoon.

下午 is the period after noon; context can make the planned visit future.

下午的课三点开始。 (xiàwǔ de kè sān diǎn kāishǐ.)

The afternoon class starts at three.

HSK entry 232
现在 (xiànzài)

now

noun · introduced

现在是上午十点。 (xiànzài shì shàngwǔ shí diǎn.)

It is ten in the morning now.

上午十点 is 10 a.m.; 现在 gives the current time frame.

现在开始上课。 (xiànzài kāishǐ shàngkè.)

Class starts now.

Temporal noun meaning now, not a verb.

HSK entry 236
小时 (xiǎoshí)

hour

noun · introduced

我每天学习两个小时。 (wǒ měitiān xuéxí liǎng ge xiǎoshí.)

I study for two hours every day.

小时 expresses duration in hours; 两个小时 means two hours.

下午我们要上两个小时的课。 (xiàwǔ wǒmen yào shàng liǎng gè xiǎoshí de kè.)

We have two hours of class in the afternoon.

HSK entry 273
早 (zǎo)

early

adjective · introduced

我今天来得很早。 (wǒ jīntiān lái de hěn zǎo.)

I came very early today.

来得很早 uses 得 to describe the manner or timing of arriving.

他每天很早起床。 (tā měitiān hěn zǎo qǐchuáng.)

He gets up very early every day.

很早 means early; this avoids repeating 早 in the same sentence.

HSK entry 275
早上 (zǎoshang)

morning

noun · introduced

早上我喝茶。 (zǎoshang wǒ hē chá.)

I drink tea in the morning.

早上 specifies the morning period; 喝茶 is the activity.

我们每天早上八点上课。 (wǒmen měitiān zǎoshang bā diǎn shàngkè.)

We have class at eight every morning.

HSK entry 291
中午 (zhōngwǔ)

noon

noun · introduced

我们中午吃饭。 (wǒmen zhōngwǔ chī fàn.)

We have lunch at noon.

中午 means noon; 吃饭 can mean having lunch in this setting.

中午十二点到一点是午饭时间。 (zhōngwǔ shí èr diǎn dào yì diǎn shì wǔfàn shíjiān.)

Lunch is from twelve to one.

Usage and grammar

Stating a clock time

Clock expressions place the hour before the minute.

现在几点？ (xiànzài jǐ diǎn?)

What time is it now?

几点 asks for the hour, while 现在 anchors the question to the present.

Durations in minutes

A duration word differs from the minute marker inside a clock reading.

请等五分钟。 (qǐng děng wǔ fēnzhōng.)

Please wait five minutes.

请 makes the request courteous; 五分钟 is a five-minute duration.
