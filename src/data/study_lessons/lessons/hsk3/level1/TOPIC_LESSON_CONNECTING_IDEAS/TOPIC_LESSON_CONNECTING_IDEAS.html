Degree, Coordination, Repetition and Ongoing Action

Learning objectives

  • Express degree and emphasis.
  • Recognize coordination and repetition.
  • Understand ongoing actions in simple sentences.

Overview

Use common function words for emphasis, coordination, repetition and progressive actions.

Express degree and emphasis.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 41
都 (dōu)

all; both

adverb · introduced

我们都是学生。 (wǒmen dōu shì xuéshēng.)

We are all students.

都 applies to everyone in the subject group; omit it and the “all” nuance disappears.

学生们都在教室里。 (xuéshēngmen dōu zài jiàoshì lǐ.)

All the students are in the classroom.

HSK entry 53
非常 (fēicháng)

extremely

adverb · introduced

我今天非常高兴。 (wǒ jīntiān fēicháng gāoxìng.)

I am extremely happy today.

非常 intensifies 高兴 more strongly than the common degree word 很.

这件衣服非常漂亮。 (zhè jiàn yīfu fēicháng piàoliang.)

This piece of clothing is very pretty.

HSK entry 67
还 (hái)

still; also

adverb · introduced

他还在学校。 (tā hái zài xuéxiào.)

He is still at school.

还 means “still,” showing that the school situation continues.

这家店还没有开门。 (zhè jiā diàn hái méiyǒu kāimén.)

This shop hasn't opened yet.

HSK entry 78
和 (hé)

and; with

conjunction · introduced

我和你一起去。 (wǒ hé nǐ yìqǐ qù.)

I will go with you.

和 joins two people; 一起 clarifies that they go together.

我们和老师一起学习。 (wǒmen hé lǎoshī yìqǐ xuéxí.)

We study together with the teacher.

Connects nouns and nominal phrases as and; with is a prepositional use.

HSK entry 79
很 (hěn)

very

adverb · introduced

他很高兴。 (tā hěn gāoxìng.)

He is very happy.

很 often functions as a natural degree linker before adjectives, not an emphatic “very.”

这家商店很大。 (zhè jiā shāngdiàn hěn dà.)

This shop is very big.

HSK entry 206
太 (tài)

too; very

adverb · introduced

这本书太贵了。 (zhè běn shū tài guì le.)

This book is too expensive.

太…了 indicates an excessive degree, not simply a high price.

今天天气太热了。 (jīntiān tiānqì tài rè le.)

The weather is too hot today.

HSK entry 253
也 (yě)

also

adverb · introduced

我也喜欢茶。 (wǒ yě xǐhuan chá.)

I also like tea.

也 means also; it normally appears before the verb or adjective predicate.

学生也可以在这里看书。 (xuéshēng yě kěyǐ zài zhèlǐ kàn shū.)

Students can also read books here.

HSK entry 270
再 (zài)

again

adverb · introduced

请再说一次。 (qǐng zài shuō yí cì.)

Please say it again.

再 indicates repetition; 一次 is one occurrence, and 一 is pronounced yí before 次.

请明天再来。 (qǐng míngtiān zài lái.)

Please come back tomorrow.

HSK entry 271
在 (zài)

at; in

verb · introduced

我在家。 (wǒ zài jiā.)

I am at home.

在 + location states where the speaker is, not what they are doing.

我们在学校上课。 (wǒmen zài xuéxiào shàngkè.)

We have class at school.

Three common functions: verb of location (我在家), preposition/coverb introducing a place (我们在学校上课), and progressive adverb before a verb (我在看书). The first two are illustrated here; the primary lexicographic POS remains Verb.

HSK entry 285
真 (zhēn)

really

adverb · introduced

今天天气真好！ (jīntiān tiānqì zhēn hǎo!)

The weather is really nice today!

真 adds strong, spontaneous positive emphasis to 好.

这家饭店的菜真好吃！ (zhè jiā fàndiàn de cài zhēn hǎochī!)

The food at this restaurant is really delicious!

Degree adverb truly/really, sometimes adjectival.

HSK entry 286
正在 (zhèngzài)

currently; in progress

adverb · introduced

我正在看书。 (wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū.)

I am reading a book right now.

正在 makes the ongoing action explicit: reading right now.

学生们正在上课。 (xuéshēngmen zhèngzài shàngkè.)

The students are having class right now.

Usage and grammar

Doing something again

A repetition adverb can indicate a future or additional occurrence.

请再说一次。 (qǐng zài shuō yí cì.)

Please say it again.

再 indicates repetition; 一次 is one occurrence, and 一 is pronounced yí before 次.

Ongoing actions

A progressive marker before a verb highlights an action underway.

我正在看书。 (wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū.)

I am reading a book right now.

正在 makes the ongoing action explicit: reading right now.
