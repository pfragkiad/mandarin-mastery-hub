1 · Conversation — Connecting and Emphasizing Ideas

Two students compare their activities. Practise linkers, emphasis, and the difference between 在 (zài) and 正在 (zhèngzài).

A

你在看书吗？ (Nǐ zài kàn shū ma?)

Are you reading?

B

是，我也在听歌。 (Shì, wǒ yě zài tīng gē.)

Yes, and I am also listening to music.

A

你今天很忙吗？ (Nǐ jīntiān hěn máng ma?)

Are you busy today?

B

很忙，但是我还想看电影。 (Hěn máng, dànshì wǒ hái xiǎng kàn diànyǐng.)

Very busy, but I still want to watch a movie.

2 · How the language works

Adding and combining information

也 (yě) means “also” and usually goes before the verb. 都 (dōu) means “all/both” when applied to an established group; 和 (hé) connects nouns or noun phrases.

主语 + 也 + 动词; 一群人 + 都 + 动词
zhǔyǔ + yě + dòngcí; yì qún rén + dōu + dòngcí
<subject> + 也 + <verb>; <group> + 都 + <verb>

妹妹也喜欢喝茶。 (Mèimei yě xǐhuan hē chá.)

My younger sister also likes drinking tea.

我们都喜欢中文。 (Wǒmen dōu xǐhuan Zhōngwén.)

We all like Chinese.

Degree and ongoing actions

很 (hěn), 非常 (fēicháng), 真 (zhēn) and 太 (tài) modify degree with different nuance. 正在 (zhèngzài) explicitly signals an activity in progress.

主语 + 正在 + 动词
zhǔyǔ + zhèngzài + dòngcí
<subject> + 正在 + <verb>

她正在做饭。 (Tā zhèngzài zuò fàn.)

She is cooking right now.

今天真热！ (Jīntiān zhēn rè!)

It is really hot today!

3 · Build new sentences

Use 也 to add another person doing the activity.

Worked model

我看书。她也看书。 (Wǒ kàn shū. Tā yě kàn shū.)

I read. She reads too.

Change 1: studying
Reveal a model response

我学习。他也学习。 (Wǒ xuéxí. Tā yě xuéxí.)

I study. He studies too.

Change 2: drinking water
Reveal a model response

我喝水。你也喝水。 (Wǒ hē shuǐ. Nǐ yě hē shuǐ.)

I drink water. You drink water too.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

哥哥和妹妹都在家。哥哥正在看书，妹妹在写字。他们也听歌。 (Gēge hé mèimei dōu zài jiā. Gēge zhèngzài kàn shū, mèimei zài xiě zì. Tāmen yě tīng gē.)

The older brother and younger sister are both at home. He is reading while she is writing. They also listen to songs.

1. Where are the brother and sister?
Show the answer

Both are at home.

2. Who is writing?
Show the answer

The younger sister.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Explain what you and a friend both like.
Show one possible response

我们都喜欢看电影。 (Wǒmen dōu xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.)

We both like watching movies.

2. Describe what someone is doing right now.
Show one possible response

他正在写字。 (Tā zhèngzài xiě zì.)

He is writing characters right now.

6 · Watch out

和 (hé) normally joins nouns, not two independent clauses in the way English “and” does. Use a comma or appropriate connector instead.

Pronunciation clinic

Distinguish 在 (zài) as a location preposition from 在 (zài) as an aspect marker; word order supplies the interpretation.
