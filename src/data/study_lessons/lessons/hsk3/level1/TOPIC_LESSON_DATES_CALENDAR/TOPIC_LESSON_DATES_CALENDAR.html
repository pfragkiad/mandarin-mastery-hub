Overview

Use day, month, year and week words.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 76
号 (hào)

number; date

classifier · introduced

今天是十月十四号。 (jīntiān shì shí yuè shísì hào.)

Today is October 14.

号 is a common spoken way to state a day of the month; 月 identifies the month.

十月五号是星期一。 (shí yuè wǔ hào shì xīngqīyī.)

October 5 is a Monday.

HSK entry 93
今年 (jīnnián)

this year

noun · introduced

我今年在学汉语。 (wǒ jīnnián zài xué Hànyǔ.)

I'm studying Chinese this year.

今年 sets the time frame; 在学 marks learning in progress, rather than a finished action.

今年学校有很多新学生。 (jīnnián xuéxiào yǒu hěn duō xīn xuéshēng.)

There are many new students at the school this year.

HSK entry 94
今天 (jīntiān)

today

noun · introduced

今天我有汉语课。 (jīntiān wǒ yǒu Hànyǔ kè.)

I have Chinese class today.

今天 marks the current day; 汉语课 means Chinese-language class.

今天下午我们不上课。 (jīntiān xiàwǔ wǒmen bú shàngkè.)

We don't have class this afternoon.

HSK entry 128
明年 (míngnián)

next year

noun · introduced

明年我去中国。 (míngnián wǒ qù Zhōngguó.)

Next year I will go to China.

明年 places the trip next year; Chinese verbs do not change for future tense.

明年学校有新的汉语课。 (míngnián xuéxiào yǒu xīn de Hànyǔ kè.)

The school will have new Chinese-language classes next year.

HSK entry 129
明天 (míngtiān)

tomorrow

noun · introduced

明天我们去学校。 (míngtiān wǒmen qù xuéxiào.)

Tomorrow we are going to school.

明天 sets the time for a planned future action.

明天上午八点开始上课。 (míngtiān shàngwǔ bā diǎn kāishǐ shàngkè.)

Class starts at eight tomorrow morning.

HSK entry 149
年 (nián)

year

noun · introduced

一年有十二个月。 (yì nián yǒu shí'èr ge yuè.)

There are twelve months in a year.

年 is a unit for a year; twelve months make one year.

我们在这里住了两年。 (wǒmen zài zhèlǐ zhù le liǎng nián.)

We have lived here for two years.

HSK entry 168
去年 (qùnián)

last year

noun · introduced

去年我在中国。 (qùnián wǒ zài Zhōngguó.)

Last year I was in China.

去年 is last year; the sentence reports a past location without verb inflection.

去年我在这个学校学习。 (qùnián wǒ zài zhège xuéxiào xuéxí.)

I studied at this school last year.

HSK entry 172
日 (rì)

day; sun

classifier · introduced

今天是十月十四日。 (jīntiān shì shí yuè shísì rì.)

Today is October 14.

日 is used for the day of a month in more formal or written dates; 号 is common in speech.

今天是五月一日。 (jīntiān shì wǔ yuè yī rì.)

Today is May 1.

Day/date word; also a measure word for dates.

HSK entry 199
岁 (suì)

years old

classifier · introduced

我今年二十岁。 (wǒ jīnnián èrshí suì.)

I am twenty years old this year.

岁 expresses age; no 是 is used before the age number.

这个孩子今年八岁。 (zhège háizi jīnnián bā suì.)

This child is eight years old this year.

Measure word for age.

HSK entry 207
天 (tiān)

day; sky

noun · introduced

我在这里住了三天。 (wǒ zài zhèlǐ zhù le sān tiān.)

I stayed here for three days.

这个星期有三天不上课。 (zhège xīngqī yǒu sān tiān bú shàngkè.)

We don't have class on three days this week.

Day as a time noun; also a measure word for duration.

HSK entry 243
星期 (xīngqī)

week

noun · introduced

今天是星期三。 (jīntiān shì xīngqī sān.)

Today is Wednesday.

星期三 is Wednesday; weekdays are numbered from Monday as one.

这个星期有三天汉语课。 (zhège xīngqī yǒu sān tiān Hànyǔ kè.)

We have Chinese classes on three days this week.

HSK entry 244
星期日 (xīngqīrì)

Sunday

noun · introduced

星期日我休息。 (xīngqīrì wǒ xiūxi.)

I rest on Sunday.

星期日 is a standard way to say Sunday; 星期天 has the same meaning.

学校星期日不上课。 (xuéxiào xīngqīrì bú shàngkè.)

The school has no classes on Sundays.

HSK entry 245
星期天 (xīngqītiān)

Sunday

noun · introduced

星期天我们去超市。 (xīngqītiān wǒmen qù chāoshì.)

We're going to the supermarket on Sunday.

这个星期天我们去看电影。 (zhège xīngqītiān wǒmen qù kàn diànyǐng.)

We are going to watch a movie this Sunday.

HSK entry 269
月 (yuè)

month; moon

noun · introduced

下个月我去中国。 (xià gè yuè wǒ qù Zhōngguó.)

I will go to China next month.

下个月 means next month; no future-tense inflection is needed.

明年五月我们去中国。 (míngnián wǔ yuè wǒmen qù Zhōngguó.)

We are going to China next May.

HSK entry 297
昨天 (zuótiān)

yesterday

noun · introduced

昨天下雨了。 (zuótiān xià yǔ le.)

It rained yesterday.

昨天 places a completed rain event on the previous day.

昨天上午我在学校上课。 (zuótiān shàngwǔ wǒ zài xuéxiào shàngkè.)

I had class at school yesterday morning.

Usage and grammar

Naming months

The month follows the number in a date expression.

下个月我去中国。 (xià gè yuè wǒ qù Zhōngguó.)

I will go to China next month.

下个月 means next month; no future-tense inflection is needed.

Reference years

Calendar expressions distinguish this, last and next year.

我今年在学汉语。 (wǒ jīnnián zài xué Hànyǔ.)

I'm studying Chinese this year.

今年 sets the time frame; 在学 marks learning in progress, rather than a finished action.
