Learning objectives

  • Identify foods, drinks and meals.

  • Talk about meals and restaurants.

Overview

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 8
包子 (bāozi)

steamed bun

noun · introduced

我早上吃包子。 (wǒ zǎoshang chī bāozi.)

I eat steamed buns in the morning.

包子 is a filled steamed bun; 早上 sets the morning time frame.

这家店的包子很好吃。 (zhè jiā diàn de bāozi hěn hǎochī.)

The steamed buns at this shop are delicious.

HSK entry 16
菜 (cài)

vegetable; dish

noun · introduced

这个菜很好吃。 (zhège cài hěn hǎochī.)

This dish is delicious.

菜 usually refers to a prepared dish here, and 好吃 describes how it tastes.

饭店里有很多菜。 (fàndiàn lǐ yǒu hěn duō cài.)

There are many dishes at the restaurant.

HSK entry 17
茶 (chá)

tea

noun · introduced

我喜欢喝茶。 (wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.)

I like drinking tea.

喜欢 + verb expresses an enjoyable activity; 喝茶 is “drink tea.”

这家店卖茶。 (zhè jiā diàn mài chá.)

This shop sells tea.

HSK entry 21
吃 (chī)

eat

verb · introduced

我们一起吃饭。 (wǒmen yìqǐ chī fàn.)

We eat together.

一起 emphasizes a shared activity; 吃饭 can mean having a meal in general.

请在十二点吃午饭。 (qǐng zài shí èr diǎn chī wǔfàn.)

Please have lunch at twelve o'clock.

HSK entry 50
饭 (fàn)

meal; cooked rice

noun · introduced

今天的饭很好吃。 (jīntiān de fàn hěn hǎochī.)

Today’s meal tastes delicious.

饭 can mean the whole cooked meal here, not just rice; 好吃 evaluates its taste.

家人一起吃晚饭。 (jiārén yìqǐ chī wǎnfàn.)

The family eats dinner together.

HSK entry 51
饭店 (fàndiàn)

restaurant

noun · introduced

这家饭店的菜很好吃。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn de cài hěn hǎochī.)

The food at this restaurant is delicious.

家 is a classifier for restaurants, and the praise is specifically about the food served there.

这家饭店每天早上七点开门。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn měitiān zǎoshang qī diǎn kāimén.)

This restaurant opens at seven every morning.

HSK entry 72
好吃 (hǎochī)

tasty

adjective · introduced

这个苹果很好吃。 (zhège píngguǒ hěn hǎochī.)

This apple tastes good.

好吃 evaluates the taste of food, not its appearance.

这家饭店的面条儿很好吃。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn de miàntiáor hěn hǎochī.)

The noodles at this restaurant are delicious.

HSK entry 77
喝 (hē)

drink

verb · introduced

我想喝水。 (wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.)

I want to drink water.

想 + verb expresses a wish; 想喝 is wanting to drink.

学生们每天喝水。 (xuéshēngmen měitiān hē shuǐ.)

The students drink water every day.

HSK entry 84
鸡蛋 (jīdàn)

egg

noun · introduced

我早饭吃鸡蛋。 (wǒ zǎofàn chī jīdàn.)

I eat eggs for breakfast.

早饭 establishes the meal; 吃鸡蛋 names the food eaten.

今天的早饭有鸡蛋。 (jīntiān de zǎofàn yǒu jīdàn.)

There are eggs for breakfast today.

HSK entry 90
饺子 (jiǎozi)

dumplings

noun · introduced

我喜欢吃饺子。 (wǒ xǐhuan chī jiǎozi.)

I like eating dumplings.

饺子 are dumplings; 喜欢吃 specifies enjoyment of eating them.

这家饭店卖饺子。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn mài jiǎozi.)

This restaurant sells dumplings.

HSK entry 125
米饭 (mǐfàn)

cooked rice

noun · introduced

我中午吃米饭。 (wǒ zhōngwǔ chī mǐfàn.)

I eat rice at noon.

米饭 usually means cooked rice, not raw rice.

这家饭店中午有米饭。 (zhè jiā fàndiàn zhōngwǔ yǒu mǐfàn.)

This restaurant serves rice at lunchtime.

HSK entry 126
面包 (miànbāo)

bread

noun · introduced

早饭我吃面包。 (zǎofàn wǒ chī miànbāo.)

I eat bread for breakfast.

面包 means bread; 早饭 places the action at breakfast.

早饭有面包和牛奶。 (zǎofàn yǒu miànbāo hé niúnǎi.)

There is bread and milk for breakfast.

HSK entry 127
面条儿 (miàntiáor)

noodles

noun · introduced

我想吃面条儿。 (wǒ xiǎng chī miàntiáor.)

I want to eat noodles.

面条儿 contains a northern 儿 ending; 面条 is also widely used.

这家店的面条儿很好吃。 (zhè jiā diàn de miàntiáor hěn hǎochī.)

The noodles at this shop are delicious.

HSK entry 151
牛奶 (niúnǎi)

milk

noun · introduced

我每天喝牛奶。 (wǒ měitiān hē niúnǎi.)

I drink milk every day.

每天 marks a daily routine; 喝牛奶 is a common verb-object phrase.

早饭有牛奶和面包。 (zǎofàn yǒu niúnǎi hé miànbāo.)

There is milk and bread for breakfast.

HSK entry 159
苹果 (píngguǒ)

apple

noun · introduced

我每天吃一个苹果。 (wǒ měitiān chī yí ge píngguǒ.)

I eat an apple every day.

一个苹果 counts one apple; 每天 makes the action habitual.

这家店的苹果很便宜。 (zhè jiā diàn de píngguǒ hěn piányi.)

The apples at this shop are very cheap.

HSK entry 192
水 (shuǐ)

water

noun · introduced

我每天喝很多水。 (wǒ měitiān hē hěn duō shuǐ.)

I drink plenty of water every day.

水 means water; 喝很多水 expresses drinking plenty of water regularly.

今天很热，请多喝水。 (jīntiān hěn rè, qǐng duō hē shuǐ.)

It's very hot today; please drink plenty of water.

HSK entry 193
水果 (shuǐguǒ)

fruit

noun · introduced

我喜欢吃水果。 (wǒ xǐhuan chī shuǐguǒ.)

I like eating fruit.

水果 refers to fruit as a food category, not necessarily a single piece.

这家超市卖很多水果。 (zhè jiā chāoshì mài hěn duō shuǐguǒ.)

This supermarket sells lots of fruit.

HSK entry 216
晚饭 (wǎnfàn)

dinner

noun · introduced

我们七点吃晚饭。 (wǒmen qī diǎn chī wǎnfàn.)

We eat dinner at seven.

晚饭 is the evening meal; 七点 gives the meal time.

晚饭六点半开始。 (wǎnfàn liù diǎn bàn kāishǐ.)

Dinner starts at six thirty.

HSK entry 224
午饭 (wǔfàn)

lunch

noun · introduced

我们十二点吃午饭。 (wǒmen shí'èr diǎn chī wǔfàn.)

We eat lunch at twelve.

午饭 means lunch, usually eaten around the middle of the day.

学生中午十二点吃午饭。 (xuéshēng zhōngwǔ shí èr diǎn chī wǔfàn.)

The students eat lunch at twelve noon.

HSK entry 274
早饭 (zǎofàn)

breakfast

noun · introduced

我七点吃早饭。 (wǒ qī diǎn chī zǎofàn.)

I eat breakfast at seven.

吃早饭 means have breakfast, and 七点 gives the time.

今天的早饭有包子和鸡蛋。 (jīntiān de zǎofàn yǒu bāozi hé jīdàn.)

There are steamed buns and eggs for breakfast today.

HSK entry 300
做饭 (zuò fàn)

cook

verb · introduced

我爸爸在做饭。 (wǒ bàba zài zuò fàn.)

My dad is cooking.

做饭 means prepare/cook a meal, not just eat rice.

妈妈每天晚上做饭。 (māma měitiān wǎnshang zuò fàn.)

Mom cooks every evening.

Usage and grammar

Having a meal

The eating verb appears in ordinary descriptions of meals.

我们一起吃饭。 (wǒmen yìqǐ chī fàn.)

We eat together.

一起 emphasizes a shared activity; 吃饭 can mean having a meal in general.

Describing food

A common adjective evaluates whether food tastes good.

这个苹果很好吃。 (zhège píngguǒ hěn hǎochī.)

This apple tastes good.

好吃 evaluates the taste of food, not its appearance.
