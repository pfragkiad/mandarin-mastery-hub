Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 12
病 (bìng)

illness

noun · introduced

他生病了。 (tā shēngbìng le.)

He is ill.

生病 is the ordinary way to say someone is ill; 有病 can sound judgmental in conversation.

他的病好了。 (tā de bìng hǎo le.)

His illness has gotten better.

Illness as a noun; also occurs in verbal uses.

HSK entry 100
看病 (kànbìng)

see a doctor

verb · introduced

我生病了，要去看病。 (wǒ shēngbìng le, yào qù kànbìng.)

I am sick and need to see a doctor.

看病 means consult a doctor or seek medical care, not merely “look at illness.”

医生上午九点开始看病。 (yīshēng shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn kāishǐ kànbìng.)

The doctor starts seeing patients at nine in the morning.

HSK entry 104
口 (kǒu)

mouth

noun · introduced

我喝了一口水。 (wǒ hē le yì kǒu shuǐ.)

I took a sip of water.

口 acts as a measure word for a sip or mouthful, not the noun “mouth” here.

他喝了三口水。 (tā hē le sān kǒu shuǐ.)

He took three sips of water.

Noun for mouth; also classifier in expressions such as population count.

HSK entry 161
起床 (qǐchuáng)

get up

verb · introduced

我早上七点起床。 (wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.)

I get up at seven in the morning.

起床 refers to getting out of bed; 七点 gives the hour.

起床后，我喝一点儿水。 (qǐchuáng hòu, wǒ hē yìdiǎnr shuǐ.)

After getting up, I drink a little water.

HSK entry 183
生病 (shēngbìng)

fall ill

verb · introduced

我昨天生病了。 (wǒ zuótiān shēngbìng le.)

I became ill yesterday.

生病了 describes a change to an ill state; 昨天 puts it in the past.

她昨天生病，没有上课。 (tā zuótiān shēngbìng, méiyǒu shàngkè.)

She was sick yesterday and didn't go to class.

HSK entry 194
睡 (shuì)

sleep

verb · introduced

我每天睡八个小时。 (wǒ měitiān shuì bā gè xiǎoshí.)

I sleep eight hours every day.

每天 expresses habitual frequency and 八个小时 expresses duration of sleep.

妹妹每天晚上九点睡。 (mèimei měitiān wǎnshang jiǔ diǎn shuì.)

My younger sister goes to sleep at nine every night.

HSK entry 195
睡觉 (shuìjiào)

go to sleep

verb · introduced

我十点睡觉。 (wǒ shí diǎn shuìjiào.)

I go to bed at ten.

睡觉 can mean sleep or go to bed, depending on the situation.

小朋友晚上九点睡觉。 (xiǎopéngyou wǎnshang jiǔ diǎn shuìjiào.)

Young children go to bed at nine at night.

HSK entry 246
休息 (xiūxi)

rest

verb · introduced

我今天想休息。 (wǒ jīntiān xiǎng xiūxi.)

I want to rest today.

休息 means rest or take a break, not necessarily go to sleep.

学生下课后可以休息。 (xuéshēng xiàkè hòu kěyǐ xiūxi.)

The students can rest after class.

HSK entry 256
医生 (yīshēng)

doctor

noun · introduced

医生在医院工作。 (yīshēng zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.)

Doctors work in hospitals.

医生 is a doctor; 工作 describes their profession’s activity.

这家医院有很多医生。 (zhè jiā yīyuàn yǒu hěn duō yīshēng.)

This hospital has many doctors.

HSK entry 257
医院 (yīyuàn)

hospital

noun · introduced

她今天去医院。 (tā jīntiān qù yīyuàn.)

She goes to the hospital today.

医院 means hospital; 去 expresses going to that destination.

医院上午九点开始看病。 (yīyuàn shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn kāishǐ kànbìng.)

The hospital starts seeing patients at nine in the morning.

Usage and grammar

Becoming ill

A common compound describes being sick.

我昨天生病了。 (wǒ zuótiān shēngbìng le.)

I became ill yesterday.

生病了 describes a change to an ill state; 昨天 puts it in the past.

Taking a rest

A rest verb can be used independently for everyday needs.

我今天想休息。 (wǒ jīntiān xiǎng xiūxi.)

I want to rest today.

休息 means rest or take a break, not necessarily go to sleep.
