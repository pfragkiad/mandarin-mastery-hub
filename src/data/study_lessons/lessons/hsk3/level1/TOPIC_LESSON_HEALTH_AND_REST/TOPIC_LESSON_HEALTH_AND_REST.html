1 · Conversation — Health and Rest

A student is unwell, explains symptoms simply, and asks for time to rest.

A

你今天怎么了？ (Nǐ jīntiān zěnme le?)

What is wrong today?

B

我生病了，想休息。 (Wǒ shēngbìng le, xiǎng xiūxi.)

I am sick and want to rest.

A

你去医院看病吗？ (Nǐ qù yīyuàn kànbìng ma?)

Are you going to the hospital to see a doctor?

B

去，我下午去。 (Qù, wǒ xiàwǔ qù.)

Yes, I am going this afternoon.

2 · How the language works

A condition versus getting medical attention

生病 (shēngbìng) describes becoming or being ill. 看病 (kànbìng) refers to consulting a doctor about illness; 医生 (yīshēng) is the person, 医院 (yīyuàn) the place.

主语 + 生病了; 主语 + 去 + 医院 + 看病
zhǔyǔ + shēngbìng le; zhǔyǔ + qù + yīyuàn + kànbìng
<subject> + 生病了; <subject> + 去 + 医院 + 看病

弟弟生病了。 (Dìdi shēngbìng le.)

My younger brother is ill.

妈妈去看病。 (Māma qù kànbìng.)

Mom is going to see a doctor.

Rest and sleep

休息 (xiūxi) means to rest; 睡觉 (shuìjiào) specifically means to sleep. 起床 (qǐchuáng) means getting out of bed.

时间 + 动词 + 了
shíjiān + dòngcí + le
<time> + <verb> + 了

我晚上十点睡觉。 (Wǒ wǎnshang shí diǎn shuìjiào.)

I go to sleep at ten at night.

他早上七点起床。 (Tā zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.)

He gets up at seven in the morning.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

昨天我很忙，晚上睡得很晚。今天早上我不想起床，想在家休息。 (Zuótiān wǒ hěn máng, wǎnshang shuì de hěn wǎn. Jīntiān zǎoshang wǒ bù xiǎng qǐchuáng, xiǎng zài jiā xiūxi.)

I was very busy yesterday and slept late at night. This morning I do not want to get up; I want to rest at home.

1. Why does the speaker want to rest?
Show the answer

They were very busy and went to sleep late.

2. Where does the speaker want to rest?
Show the answer

At home.

6 · Watch out

休息 (xiūxi) does not necessarily mean sleeping. To say “sleep,” use 睡觉 (shuìjiào).

Pronunciation clinic

休息 (xiūxi) has a neutral second syllable.
