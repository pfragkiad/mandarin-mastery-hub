Asking and Pointing

Question Words, This and That

reference-questions · places-directions · #question-words · #demonstratives · #pointing

Learning objectives

  • Ask basic information questions.
  • Use this/that and here/there naturally.
  • Choose a demonstrative expression for singular or plural things.

Overview

Ask who, what, where and how, and identify people and objects as here or there.

Ask basic information questions.

Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 47
多少 (duōshao)

how many; how much

pronoun · introduced

这个多少钱？ (zhège duōshao qián?)

How much does this cost?

多少钱 is the routine shopping question for a price.

这个班有多少学生？ (zhège bān yǒu duōshao xuéshēng?)

How many students are in this class?

Interrogative pronoun asking how many or how much (多少钱、多少人). It is not itself a classifier and can directly precede a noun.

HSK entry 131
哪 (nǎ)

which

pronoun · introduced

你喜欢哪本书？ (nǐ xǐhuan nǎ běn shū?)

Which book do you like?

哪 selects one among known choices; 本 is the measure word for books.

你想读哪本书？ (nǐ xiǎng dú nǎ běn shū?)

Which book do you want to read?

HSK entry 132
哪个 (nǎge)

which one

pronoun · introduced

你想要哪个？ (nǐ xiǎng yào nǎge?)

Which one do you want?

哪个 asks which one; 个 stands for the classifier of the implied object.

哪个学生今天没有来？ (nǎge xuéshēng jīntiān méiyǒu lái?)

Which student didn't come today?

HSK entry 133
哪里 (nǎlǐ)

where

pronoun · introduced

学校在哪里？ (xuéxiào zài nǎlǐ?)

Where is the school?

哪里 asks where an institution or object is located.

“请问，书店在哪里？”她问。 (“qǐngwèn, shūdiàn zài nǎlǐ?” tā wèn.)

"Excuse me, where is the bookstore?" she asked.

Written dialogue: 哪里 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

HSK entry 134
哪儿 (nǎr)

where

pronoun · introduced

你去哪儿？ (nǐ qù nǎr?)

Where are you going?

哪儿 means where, with the 儿 pronunciation typical of northern Mandarin.

“学校在哪儿？”他问。 (“xuéxiào zài nǎr?” tā wèn.)

"Where is the school?" he asked.

Written dialogue: 哪儿 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

HSK entry 135
哪些 (nǎxiē)

which ones

pronoun · introduced

你喜欢哪些书？ (nǐ xǐhuan nǎxiē shū?)

Which books do you like?

哪些 means which ones when choosing several items.

哪些学生会说汉语？ (nǎxiē xuéshēng huì shuō Hànyǔ?)

Which students can speak Chinese?

HSK entry 136
那 (nà)

that

pronoun · introduced

那是我的朋友。 (nà shì wǒ de péngyou.)

That is my friend.

那 points to something away from the speaker, in contrast to 这.

那是学校的车。 (nà shì xuéxiào de chē.)

That is the school's car.

Demonstrative pronoun; can also have discourse-connecting uses.

HSK entry 137
那边 (nàbiān)

over there

pronoun · introduced

商店在那边。 (shāngdiàn zài nàbiān.)

The store is over there.

那边 denotes a place over there, away from the speaker.

那边有一家饭店。 (nàbiān yǒu yì jiā fàndiàn.)

There is a restaurant over there.

HSK entry 138
那个 (nàge)

that one

pronoun · introduced

那个苹果很大。 (nàge píngguǒ hěn dà.)

That apple is big.

那个 identifies a particular item; 个 is its classifier.

那个学生会说汉语。 (nàge xuéshēng huì shuō Hànyǔ.)

That student can speak Chinese.

HSK entry 139
那里 (nàlǐ)

there

pronoun · introduced

我的学校在那里。 (wǒ de xuéxiào zài nàlǐ.)

My school is there.

那里 means “there” and is a full location word.

那里有很多学生。 (nàlǐ yǒu hěn duō xuéshēng.)

There are many students there.

HSK entry 140
那儿 (nàr)

there

pronoun · introduced

你去那儿吗？ (nǐ qù nàr ma?)

Are you going there?

那儿 is a northern-style alternative to 那里 for “there.”

请在那儿等老师。 (qǐng zài nàr děng lǎoshī.)

Please wait for the teacher there.

HSK entry 141
那些 (nàxiē)

those

pronoun · introduced

那些书是我的。 (nàxiē shū shì wǒ de.)

Those books are mine.

那些 points to several items away from the speaker.

那些学生都在教室里。 (nàxiē xuéshēng dōu zài jiàoshì lǐ.)

Those students are all in the classroom.

HSK entry 181
谁 (shéi / shuí)

who

pronoun · introduced

那个人是谁？ (nàge rén shì shéi?)

Who is that person?

谁 asks for a person’s identity; shéi is used here, while shuí is an accepted alternative.

“这是谁的书？”老师问。 (“zhè shì shéi de shū?” lǎoshī wèn.)

"Whose book is this?" the teacher asked.

Written dialogue: 谁 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

HSK entry 182
什么 (shénme)

what

pronoun · introduced

你想吃什么？ (nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?)

What do you want to eat?

什么 asks what food the listener wants; it is a general interrogative pronoun.

“你想喝什么？”她问。 (“nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?” tā wèn.)

"What do you want to drink?" she asked.

Written dialogue: 什么 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

HSK entry 276
怎么 (zěnme)

how

pronoun · introduced

这个字怎么写？ (zhège zì zěnme xiě?)

How do you write this character?

怎么写 asks how to write a particular Chinese character.

这个问题怎么做？ (zhège wèntí zěnme zuò?)

How do you do this question?

Interrogative pro-form meaning how, often adverbial in sentences.

HSK entry 277
怎么样 (zěnmeyàng)

how is it; how about

pronoun · introduced

你觉得怎么样？ (nǐ juéde zěnmeyàng?)

What do you think?

你觉得怎么样 asks for someone’s impression or opinion.

这个饭店怎么样？ (zhège fàndiàn zěnmeyàng?)

What do you think of this restaurant?

Interrogative pro-form meaning how/how about; often adverbial in sentences.

HSK entry 279
这 (zhè)

this

pronoun · introduced

这是什么？ (zhè shì shénme?)

What is this?

这 means this; 这是什么 is a standard way to ask what something is.

这本书是新的。 (zhè běn shū shì xīn de.)

This book is new.

HSK entry 280
这边 (zhèbiān)

this side

pronoun · introduced

请到这边来。 (qǐng dào zhèbiān lái.)

Please come this way.

请到这边来 invites someone to come over to this side.

学校在这边，医院在那边。 (xuéxiào zài zhèbiān, yīyuàn zài nàbiān.)

The school is on this side, and the hospital is over there.

HSK entry 281
这个 (zhège)

this one

pronoun · introduced

这个很好吃。 (zhège hěn hǎochī.)

This one is delicious.

这个 means “this one,” with the noun inferred from context.

这个学生会说中文。 (zhège xuéshēng huì shuō Zhōngwén.)

This student can speak Chinese.

HSK entry 282
这里 (zhèlǐ)

here

pronoun · introduced

我住在这里。 (wǒ zhù zài zhèlǐ.)

I live here.

这里 is a full location word meaning “here.”

这里可以喝茶，也可以看书。 (zhèlǐ kěyǐ hē chá, yě kěyǐ kàn shū.)

You can drink tea and read books here.

HSK entry 283
这儿 (zhèr)

here

pronoun · introduced

请来这儿。 (qǐng lái zhèr.)

Please come here.

这儿 is a northern-style spoken alternative to 这里.

这儿有一家书店。 (zhèr yǒu yì jiā shūdiàn.)

There is a bookstore here.

HSK entry 284
这些 (zhèxiē)

these

pronoun · introduced

这些苹果很好吃。 (zhèxiē píngguǒ hěn hǎochī.)

These apples are delicious.

这些 means these plural items, here the apples.

这些学生都在学汉语。 (zhèxiē xuéshēng dōu zài xué Hànyǔ.)

These students are all learning Chinese.

Usage and grammar

Asking what

Question words generally stay in the position of the information being requested.

你想吃什么？ (nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?)

What do you want to eat?

什么 asks what food the listener wants; it is a general interrogative pronoun.

Pointing nearby

Demonstratives identify nearby or distant people, objects and places.

这是什么？ (zhè shì shénme?)

What is this?

这 means this; 这是什么 is a standard way to ask what something is.
