# Asking and Pointing

**Subtopics:** position-direction

Ask who, what, where and how, and identify people and objects as here or there.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask basic information questions.

- Use this/that and here/there naturally.

- Choose a demonstrative expression for singular or plural things.

## 1. Conversation — Asking and Pointing

At a bookstore, one friend asks which object and where; the other uses near/far demonstratives.

**A:** 你要哪本书？ (Nǐ yào nǎ běn shū?) — Which book do you want?

**B:** 我要这本。 (Wǒ yào zhè běn.) — I want this one.

**A:** 那本呢？ (Nà běn ne?) — What about that one?

**B:** 那本太贵了。 (Nà běn tài guì le.) — That one is too expensive.

## 2. How the language works

### This versus that

这 (zhè) points to something closer in the discourse or situation; 那 (nà) points to something more distant. Add a classifier when identifying a specific item.

**Pattern**

这/那 + 量词 + 名词  
*zhè/nà + liàngcí + míngcí*  
**这**/**那** + &lt;classifier&gt; + &lt;noun&gt;

**这个苹果很好吃。**
*Zhège píngguǒ hěn hǎochī.*
This apple is tasty.

**那件衣服很漂亮。**
*Nà jiàn yīfu hěn piàoliang.*
That item of clothing is pretty.

### Choose the right question word

什么 (shénme) asks “what”; 谁 (shéi) asks “who”; 哪 (nǎ) asks “which”; 哪里 (nǎlǐ) asks “where”; 怎么 (zěnme) asks “how”.

**Pattern**

谁 + 动词？ / 在 + 哪里？  
*shéi + dòngcí? / zài + nǎlǐ?*  
**谁** + &lt;verb&gt;？ / **在** + **哪里**？

**谁是你的老师？**
*Shéi shì nǐ de lǎoshī?*
Who is your teacher?

**你住在哪里？**
*Nǐ zhù zài nǎlǐ?*
Where do you live?

## 3. Build new sentences

Move from a general what-question to a which-question.

**Worked model:** **你想买什么？**
*Nǐ xiǎng mǎi shénme?*
What do you want to buy?

**Change 1: which book**

*Model response:* **你想买哪本书？**
*Nǐ xiǎng mǎi nǎ běn shū?*
Which book do you want to buy?

**Change 2: which clothes**

*Model response:* **你想买哪件衣服？**
*Nǐ xiǎng mǎi nǎ jiàn yīfu?*
Which item of clothing do you want to buy?

## 4. Read for meaning — A short connected story

**小李在商店里找手机。店里有很多手机。他喜欢这个，但是那个便宜一点儿。**
*Xiǎo Lǐ zài shāngdiàn lǐ zhǎo shǒujī. Diàn lǐ yǒu hěn duō shǒujī. Tā xǐhuan zhège, dànshì nàge piányi yìdiǎnr.*
Xiao Li is looking at phones in a store. There are many phones. He likes this one, but that one is a little cheaper.

**Q1. What is Xiao Li shopping for?** Suggested answer: A mobile phone.

**Q2. Which phone is cheaper?** Suggested answer: That one.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Ask where the school is.

*Possible response:* **这个学校在哪里？**
*Zhège xuéxiào zài nǎlǐ?*
Where is this school?

**Speaking 2:** Ask which book someone wants.

*Possible response:* **你要哪本书？**
*Nǐ yào nǎ běn shū?*
Which book do you want?

## 6. Watch out

哪 (nǎ, which) and 那 (nà, that) are different characters and tones. Do not write 那本 (nà běn) when asking “which book?”.

**Pronunciation:** In spontaneous speech, 谁 has common readings shéi and shuí; avoid treating them as different words.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which expression means “how many”?
- a. 怎么 (zěnme) — 
- b. 多少 (duōshao) — 
**Answer:** b. The correct vocabulary entry means “how many; how much”.

**Selection:** Which expression means “those”?
- a. 那些 (nàxiē) — 
- b. 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng) — 
**Answer:** a. The correct vocabulary entry means “those”.

**Selection:** Which expression means “these”?
- a. 这 (zhè) — 
- b. 这些 (zhèxiē) — 
**Answer:** b. The correct vocabulary entry means “these”.

**Comprehension:** In the example “这个多少钱？” (zhège duōshao qián?), what is happening?
**Answer:** How much does this cost?. Use the complete sentence meaning as your guide.

**Production:** Write this in Mandarin: “How many students are in this class?”
**Answer:** 这个班有多少学生？ (zhège bān yǒu duōshao xuéshēng?). Compare your answer with the example and its tone-marked pinyin.
