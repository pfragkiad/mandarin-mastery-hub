# Asking and Pointing

Ask who, what, where and how, and identify people and objects as here or there.

**Topics:** reference-questions, places-directions

## Learning objectives

- Ask basic information questions.

- Use this/that and here/there naturally.

- Choose a demonstrative expression for singular or plural things.

## Overview

Ask who, what, where and how, and identify people and objects as here or there.

## Vocabulary and examples

### 多少 (duōshao) — how many; how much

HSK entry: 47 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**这个多少钱？**
*zhège duōshao qián?*
How much does this cost?
**Usage:** 多少钱 is the routine shopping question for a price.

**这个班有多少学生？**
*zhège bān yǒu duōshao xuéshēng?*
How many students are in this class?

Interrogative pronoun asking how many or how much (多少钱、多少人). It is not itself a classifier and can directly precede a noun.

### 哪 (nǎ) — which

HSK entry: 131 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你喜欢哪本书？**
*nǐ xǐhuan nǎ běn shū?*
Which book do you like?
**Usage:** 哪 selects one among known choices; 本 is the measure word for books.

**你想读哪本书？**
*nǐ xiǎng dú nǎ běn shū?*
Which book do you want to read?

### 哪个 (nǎge) — which one

HSK entry: 132 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你想要哪个？**
*nǐ xiǎng yào nǎge?*
Which one do you want?
**Usage:** 哪个 asks which one; 个 stands for the classifier of the implied object.

**哪个学生今天没有来？**
*nǎge xuéshēng jīntiān méiyǒu lái?*
Which student didn't come today?

### 哪里 (nǎlǐ) — where

HSK entry: 133 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**学校在哪里？**
*xuéxiào zài nǎlǐ?*
Where is the school?
**Usage:** 哪里 asks where an institution or object is located.

**“请问，书店在哪里？”她问。**
*“qǐngwèn, shūdiàn zài nǎlǐ?” tā wèn.*
"Excuse me, where is the bookstore?" she asked.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 哪里 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 哪儿 (nǎr) — where

HSK entry: 134 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你去哪儿？**
*nǐ qù nǎr?*
Where are you going?
**Usage:** 哪儿 means where, with the 儿 pronunciation typical of northern Mandarin.

**“学校在哪儿？”他问。**
*“xuéxiào zài nǎr?” tā wèn.*
"Where is the school?" he asked.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 哪儿 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 哪些 (nǎxiē) — which ones

HSK entry: 135 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你喜欢哪些书？**
*nǐ xǐhuan nǎxiē shū?*
Which books do you like?
**Usage:** 哪些 means which ones when choosing several items.

**哪些学生会说汉语？**
*nǎxiē xuéshēng huì shuō Hànyǔ?*
Which students can speak Chinese?

### 那 (nà) — that

HSK entry: 136 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**那是我的朋友。**
*nà shì wǒ de péngyou.*
That is my friend.
**Usage:** 那 points to something away from the speaker, in contrast to 这.

**那是学校的车。**
*nà shì xuéxiào de chē.*
That is the school's car.

Demonstrative pronoun; can also have discourse-connecting uses.

### 那边 (nàbiān) — over there

HSK entry: 137 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**商店在那边。**
*shāngdiàn zài nàbiān.*
The store is over there.
**Usage:** 那边 denotes a place over there, away from the speaker.

**那边有一家饭店。**
*nàbiān yǒu yì jiā fàndiàn.*
There is a restaurant over there.

### 那个 (nàge) — that one

HSK entry: 138 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**那个苹果很大。**
*nàge píngguǒ hěn dà.*
That apple is big.
**Usage:** 那个 identifies a particular item; 个 is its classifier.

**那个学生会说汉语。**
*nàge xuéshēng huì shuō Hànyǔ.*
That student can speak Chinese.

### 那里 (nàlǐ) — there

HSK entry: 139 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**我的学校在那里。**
*wǒ de xuéxiào zài nàlǐ.*
My school is there.
**Usage:** 那里 means “there” and is a full location word.

**那里有很多学生。**
*nàlǐ yǒu hěn duō xuéshēng.*
There are many students there.

### 那儿 (nàr) — there

HSK entry: 140 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你去那儿吗？**
*nǐ qù nàr ma?*
Are you going there?
**Usage:** 那儿 is a northern-style alternative to 那里 for “there.”

**请在那儿等老师。**
*qǐng zài nàr děng lǎoshī.*
Please wait for the teacher there.

### 那些 (nàxiē) — those

HSK entry: 141 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**那些书是我的。**
*nàxiē shū shì wǒ de.*
Those books are mine.
**Usage:** 那些 points to several items away from the speaker.

**那些学生都在教室里。**
*nàxiē xuéshēng dōu zài jiàoshì lǐ.*
Those students are all in the classroom.

### 谁 (shéi / shuí) — who

HSK entry: 181 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**那个人是谁？**
*nàge rén shì shéi?*
Who is that person?
**Usage:** 谁 asks for a person’s identity; shéi is used here, while shuí is an accepted alternative.

**“这是谁的书？”老师问。**
*“zhè shì shéi de shū?” lǎoshī wèn.*
"Whose book is this?" the teacher asked.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 谁 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 什么 (shénme) — what

HSK entry: 182 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你想吃什么？**
*nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?*
What do you want to eat?
**Usage:** 什么 asks what food the listener wants; it is a general interrogative pronoun.

**“你想喝什么？”她问。**
*“nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?” tā wèn.*
"What do you want to drink?" she asked.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 什么 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 怎么 (zěnme) — how

HSK entry: 276 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**这个字怎么写？**
*zhège zì zěnme xiě?*
How do you write this character?
**Usage:** 怎么写 asks how to write a particular Chinese character.

**这个问题怎么做？**
*zhège wèntí zěnme zuò?*
How do you do this question?

Interrogative pro-form meaning how, often adverbial in sentences.

### 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng) — how is it; how about

HSK entry: 277 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**你觉得怎么样？**
*nǐ juéde zěnmeyàng?*
What do you think?
**Usage:** 你觉得怎么样 asks for someone’s impression or opinion.

**这个饭店怎么样？**
*zhège fàndiàn zěnmeyàng?*
What do you think of this restaurant?

Interrogative pro-form meaning how/how about; often adverbial in sentences.

### 这 (zhè) — this

HSK entry: 279 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**这是什么？**
*zhè shì shénme?*
What is this?
**Usage:** 这 means this; 这是什么 is a standard way to ask what something is.

**这本书是新的。**
*zhè běn shū shì xīn de.*
This book is new.

### 这边 (zhèbiān) — this side

HSK entry: 280 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**请到这边来。**
*qǐng dào zhèbiān lái.*
Please come this way.
**Usage:** 请到这边来 invites someone to come over to this side.

**学校在这边，医院在那边。**
*xuéxiào zài zhèbiān, yīyuàn zài nàbiān.*
The school is on this side, and the hospital is over there.

### 这个 (zhège) — this one

HSK entry: 281 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**这个很好吃。**
*zhège hěn hǎochī.*
This one is delicious.
**Usage:** 这个 means “this one,” with the noun inferred from context.

**这个学生会说中文。**
*zhège xuéshēng huì shuō Zhōngwén.*
This student can speak Chinese.

### 这里 (zhèlǐ) — here

HSK entry: 282 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**我住在这里。**
*wǒ zhù zài zhèlǐ.*
I live here.
**Usage:** 这里 is a full location word meaning “here.”

**这里可以喝茶，也可以看书。**
*zhèlǐ kěyǐ hē chá, yě kěyǐ kàn shū.*
You can drink tea and read books here.

### 这儿 (zhèr) — here

HSK entry: 283 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**请来这儿。**
*qǐng lái zhèr.*
Please come here.
**Usage:** 这儿 is a northern-style spoken alternative to 这里.

**这儿有一家书店。**
*zhèr yǒu yì jiā shūdiàn.*
There is a bookstore here.

### 这些 (zhèxiē) — these

HSK entry: 284 · POS: pronoun · introduced

**这些苹果很好吃。**
*zhèxiē píngguǒ hěn hǎochī.*
These apples are delicious.
**Usage:** 这些 means these plural items, here the apples.

**这些学生都在学汉语。**
*zhèxiē xuéshēng dōu zài xué Hànyǔ.*
These students are all learning Chinese.

## Usage and grammar

### Asking what

Question words generally stay in the position of the information being requested.

### Pointing nearby

Demonstratives identify nearby or distant people, objects and places.

## Practice

**Selection:** Which expression means “how many”?
- a. 怎么 (zěnme) — 
- b. 多少 (duōshao) — 
**Answer:** b. The correct vocabulary entry means “how many; how much”.

**Selection:** Which expression means “those”?
- a. 那些 (nàxiē) — 
- b. 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng) — 
**Answer:** a. The correct vocabulary entry means “those”.

**Selection:** Which expression means “these”?
- a. 这 (zhè) — 
- b. 这些 (zhèxiē) — 
**Answer:** b. The correct vocabulary entry means “these”.

**Comprehension:** In the example “这个多少钱？” (zhège duōshao qián?), what is happening?
