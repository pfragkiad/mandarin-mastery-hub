# Greeting and Talking to People

Greet people, ask politely, apologize, thank others and talk about everyday matters.

**Topics:** communication-social, abstract-concepts

## Learning objectives

- Greet someone and say goodbye.

- Thank, apologize and respond naturally.

- Ask a polite question and refer to a matter or issue.

## Overview

## Vocabulary and examples

### 不客气 (bú kèqi) — you're welcome

HSK entry: 14 · POS: fixed-expression · introduced

**不客气！**
*bú kèqi!*
You're welcome!
**Usage:** 不客气 is a standard spoken response to thanks; the short answer is a complete utterance.

**她说：“不客气。”**
*tā shuō: “bú kèqi.”*
She said, "You're welcome."
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 不客气 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Conventional response to thanks; classify as a fixed expression, not by the characters separately.

### 打电话 (dǎ diànhuà) — make a phone call

HSK entry: 24 · POS: verb · introduced

**我要给妈妈打电话。**
*wǒ yào gěi māma dǎ diànhuà.*
I want to call my mother.
**Usage:** 给妈妈 identifies the recipient of the phone call; 打电话 is a verb-object expression.

**请明天上午给老师打电话。**
*qǐng míngtiān shàngwǔ gěi lǎoshī dǎ diànhuà.*
Please call the teacher tomorrow morning.

### 对不起 (duìbuqǐ) — sorry

HSK entry: 45 · POS: fixed-expression · introduced

**对不起，我来晚了。**
*duìbuqǐ, wǒ lái wǎn le.*
Sorry, I came late.
**Usage:** 对不起 is an apology; 来晚了 means arriving late, with 了 indicating the completed situation.

**“对不起，我没有看见你。”她说。**
*“duìbuqǐ, wǒ méiyǒu kànjiàn nǐ.” tā shuō.*
"I'm sorry, I didn't see you," she said.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 对不起 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Conventional apology; can be analyzed as a verb phrase, but functions as a fixed social expression.

### 没关系 (méi guānxi) — it doesn't matter

HSK entry: 120 · POS: fixed-expression · introduced

**没关系，我们再来一次。**
*méi guānxi, wǒmen zài lái yí cì.*
It's okay; let's do it one more time.

**“没关系，我们明天再来。”她说。**
*“méi guānxi, wǒmen míngtiān zài lái.” tā shuō.*
"It's okay; we can come back tomorrow," she said.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 没关系 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Fixed reply meaning it is all right/it does not matter.

### 没事 (méishì) — no problem

HSK entry: 121 · POS: fixed-expression · introduced

**没事，我很好。**
*méishì, wǒ hěn hǎo.*
No problem, I am fine.
**Usage:** 没事 is conversational reassurance, meaning that nothing is wrong.

**“没事，你可以回家。”他说。**
*“méishì, nǐ kěyǐ huí jiā.” tā shuō.*
"It’s okay; you can go home," he said.

Fixed reassurance meaning nothing is wrong/no problem; other verbal uses exist.

### 你好 (nǐ hǎo) — hello

HSK entry: 147 · POS: fixed-expression · introduced

**你好！很高兴认识你。**
*nǐ hǎo! hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.*
Hello! Nice to meet you.
**Usage:** 很高兴认识你 is a polite formula for meeting someone.

**“你好，请问这里有人吗？”她问。**
*“nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn zhèlǐ yǒu rén ma?” tā wèn.*
"Hello, excuse me, is anyone here?" she asked.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 你好 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 请 (qǐng) — please; invite

HSK entry: 165 · POS: verb · introduced

**请坐。**
*qǐng zuò.*
Please sit down.
**Usage:** 请坐 is a concise polite invitation to take a seat.

**请在这里坐一下。**
*qǐng zài zhèlǐ zuò yíxià.*
Please sit here for a moment.

Polite request/please, grammaticalized use of the verb invite/ask.

### 请问 (qǐngwèn) — may I ask

HSK entry: 166 · POS: verb · introduced

**请问，医院在哪儿？**
*qǐngwèn, yīyuàn zài nǎr?*
Excuse me, where is the hospital?
**Usage:** 请问 introduces a courteous question to someone you may not know.

**“请问，电影院在哪儿？”她问。**
*“qǐngwèn, diànyǐngyuàn zài nǎr?” tā wèn.*
"Excuse me, where is the cinema?" she asked.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 请问 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 认识 (rènshi) — know (someone)

HSK entry: 171 · POS: verb · introduced

**我认识这位老师。**
*wǒ rènshi zhè wèi lǎoshī.*
I know this teacher.
**Usage:** 认识 means to know or be acquainted with a person, not just to know a fact.

**学生们都认识这个老师。**
*xuéshēngmen dōu rènshi zhège lǎoshī.*
All the students know this teacher.

### 喂 (wèi) — hello (on phone)

HSK entry: 218 · POS: interjection · introduced

**喂，你好！**
*wéi, nǐ hǎo!*
Hello!
**Usage:** 喂 is used to answer the telephone and is commonly pronounced wéi in that context, though wèi is also listed.

**“喂，你好，我想找老师。”她说。**
*“wéi, nǐ hǎo, wǒ xiǎng zhǎo lǎoshī.” tā shuō.*
"Hello, I’d like to speak to the teacher," she said.

Attention-getting interjection; phone usage may be wéi or wèi depending on context.

### 问 (wèn) — ask

HSK entry: 219 · POS: verb · introduced

**我想问一个问题。**
*wǒ xiǎng wèn yí ge wèntí.*
I want to ask a question.
**Usage:** 问 introduces a question; 一个问题 is “one question.”

**课后可以问老师。**
*kè hòu kěyǐ wèn lǎoshī.*
You can ask the teacher after class.

### 问题 (wèntí) — question; problem

HSK entry: 220 · POS: noun · introduced

**我有一个问题。**
*wǒ yǒu yí ge wèntí.*
I have a question.

**有问题可以问老师。**
*yǒu wèntí kěyǐ wèn lǎoshī.*
If you have questions, you can ask the teacher.

### 谢谢 (xièxie) — thank you

HSK entry: 241 · POS: verb · introduced

**谢谢你帮我！**
*xièxie nǐ bāng wǒ!*
Thank you for helping me!

**“谢谢老师！”学生说。**
*“xièxie lǎoshī!” xuéshēng shuō.*
"Thank you, teacher!" the student said.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 谢谢 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

### 再见 (zàijiàn) — goodbye

HSK entry: 272 · POS: fixed-expression · introduced

**再见，明天见！**
*zàijiàn, míngtiān jiàn!*
Goodbye, see you tomorrow!
**Usage:** 再见 is the standard goodbye; 明天见 adds the expectation of meeting tomorrow.

**“再见，下个星期见！”她说。**
*“zàijiàn, xià ge xīngqī jiàn!” tā shuō.*
"Goodbye! See you next week!" she said.
**Usage:** Written dialogue: 再见 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

Conventional farewell used as a fixed social expression; also analyzable as a verb.

### 事 (shì) — matter; thing

HSK entry: 187 · POS: noun · introduced

**我有一件事要说。**
*wǒ yǒu yí jiàn shì yào shuō.*
I have something to say.
**Usage:** 件 can count matters or incidents; 事 means a matter or thing to discuss.

**有事的时候，可以给老师打电话。**
*yǒu shì de shíhou, kěyǐ gěi lǎoshī dǎ diànhuà.*
If something comes up, you can call the teacher.

Noun meaning a matter, thing, or affair (有事、一件事); a general concept rather than an action verb.

## Usage and grammar

### Responding to thanks

A fixed courtesy expression is the usual response to a thank-you.

### Polite requests

A short politeness marker can make a request courteous.
