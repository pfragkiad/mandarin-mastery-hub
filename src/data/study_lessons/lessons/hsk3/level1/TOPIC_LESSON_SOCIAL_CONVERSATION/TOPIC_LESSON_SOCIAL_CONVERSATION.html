1 · Conversation — Greeting and Talking to People

Use realistic first-meeting and telephone exchanges. Focus on conventional replies, not word-for-word translation.

A

喂，你好！请问，李老师在吗？ (Wèi, nǐ hǎo! Qǐngwèn, Lǐ lǎoshī zài ma?)

Hello. May I ask if Teacher Li is there?

B

在。请问，您是谁？ (Zài. Qǐngwèn, nín shì shéi?)

Yes. May I ask who you are?

A

我是王明的朋友。 (Wǒ shì Wáng Míng de péngyou.)

I am Wang Ming’s friend.

B

好的，请等一下。 (Hǎo de, qǐng děng yíxià.)

All right, please wait a moment.

2 · How the language works

Polite openings

请问 (qǐngwèn) introduces a polite request or question. 您 (nín) shows respect. At the start of a phone conversation 喂 (wèi) functions as a greeting/attention-getter.

请问 + 问题
qǐngwèn + wèntí
请问 + <question>

请问，您认识王老师吗？ (Qǐngwèn, nín rènshi Wáng lǎoshī ma?)

Excuse me, do you know Teacher Wang?

请问，医院在哪里？ (Qǐngwèn, yīyuàn zài nǎlǐ?)

Excuse me, where is the hospital?

Apologies and thanks

Use the conventional response matched to the situation: 对不起 (duìbuqǐ) → 没关系 (méi guānxi); 谢谢 (xièxie) → 不客气 (bú kèqi).

对不起 → 没关系 / 谢谢 → 不客气
duìbuqǐ → méi guānxi / xièxie → bú kèqi
对不起 → 没关系 / 谢谢 → 不客气

对不起，我今天来晚了。 (Duìbuqǐ, wǒ jīntiān lái wǎn le.)

Sorry, I am late today.

没关系，我们现在开始吧。 (Méi guānxi, wǒmen xiànzài kāishǐ ba.)

That is all right. Let us start now.

Today Xiao Li meets Teacher Wang for the first time. He says, “Hello, pleased to meet you!” Teacher Wang replies, “I am pleased too.”

6 · Watch out

Do not answer 谢谢 (xièxie) with 没关系 (méi guānxi) as your default. 不客气 (bú kèqi) is the standard “you are welcome” reply.

Pronunciation clinic

The tone sequence in 你好 (nǐ hǎo) normally involves third-tone sandhi in connected speech.
