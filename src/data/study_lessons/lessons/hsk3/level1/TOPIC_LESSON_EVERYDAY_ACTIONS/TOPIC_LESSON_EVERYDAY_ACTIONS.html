Vocabulary and examples

HSK entry 23
穿 (chuān)

wear

verb · introduced

今天我穿新衣服。 (jīntiān wǒ chuān xīn yīfu.)

I am wearing new clothes today.

穿 refers to wearing clothes; Chinese does not need a progressive form for the current outfit.

天气冷的时候，请多穿衣服。 (tiānqì lěng de shíhou, qǐng duō chuān yīfu.)

When it is cold, please wear more clothes.

HSK entry 61
给 (gěi)

give; for

verb · introduced

可以给我一点儿水吗？ (kěyǐ gěi wǒ yìdiǎnr shuǐ ma?)

Could you give me a little water?

老师给我们一本书。 (lǎoshī gěi wǒmen yì běn shū.)

The teacher gives us a book.

Verb meaning give, and a coverb/preposition meaning for/to.

HSK entry 88
见 (jiàn)

see; meet

verb · introduced

明天见！ (míngtiān jiàn!)

See you tomorrow!

明天见 is a short, common parting expression meaning “see you tomorrow.”

“星期一见！”她说。 (“xīngqīyī jiàn!” tā shuō.)

"See you on Monday!" she said.

Written dialogue: 见 is used naturally in a spoken exchange, rather than forced into formal prose.

HSK entry 91
叫 (jiào)

be called; call

verb · introduced

你叫什么名字？ (nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?)

What is your name?

孩子们叫她妈妈。 (háizimen jiào tā māma.)

The children call her Mom.

HSK entry 97
开 (kāi)

open

verb · introduced

请开门。 (qǐng kāi mén.)

Please open the door.

请 softens the imperative; 开门 means open the door.

商店早上八点开门。 (shāngdiàn zǎoshang bā diǎn kāimén.)

The shop opens at eight in the morning.

HSK entry 99
看 (kàn)

look; watch

verb · introduced

你看，这本书很好。 (nǐ kàn, zhè běn shū hěn hǎo.)

Look, this book is very good.

请看这本中文书。 (qǐng kàn zhè běn Zhōngwén shū.)

Please look at this Chinese book.

HSK entry 101
看见 (kànjiàn)

catch sight of

verb · introduced

我看见一只猫。 (wǒ kànjiàn yì zhī māo.)

I see a cat.

看见 emphasizes successful visual perception; here the speaker spots a cat.

从这里可以看见学校。 (cóng zhèlǐ kěyǐ kànjiàn xuéxiào.)

You can see the school from here.

HSK entry 214
玩 (wán)

play

verb · introduced

孩子们在玩。 (háizimen zài wán.)

The children are playing.

在 before 玩 marks ongoing play; 孩子们 refers to the children as a group.

学生们下课后在外边玩。 (xuéshēngmen xiàkè hòu zài wàibian wán.)

The students play outside after class.

HSK entry 278
找 (zhǎo)

look for

verb · introduced

我在找我的手机。 (wǒ zài zhǎo wǒ de shǒujī.)

I am looking for my phone.

在 + 找 signals a search that is currently in progress.

我去学校找老师。 (wǒ qù xuéxiào zhǎo lǎoshī.)

I'm going to the school to find the teacher.

HSK entry 288
知道 (zhīdào)

know

verb · introduced

我知道你的名字。 (wǒ zhīdào nǐ de míngzi.)

I know your name.

知道 means know a fact or piece of information, unlike 认识 for familiarity.

老师知道我的名字。 (lǎoshī zhīdào wǒ de míngzi.)

The teacher knows my name.

HSK entry 294
住 (zhù)

live; reside

verb · introduced

我住在这边。 (wǒ zhù zài zhèbiān.)

I live over here.

现在很多学生住在学校里。 (xiànzài hěn duō xuéshēng zhù zài xuéxiào lǐ.)

Many students now live at school.

HSK entry 298
坐 (zuò)

sit

verb · introduced

请坐在这里。 (qǐng zuò zài zhèlǐ.)

Please sit here.

请 makes the request polite; 坐在这里 includes an explicit location marker.

学生们坐在教室里。 (xuéshēngmen zuò zài jiàoshì lǐ.)

The students are sitting in the classroom.

HSK entry 299
做 (zuò)

do; make

verb · introduced

我今天要做什么？ (wǒ jīntiān yào zuò shénme?)

What do I have to do today?

做 means do or make; 什么 identifies the task not yet specified.

现在做第一题。 (xiànzài zuò dì yī tí.)

Now do question one.

Usage and grammar

Watching and looking

The perception verb combines with different objects for common actions.

你看，这本书很好。 (nǐ kàn, zhè běn shū hěn hǎo.)

Look, this book is very good.

Doing actions

A broad everyday verb can describe performing an activity or task.

我今天要做什么？ (wǒ jīntiān yào zuò shénme?)

What do I have to do today?

做 means do or make; 什么 identifies the task not yet specified.
