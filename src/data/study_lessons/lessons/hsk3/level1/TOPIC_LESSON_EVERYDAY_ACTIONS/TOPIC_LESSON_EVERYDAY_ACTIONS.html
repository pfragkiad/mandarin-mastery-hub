1 · Conversation — Things We Do Every Day

Two people are getting ready to go out. Follow the order of activities and the difference between 看 (kàn) and 看见 (kànjiàn).

A

你在做什么？ (Nǐ zài zuò shénme?)

What are you doing?

B

我正在找我的手机。 (Wǒ zhèngzài zhǎo wǒ de shǒujī.)

I am looking for my phone right now.

A

你看见了吗？ (Nǐ kànjiàn le ma?)

Have you seen it?

B

看见了，在桌子上。 (Kànjiàn le, zài zhuōzi shàng.)

Yes, I have; it is on the table.

2 · How the language works

Looking versus seeing

看 (kàn) is the act of looking; 看见 (kànjiàn) expresses the result of perceiving visually. Searching with 找 (zhǎo) does not guarantee you find the object.

look/search → result

我在看外边。 (Wǒ zài kàn wàibian.)

I am looking outside.

我看见那只猫了。 (Wǒ kànjiàn nà zhī māo le.)

I spotted that cat.

Give and receive

给 (gěi) can introduce a recipient before a noun phrase or, in simple requests, act as a verb meaning “give”.

给 + person + object

请给我那本书。 (Qǐng gěi wǒ nà běn shū.)

Please give me that book.

我给朋友打电话。 (Wǒ gěi péngyou dǎ diànhuà.)

I call a friend.

4 · Read for meaning — A short connected story

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

小红今天要去学校。她找不到书，所以给同学打电话。同学说书在教室里。 (Xiǎo Hóng jīntiān yào qù xuéxiào. Tā zhǎo bú dào shū, suǒyǐ gěi tóngxué dǎ diànhuà. Tóngxué shuō shū zài jiàoshì lǐ.)

Xiao Hong is going to school today. She cannot find her book, so she calls a classmate. The classmate says it is in the classroom.

1. Why does Xiao Hong call a classmate?
Show the answer

She cannot find her book.

2. Where is the book?
Show the answer

In the classroom.

6 · Watch out

Do not confuse 看 (kàn, look) with 看见 (kànjiàn, perceive/see). The result is expressed in the latter.

Pronunciation clinic

In 找不到 (zhǎo bú dào), 不 becomes bú before the fourth-tone 到 (dào).
