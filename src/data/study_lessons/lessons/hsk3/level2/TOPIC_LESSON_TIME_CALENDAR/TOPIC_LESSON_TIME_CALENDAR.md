# Talking About Time and Habits

Describe regular activities, birthdays, past habits and things about to happen.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Describe frequency with 经常 (jīngcháng) and 有时 (yǒushí).

- Talk about childhood and the past.

- Describe an approaching event using 快要 (kuàiyào).

## 1. Conversation — Talking About Time and Habits

Describe regular activities, birthdays, past habits and things about to happen.

**A:** 你下周过生日吗？ (Nǐ xià zhōu guò shēngrì ma?) — Is your birthday next week?

**B:** 对，我的生日快要到了！ (Duì, wǒ de shēngrì kuàiyào dào le!) — Yes, my birthday is coming soon!

**A:** 你小时候经常怎么过生日？ (Nǐ xiǎoshíhou jīngcháng zěnme guò shēngrì?) — How did you often celebrate birthdays as a child?

**B:** 我从小喜欢和家人一起吃饭。有时还唱歌。 (Wǒ cóngxiǎo xǐhuan hé jiārén yìqǐ chī fàn. Yǒushí hái chàng gē.) — Since childhood I have liked eating with family. Sometimes we also sang.

## 2. How the language works

### Frequency in daily life

经常 (jīngcháng) means often; 有时 (yǒushí) means sometimes. Put the frequency word before the main action.

**Pattern:** Subject + frequency adverb + verb

**我经常星期六去运动。**
*Wǒ jīngcháng xīngqīliù qù yùndòng.*
I often exercise on Saturdays.

**对，我的生日快要到了！**
*Duì, wǒ de shēngrì kuàiyào dào le!*
Yes, my birthday is coming soon!

### An event is about to happen

Use 快要 (kuàiyào) before an event or verb phrase, often with 了 (le) to signal an impending change.

**Pattern:** 快要 + event + 了

**电影快要开始了，我们进去吧。**
*Diànyǐng kuàiyào kāishǐ le, wǒmen jìnqù ba.*
The movie is about to start; let’s go in.

**我从小喜欢和家人一起吃饭。有时还唱歌。**
*Wǒ cóngxiǎo xǐhuan hé jiārén yìqǐ chī fàn. Yǒushí hái chàng gē.*
Since childhood I have liked eating with family. Sometimes we also sang.

## 4. Read for meaning — Talking About Time and Habits — short reading

**小丽每周去看一次爷爷。有时她只坐一会儿，有时会待几个小时。**
*Xiǎo Lì měi zhōu qù kàn yí cì yéye. Yǒushí tā zhǐ zuò yíhuìr, yǒushí huì dāi jǐ ge xiǎoshí.*
Xiaoli visits her grandpa every week. Sometimes she stays only a short while; sometimes several hours.

**今年快要过年了，家里开始准备很多好吃的东西。**
*Jīnnián kuàiyào guònián le, jiālǐ kāishǐ zhǔnbèi hěn duō hǎochī de dōngxi.*
The New Year is approaching, so the family is starting to prepare lots of tasty food.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiaoli visits her grandpa every week. Sometimes she stays only a short while; sometimes several hours.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: The New Year is approaching, so the family is starting to prepare lots of tasty food.

## 6. Watch out

Avoid confusing 小时候 (xiǎoshíhou), meaning when someone was little, with 小时 (xiǎoshí), a unit of sixty minutes.

**Pronunciation:** Listen for the tone change in 一会儿 (yíhuìr) and the neutral suffix in 小时候 (xiǎoshíhou).
