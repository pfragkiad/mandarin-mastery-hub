# Nature and the Weather

**Subtopics:** weather-climate

Describe an outdoor scene with weather, birds, flowers and water.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Contrast sunny and cloudy conditions.

- Describe a bird flying and things moving through water.

- Talk about flowers in a natural outdoor setting.

## 1. Conversation — Nature and the Weather

Describe an outdoor scene with weather, birds, flowers and water.

**A:** 今天天气怎么样？我们出去吧。 (Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? Wǒmen chūqù ba.) — What’s the weather like today? Let’s go out.

**B:** 现在是晴天，公园里有很多花。 (Xiànzài shì qíngtiān, gōngyuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō huā.) — It’s sunny now, and there are lots of flowers in the park.

**A:** 你看，那只鸟飞得真高！ (Nǐ kàn, nà zhī niǎo fēi de zhēn gāo!) — Look, that bird is flying really high!

**B:** 一会儿可能会阴天，我们先去湖边看看。 (Yíhuìr kěnéng huì yīntiān, wǒmen xiān qù hú biān kànkan.) — It may get cloudy soon; let’s look by the lake first.

## 2. How the language works

### Talking about conditions

晴 (qíng) refers to clear, sunny weather; 阴 (yīn) refers to cloudy or overcast conditions.

**Pattern:** 今天 + 晴/阴 + 天

**那只小鸟会飞。**
*Nà zhī xiǎo niǎo huì fēi.*
That little bird can fly.

**现在是晴天，公园里有很多花。**
*Xiànzài shì qíngtiān, gōngyuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō huā.*
It’s sunny now, and there are lots of flowers in the park.

### Describing movement in nature

飞 (fēi) describes flying, while 游 (yóu) describes moving through water.

**Pattern:** Animal + 在 + place + movement verb

**今天阴天，我们在家看书吧。**
*Jīntiān yīntiān, wǒmen zài jiā kàn shū ba.*
It’s cloudy today; let’s read at home.

**一会儿可能会阴天，我们先去湖边看看。**
*Yíhuìr kěnéng huì yīntiān, wǒmen xiān qù hú biān kànkan.*
It may get cloudy soon; let’s look by the lake first.

## 4. Read for meaning — Nature and the Weather — short reading

**星期六，两个孩子到公园玩。天气很晴朗，鸟儿在树上唱歌。**
*Xīngqīliù, liǎng ge háizi dào gōngyuán wán. Tiānqì hěn qínglǎng, niǎor zài shù shang chàng gē.*
On Saturday, two children play in the park. The weather is clear, and birds sing in the trees.

**下午天慢慢阴了，风把花吹得动起来。湖里还有鱼在游。**
*Xiàwǔ tiān mànman yīn le, fēng bǎ huā chuī de dòng qǐlái. Hú lǐ hái yǒu yú zài yóu.*
In the afternoon it gradually grows cloudy. The wind makes the flowers move, while fish swim in the lake.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: On Saturday, two children play in the park. The weather is clear, and birds sing in the trees.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: In the afternoon it gradually grows cloudy. The wind makes the flowers move, while fish swim in the lake.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

*Possible response:* **那只小鸟会飞。**
*Nà zhī xiǎo niǎo huì fēi.*

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: It’s cloudy today; let’s read at home.

*Possible response:* **今天阴天，我们在家看书吧。**
*Jīntiān yīntiān, wǒmen zài jiā kàn shū ba.*
It’s cloudy today; let’s read at home.

## 6. Watch out

游 (yóu) means swim or move through water here; 旅游 (lǚyóu), travel, is a different word. 花 (huā) here means flower, not spend.

**Pronunciation:** Distinguish 晴 (qíng), second tone, from 阴 (yīn), first tone, and 飞 (fēi), first tone.
