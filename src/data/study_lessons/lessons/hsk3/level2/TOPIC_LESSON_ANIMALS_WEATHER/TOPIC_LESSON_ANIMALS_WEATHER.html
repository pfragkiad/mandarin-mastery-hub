Nature and the Weather

Birds, flowers, sunshine and clouds

Your goal

  • Contrast sunny and cloudy conditions.
  • Describe a bird flying and things moving through water.
  • Talk about flowers in a natural outdoor setting.

1 · Conversation — Nature and the Weather

Describe an outdoor scene with weather, birds, flowers and water.

A

今天天气怎么样？我们出去吧。 (Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? Wǒmen chūqù ba.)

What’s the weather like today? Let’s go out.

B

现在是晴天，公园里有很多花。 (Xiànzài shì qíngtiān, gōngyuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō huā.)

It’s sunny now, and there are lots of flowers in the park.

A

你看，那只鸟飞得真高！ (Nǐ kàn, nà zhī niǎo fēi de zhēn gāo!)

Look, that bird is flying really high!

B

一会儿可能会阴天，我们先去湖边看看。 (Yíhuìr kěnéng huì yīntiān, wǒmen xiān qù hú biān kànkan.)

It may get cloudy soon; let’s look by the lake first.

2 · How the language works

Talking about conditions

晴 (qíng) refers to clear, sunny weather; 阴 (yīn) refers to cloudy or overcast conditions.

今天 + 是 + 晴天/阴天
jīntiān + shì + qíngtiān/yīntiān
今天 + 是 + 晴天/阴天

那只小鸟会飞。 (Nà zhī xiǎo niǎo huì fēi.)

That little bird can fly.

现在是晴天，公园里有很多花。 (Xiànzài shì qíngtiān, gōngyuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō huā.)

It’s sunny now, and there are lots of flowers in the park.

Describing movement in nature

飞 (fēi) describes flying, while 游 (yóu) describes moving through water.

动物 + 在 + 地点 + 位移动词
dòngwù + zài + dìdiǎn + wèiyí dòngcí
<animal> + 在 + <place> + <movement verb>

今天阴天，我们在家看书吧。 (Jīntiān yīntiān, wǒmen zài jiā kàn shū ba.)

It’s cloudy today; let’s read at home.

一会儿可能会阴天，我们先去湖边看看。 (Yíhuìr kěnéng huì yīntiān, wǒmen xiān qù hú biān kànkan.)

It may get cloudy soon; let’s look by the lake first.

4 · Read for meaning — Nature and the Weather — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

星期六，两个孩子到公园玩。天气很晴朗，鸟儿在树上唱歌。 (Xīngqīliù, liǎng ge háizi dào gōngyuán wán. Tiānqì hěn qínglǎng, niǎor zài shù shang chàng gē.)

On Saturday, two children play in the park. The weather is clear, and birds sing in the trees.

下午天慢慢阴了，风把花吹得动起来。湖里还有鱼在游。 (Xiàwǔ tiān mànman yīn le, fēng bǎ huā chuī de dòng qǐlái. Hú lǐ hái yǒu yú zài yóu.)

In the afternoon it gradually grows cloudy. The wind makes the flowers move, while fish swim in the lake.

6 · Watch out

游 (yóu) means swim or move through water here; 旅游 (lǚyóu), travel, is a different word. 花 (huā) here means flower, not spend.

Pronunciation clinic

Distinguish 晴 (qíng), second tone, from 阴 (yīn), first tone, and 飞 (fēi), first tone.
