## 1. Conversation — Describing Actions More Precisely

Add useful details to sentences with aspect markers, complements and negative instructions.

**A:** 你去过北京吗？ (Nǐ qù guo Běijīng ma?) — Have you ever been to Beijing?

**B:** 去过啊！我已经去过两次了。 (Qù guo a! Wǒ yǐjīng qù guo liǎng cì le.) — Yes! I’ve already been twice.

**A:** 你现在正在做什么？ (Nǐ xiànzài zhèngzài zuò shénme?) — What are you doing right now?

**B:** 我正认真地写字呢。别说话，我写得很慢。 (Wǒ zhèng rènzhēn de xiě zì ne. Bié shuōhuà, wǒ xiě de hěn màn.) — I’m writing carefully right now. Please don’t talk; I write slowly.

## 2. How the language works

### Experience and continuing state

过 (guo) after a verb points to an experience, while 着 (zhe) after a verb can mark a persisting state; they do not mean the same thing.

**Pattern**

动词 + 过 / 动词 + 着  
*dòngcí + guo / dòngcí + zhe*  
&lt;verb&gt; + **过** / &lt;verb&gt; + **着**

**我看过这部电影。**
*Wǒ kàn guo zhè bù diànyǐng.*
I have seen this film before.

**去过啊！我已经去过两次了。**
*Qù guo a! Wǒ yǐjīng qù guo liǎng cì le.*
Yes! I’ve already been twice.

### Modifying an action

地 (de) can link a manner modifier to a verb, while 得 (de) follows the verb and introduces a complement describing its degree or result.

**Pattern**

修饰语 + 地 + 动词 / 动词 + 得 + 补语  
*xiūshìyǔ + de + dòngcí / dòngcí + de + bǔyǔ*  
&lt;modifier&gt; + **地** + &lt;verb&gt; / &lt;verb&gt; + **得** + &lt;complement&gt;

**她跑得很快。**
*Tā pǎo de hěn kuài.*
She runs fast.

**我正认真地写字呢。别说话，我写得很慢。**
*Wǒ zhèng rènzhēn de xiě zì ne. Bié shuōhuà, wǒ xiě de hěn màn.*
I’m writing carefully right now. Please don’t talk; I write slowly.

## 4. Read for meaning — Describing Actions More Precisely — short reading

**教室里，老师正站着说话，学生们安静地听。今天大家已经学过这个词。**
*Jiàoshì lǐ, lǎoshī zhèng zhànzhe shuōhuà, xuéshēngmen ānjìng de tīng. Jīntiān dàjiā yǐjīng xué guo zhè ge cí.*
In the classroom, the teacher is standing and talking, and the students listen quietly. They have already studied this word today.

## 6. Watch out

Do not mix up the written characters 地 (de), 得 (de) and 的 (de); their grammatical roles and positions differ. 别 (bié) is used to tell someone not to do something.

**Pronunciation:** The grammatical particles 过 (guo), 着 (zhe), 地 (de) and 得 (de) are usually read in the neutral tone in these constructions.
