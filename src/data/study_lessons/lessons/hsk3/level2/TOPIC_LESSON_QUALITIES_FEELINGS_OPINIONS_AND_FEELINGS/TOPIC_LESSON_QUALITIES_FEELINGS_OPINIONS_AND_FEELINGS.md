# Qualities, Feelings & Evaluation — Part 2: Sharing Opinions and Feelings

**Subtopics:** judgments-evaluation

Say whether an activity is interesting, correct a mistake and express a personal hope.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Describe something as interesting or boring.

- Respond to an error without sounding harsh.

- Express happiness and a simple hope.

## 1. Conversation — Sharing Opinions and Feelings

Say whether an activity is interesting, correct a mistake and express a personal hope.

**A:** 你觉得今天的电影怎么样？ (Nǐ juéde jīntiān de diànyǐng zěnmeyàng?) — What do you think of today’s film?

**B:** 我觉得很有意思！你呢？ (Wǒ juéde hěn yǒu yìsi! Nǐ ne?) — I think it is very interesting! How about you?

**A:** 我觉得还不错，但是最后有点儿没意思。 (Wǒ juéde hái búcuò, dànshì zuìhòu yǒudiǎnr méi yìsi.) — I think it is fairly good, but the end is a little boring.

**B:** 没关系！希望下次我们能看更好的电影。 (Méi guānxi! Xīwàng xià cì wǒmen néng kàn gèng hǎo de diànyǐng.) — No problem! I hope we can watch a better movie next time.

## 2. How the language works

### Evaluating an activity

有意思 (yǒu yìsi) means interesting; 没意思 (méi yìsi) expresses that something is uninteresting.

**Pattern**

活动 + 很有意思 / 活动 + 没意思  
*huódòng + hěn yǒu yìsi / huódòng + méiyìsi*  
&lt;activity&gt; + **很有意思** / &lt;activity&gt; + **没意思**

**这个故事真有意思！**
*Zhè ge gùshi zhēn yǒu yìsi!*
This story is really interesting!

**我觉得很有意思！你呢？**
*Wǒ juéde hěn yǒu yìsi! Nǐ ne?*
I think it is very interesting! How about you?

### Expressing a hope

希望 (xīwàng) introduces what you hope will happen, followed by a clause.

**Pattern**

主语 + 希望 + 分句  
*zhǔyǔ + xīwàng + fēnjù*  
&lt;subject&gt; + **希望** + &lt;clause&gt;

**我希望明天的天气不错。**
*Wǒ xīwàng míngtiān de tiānqì búcuò.*
I hope tomorrow’s weather will be good.

**没关系！希望下次我们能看更好的电影。**
*Méi guānxi! Xīwàng xià cì wǒmen néng kàn gèng hǎo de diànyǐng.*
No problem! I hope we can watch a better movie next time.

## 4. Read for meaning — Sharing Opinions and Feelings — short reading

**小美今天很快乐，因为她和朋友一起去看电影。她觉得故事很有意思。**
*Xiǎoměi jīntiān hěn kuàilè, yīnwèi tā hé péngyou yìqǐ qù kàn diànyǐng. Tā juéde gùshi hěn yǒu yìsi.*
Xiaomei is happy today because she went to the cinema with friends. She finds the story interesting.

**小王说自己买错了票，不过大家没有生气。他们希望明天还能一起出去。**
*Xiǎo Wáng shuō zìjǐ mǎi cuò le piào, búguò dàjiā méiyǒu shēngqì. Tāmen xīwàng míngtiān hái néng yìqǐ chūqù.*
Xiao Wang says he bought the wrong ticket, but nobody gets angry. They hope they can go out together tomorrow.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiaomei is happy today because she went to the cinema with friends. She finds the story interesting.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiao Wang says he bought the wrong ticket, but nobody gets angry. They hope they can go out together tomorrow.

## 6. Watch out

不错 (búcuò) is a positive evaluation meaning “not bad”; 错 (cuò) by itself means wrong or incorrect. Avoid treating them as synonyms.

**Pronunciation:** The second syllable in 意思 (yìsi) is neutral; 不错 (búcuò) has second-tone 不 before fourth-tone 错.
