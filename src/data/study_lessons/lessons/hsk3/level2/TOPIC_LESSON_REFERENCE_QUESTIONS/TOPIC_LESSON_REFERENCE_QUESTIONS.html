Explaining Why and How

Reasons, descriptions and the self

reference-questions · #reasons · #hsk-level-2 · #topic-study

Your goal

  • Ask reasons with 为什么 (wèi shénme).
  • Distinguish 这么 (zhème) and 那么 (nàme) in a description.
  • Refer back to oneself with 自己 (zìjǐ).

1 · Conversation — Explaining Why and How

Ask why something happened and describe different ways of doing things.

A

你为什么这么高兴？ (Nǐ wèi shénme zhème gāoxìng?)

Why are you so happy?

B

因为我自己做完了这道题！ (Yīnwèi wǒ zìjǐ zuò wán le zhè dào tí!)

Because I finished this problem by myself!

A

你是怎么做的？ (Nǐ shì zěnme zuò de?)

How did you do it?

B

我先看书，再照着上面的方法做。 (Wǒ xiān kàn shū, zài zhàozhe shàngmian de fāngfǎ zuò.)

I read the book first, then followed the method in it.

2 · How the language works

Asking for a reason

为什么 (wèi shénme) asks for the reason; a natural reply often begins with 因为 (yīnwèi).

为什么 + 分句？ / 主语 + 为什么 + 动词短语？
wèishénme + fēnjù? / zhǔyǔ + wèishénme + dòngcí duǎnyǔ?
为什么 + <clause>？ / <subject> + 为什么 + <verb phrase>？

为什么你今天不去学校？ (Wèi shénme nǐ jīntiān bú qù xuéxiào?)

Why are you not going to school today?

因为我自己做完了这道题！ (Yīnwèi wǒ zìjǐ zuò wán le zhè dào tí!)

Because I finished this problem by myself!

Referring to manner and self

这样 (zhèyàng) or 那样 (nàyàng) indicates a way of doing something; 自己 (zìjǐ) identifies the person acting independently.

人 + 自己 + 动作
rén + zìjǐ + dòngzuò
<person> + 自己 + <action>

我想自己试一试。 (Wǒ xiǎng zìjǐ shì yi shì.)

I want to try it myself.

我先看书，再照着上面的方法做。 (Wǒ xiān kàn shū, zài zhàozhe shàngmian de fāngfǎ zuò.)

I read the book first, then followed the method in it.

4 · Read for meaning — Explaining Why and How — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

老师问学生为什么这样写，学生说自己以前学过这个词。 (Lǎoshī wèn xuéshēng wèi shénme zhèyàng xiě, xuéshēng shuō zìjǐ yǐqián xué guo zhè ge cí.)

6 · Watch out

Do not translate 这么 (zhème) and 这样 (zhèyàng) as identical in every context: one often modifies degree, the other a way or manner.

Pronunciation clinic

Practise the fourth tone in 这 (zhè) and 那 (nà), and the third-tone pair in 自己 (zìjǐ).
