# Explaining Why and How

Ask why something happened and describe different ways of doing things.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask reasons with 为什么 (wèi shénme).

- Distinguish 这么 (zhème) and 那么 (nàme) in a description.

- Refer back to oneself with 自己 (zìjǐ).

## 1. Conversation — Explaining Why and How

Ask why something happened and describe different ways of doing things.

**A:** 你为什么这么高兴？ (Nǐ wèi shénme zhème gāoxìng?) — Why are you so happy?

**B:** 因为我自己做完了这道题！ (Yīnwèi wǒ zìjǐ zuò wán le zhè dào tí!) — Because I finished this problem by myself!

**A:** 你是怎么做的？ (Nǐ shì zěnme zuò de?) — How did you do it?

**B:** 我先看书，再照着上面的方法做。 (Wǒ xiān kàn shū, zài zhàozhe shàngmian de fāngfǎ zuò.) — I read the book first, then followed the method in it.

## 2. How the language works

### Asking for a reason

为什么 (wèi shénme) asks for the reason; a natural reply often begins with 因为 (yīnwèi).

**Pattern**

为什么 + 分句？ / 主语 + 为什么 + 动词短语？  
*wèishénme + fēnjù? / zhǔyǔ + wèishénme + dòngcí duǎnyǔ?*  
**为什么** + &lt;clause&gt;？ / &lt;subject&gt; + **为什么** + &lt;verb phrase&gt;？

**为什么你今天不去学校？**
*Wèi shénme nǐ jīntiān bú qù xuéxiào?*
Why are you not going to school today?

**因为我自己做完了这道题！**
*Yīnwèi wǒ zìjǐ zuò wán le zhè dào tí!*
Because I finished this problem by myself!

### Referring to manner and self

这样 (zhèyàng) or 那样 (nàyàng) indicates a way of doing something; 自己 (zìjǐ) identifies the person acting independently.

**Pattern**

人 + 自己 + 动作  
*rén + zìjǐ + dòngzuò*  
&lt;person&gt; + **自己** + &lt;action&gt;

**我想自己试一试。**
*Wǒ xiǎng zìjǐ shì yi shì.*
I want to try it myself.

**我先看书，再照着上面的方法做。**
*Wǒ xiān kàn shū, zài zhàozhe shàngmian de fāngfǎ zuò.*
I read the book first, then followed the method in it.

## 3. Build new sentences

Make your own sentence from each cue, say it aloud, then reveal the model answer.

**Worked model:** **你为什么这么高兴？**

**Change 1: Say in Mandarin: Why are you not going to school today?**

*Model response:* **为什么你今天不去学校？**
*Wèi shénme nǐ jīntiān bú qù xuéxiào?*

**Change 2: Say in Mandarin: I want to try it myself.**

*Model response:* **我想自己试一试。**
*Wǒ xiǎng zìjǐ shì yi shì.*
I want to try it myself.

**Change 3: Say in Mandarin: How did you do it?**

*Model response:* **你是怎么做的？**
*Nǐ shì zěnme zuò de?*

## 4. Read for meaning — Explaining Why and How — short reading

**老师问学生为什么这样写，学生说自己以前学过这个词。**
*Lǎoshī wèn xuéshēng wèi shénme zhèyàng xiě, xuéshēng shuō zìjǐ yǐqián xué guo zhè ge cí.*
The teacher asks why the student wrote it that way, and the student says they learned the word before.

**另一个学生说：“不要那么写，应该这么写。”老师请他再解释一下。**
*Lìng yí ge xuéshēng shuō: “Bú yào nàme xiě, yīnggāi zhème xiě.” Lǎoshī qǐng tā zài jiěshì yíxià.*
Another student says, “Don’t write it that way; write it this way.” The teacher asks him to explain again.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: The teacher asks why the student wrote it that way, and the student says they learned the word before.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Another student says, “Don’t write it that way; write it this way.” The teacher asks him to explain again.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Explain to a classmate: Why are you not going to school today?

*Possible response:* **为什么你今天不去学校？**
*Wèi shénme nǐ jīntiān bú qù xuéxiào?*
Why are you not going to school today?

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: I want to try it myself.

*Possible response:* **我想自己试一试。**
*Wǒ xiǎng zìjǐ shì yi shì.*
I want to try it myself.

## 6. Watch out

Do not translate 这么 (zhème) and 这样 (zhèyàng) as identical in every context: one often modifies degree, the other a way or manner.

**Pronunciation:** Practise the fourth tone in 这 (zhè) and 那 (nà), and the third-tone pair in 自己 (zìjǐ).
