# Planning a Journey

Buy transport tickets and describe a journey by metro, bus or taxi.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Name a station, airport and hotel.

- Choose a transport method.

- Talk about tickets and a trip abroad.

## 1. Conversation — Planning a Journey

Buy transport tickets and describe a journey by metro, bus or taxi.

**A:** 你明天去机场吗？ (Nǐ míngtiān qù jīchǎng ma?) — Are you going to the airport tomorrow?

**B:** 对，我的机票已经买好了。 (Duì, wǒ de jīpiào yǐjīng mǎi hǎo le.) — Yes, I’ve already bought my plane ticket.

**A:** 你想打车还是坐地铁？ (Nǐ xiǎng dǎchē háishi zuò dìtiě?) — Do you want a taxi or to take the metro?

**B:** 先坐公交车到车站，再坐地铁。 (Xiān zuò gōngjiāochē dào chēzhàn, zài zuò dìtiě.) — First I’ll take the bus to the station, then the metro.

## 2. How the language works

### A journey with transfers

Use 先 (xiān) and 再 (zài) to describe successive stages of a trip: first one vehicle, then another.

**Pattern:** 先 + action A, 再 + action B

**从酒店到车站要走多远？**
*Cóng jiǔdiàn dào chēzhàn yào zǒu duō yuǎn?*
How far is it to walk from the hotel to the station?

**对，我的机票已经买好了。**
*Duì, wǒ de jīpiào yǐjīng mǎi hǎo le.*
Yes, I’ve already bought my plane ticket.

### Travel tickets and destinations

机票 (jīpiào) is an air ticket; 票 (piào) is a general ticket. Use 去 (qù) followed by the destination.

**Pattern:** 去 + destination / 买 + 票

**我想买两张地铁票。**
*Wǒ xiǎng mǎi liǎng zhāng dìtiě piào.*
I would like to buy two metro tickets.

**先坐公交车到车站，再坐地铁。**
*Xiān zuò gōngjiāochē dào chēzhàn, zài zuò dìtiě.*
First I’ll take the bus to the station, then the metro.

## 4. Read for meaning — Planning a Journey — short reading

**周末，小张想去外地旅游。他先在网上看酒店和机票，最后买了票。**
*Zhōumò, Xiǎo Zhāng xiǎng qù wàidì lǚyóu. Tā xiān zài wǎngshang kàn jiǔdiàn hé jīpiào, zuìhòu mǎi le piào.*
On the weekend, Xiao Zhang wants to travel to another region. He checks hotels and air tickets online, then buys a ticket.

**出国那天，他在路上等公交车，后来决定打车去机场。**
*Chūguó nà tiān, tā zài lùshang děng gōngjiāochē, hòulái juédìng dǎchē qù jīchǎng.*
On the day of his trip abroad, he waits for the bus, then decides to take a taxi to the airport.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: On the weekend, Xiao Zhang wants to travel to another region. He checks hotels and air tickets online, then buys a ticket.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: On the day of his trip abroad, he waits for the bus, then decides to take a taxi to the airport.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: I would like to buy two metro tickets.

*Possible response:* **我想买两张地铁票。**
*Wǒ xiǎng mǎi liǎng zhāng dìtiě piào.*
I would like to buy two metro tickets.

## 6. Watch out

车站 (chēzhàn) is a station; 机场 (jīchǎng) is an airport. 站 (zhàn) alone has other uses, so retain context.

**Pronunciation:** Note the fourth tone in 站 (zhàn), and the first tone in 机票 (jīpiào) starts with jī.
