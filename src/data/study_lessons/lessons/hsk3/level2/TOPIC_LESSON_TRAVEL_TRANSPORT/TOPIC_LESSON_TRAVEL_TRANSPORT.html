Stations, tickets and getting around

1 · Conversation — Planning a Journey

Buy transport tickets and describe a journey by metro, bus or taxi.

A

你明天去机场吗？ (Nǐ míngtiān qù jīchǎng ma?)

Are you going to the airport tomorrow?

B

对，我的机票已经买好了。 (Duì, wǒ de jīpiào yǐjīng mǎi hǎo le.)

Yes, I’ve already bought my plane ticket.

A

你想打车还是坐地铁？ (Nǐ xiǎng dǎchē háishi zuò dìtiě?)

Do you want a taxi or to take the metro?

B

先坐公交车到车站，再坐地铁。 (Xiān zuò gōngjiāochē dào chēzhàn, zài zuò dìtiě.)

First I’ll take the bus to the station, then the metro.

2 · How the language works

A journey with transfers

Use 先 (xiān) and 再 (zài) to describe successive stages of a trip: first one vehicle, then another.

先 + 第一个动作，再 + 第二个动作
xiān + dì yī ge dòngzuò, zài + dì èr ge dòngzuò
先 + <action A>，再 + <action B>

从酒店到车站要走多远？ (Cóng jiǔdiàn dào chēzhàn yào zǒu duō yuǎn?)

How far is it to walk from the hotel to the station?

对，我的机票已经买好了。 (Duì, wǒ de jīpiào yǐjīng mǎi hǎo le.)

Yes, I’ve already bought my plane ticket.

Travel tickets and destinations

机票 (jīpiào) is an air ticket; 票 (piào) is a general ticket. Use 去 (qù) followed by the destination.

去 + 目的地 / 买 + 票
qù + mùdìdì / mǎi + piào
去 + <destination> / 买 + 票

我想买两张地铁票。 (Wǒ xiǎng mǎi liǎng zhāng dìtiě piào.)

I would like to buy two metro tickets.

先坐公交车到车站，再坐地铁。 (Xiān zuò gōngjiāochē dào chēzhàn, zài zuò dìtiě.)

First I’ll take the bus to the station, then the metro.

4 · Read for meaning — Planning a Journey — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

周末，小张想去外地旅游。他先在网上看酒店和机票，最后买了票。 (Zhōumò, Xiǎo Zhāng xiǎng qù wàidì lǚyóu. Tā xiān zài wǎngshang kàn jiǔdiàn hé jīpiào, zuìhòu mǎi le piào.)

On the weekend, Xiao Zhang wants to travel to another region. He checks hotels and air tickets online, then buys a ticket.

出国那天，他在路上等公交车，后来决定打车去机场。 (Chūguó nà tiān, tā zài lùshang děng gōngjiāochē, hòulái juédìng dǎchē qù jīchǎng.)

On the day of his trip abroad, he waits for the bus, then decides to take a taxi to the airport.

6 · Watch out

车站 (chēzhàn) is a station; 机场 (jīchǎng) is an airport. 站 (zhàn) alone has other uses, so retain context.

Pronunciation clinic

Note the fourth tone in 站 (zhàn), and the first tone in 机票 (jīpiào) starts with jī.
