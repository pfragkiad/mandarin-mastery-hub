# Counting Things and Occasions

Count repeated events and use everyday measure words for people, rooms and long objects.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Use 次 (cì) to count occurrences.

- Choose a measure word for a person or long object.

- Understand larger numbers such as 万 (wàn).

## 1. Conversation — Counting Things and Occasions

Count repeated events and use everyday measure words for people, rooms and long objects.

**A:** 你来过这家饭馆几次？ (Nǐ lái guo zhè jiā fànguǎn jǐ cì?) — How many times have you been to this restaurant?

**B:** 我来过两次。这儿有很多人。 (Wǒ lái guo liǎng cì. Zhèr yǒu hěn duō rén.) — I have been here twice. There are many people here.

**A:** 我们有几位客人？ (Wǒmen yǒu jǐ wèi kèrén?) — How many guests do we have?

**B:** 三位。请给我们一间安静的房间。 (Sān wèi. Qǐng gěi wǒmen yì jiān ānjìng de fángjiān.) — Three. Please give us a quiet room.

## 2. How the language works

### Counting repeated actions

Use 次 (cì) after a verb phrase to count how many times an action happened.

**Pattern**

动作 + 数字 + 次  
*dòngzuò + shùzì + cì*  
&lt;action&gt; + &lt;number&gt; + **次**

**我每周来这个地方两次。**
*Wǒ měi zhōu lái zhè ge dìfang liǎng cì.*
I come to this place twice a week.

**我来过两次。这儿有很多人。**
*Wǒ lái guo liǎng cì. Zhèr yǒu hěn duō rén.*
I have been here twice. There are many people here.

### Choosing a classifier

Use 间 (jiān) for rooms, 位 (wèi) politely for people, and 条 (tiáo) for many long, narrow things.

**Pattern**

数字 + 量词 + 名词  
*shùzì + liàngcí + míngcí*  
&lt;number&gt; + &lt;classifier&gt; + &lt;noun&gt;

**这里有四间教室。**
*Zhèlǐ yǒu sì jiān jiàoshì.*
There are four classrooms here.

**三位。请给我们一间安静的房间。**
*Sān wèi. Qǐng gěi wǒmen yì jiān ānjìng de fángjiān.*
Three. Please give us a quiet room.

## 4. Read for meaning — Counting Things and Occasions — short reading

**学校里有很多名学生。每个星期，他们都去图书馆看书。**
*Xuéxiào lǐ yǒu hěn duō míng xuéshēng. Měi ge xīngqī, tāmen dōu qù túshūguǎn kàn shū.*
There are many students at the school. Every week, they go to the library to read.

**老师拿来一条很长的纸，上面写着：“一万。”学生们一起读这个数字。**
*Lǎoshī ná lái yì tiáo hěn cháng de zhǐ, shàngmian xiězhe: “Yí wàn.” Xuéshēngmen yìqǐ dú zhè ge shùzì.*
The teacher brings a long strip of paper bearing the number “ten thousand,” which the students read together.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: There are many students at the school. Every week, they go to the library to read.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: The teacher brings a long strip of paper bearing the number “ten thousand,” which the students read together.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Explain to a classmate: I come to this place twice a week.

*Possible response:* **我每周来这个地方两次。**
*Wǒ měi zhōu lái zhè ge dìfang liǎng cì.*
I come to this place twice a week.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: There are four classrooms here.

*Possible response:* **这里有四间教室。**
*Zhèlǐ yǒu sì jiān jiàoshì.*
There are four classrooms here.

## 6. Watch out

A classifier is not interchangeable with every noun. For example, a room uses 一间 (yì jiān), not 一位 (yí wèi).

**Pronunciation:** Listen for the fourth tone of 次 (cì) and the neutral ending of 每个 (měi ge).
