Counting Things and Occasions

Times, measures and polite counters

numbers-quantities · #times · #hsk-level-2 · #topic-study

Your goal

  • Use 次 (cì) to count occurrences.
  • Choose a measure word for a person or long object.
  • Understand larger numbers such as 万 (wàn).

1 · Conversation — Counting Things and Occasions

Count repeated events and use everyday measure words for people, rooms and long objects.

A

你来过这家饭馆几次？ (Nǐ lái guo zhè jiā fànguǎn jǐ cì?)

How many times have you been to this restaurant?

B

我来过两次。这儿有很多人。 (Wǒ lái guo liǎng cì. Zhèr yǒu hěn duō rén.)

I have been here twice. There are many people here.

A

我们有几位客人？ (Wǒmen yǒu jǐ wèi kèrén?)

How many guests do we have?

B

三位。请给我们一间安静的房间。 (Sān wèi. Qǐng gěi wǒmen yì jiān ānjìng de fángjiān.)

Three. Please give us a quiet room.

2 · How the language works

Counting repeated actions

Use 次 (cì) after a verb phrase to count how many times an action happened.

动作 + 数字 + 次
dòngzuò + shùzì + cì
<action> + <number> + 次

我每周来这个地方两次。 (Wǒ měi zhōu lái zhè ge dìfang liǎng cì.)

I come to this place twice a week.

我来过两次。这儿有很多人。 (Wǒ lái guo liǎng cì. Zhèr yǒu hěn duō rén.)

I have been here twice. There are many people here.

Choosing a classifier

Use 间 (jiān) for rooms, 位 (wèi) politely for people, and 条 (tiáo) for many long, narrow things.

数字 + 量词 + 名词
shùzì + liàngcí + míngcí
<number> + <classifier> + <noun>

这里有四间教室。 (Zhèlǐ yǒu sì jiān jiàoshì.)

There are four classrooms here.

三位。请给我们一间安静的房间。 (Sān wèi. Qǐng gěi wǒmen yì jiān ānjìng de fángjiān.)

Three. Please give us a quiet room.

6 · Watch out

A classifier is not interchangeable with every noun. For example, a room uses 一间 (yì jiān), not 一位 (yí wèi).

Pronunciation clinic

Listen for the fourth tone of 次 (cì) and the neutral ending of 每个 (měi ge).
