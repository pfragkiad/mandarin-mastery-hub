Asking for Help and Making Introductions

Polite requests and helpful answers

communication-social · #polite-requests-and-helpful-answers · #hsk-level-2 · #topic-study

Your goal

  • Ask for help politely with 帮忙 (bāngmáng).
  • Use 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi) before an interruption or apology.
  • Introduce others and relay simple information.

1 · Conversation — Asking for Help and Making Introductions

Ask for assistance, apologize politely, introduce a friend and pass along information.

A

不好意思，你能帮我一个忙吗？ (Bù hǎoyìsi, nǐ néng bāng wǒ yí ge máng ma?)

Excuse me, can you help me with something?

B

当然可以！你想让我做什么？ (Dāngrán kěyǐ! Nǐ xiǎng ràng wǒ zuò shénme?)

Of course! What would you like me to do?

A

请告诉我教室在哪儿。 (Qǐng gàosu wǒ jiàoshì zài nǎr.)

Please tell me where the classroom is.

B

我带你去，还可以介绍我的朋友。 (Wǒ dài nǐ qù, hái kěyǐ jièshào wǒ de péngyou.)

I will take you there and can introduce my friend too.

2 · How the language works

Making a polite request

Use 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi) as a polite opening, then ask someone to 帮忙 (bāngmáng) or 帮 (bāng) you with a specific action.

不好意思 + request

请帮我拿一下这个包。 (Qǐng bāng wǒ ná yíxià zhè ge bāo.)

Please help me carry this bag.

当然可以！你想让我做什么？ (Dāngrán kěyǐ! Nǐ xiǎng ràng wǒ zuò shénme?)

Of course! What would you like me to do?

Telling and introducing

告诉 (gàosu) introduces information shared with someone, while 介绍 (jièshào) introduces a person or explains something to someone.

告诉 + person + information

我来介绍一下：这是我的同学。 (Wǒ lái jièshào yíxià: zhè shì wǒ de tóngxué.)

Let me introduce someone: this is my classmate.

我带你去，还可以介绍我的朋友。 (Wǒ dài nǐ qù, hái kěyǐ jièshào wǒ de péngyou.)

I will take you there and can introduce my friend too.

小张第一次来学校，不知道教室在哪儿。小李主动来帮忙。 (Xiǎo Zhāng dì yī cì lái xuéxiào, bù zhīdào jiàoshì zài nǎr. Xiǎo Lǐ zhǔdòng lái bāngmáng.)

6 · Watch out

帮忙 (bāngmáng) commonly means lend a hand; 帮 (bāng) can take a person and the action directly. Keep the person and requested action clear.

Pronunciation clinic

Practise the neutral ending of 告诉 (gàosu) and the fourth tone in 介绍 (jièshào).
