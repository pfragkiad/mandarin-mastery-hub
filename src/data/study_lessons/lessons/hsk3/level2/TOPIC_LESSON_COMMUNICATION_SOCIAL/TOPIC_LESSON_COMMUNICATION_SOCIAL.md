# Asking for Help and Making Introductions

Ask for assistance, apologize politely, introduce a friend and pass along information.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask for help politely with 帮忙 (bāngmáng).

- Use 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi) before an interruption or apology.

- Introduce others and relay simple information.

## 1. Conversation — Asking for Help and Making Introductions

Ask for assistance, apologize politely, introduce a friend and pass along information.

**A:** 不好意思，你能帮我一个忙吗？ (Bù hǎoyìsi, nǐ néng bāng wǒ yí ge máng ma?) — Excuse me, can you help me with something?

**B:** 当然可以！你想让我做什么？ (Dāngrán kěyǐ! Nǐ xiǎng ràng wǒ zuò shénme?) — Of course! What would you like me to do?

**A:** 请告诉我教室在哪儿。 (Qǐng gàosu wǒ jiàoshì zài nǎr.) — Please tell me where the classroom is.

**B:** 我带你去，还可以介绍我的朋友。 (Wǒ dài nǐ qù, hái kěyǐ jièshào wǒ de péngyou.) — I will take you there and can introduce my friend too.

## 2. How the language works

### Making a polite request

Use 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi) as a polite opening, then ask someone to 帮忙 (bāngmáng) or 帮 (bāng) you with a specific action.

**Pattern:** 不好意思 + request

**请帮我拿一下这个包。**
*Qǐng bāng wǒ ná yíxià zhè ge bāo.*
Please help me carry this bag.

**当然可以！你想让我做什么？**
*Dāngrán kěyǐ! Nǐ xiǎng ràng wǒ zuò shénme?*
Of course! What would you like me to do?

### Telling and introducing

告诉 (gàosu) introduces information shared with someone, while 介绍 (jièshào) introduces a person or explains something to someone.

**Pattern:** 告诉 + person + information

**我来介绍一下：这是我的同学。**
*Wǒ lái jièshào yíxià: zhè shì wǒ de tóngxué.*
Let me introduce someone: this is my classmate.

**我带你去，还可以介绍我的朋友。**
*Wǒ dài nǐ qù, hái kěyǐ jièshào wǒ de péngyou.*
I will take you there and can introduce my friend too.

## 4. Read for meaning — Asking for Help and Making Introductions — short reading

**小张第一次来学校，不知道教室在哪儿。小李主动来帮忙。**
*Xiǎo Zhāng dì yī cì lái xuéxiào, bù zhīdào jiàoshì zài nǎr. Xiǎo Lǐ zhǔdòng lái bāngmáng.*
Xiao Zhang is at school for the first time and does not know where the classroom is. Xiao Li offers to help.

**老师让小李介绍新同学。大家听了以后都笑了，因为新同学说话很有意思。**
*Lǎoshī ràng Xiǎo Lǐ jièshào xīn tóngxué. Dàjiā tīng le yǐhòu dōu xiào le, yīnwèi xīn tóngxué shuōhuà hěn yǒu yìsi.*
The teacher asks Xiao Li to introduce the new classmate. Everyone laughs because the newcomer speaks amusingly.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiao Zhang is at school for the first time and does not know where the classroom is. Xiao Li offers to help.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: The teacher asks Xiao Li to introduce the new classmate. Everyone laughs because the newcomer speaks amusingly.

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

*Possible response:* **请帮我拿一下这个包。**
*Qǐng bāng wǒ ná yíxià zhè ge bāo.*

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: Let me introduce someone: this is my classmate.

*Possible response:* **我来介绍一下：这是我的同学。**
*Wǒ lái jièshào yíxià: zhè shì wǒ de tóngxué.*
Let me introduce someone: this is my classmate.

## 6. Watch out

帮忙 (bāngmáng) commonly means lend a hand; 帮 (bāng) can take a person and the action directly. Keep the person and requested action clear.

**Pronunciation:** Practise the neutral ending of 告诉 (gàosu) and the fourth tone in 介绍 (jièshào).
