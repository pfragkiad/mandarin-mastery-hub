# How Are You Feeling?

Describe pain and tiredness, ask about health and suggest exercise or rest.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Identify basic body parts and pain.

- Talk about feeling comfortable or tired.

- Explain why you are visiting a pharmacy.

## 1. Conversation — How Are You Feeling?

Describe pain and tiredness, ask about health and suggest exercise or rest.

**A:** 你怎么了？看起来很累。 (Nǐ zěnme le? Kàn qǐlái hěn lèi.) — What’s wrong? You look tired.

**B:** 我头疼，眼睛也不舒服。 (Wǒ tóu téng, yǎnjing yě bù shūfu.) — I have a headache, and my eyes feel uncomfortable too.

**A:** 那你要去药店买药吗？ (Nà nǐ yào qù yàodiàn mǎi yào ma?) — Then do you need to go to the pharmacy to buy medicine?

**B:** 我先休息一下，明天身体好了再去游泳。 (Wǒ xiān xiūxi yíxià, míngtiān shēntǐ hǎo le zài qù yóuyǒng.) — I’ll rest first and go swimming tomorrow when I feel better.

## 2. How the language works

### Describing pain

Put 疼 (téng) after a body part such as 头 (tóu) or 手 (shǒu) to describe where something hurts.

**Pattern:** Body part + 疼

**我今天很累，想早点儿睡觉。**
*Wǒ jīntiān hěn lèi, xiǎng zǎo diǎnr shuìjiào.*
I am tired today and want to sleep earlier.

**我头疼，眼睛也不舒服。**
*Wǒ tóu téng, yǎnjing yě bù shūfu.*
I have a headache, and my eyes feel uncomfortable too.

### Describing how you feel

舒服 (shūfu) means comfortable; 不舒服 (bù shūfu) means uncomfortable or unwell.

**Pattern:** Person + 不 + 舒服

**运动以后，我觉得很舒服。**
*Yùndòng yǐhòu, wǒ juéde hěn shūfu.*
I feel comfortable after exercising.

**我先休息一下，明天身体好了再去游泳。**
*Wǒ xiān xiūxi yíxià, míngtiān shēntǐ hǎo le zài qù yóuyǒng.*
I’ll rest first and go swimming tomorrow when I feel better.

## 4. Read for meaning — How Are You Feeling? — short reading

**小李平时很喜欢运动，常常跑步，有时也去游泳。**
*Xiǎo Lǐ píngshí hěn xǐhuan yùndòng, chángcháng pǎobù, yǒushí yě qù yóuyǒng.*
Xiao Li usually likes exercise, often runs and sometimes swims.

**今天他的手有点儿疼，身体不太舒服，所以决定休息一天。**
*Jīntiān tā de shǒu yǒudiǎnr téng, shēntǐ bú tài shūfu, suǒyǐ juédìng xiūxi yì tiān.*
Today his hand hurts a bit and he feels unwell, so he decides to rest for a day.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiao Li usually likes exercise, often runs and sometimes swims.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Today his hand hurts a bit and he feels unwell, so he decides to rest for a day.

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: I feel comfortable after exercising.

I feel comfortable after exercising.

## 6. Watch out

疼 (téng) identifies pain; 累 (lèi) identifies tiredness. Do not use them as interchangeable descriptions.

**Pronunciation:** Practise the neutral endings of 舒服 (shūfu) and 眼睛 (yǎnjing).
