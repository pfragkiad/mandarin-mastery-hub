Choosing Drinks and Food

Ordering tea, coffee and a meal

food-drink · #ordering-tea · #hsk-level-2 · #topic-study

Your goal

  • Order beverages such as 红茶 (hóngchá) and 奶茶 (nǎichá).
  • Talk about meat and fish as foods.
  • Use 点 (diǎn) to order food.

1 · Conversation — Choosing Drinks and Food

Read a simple menu and order different drinks and dishes at a restaurant.

A

欢迎！你们想点什么？ (Huānyíng! Nǐmen xiǎng diǎn shénme?)

Welcome! What would you like to order?

B

我要一杯绿茶，朋友想喝奶茶。 (Wǒ yào yì bēi lǜchá, péngyou xiǎng hē nǎichá.)

I’ll have a cup of green tea, and my friend wants milk tea.

A

吃的呢？今天有鱼，也有肉。 (Chī de ne? Jīntiān yǒu yú, yě yǒu ròu.)

And for food? Today we have fish and meat.

B

我要鱼。请再给我一杯咖啡。 (Wǒ yào yú. Qǐng zài gěi wǒ yì bēi kāfēi.)

I’ll have the fish. Please bring me a coffee too.

2 · How the language works

Ordering at a restaurant

点 (diǎn) is a verb meaning to order in a restaurant, as well as having other uses in the language.

Person + 点 + food/drink

你想喝红茶还是绿茶？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē hóngchá háishi lǜchá?)

Would you like black tea or green tea?

我要一杯绿茶，朋友想喝奶茶。 (Wǒ yào yì bēi lǜchá, péngyou xiǎng hē nǎichá.)

I’ll have a cup of green tea, and my friend wants milk tea.

Kinds of tea and drinks

Use 红茶 (hóngchá), 绿茶 (lǜchá), 奶茶 (nǎichá), or 咖啡 (kāfēi) as the ordered drink.

一杯 + drink

我想点一杯咖啡。 (Wǒ xiǎng diǎn yì bēi kāfēi.)

I would like to order a coffee.

我要鱼。请再给我一杯咖啡。 (Wǒ yào yú. Qǐng zài gěi wǒ yì bēi kāfēi.)

I’ll have the fish. Please bring me a coffee too.

4 · Read for meaning — Choosing Drinks and Food — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

晚上，小王和朋友走进一家饭馆。小王喝红茶，朋友点了一杯奶茶。 (Wǎnshang, Xiǎo Wáng hé péngyou zǒu jìn yì jiā fànguǎn. Xiǎo Wáng hē hóngchá, péngyou diǎn le yì bēi nǎichá.)

In the evening, Xiao Wang and a friend enter a restaurant. Xiao Wang drinks black tea, and the friend orders milk tea.

他们一起吃了鱼和肉，觉得这家饭馆的菜不错。 (Tāmen yìqǐ chī le yú hé ròu, juéde zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài búcuò.)

They eat fish and meat together and think the restaurant’s food is pretty good.

6 · Watch out

The colors in 红茶 (hóngchá) and 绿茶 (lǜchá) identify established types of tea; do not infer the English beverage name from the color alone.

Pronunciation clinic

Distinguish kāfēi (咖啡) from fànguǎn (饭馆); 茶 (chá) is second tone.
