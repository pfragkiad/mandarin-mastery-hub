# Choosing Drinks and Food

Read a simple menu and order different drinks and dishes at a restaurant.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Order beverages such as 红茶 (hóngchá) and 奶茶 (nǎichá).

- Talk about meat and fish as foods.

- Use 点 (diǎn) to order food.

## 1. Conversation — Choosing Drinks and Food

Read a simple menu and order different drinks and dishes at a restaurant.

**A:** 欢迎！你们想点什么？ (Huānyíng! Nǐmen xiǎng diǎn shénme?) — Welcome! What would you like to order?

**B:** 我要一杯绿茶，朋友想喝奶茶。 (Wǒ yào yì bēi lǜchá, péngyou xiǎng hē nǎichá.) — I’ll have a cup of green tea, and my friend wants milk tea.

**A:** 吃的呢？今天有鱼，也有肉。 (Chī de ne? Jīntiān yǒu yú, yě yǒu ròu.) — And for food? Today we have fish and meat.

**B:** 我要鱼。请再给我一杯咖啡。 (Wǒ yào yú. Qǐng zài gěi wǒ yì bēi kāfēi.) — I’ll have the fish. Please bring me a coffee too.

## 2. How the language works

### Ordering at a restaurant

点 (diǎn) is a verb meaning to order in a restaurant, as well as having other uses in the language.

**Pattern**

人 + 点 + 食物/饮料  
*rén + diǎn + shíwù/yǐnliào*  
&lt;person&gt; + **点** + &lt;food&gt;/&lt;drink&gt;

**你想喝红茶还是绿茶？**
*Nǐ xiǎng hē hóngchá háishi lǜchá?*
Would you like black tea or green tea?

**我要一杯绿茶，朋友想喝奶茶。**
*Wǒ yào yì bēi lǜchá, péngyou xiǎng hē nǎichá.*
I’ll have a cup of green tea, and my friend wants milk tea.

### Kinds of tea and drinks

Use 红茶 (hóngchá), 绿茶 (lǜchá), 奶茶 (nǎichá), or 咖啡 (kāfēi) as the ordered drink.

**Pattern**

一杯 + 饮料  
*yì bēi + yǐnliào*  
**一杯** + &lt;drink&gt;

**我想点一杯咖啡。**
*Wǒ xiǎng diǎn yì bēi kāfēi.*
I would like to order a coffee.

**我要鱼。请再给我一杯咖啡。**
*Wǒ yào yú. Qǐng zài gěi wǒ yì bēi kāfēi.*
I’ll have the fish. Please bring me a coffee too.

## 4. Read for meaning — Choosing Drinks and Food — short reading

**晚上，小王和朋友走进一家饭馆。小王喝红茶，朋友点了一杯奶茶。**
*Wǎnshang, Xiǎo Wáng hé péngyou zǒu jìn yì jiā fànguǎn. Xiǎo Wáng hē hóngchá, péngyou diǎn le yì bēi nǎichá.*
In the evening, Xiao Wang and a friend enter a restaurant. Xiao Wang drinks black tea, and the friend orders milk tea.

**他们一起吃了鱼和肉，觉得这家饭馆的菜不错。**
*Tāmen yìqǐ chī le yú hé ròu, juéde zhè jiā fànguǎn de cài búcuò.*
They eat fish and meat together and think the restaurant’s food is pretty good.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: In the evening, Xiao Wang and a friend enter a restaurant. Xiao Wang drinks black tea, and the friend orders milk tea.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: They eat fish and meat together and think the restaurant’s food is pretty good.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Explain to a classmate: Would you like black tea or green tea?

*Possible response:* **你想喝红茶还是绿茶？**
*Nǐ xiǎng hē hóngchá háishi lǜchá?*
Would you like black tea or green tea?

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: I would like to order a coffee.

*Possible response:* **我想点一杯咖啡。**
*Wǒ xiǎng diǎn yì bēi kāfēi.*
I would like to order a coffee.

## 6. Watch out

The colors in 红茶 (hóngchá) and 绿茶 (lǜchá) identify established types of tea; do not infer the English beverage name from the color alone.

**Pronunciation:** Distinguish kāfēi (咖啡) from fànguǎn (饭馆); 茶 (chá) is second tone.
