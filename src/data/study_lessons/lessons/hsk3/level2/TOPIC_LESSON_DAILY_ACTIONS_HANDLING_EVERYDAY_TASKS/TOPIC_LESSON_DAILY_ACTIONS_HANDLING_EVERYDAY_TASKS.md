# Daily Actions & Activities — Part 1: Helping with Everyday Tasks

**Subtopics:** physical-actions

Ask someone to help with simple tasks, handle objects and complete everyday actions.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Use 帮 (bāng) to offer or request help.

- Give instructions with 打开 (dǎkāi) and 拿 (ná).

- Describe waiting, moving and finishing a task.

## 1. Conversation — Helping with Everyday Tasks

Ask someone to help with simple tasks, handle objects and complete everyday actions.

**A:** 请帮我打开门，好吗？ (Qǐng bāng wǒ dǎkāi mén, hǎo ma?) — Please help me open the door, okay?

**B:** 好，我先把包拿起来。 (Hǎo, wǒ xiān bǎ bāo ná qǐlái.) — Sure, I’ll pick up the bag first.

**A:** 谢谢！你等我一下，我洗完手就来。 (Xièxie! Nǐ děng wǒ yíxià, wǒ xǐ wán shǒu jiù lái.) — Thanks! Wait a moment; I’ll come as soon as I finish washing my hands.

**B:** 没问题。我把这些东西送进去。 (Méi wèntí. Wǒ bǎ zhèxiē dōngxi sòng jìnqù.) — No problem. I’ll take these things inside.

## 2. How the language works

### Helping someone do an action

帮 (bāng) can precede a person and the action you assist them with.

**Pattern:** 帮 + person + verb

**请帮我拿一下这本书。**
*Qǐng bāng wǒ ná yíxià zhè běn shū.*
Please help me take this book.

**好，我先把包拿起来。**
*Hǎo, wǒ xiān bǎ bāo ná qǐlái.*
Sure, I’ll pick up the bag first.

### Finishing one step

完 (wán) follows a verb to indicate completion, as in 洗完 (xǐ wán).

**Pattern:** Verb + 完 + object

**我洗完衣服再出去。**
*Wǒ xǐ wán yīfu zài chūqù.*
I’ll go out after I finish washing my clothes.

**没问题。我把这些东西送进去。**
*Méi wèntí. Wǒ bǎ zhèxiē dōngxi sòng jìnqù.*
No problem. I’ll take these things inside.

## 4. Read for meaning — Helping with Everyday Tasks — short reading

**今天小刘很忙。早上他先打开窗户，再拿来一杯水。**
*Jīntiān Xiǎo Liú hěn máng. Zǎoshang tā xiān dǎkāi chuānghu, zài ná lái yì bēi shuǐ.*
Xiao Liu is busy today. In the morning he opens the window and brings a glass of water.

**下午他帮朋友送书，等大家都走了才把门关上。**
*Xiàwǔ tā bāng péngyou sòng shū, děng dàjiā dōu zǒu le cái bǎ mén guān shàng.*
In the afternoon he helps a friend deliver books, and shuts the door after everyone leaves.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiao Liu is busy today. In the morning he opens the window and brings a glass of water.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: In the afternoon he helps a friend deliver books, and shuts the door after everyone leaves.

## 6. Watch out

打开 (dǎkāi) means open or switch on depending on the object; it should not be used without regard to what is being opened.

**Pronunciation:** Contrast 拿 (ná), second tone, and 打开 (dǎkāi), two third/first tones.
