## 1. Conversation — Routines and Making Plans

Talk about personal schedules, remembered tasks, and getting ready to leave.

**A:** 你准备好了吗？我们一起出门吧。 (Nǐ zhǔnbèi hǎo le ma? Wǒmen yìqǐ chūmén ba.) — Are you ready? Let’s leave together.

**B:** 等一下，我忘了拿书包。 (Děng yíxià, wǒ wàng le ná shūbāo.) — Wait, I forgot to take my schoolbag.

**A:** 你记得今天下午要做什么吗？ (Nǐ jìde jīntiān xiàwǔ yào zuò shénme ma?) — Do you remember what we need to do this afternoon?

**B:** 记得！先去学校，然后一起跑步。 (Jìde! Xiān qù xuéxiào, ránhòu yìqǐ pǎobù.) — Yes! First we go to school, then we run together.

## 2. How the language works

### Remembering and forgetting

记得 (jìde) means remember; 忘 (wàng) means forget. Place a following verb phrase after them when describing what happened.

**Pattern**

记得 + 动词短语 / 忘了 + 动词短语  
*jìde + dòngcí duǎnyǔ / wàng le + dòngcí duǎnyǔ*  
**记得** + &lt;verb phrase&gt; / **忘了** + &lt;verb phrase&gt;

**我记得明天要考试。**
*Wǒ jìde míngtiān yào kǎoshì.*
I remember we have an exam tomorrow.

**等一下，我忘了拿书包。**
*Děng yíxià, wǒ wàng le ná shūbāo.*
Wait, I forgot to take my schoolbag.

### Doing an activity together

一起 (yìqǐ) comes before the verb phrase when multiple people do something together.

**Pattern**

人们 + 一起 + 动词  
*rénmen + yìqǐ + dòngcí*  
&lt;people&gt; + **一起** + &lt;verb&gt;

**我们准备好了，可以走了。**
*Wǒmen zhǔnbèi hǎo le, kěyǐ zǒu le.*
We’re ready; we can go.

**记得！先去学校，然后一起跑步。**
*Jìde! Xiān qù xuéxiào, ránhòu yìqǐ pǎobù.*
Yes! First we go to school, then we run together.

## 4. Read for meaning — Routines and Making Plans — short reading

**小华每天早上七点出门。他不喜欢坐车，经常走路去学校。**
*Xiǎo Huá měitiān zǎoshang qī diǎn chūmén. Tā bù xǐhuan zuò chē, jīngcháng zǒulù qù xuéxiào.*
Xiaohua leaves home at seven every morning. He does not like taking a vehicle and often walks to school.

**星期天朋友们准备去公园，大家一起跑了很远，最后走回家。**
*Xīngqītiān péngyoumen zhǔnbèi qù gōngyuán, dàjiā yìqǐ pǎo le hěn yuǎn, zuìhòu zǒu huí jiā.*
On Sunday, friends prepare to go to the park. They run a long way together and finally walk home.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiaohua leaves home at seven every morning. He does not like taking a vehicle and often walks to school.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: On Sunday, friends prepare to go to the park. They run a long way together and finally walk home.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: We’re ready; we can go.

## 6. Watch out

走路 (zǒulù) specifically refers to walking, while 走 (zǒu) has broader motion meanings; 跑 (pǎo) means run.

**Pronunciation:** The second syllable of 记得 (jìde) is neutral; 一起 (yìqǐ) has a fourth-tone 一 before the third tone.
