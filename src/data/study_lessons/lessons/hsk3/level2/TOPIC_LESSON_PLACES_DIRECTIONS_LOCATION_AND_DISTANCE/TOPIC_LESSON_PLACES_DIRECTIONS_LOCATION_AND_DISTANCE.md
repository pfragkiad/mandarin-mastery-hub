# Places, Position & Direction — Part 1: Describing Location and Distance

**Subtopics:** position-direction

Locate people and objects in buildings and compare relative positions.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Use 旁边 (pángbiān), 前面 (qiánmiàn) and 后面 (hòumiàn).

- Ask whether a place is near or far.

- Locate a place above, below or inside another.

## 1. Conversation — Describing Location and Distance

Locate people and objects in buildings and compare relative positions.

**A:** 请问，教室在几楼？ (Qǐngwèn, jiàoshì zài jǐ lóu?) — Excuse me, which floor is the classroom on?

**B:** 在二楼。门就在左边。 (Zài èr lóu. Mén jiù zài zuǒbian.) — On the second floor. The door is on the left.

**A:** 从这儿走远吗？ (Cóng zhèr zǒu yuǎn ma?) — Is it far to walk from here?

**B:** 不远，就在洗手间旁边，办公室前面。 (Bù yuǎn, jiù zài xǐshǒujiān pángbiān, bàngōngshì qiánmiàn.) — Not far, next to the restroom, in front of the office.

## 2. How the language works

### Relative positions

Place 旁边 (pángbiān), 左边 (zuǒbian), 右边 (yòubian) or another position term after a landmark to describe a location.

**Pattern:** Landmark + position + 有/是 + place

**我的书在桌子下面。**
*Wǒ de shū zài zhuōzi xiàmiàn.*
My book is under the table.

**在二楼。门就在左边。**
*Zài èr lóu. Mén jiù zài zuǒbian.*
On the second floor. The door is on the left.

### Distance from a landmark

离 (lí) introduces a reference point; combine it with 近 (jìn) or 远 (yuǎn).

**Pattern:** Place A + 离 + place B + 近/远

**我们家离学校很近。**
*Wǒmen jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn.*
Our home is close to the school.

**不远，就在洗手间旁边，办公室前面。**
*Bù yuǎn, jiù zài xǐshǒujiān pángbiān, bàngōngshì qiánmiàn.*
Not far, next to the restroom, in front of the office.

## 4. Read for meaning — Describing Location and Distance — short reading

**学校里面有两座楼。第一座楼比较高，后面那座楼比较小。**
*Xuéxiào lǐmiàn yǒu liǎng zuò lóu. Dì yī zuò lóu bǐjiào gāo, hòumiàn nà zuò lóu bǐjiào xiǎo.*
There are two buildings inside the school. The first is taller, and the one behind is smaller.

**图书馆在右边的楼上面，离大门很近；车站在学校外面，离这儿有点儿远。**
*Túshūguǎn zài yòubian de lóu shàngmiàn, lí dà mén hěn jìn; chēzhàn zài xuéxiào wàimiàn, lí zhèr yǒudiǎnr yuǎn.*
The library is upstairs in the building on the right, close to the main gate. The station is outside the school and a little far away.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: There are two buildings inside the school. The first is taller, and the one behind is smaller.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: The library is upstairs in the building on the right, close to the main gate. The station is outside the school and a little far away.

## 6. Watch out

Do not confuse 近 (jìn), near, with 进 (jìn), enter. They have the same pinyin but different written characters and functions.

**Pronunciation:** Contrast 左 (zuǒ), third tone, and 右 (yòu), fourth tone; 里面 (lǐmiàn) has two full-toned syllables.
