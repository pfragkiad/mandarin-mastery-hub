Near, far, left, right and inside

1 · Conversation — Describing Location and Distance

Locate people and objects in buildings and compare relative positions.

A

请问，教室在几楼？ (Qǐngwèn, jiàoshì zài jǐ lóu?)

Excuse me, which floor is the classroom on?

B

在二楼。门就在左边。 (Zài èr lóu. Mén jiù zài zuǒbian.)

On the second floor. The door is on the left.

A

从这儿走远吗？ (Cóng zhèr zǒu yuǎn ma?)

Is it far to walk from here?

B

不远，就在洗手间旁边，办公室前面。 (Bù yuǎn, jiù zài xǐshǒujiān pángbiān, bàngōngshì qiánmiàn.)

Not far, next to the restroom, in front of the office.

2 · How the language works

Relative positions

Place 旁边 (pángbiān), 左边 (zuǒbian), 右边 (yòubian) or another position term after a landmark to describe a location.

地标 + 方位 + 有/是 + 地点
dìbiāo + fāngwèi + yǒu/shì + dìdiǎn
<landmark> + <position> + 有/是 + <place>

我的书在桌子下面。 (Wǒ de shū zài zhuōzi xiàmiàn.)

My book is under the table.

在二楼。门就在左边。 (Zài èr lóu. Mén jiù zài zuǒbian.)

On the second floor. The door is on the left.

Distance from a landmark

离 (lí) introduces a reference point; combine it with 近 (jìn) or 远 (yuǎn).

地点甲 + 离 + 地点乙 + 近/远
dìdiǎn jiǎ + lí + dìdiǎn yǐ + jìn/yuǎn
<place A> + 离 + <place B> + 近/远

我们家离学校很近。 (Wǒmen jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn.)

Our home is close to the school.

不远，就在洗手间旁边，办公室前面。 (Bù yuǎn, jiù zài xǐshǒujiān pángbiān, bàngōngshì qiánmiàn.)

Not far, next to the restroom, in front of the office.

4 · Read for meaning — Describing Location and Distance — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

学校里面有两座楼。第一座楼比较高，后面那座楼比较小。 (Xuéxiào lǐmiàn yǒu liǎng zuò lóu. Dì yī zuò lóu bǐjiào gāo, hòumiàn nà zuò lóu bǐjiào xiǎo.)

There are two buildings inside the school. The first is taller, and the one behind is smaller.

图书馆在右边的楼上面，离大门很近；车站在学校外面，离这儿有点儿远。 (Túshūguǎn zài yòubian de lóu shàngmiàn, lí dà mén hěn jìn; chēzhàn zài xuéxiào wàimiàn, lí zhèr yǒudiǎnr yuǎn.)

The library is upstairs in the building on the right, close to the main gate. The station is outside the school and a little far away.

6 · Watch out

Do not confuse 近 (jìn), near, with 进 (jìn), enter. They have the same pinyin but different written characters and functions.

Pronunciation clinic

Contrast 左 (zuǒ), third tone, and 右 (yòu), fourth tone; 里面 (lǐmiàn) has two full-toned syllables.
