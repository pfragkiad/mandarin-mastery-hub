Making Plans Together

Choices, hobbies and an afternoon with friends

leisure-media · daily-actions · grammar-functions · time-calendar · health-wellbeing · reference-questions · ↳ sports-recreation · #making-plans · #sports · #choices · #preferences

Your goal

  • Offer two alternatives with 还是 (háishi) in a question.
  • Invite someone to do an activity together using 一起 (yìqǐ).
  • Give a reason using 所以 (suǒyǐ) and request a short break using 一会儿 (yíhuìr).
  • Express a favourite activity using 最 (zuì) and discuss sports naturally.

1 · Conversation — An afternoon plan

Two classmates decide whether to play football or watch a movie, and agree on a short rest and a meeting time.

Xiaoyu

今天下午你想做什么？ (Jīntiān xiàwǔ nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme?)

What do you want to do this afternoon?

Xiaoming

我想和同学一起运动。 (Wǒ xiǎng hé tóngxué yìqǐ yùndòng.)

I want to exercise with classmates.

Xiaoyu

我们踢足球还是去看电影？ (Wǒmen tī zúqiú háishi qù kàn diànyǐng?)

Shall we play football or go to see a movie?

Xiaoming

我最喜欢足球。我们去踢足球吧！ (Wǒ zuì xǐhuan zúqiú. Wǒmen qù tī zúqiú ba!)

I like football most. Let’s go play football!

Xiaoyu

我今天有点儿累，所以想休息一会儿。 (Wǒ jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng xiūxi yíhuìr.)

I’m a little tired today, so I want to rest for a while.

Xiaoming

可以。我们休息一会儿，再一起去。 (Kěyǐ. Wǒmen xiūxi yíhuìr, zài yìqǐ qù.)

Okay. We can rest for a bit, then go together.

Xiaoyu

学校后面可以踢足球吗？ (Xuéxiào hòumiàn kěyǐ tī zúqiú ma?)

Can we play football behind the school?

Xiaoming

可以！我们下午三点在那儿见。 (Kěyǐ! Wǒmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn zài nàr jiàn.)

Yes! Let’s meet there at three in the afternoon.

2 · How the language works

还是 (háishi): ask which alternative

In a question, place 还是 (háishi) between two options. Answer by naming the option you prefer. Here it means “or” in a question; other meanings are learned separately.

Option A + 还是 (háishi) + option B + ?

你想喝茶还是喝水？ (Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?)

Would you like tea or water?

你明天想去学校还是在家学习？ (Nǐ míngtiān xiǎng qù xuéxiào háishi zài jiā xuéxí?)

Do you want to go to school or study at home tomorrow?

一起 (yìqǐ): do something together

Place 一起 (yìqǐ) before the verb phrase. Use 和 (hé) to name the person accompanying you when needed.

Person(s) + 一起 (yìqǐ) + action

我和朋友一起学中文。 (Wǒ hé péngyou yìqǐ xué Zhōngwén.)

My friend and I study Chinese together.

明天下午我们一起去运动。 (Míngtiān xiàwǔ wǒmen yìqǐ qù yùndòng.)

We will go exercise together tomorrow afternoon.

所以 (suǒyǐ): explain the result

State a reason and then use 所以 (suǒyǐ) to introduce the result. You do not need an English-style “because” clause for this basic pattern.

Reason + ，所以 (suǒyǐ) + result

今天下雨，所以我们在家看电影。 (Jīntiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen zài jiā kàn diànyǐng.)

It is raining today, so we are watching a movie at home.

他今天很忙，所以晚上不能来。 (Tā jīntiān hěn máng, suǒyǐ wǎnshang bù néng lái.)

He is busy today, so he cannot come tonight.

最 (zuì): say what you like most

Place 最 (zuì) before 喜欢 (xǐhuan) to express the strongest preference within a set of possibilities.

Person + 最 (zuì) + 喜欢 (xǐhuan) + activity or thing

我最喜欢看电影。 (Wǒ zuì xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.)

I like watching movies most.

你最喜欢什么运动？ (Nǐ zuì xǐhuan shénme yùndòng?)

Which sport do you like most?

4 · Read for meaning — Two friends make a Saturday plan

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

星期六下午，小美想和朋友一起运动。她最喜欢足球。小强今天有点儿累，所以想休息一会儿。 (Xīngqīliù xiàwǔ, Xiǎoměi xiǎng hé péngyou yìqǐ yùndòng. Tā zuì xǐhuan zúqiú. Xiǎoqiáng jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng xiūxi yíhuìr.)

On Saturday afternoon, Xiaomei wants to exercise with a friend. Football is her favourite. Xiaoqiang is a little tired today, so he wants to rest for a while.

小美问：“我们踢足球还是看电影？”小强说：“休息一会儿，我们再去踢足球吧！”他们下午三点在学校见。 (Xiǎoměi wèn: “Wǒmen tī zúqiú háishi kàn diànyǐng?” Xiǎoqiáng shuō: “Xiūxi yíhuìr, wǒmen zài qù tī zúqiú ba!” Tāmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn zài xuéxiào jiàn.)

Xiaomei asks, “Shall we play football or watch a movie?” Xiaoqiang says, “Let’s rest a little, then go play football!” They meet at school at three in the afternoon.

1. Why does Xiaoqiang want to rest?
Show the answer

Because he is a little tired.

2. Which two activities does Xiaomei suggest?
Show the answer

Playing football and watching a movie.

3. When and where do they meet?
Show the answer

At school at 3 p.m.

6 · Watch out

In a two-option question, use 还是 (háishi) between the alternatives; do not treat it like “and.” Note that 还是 can also mean “still” in another context, which is not the use practised here. Put 一起 (yìqǐ) before the shared action, 最 (zuì) before 喜欢 (xǐhuan), and 所以 (suǒyǐ) before the consequence.

Pronunciation clinic

Compare 一起 (yìqǐ), where 一 (yī) is pronounced yì before a third tone, with 一会儿 (yíhuìr), where 一 becomes yí before a fourth tone. The source citation spelling for 一会儿 is yíhuìr, and 还是 (háishi) has a light second syllable. Keep 累 (lèi) as a fourth tone.

7 · Check what you can do

Selection

Which sentence offers two alternatives?

a. 你想喝茶还是喝水？ (nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?) — Would you like tea or water?

b. 你想喝茶和喝水。 (nǐ xiǎng hē chá hé hē shuǐ.) — You want both tea and water.

c. 你想喝茶，所以喝水。 (nǐ xiǎng hē chá, suǒyǐ hē shuǐ.) — You want tea, so you drink water.

Show answer
a

还是 (háishi) connects the two alternatives in a choice question.

Selection

Which sentence invites someone to go together?

a. 我们一起去。 (wǒmen yìqǐ qù.) — We will go together.

b. 我一个人去。 (wǒ yí gè rén qù.) — I’m going alone.

c. 我们现在不去。 (wǒmen xiànzài bú qù.) — We are not going now.

Show answer
a

一起 (yìqǐ) means together, placed before the action 去 (qù).

Multi Selection

Choose BOTH sentences that correctly use 所以 (suǒyǐ) to introduce a result.

a. 今天很热，所以我想喝水。 (jīntiān hěn rè, suǒyǐ wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — It is hot, so I want to drink water.

b. 今天很热，还是我想喝水。 (jīntiān hěn rè, háishi wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — (Not a natural causal construction.)

c. 今天下雨，所以我们不出去。 (jīntiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen bù chūqù.) — It is raining, so we are not going out.

d. 今天下雨，最我们不出去。 (jīntiān xià yǔ, zuì wǒmen bù chūqù.) — (Incorrect use of 最.)

Show answer
a, c

The reason comes first, then 所以 (suǒyǐ) introduces the result.

Comprehension

Why does Xiaoqiang want to rest in the reading?

Show answer
He is a little tired; 小强今天有点儿累 (Xiǎoqiáng jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lèi).

His tiredness is the reason for wanting a short break.
