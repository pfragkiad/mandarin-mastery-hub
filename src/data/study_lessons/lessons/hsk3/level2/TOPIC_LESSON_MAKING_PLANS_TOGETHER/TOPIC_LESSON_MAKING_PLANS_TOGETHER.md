# Making Plans Together

**Subtopics:** sports-recreation

Make plans with friends, choose between activities, explain why you need a break, and talk about your favourite sports.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Offer two alternatives with 还是 (háishi) in a question.

- Invite someone to do an activity together using 一起 (yìqǐ).

- Give a reason using 所以 (suǒyǐ) and request a short break using 一会儿 (yíhuìr).

- Express a favourite activity using 最 (zuì) and discuss sports naturally.

## 1. Conversation — An afternoon plan

Two classmates decide whether to play football or watch a movie, and agree on a short rest and a meeting time.

**Xiaoyu:** 今天下午你想做什么？ (Jīntiān xiàwǔ nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme?) — What do you want to do this afternoon?

**Xiaoming:** 我想和同学一起运动。 (Wǒ xiǎng hé tóngxué yìqǐ yùndòng.) — I want to exercise with classmates.

**Xiaoyu:** 我们踢足球还是去看电影？ (Wǒmen tī zúqiú háishi qù kàn diànyǐng?) — Shall we play football or go to see a movie?

**Xiaoming:** 我最喜欢足球。我们去踢足球吧！ (Wǒ zuì xǐhuan zúqiú. Wǒmen qù tī zúqiú ba!) — I like football most. Let’s go play football!

**Xiaoyu:** 我今天有点儿累，所以想休息一会儿。 (Wǒ jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng xiūxi yíhuìr.) — I’m a little tired today, so I want to rest for a while.

**Xiaoming:** 可以。我们休息一会儿，再一起去。 (Kěyǐ. Wǒmen xiūxi yíhuìr, zài yìqǐ qù.) — Okay. We can rest for a bit, then go together.

**Xiaoyu:** 学校后面可以踢足球吗？ (Xuéxiào hòumiàn kěyǐ tī zúqiú ma?) — Can we play football behind the school?

**Xiaoming:** 可以！我们下午三点在那儿见。 (Kěyǐ! Wǒmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn zài nàr jiàn.) — Yes! Let’s meet there at three in the afternoon.

## 2. How the language works

### 还是 (háishi): ask which alternative

In a question, place 还是 (háishi) between two options. Answer by naming the option you prefer. Here it means “or” in a question; other meanings are learned separately.

**Pattern:** Option A + 还是 (háishi) + option B + ?

**你想喝茶还是喝水？**
*Nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?*
Would you like tea or water?

**你明天想去学校还是在家学习？**
*Nǐ míngtiān xiǎng qù xuéxiào háishi zài jiā xuéxí?*
Do you want to go to school or study at home tomorrow?

### 一起 (yìqǐ): do something together

Place 一起 (yìqǐ) before the verb phrase. Use 和 (hé) to name the person accompanying you when needed.

**Pattern:** Person(s) + 一起 (yìqǐ) + action

**我和朋友一起学中文。**
*Wǒ hé péngyou yìqǐ xué Zhōngwén.*
My friend and I study Chinese together.

**明天下午我们一起去运动。**
*Míngtiān xiàwǔ wǒmen yìqǐ qù yùndòng.*
We will go exercise together tomorrow afternoon.

### 所以 (suǒyǐ): explain the result

State a reason and then use 所以 (suǒyǐ) to introduce the result. You do not need an English-style “because” clause for this basic pattern.

**Pattern:** Reason + ，所以 (suǒyǐ) + result

**今天下雨，所以我们在家看电影。**
*Jīntiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen zài jiā kàn diànyǐng.*
It is raining today, so we are watching a movie at home.

**他今天很忙，所以晚上不能来。**
*Tā jīntiān hěn máng, suǒyǐ wǎnshang bù néng lái.*
He is busy today, so he cannot come tonight.

### 最 (zuì): say what you like most

Place 最 (zuì) before 喜欢 (xǐhuan) to express the strongest preference within a set of possibilities.

**Pattern:** Person + 最 (zuì) + 喜欢 (xǐhuan) + activity or thing

**我最喜欢看电影。**
*Wǒ zuì xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.*
I like watching movies most.

**你最喜欢什么运动？**
*Nǐ zuì xǐhuan shénme yùndòng?*
Which sport do you like most?

## 3. Build new sentences

Build a plan step by step. Read the cue, say your own Mandarin sentence aloud, and reveal the sample answer.

**Worked model:** **我们下午一起运动吧。**
*Wǒmen xiàwǔ yìqǐ yùndòng ba.*
Let’s exercise together this afternoon.

**Change 1: Invite a friend to watch a movie together tonight.**

*Model response:* **我们晚上一起看电影吧。**
*Wǒmen wǎnshang yìqǐ kàn diànyǐng ba.*
Let’s watch a movie together tonight.

*Model response:* **你想踢足球还是看电影？**

Do you want to play football or watch a movie?

**Change 3: Say you are a little tired and want to rest briefly.**

*Wǒ yǒudiǎnr lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng xiūxi yíhuìr.*
I am a little tired, so I want to rest for a while.

**Change 4: Say that you like exercise most.**

*Model response:* **我最喜欢运动。**
*Wǒ zuì xǐhuan yùndòng.*
I like exercising most.

## 4. Read for meaning — Two friends make a Saturday plan

**星期六下午，小美想和朋友一起运动。她最喜欢足球。小强今天有点儿累，所以想休息一会儿。**
*Xīngqīliù xiàwǔ, Xiǎoměi xiǎng hé péngyou yìqǐ yùndòng. Tā zuì xǐhuan zúqiú. Xiǎoqiáng jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng xiūxi yíhuìr.*
On Saturday afternoon, Xiaomei wants to exercise with a friend. Football is her favourite. Xiaoqiang is a little tired today, so he wants to rest for a while.

**小美问：“我们踢足球还是看电影？”小强说：“休息一会儿，我们再去踢足球吧！”他们下午三点在学校见。**
*Xiǎoměi wèn: “Wǒmen tī zúqiú háishi kàn diànyǐng?” Xiǎoqiáng shuō: “Xiūxi yíhuìr, wǒmen zài qù tī zúqiú ba!” Tāmen xiàwǔ sān diǎn zài xuéxiào jiàn.*
Xiaomei asks, “Shall we play football or watch a movie?” Xiaoqiang says, “Let’s rest a little, then go play football!” They meet at school at three in the afternoon.

**Q1. Why does Xiaoqiang want to rest?** Suggested answer: Because he is a little tired.

**Q2. Which two activities does Xiaomei suggest?** Suggested answer: Playing football and watching a movie.

**Q3. When and where do they meet?** Suggested answer: At school at 3 p.m.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Invite a friend to exercise together on Saturday afternoon.

*Possible response:* **星期六下午我们一起运动吧！**
*Xīngqīliù xiàwǔ wǒmen yìqǐ yùndòng ba!*
Let’s exercise together on Saturday afternoon!

**Speaking 2:** Offer your friend two options: football or a movie.

*Possible response:* **你想踢足球还是看电影？**
*Nǐ xiǎng tī zúqiú háishi kàn diànyǐng?*
Do you want to play football or watch a movie?

**Speaking 3:** Tell your friend you are tired and want a short break.

*Possible response:* **我有点儿累，所以想休息一会儿。**
*Wǒ yǒudiǎnr lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng xiūxi yíhuìr.*
I’m a little tired, so I want to rest for a while.

**Speaking 4:** Tell a friend which activity you like most.

*Possible response:* **我最喜欢运动。**
*Wǒ zuì xǐhuan yùndòng.*
I like exercising most.

**Speaking 5:** Tell a classmate that your hobby is playing football.

*Possible response:* **我的爱好是踢足球。**
*Wǒ de àihào shì tī zúqiú.*
My hobby is playing football.

## 6. Watch out

In a two-option question, use 还是 (háishi) between the alternatives; do not treat it like “and.” Note that 还是 can also mean “still” in another context, which is not the use practised here. Put 一起 (yìqǐ) before the shared action, 最 (zuì) before 喜欢 (xǐhuan), and 所以 (suǒyǐ) before the consequence.

**Pronunciation:** Compare 一起 (yìqǐ), where 一 (yī) is pronounced yì before a third tone, with 一会儿 (yíhuìr), where 一 becomes yí before a fourth tone. The source citation spelling for 一会儿 is yíhuìr, and 还是 (háishi) has a light second syllable. Keep 累 (lèi) as a fourth tone.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Selection:** Which sentence offers two alternatives?
- a. 你想喝茶还是喝水？ (nǐ xiǎng hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?) — Would you like tea or water?
- b. 你想喝茶和喝水。 (nǐ xiǎng hē chá hé hē shuǐ.) — You want both tea and water.
- c. 你想喝茶，所以喝水。 (nǐ xiǎng hē chá, suǒyǐ hē shuǐ.) — You want tea, so you drink water.
**Answer:** a. 还是 (háishi) connects the two alternatives in a choice question.

**Selection:** Which sentence invites someone to go together?
- a. 我们一起去。 (wǒmen yìqǐ qù.) — We will go together.
- b. 我一个人去。 (wǒ yí gè rén qù.) — I’m going alone.
- c. 我们现在不去。 (wǒmen xiànzài bú qù.) — We are not going now.
**Answer:** a. 一起 (yìqǐ) means together, placed before the action 去 (qù).

**Multi Selection:** Choose BOTH sentences that correctly use 所以 (suǒyǐ) to introduce a result.
- a. 今天很热，所以我想喝水。 (jīntiān hěn rè, suǒyǐ wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — It is hot, so I want to drink water.
- b. 今天很热，还是我想喝水。 (jīntiān hěn rè, háishi wǒ xiǎng hē shuǐ.) — (Not a natural causal construction.)
- c. 今天下雨，所以我们不出去。 (jīntiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen bù chūqù.) — It is raining, so we are not going out.
- d. 今天下雨，最我们不出去。 (jīntiān xià yǔ, zuì wǒmen bù chūqù.) — (Incorrect use of 最.)
**Answer:** a, c. The reason comes first, then 所以 (suǒyǐ) introduces the result.

**Comprehension:** Why does Xiaoqiang want to rest in the reading?
**Answer:** He is a little tired; 小强今天有点儿累 (Xiǎoqiáng jīntiān yǒudiǎnr lèi).. His tiredness is the reason for wanting a short break.

**Production:** Write a question offering football or watching a movie. Provide both Hanzi and pinyin.
**Answer:** 你想踢足球还是看电影？ (Nǐ xiǎng tī zúqiú háishi kàn diànyǐng?). Put 还是 (háishi) between the two offered actions.

## Review and next step

- Offer friends two activities using 还是 (háishi).

- Arrange a joint activity using 一起 (yìqǐ).

- Explain that you feel tired and need a short rest using 所以 (suǒyǐ) and 一会儿 (yíhuìr).

- State your favourite activity using 最喜欢 (zuì xǐhuan).

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means “together”?
- a. 一起 (yìqǐ) — together
- b. 一会儿 (yíhuìr) — a little while
- c. 最 (zuì) — most
**Answer:** a. 一起 (yìqǐ) indicates a shared action.

**Selection:** Which expression means “a little while”?
- a. 足球 (zúqiú) — football
- b. 一会儿 (yíhuìr) — a short while
- c. 爱好 (àihào) — hobby
**Answer:** b. 一会儿 (yíhuìr) is a short amount of time.

**Selection:** Which word means “most” in 最喜欢 (zuì xǐhuan)?
- a. 所以 (suǒyǐ) — so
- b. 还是 (háishi) — or, in questions
- c. 最 (zuì) — most
**Answer:** c. 最 (zuì) indicates the highest degree.
