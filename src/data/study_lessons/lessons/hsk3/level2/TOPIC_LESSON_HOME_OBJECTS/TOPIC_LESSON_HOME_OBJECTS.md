# My Room and My Belongings

Locate everyday possessions in a bedroom and describe what to bring to school.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Name a bed, door and everyday bags.

- Describe clothing and a watch.

- Use position phrases to locate belongings.

## 1. Conversation — My Room and My Belongings

Locate everyday possessions in a bedroom and describe what to bring to school.

**A:** 我的书包在哪儿？ (Wǒ de shūbāo zài nǎr?) — Where is my schoolbag?

**B:** 在床上，旁边还有你的手表。 (Zài chuáng shang, pángbiān hái yǒu nǐ de shǒubiǎo.) — On the bed, and your watch is next to it.

**A:** 我的裤子呢？我今天要出门。 (Wǒ de kùzi ne? Wǒ jīntiān yào chūmén.) — What about my trousers? I need to go out today.

**B:** 在门后面。别忘了拿那个包！ (Zài mén hòumiàn. Bié wàng le ná nà ge bāo!) — Behind the door. Don’t forget to take that bag!

## 2. How the language works

### Locating belongings

Use 在 (zài) followed by a location phrase to say where an object is; distinguish 里面 (lǐmiàn) and 旁边 (pángbiān).

**Pattern**

宾语 + 在 + 地点  
*bīnyǔ + zài + dìdiǎn*  
&lt;object&gt; + **在** + &lt;location&gt;

**我的手表在书包里面。**
*Wǒ de shǒubiǎo zài shūbāo lǐmiàn.*
My watch is inside the schoolbag.

**在床上，旁边还有你的手表。**
*Zài chuáng shang, pángbiān hái yǒu nǐ de shǒubiǎo.*
On the bed, and your watch is next to it.

### Talking about what to take

拿 (ná) is used for picking up or taking an object; name the object directly after the verb.

**Pattern**

人 + 拿 + 宾语  
*rén + ná + bīnyǔ*  
&lt;person&gt; + **拿** + &lt;object&gt;

**请把包放在床旁边。**
*Qǐng bǎ bāo fàng zài chuáng pángbiān.*
Please put the bag beside the bed.

**在门后面。别忘了拿那个包！**
*Zài mén hòumiàn. Bié wàng le ná nà ge bāo!*
Behind the door. Don’t forget to take that bag!

## 4. Read for meaning — My Room and My Belongings — short reading

**小明早上找书包，发现书包在床下面，手表在书桌上面。**
*Xiǎomíng zǎoshang zhǎo shūbāo, fāxiàn shūbāo zài chuáng xiàmiàn, shǒubiǎo zài shūzhuō shàngmiàn.*
In the morning, Xiaoming looks for his schoolbag and finds it under the bed, while his watch is on the desk.

**他穿好裤子，拿起包，打开门走了出去。**
*Tā chuān hǎo kùzi, ná qǐ bāo, dǎkāi mén zǒu le chūqù.*
He puts on his trousers, picks up his bag, opens the door and goes out.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: In the morning, Xiaoming looks for his schoolbag and finds it under the bed, while his watch is on the desk.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: He puts on his trousers, picks up his bag, opens the door and goes out.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: Please put the bag beside the bed.

*Possible response:* **请把包放在床旁边。**

## 6. Watch out

Do not confuse 包 (bāo), a general bag, with 书包 (shūbāo), the more specific schoolbag.

**Pronunciation:** Listen for the neutral suffix in 裤子 (kùzi) and the third tone of 表 (biǎo).
