Clothes, bags and things at home

1 · Conversation — My Room and My Belongings

Locate everyday possessions in a bedroom and describe what to bring to school.

A

我的书包在哪儿？ (Wǒ de shūbāo zài nǎr?)

Where is my schoolbag?

B

在床上，旁边还有你的手表。 (Zài chuáng shang, pángbiān hái yǒu nǐ de shǒubiǎo.)

On the bed, and your watch is next to it.

A

我的裤子呢？我今天要出门。 (Wǒ de kùzi ne? Wǒ jīntiān yào chūmén.)

What about my trousers? I need to go out today.

B

在门后面。别忘了拿那个包！ (Zài mén hòumiàn. Bié wàng le ná nà ge bāo!)

Behind the door. Don’t forget to take that bag!

2 · How the language works

Locating belongings

Use 在 (zài) followed by a location phrase to say where an object is; distinguish 里面 (lǐmiàn) and 旁边 (pángbiān).

宾语 + 在 + 地点
bīnyǔ + zài + dìdiǎn
<object> + 在 + <location>

我的手表在书包里面。 (Wǒ de shǒubiǎo zài shūbāo lǐmiàn.)

My watch is inside the schoolbag.

在床上，旁边还有你的手表。 (Zài chuáng shang, pángbiān hái yǒu nǐ de shǒubiǎo.)

On the bed, and your watch is next to it.

Talking about what to take

拿 (ná) is used for picking up or taking an object; name the object directly after the verb.

人 + 拿 + 宾语
rén + ná + bīnyǔ
<person> + 拿 + <object>

请把包放在床旁边。 (Qǐng bǎ bāo fàng zài chuáng pángbiān.)

Please put the bag beside the bed.

在门后面。别忘了拿那个包！ (Zài mén hòumiàn. Bié wàng le ná nà ge bāo!)

Behind the door. Don’t forget to take that bag!

4 · Read for meaning — My Room and My Belongings — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

小明早上找书包，发现书包在床下面，手表在书桌上面。 (Xiǎomíng zǎoshang zhǎo shūbāo, fāxiàn shūbāo zài chuáng xiàmiàn, shǒubiǎo zài shūzhuō shàngmiàn.)

In the morning, Xiaoming looks for his schoolbag and finds it under the bed, while his watch is on the desk.

他穿好裤子，拿起包，打开门走了出去。 (Tā chuān hǎo kùzi, ná qǐ bāo, dǎkāi mén zǒu le chūqù.)

He puts on his trousers, picks up his bag, opens the door and goes out.

6 · Watch out

Do not confuse 包 (bāo), a general bag, with 书包 (shūbāo), the more specific schoolbag.

Pronunciation clinic

Listen for the neutral suffix in 裤子 (kùzi) and the third tone of 表 (biǎo).
