Street routes, entrances and facilities

Your goal

1 · Conversation — Finding the Way in Town

Ask where places are and follow simple walking directions in an unfamiliar area.

A

不好意思，请问洗手间在哪儿？ (Bù hǎoyìsi, qǐngwèn xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr?)

Excuse me, where is the restroom?

B

从这儿往前走，到门口再往右走。 (Cóng zhèr wǎng qián zǒu, dào ménkǒu zài wǎng yòu zǒu.)

Walk forward from here, then turn right at the entrance.

A

是在这条路上吗？ (Shì zài zhè tiáo lù shang ma?)

Is it on this road?

B

对，就在那个外国饭馆的旁边。 (Duì, jiù zài nà ge wàiguó fànguǎn de pángbiān.)

Yes, it’s right next to that foreign restaurant.

2 · How the language works

Where a route begins

从 (cóng) introduces the starting location of a journey or movement.

从 + 出发点 + 动词
cóng + chūfādiǎn + dòngcí
从 + <starting point> + <verb>

请从学校门口往左走。 (Qǐng cóng xuéxiào ménkǒu wǎng zuǒ zǒu.)

Please walk left from the school entrance.

从这儿往前走，到门口再往右走。 (Cóng zhèr wǎng qián zǒu, dào ménkǒu zài wǎng yòu zǒu.)

Walk forward from here, then turn right at the entrance.

Giving a direction

往 (wǎng) marks the direction of travel; combine it with 前 (qián), 左 (zuǒ), or 右 (yòu).

往 + 方向 + 走
wǎng + fāngxiàng + zǒu
往 + <direction> + 走

洗手间就在前面。 (Xǐshǒujiān jiù zài qiánmiàn.)

The restroom is just ahead.

对，就在那个外国饭馆的旁边。 (Duì, jiù zài nà ge wàiguó fànguǎn de pángbiān.)

Yes, it’s right next to that foreign restaurant.

6 · Watch out

从 (cóng) describes a starting point; 往 (wǎng) describes a direction. One does not replace the other.

Pronunciation clinic

Practise the third tone in 往 (wǎng), the second tone in 从 (cóng), and the neutral syllable of 门口 (ménkǒu).
