# Places, Position & Direction — Part 3: Finding the Way in Town

**Subtopics:** geography-places

Ask where places are and follow simple walking directions in an unfamiliar area.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask directions using 往 (wǎng) and 从 (cóng).

- Identify a street and a building entrance.

- Find a 洗手间 (xǐshǒujiān) politely.

## 1. Conversation — Finding the Way in Town

Ask where places are and follow simple walking directions in an unfamiliar area.

**A:** 不好意思，请问洗手间在哪儿？ (Bù hǎoyìsi, qǐngwèn xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr?) — Excuse me, where is the restroom?

**B:** 从这儿往前走，到门口再往右走。 (Cóng zhèr wǎng qián zǒu, dào ménkǒu zài wǎng yòu zǒu.) — Walk forward from here, then turn right at the entrance.

**A:** 是在这条路上吗？ (Shì zài zhè tiáo lù shang ma?) — Is it on this road?

**B:** 对，就在那个外国饭馆的旁边。 (Duì, jiù zài nà ge wàiguó fànguǎn de pángbiān.) — Yes, it’s right next to that foreign restaurant.

## 2. How the language works

### Where a route begins

从 (cóng) introduces the starting location of a journey or movement.

**Pattern:** 从 + starting point + verb

**请从学校门口往左走。**
*Qǐng cóng xuéxiào ménkǒu wǎng zuǒ zǒu.*
Please walk left from the school entrance.

**从这儿往前走，到门口再往右走。**
*Cóng zhèr wǎng qián zǒu, dào ménkǒu zài wǎng yòu zǒu.*
Walk forward from here, then turn right at the entrance.

### Giving a direction

往 (wǎng) marks the direction of travel; combine it with 前 (qián), 左 (zuǒ), or 右 (yòu).

**Pattern:** 往 + direction + 走

**洗手间就在前面。**
*Xǐshǒujiān jiù zài qiánmiàn.*
The restroom is just ahead.

**对，就在那个外国饭馆的旁边。**
*Duì, jiù zài nà ge wàiguó fànguǎn de pángbiān.*
Yes, it’s right next to that foreign restaurant.

## 4. Read for meaning — Finding the Way in Town — short reading

**小王第一次来到这个城市。他从车站出来，沿着大路往前走。**
*Xiǎo Wáng dì yī cì lái dào zhè ge chéngshì. Tā cóng chēzhàn chūlái, yánzhe dà lù wǎng qián zǒu.*
Xiao Wang arrives in this city for the first time. He leaves the station and walks forward along the main road.

**他在学校门口问路，终于找到了饭馆旁边的洗手间。**
*Tā zài xuéxiào ménkǒu wèn lù, zhōngyú zhǎodào le fànguǎn pángbiān de xǐshǒujiān.*
At the school entrance he asks for directions and finally finds the restroom beside the restaurant.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiao Wang arrives in this city for the first time. He leaves the station and walks forward along the main road.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: At the school entrance he asks for directions and finally finds the restroom beside the restaurant.

## 6. Watch out

从 (cóng) describes a starting point; 往 (wǎng) describes a direction. One does not replace the other.

**Pronunciation:** Practise the third tone in 往 (wǎng), the second tone in 从 (cóng), and the neutral syllable of 门口 (ménkǒu).
