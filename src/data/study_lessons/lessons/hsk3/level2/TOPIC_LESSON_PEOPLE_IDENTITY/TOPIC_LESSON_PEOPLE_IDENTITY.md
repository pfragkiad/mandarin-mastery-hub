# Introducing People by Name

Introduce someone, ask about surnames and describe people in a friendly conversation.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask and answer questions about a surname and full name.

- Introduce boys, girls and children politely.

- Describe someone’s height in everyday language.

## 1. Conversation — Introducing People by Name

Introduce someone, ask about surnames and describe people in a friendly conversation.

**A:** 你好！请问，你姓什么？ (Nǐ hǎo! Qǐngwèn, nǐ xìng shénme?) — Hello! Excuse me, what is your surname?

**B:** 我姓王。我的姓名是王小明。 (Wǒ xìng Wáng. Wǒ de xìngmíng shì Wáng Xiǎomíng.) — My surname is Wang. My full name is Wang Xiaoming.

**A:** 那个男孩儿是你的朋友吗？ (Nà ge nánháir shì nǐ de péngyou ma?) — Is that boy your friend?

**B:** 是的，他是我的朋友，个子很高。 (Shì de, tā shì wǒ de péngyou, gèzi hěn gāo.) — Yes, he is my friend, and he is tall.

## 2. How the language works

### Surnames and full names

姓 (xìng) introduces a family name; 姓名 (xìngmíng) means a full name. Ask 姓什么 (xìng shénme) when you want the family name.

**Pattern**

人 + 姓 + 姓氏  
*rén + xìng + xìngshì*  
&lt;person&gt; + **姓** + &lt;family name&gt;

**我姓李，叫李小红。**
*Wǒ xìng Lǐ, jiào Lǐ Xiǎohóng.*
My surname is Li, and my name is Li Xiaohong.

**我姓王。我的姓名是王小明。**
*Wǒ xìng Wáng. Wǒ de xìngmíng shì Wáng Xiǎomíng.*
My surname is Wang. My full name is Wang Xiaoming.

### Descriptions of people

Use 个子 (gèzi) with 高 (gāo) or another suitable adjective to describe a person’s height.

**Pattern**

人 + 个子 + 形容词  
*rén + gèzi + xíngróngcí*  
&lt;person&gt; + **个子** + &lt;adjective&gt;

**这个女孩儿个子不高。**
*Zhè ge nǚháir gèzi bù gāo.*
This girl is not tall.

**是的，他是我的朋友，个子很高。**
*Shì de, tā shì wǒ de péngyou, gèzi hěn gāo.*
Yes, he is my friend, and he is tall.

## 4. Read for meaning — Introducing People by Name — short reading

**今天有两个小孩儿来学校，一个男孩儿和一个女孩儿。他们是朋友。**
*Jīntiān yǒu liǎng ge xiǎoháir lái xuéxiào, yí ge nánháir hé yí ge nǚháir. Tāmen shì péngyou.*
Two children came to school today, a boy and a girl. They are friends.

**老师问他们的姓名，两个孩子都说了自己的名字。**
*Lǎoshī wèn tāmen de xìngmíng, liǎng ge háizi dōu shuō le zìjǐ de míngzi.*
The teacher asked for their full names, and both children said their names.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Two children came to school today, a boy and a girl. They are friends.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: The teacher asked for their full names, and both children said their names.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Explain to a classmate: My surname is Li, and my name is Li Xiaohong.

*Possible response:* **我姓李，叫李小红。**
*Wǒ xìng Lǐ, jiào Lǐ Xiǎohóng.*
My surname is Li, and my name is Li Xiaohong.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: This girl is not tall.

*Possible response:* **这个女孩儿个子不高。**
*Zhè ge nǚháir gèzi bù gāo.*
This girl is not tall.

## 6. Watch out

Do not confuse 姓 (xìng), a surname, with 姓名 (xìngmíng), a full name; 个子 (gèzi) describes stature, not someone’s name.

**Pronunciation:** Practise 姓 (xìng) with the fourth tone, and the neutral syllable in 个子 (gèzi).

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** What is happening in the second part of the reading?
**Answer:** The teacher asked for their full names, and both children said their names.. Use the details in the second paragraph to justify your answer.
