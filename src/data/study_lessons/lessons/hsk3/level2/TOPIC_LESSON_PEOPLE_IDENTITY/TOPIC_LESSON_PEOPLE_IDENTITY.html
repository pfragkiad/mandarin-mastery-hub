Introducing People by Name

Names, children and personal descriptions

people-identity · #names · #hsk-level-2 · #topic-study

Your goal

  • Ask and answer questions about a surname and full name.
  • Introduce boys, girls and children politely.
  • Describe someone’s height in everyday language.

1 · Conversation — Introducing People by Name

Introduce someone, ask about surnames and describe people in a friendly conversation.

A

你好！请问，你姓什么？ (Nǐ hǎo! Qǐngwèn, nǐ xìng shénme?)

Hello! Excuse me, what is your surname?

B

我姓王。我的姓名是王小明。 (Wǒ xìng Wáng. Wǒ de xìngmíng shì Wáng Xiǎomíng.)

My surname is Wang. My full name is Wang Xiaoming.

A

那个男孩儿是你的朋友吗？ (Nà ge nánháir shì nǐ de péngyou ma?)

Is that boy your friend?

B

是的，他是我的朋友，个子很高。 (Shì de, tā shì wǒ de péngyou, gèzi hěn gāo.)

Yes, he is my friend, and he is tall.

2 · How the language works

Surnames and full names

姓 (xìng) introduces a family name; 姓名 (xìngmíng) means a full name. Ask 姓什么 (xìng shénme) when you want the family name.

Person + 姓 + family name

我姓李，叫李小红。 (Wǒ xìng Lǐ, jiào Lǐ Xiǎohóng.)

My surname is Li, and my name is Li Xiaohong.

我姓王。我的姓名是王小明。 (Wǒ xìng Wáng. Wǒ de xìngmíng shì Wáng Xiǎomíng.)

My surname is Wang. My full name is Wang Xiaoming.

Descriptions of people

Use 个子 (gèzi) with 高 (gāo) or another suitable adjective to describe a person’s height.

Person + 个子 + adjective

这个女孩儿个子不高。 (Zhè ge nǚháir gèzi bù gāo.)

This girl is not tall.

是的，他是我的朋友，个子很高。 (Shì de, tā shì wǒ de péngyou, gèzi hěn gāo.)

Yes, he is my friend, and he is tall.

4 · Read for meaning — Introducing People by Name — short reading

Read the passage, then answer in English or Mandarin.

今天有两个小孩儿来学校，一个男孩儿和一个女孩儿。他们是朋友。 (Jīntiān yǒu liǎng ge xiǎoháir lái xuéxiào, yí ge nánháir hé yí ge nǚháir. Tāmen shì péngyou.)

Two children came to school today, a boy and a girl. They are friends.

老师问他们的姓名，两个孩子都说了自己的名字。 (Lǎoshī wèn tāmen de xìngmíng, liǎng ge háizi dōu shuō le zìjǐ de míngzi.)

The teacher asked for their full names, and both children said their names.

6 · Watch out

Do not confuse 姓 (xìng), a surname, with 姓名 (xìngmíng), a full name; 个子 (gèzi) describes stature, not someone’s name.

Pronunciation clinic

Practise 姓 (xìng) with the fourth tone, and the neutral syllable in 个子 (gèzi).
