# Qualities, Feelings & Evaluation — Part 1: Describing Colors and Comparing Things

## Your goal

- Recognize white, black, red and green.

- Compare two items with 比 (bǐ).

- Describe height, length and speed.

## 1. Conversation — Describing Colors and Comparing Things

Describe the colors and physical qualities of objects, then compare two things.

**A:** 你喜欢哪件衣服？红色的还是绿色的？ (Nǐ xǐhuan nǎ jiàn yīfu? Hóngsè de háishi lǜsè de?) — Which outfit do you like, the red one or the green one?

**B:** 我喜欢绿色的，不过黑色的也不错。 (Wǒ xǐhuan lǜsè de, búguò hēisè de yě búcuò.) — I like the green one, though the black one is also nice.

**A:** 这条裤子比那条长吗？ (Zhè tiáo kùzi bǐ nà tiáo cháng ma?) — Are these trousers longer than those?

**B:** 对，而且白色的那条比这条短。 (Duì, érqiě báisè de nà tiáo bǐ zhè tiáo duǎn.) — Yes, and the white pair is shorter than this one.

## 2. How the language works

### Comparing two items

比 (bǐ) introduces the item used for comparison, followed by the relevant adjective.

**Pattern:** A + 比 + B + adjective

**这条路比那条路长。**
*Zhè tiáo lù bǐ nà tiáo lù cháng.*
This road is longer than that road.

**我喜欢绿色的，不过黑色的也不错。**
*Wǒ xǐhuan lǜsè de, búguò hēisè de yě búcuò.*
I like the green one, though the black one is also nice.

### Describing colors

Use a color noun such as 红色 (hóngsè) before 的 (de) when modifying a noun.

**Pattern:** Color + 的 + noun

**她走得很慢，我走得很快。**
*Tā zǒu de hěn màn, wǒ zǒu de hěn kuài.*
She walks slowly, and I walk quickly.

**对，而且白色的那条比这条短。**
*Duì, érqiě báisè de nà tiáo bǐ zhè tiáo duǎn.*
Yes, and the white pair is shorter than this one.

## 4. Read for meaning — Describing Colors and Comparing Things — short reading

**小林和小王一起跑步。小王个子比较高，可是小林跑得更快。**
*Xiǎo Lín hé Xiǎo Wáng yìqǐ pǎobù. Xiǎo Wáng gèzi bǐjiào gāo, kěshì Xiǎo Lín pǎo de gèng kuài.*
Xiaolin and Xiao Wang run together. Xiao Wang is taller, but Xiaolin runs faster.

**他们看见一辆红色的车和一辆黑色的车，觉得红色的车很漂亮。**
*Tāmen kànjiàn yí liàng hóngsè de chē hé yí liàng hēisè de chē, juéde hóngsè de chē hěn piàoliang.*
They see a red car and a black car and think the red one is pretty.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiaolin and Xiao Wang run together. Xiao Wang is taller, but Xiaolin runs faster.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Explain to a classmate: This road is longer than that road.

*Possible response:* **这条路比那条路长。**
*Zhè tiáo lù bǐ nà tiáo lù cháng.*
This road is longer than that road.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: She walks slowly, and I walk quickly.

*Possible response:* **她走得很慢，我走得很快。**
*Tā zǒu de hěn màn, wǒ zǒu de hěn kuài.*
She walks slowly, and I walk quickly.

## 6. Watch out

In comparisons with 比 (bǐ), do not automatically insert 很 (hěn) before the adjective as in an ordinary description.

**Pronunciation:** Contrast 黑色 (hēisè), first plus fourth tone, and 红色 (hóngsè), second plus fourth tone.
