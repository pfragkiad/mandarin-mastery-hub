# Sports, Games and Hobbies

**Subtopics:** sports-recreation

Talk with friends about leisure interests and suggest a sport or creative activity.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Discuss sports and different balls.

- Express a hobby using 爱好 (àihào).

- Invite someone to dance or draw.

## 1. Conversation — Sports, Games and Hobbies

Talk with friends about leisure interests and suggest a sport or creative activity.

**A:** 你平时有什么爱好？ (Nǐ píngshí yǒu shénme àihào?) — What hobbies do you have?

**B:** 我喜欢打篮球，也喜欢踢足球。 (Wǒ xǐhuan dǎ lánqiú, yě xǐhuan tī zúqiú.) — I like playing basketball and football.

**A:** 这个球是你的吗？今天想一起去运动吗？ (Zhè ge qiú shì nǐ de ma? Jīntiān xiǎng yìqǐ qù yùndòng ma?) — Is this your ball? Want to go exercise together today?

**B:** 好啊！运动以后，我们还可以画画或者跳舞。 (Hǎo a! Yùndòng yǐhòu, wǒmen hái kěyǐ huàhuà huòzhě tiàowǔ.) — Sure! After exercising, we can also draw or dance.

## 2. How the language works

### Different sports verbs

Use 打 (dǎ) with 篮球 (lánqiú), but 踢 (tī) with 足球 (zúqiú). These are conventional verb-noun collocations.

**Pattern**

打篮球 / 踢足球  
*dǎ lánqiú / tī zúqiú*  
**打篮球** / **踢足球**

**我的爱好是打篮球。**
*Wǒ de àihào shì dǎ lánqiú.*
My hobby is playing basketball.

**我喜欢打篮球，也喜欢踢足球。**
*Wǒ xǐhuan dǎ lánqiú, yě xǐhuan tī zúqiú.*
I like playing basketball and football.

### Describing a hobby

爱好 (àihào) refers to a hobby or interest. Use 我的爱好是 (wǒ de àihào shì) before an activity.

**Pattern**

我的爱好是 + 活动  
*wǒ de àihào shì + huódòng*  
**我的爱好是** + &lt;activity&gt;

**星期六我们一起去踢足球吧。**
*Xīngqīliù wǒmen yìqǐ qù tī zúqiú ba.*
Let’s go play football together on Saturday.

**好啊！运动以后，我们还可以画画或者跳舞。**
*Hǎo a! Yùndòng yǐhòu, wǒmen hái kěyǐ huàhuà huòzhě tiàowǔ.*
Sure! After exercising, we can also draw or dance.

## 4. Read for meaning — Sports, Games and Hobbies — short reading

**周末，小美先和哥哥打篮球，下午又跟朋友去踢足球。**
*Zhōumò, Xiǎoměi xiān hé gēge dǎ lánqiú, xiàwǔ yòu gēn péngyou qù tī zúqiú.*
On the weekend, Xiaomei plays basketball with her older brother and then goes to play football with a friend.

**晚上她想在家画一幅画，妹妹却想听音乐跳舞。每个人的爱好都不一样。**
*Wǎnshang tā xiǎng zài jiā huà yì fú huà, mèimei què xiǎng tīng yīnyuè tiàowǔ. Měi ge rén de àihào dōu bù yíyàng.*
In the evening she wants to draw a picture at home, while her sister wants to dance to music. Everyone’s hobbies differ.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: On the weekend, Xiaomei plays basketball with her older brother and then goes to play football with a friend.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: In the evening she wants to draw a picture at home, while her sister wants to dance to music. Everyone’s hobbies differ.

*Wǒ de àihào shì dǎ lánqiú.*

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: Let’s go play football together on Saturday.

*Possible response:* **星期六我们一起去踢足球吧。**
*Xīngqīliù wǒmen yìqǐ qù tī zúqiú ba.*
Let’s go play football together on Saturday.

## 6. Watch out

Do not say 打足球 for ordinary football; standard Mandarin uses 踢足球 (tī zúqiú).

**Pronunciation:** Practise 篮球 (lánqiú) as two second tones and 足球 (zúqiú) as two second tones.
