# Talking About Meanings and Situations

Ask about a word’s meaning and explain an everyday situation or problem.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask what 意思 (yìsi) means in context.

- Describe a matter or situation with 事情 (shìqing).

- Use familiar words to request a clear explanation.

## 1. Conversation — Talking About Meanings and Situations

Ask about a word’s meaning and explain an everyday situation or problem.

**A:** 不好意思，我有一件事情想问你。 (Bù hǎoyìsi, wǒ yǒu yí jiàn shìqing xiǎng wèn nǐ.) — Excuse me, I have something to ask you.

**B:** 好的，是什么事情？ (Hǎo de, shì shénme shìqing?) — Okay, what is it about?

**A:** 我不明白老师说的那个词。 (Wǒ bù míngbai lǎoshī shuō de nà ge cí.) — I do not understand the word the teacher said.

**B:** 我告诉你它的意思，然后一起看看例子。 (Wǒ gàosu nǐ tā de yìsi, ránhòu yìqǐ kànkan lìzi.) — I’ll tell you what it means, and then we’ll look at examples together.

## 2. How the language works

### Asking for a meaning

Use 什么意思 (shénme yìsi) to ask what a word or phrase means. 意思 (yìsi) has other conversational meanings, but here it is about meaning.

**Pattern:** Word/phrase + 是什么意思?

**我不明白这句话的意思。**
*Wǒ bù míngbai zhè jù huà de yìsi.*
I do not understand what this sentence means.

**好的，是什么事情？**
*Hǎo de, shì shénme shìqing?*
Okay, what is it about?

### Discussing a matter

事情 (shìqing) names a matter or event, often with the classifier 件 (jiàn).

**Pattern:** 一件 + 事情

**这件事情很重要。**
*Zhè jiàn shìqing hěn zhòngyào.*
This matter is important.

**我告诉你它的意思，然后一起看看例子。**
*Wǒ gàosu nǐ tā de yìsi, ránhòu yìqǐ kànkan lìzi.*
I’ll tell you what it means, and then we’ll look at examples together.

## 4. Read for meaning — Talking About Meanings and Situations — short reading

**今天上课的时候，老师说了一个新词。小华不明白这个词的意思。**
*Jīntiān shàngkè de shíhou, lǎoshī shuō le yí ge xīn cí. Xiǎo Huá bù míngbai zhè ge cí de yìsi.*
During class today, the teacher used a new word. Xiaohua did not understand what it meant.

**下课以后，小华去问老师。老师认真地解释了这件事情，小华终于明白了。**
*Xiàkè yǐhòu, Xiǎo Huá qù wèn lǎoshī. Lǎoshī rènzhēn de jiěshì le zhè jiàn shìqing, Xiǎo Huá zhōngyú míngbai le.*
After class, Xiaohua asks the teacher. The teacher carefully explains the matter, and Xiaohua finally understands.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: During class today, the teacher used a new word. Xiaohua did not understand what it meant.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: After class, Xiaohua asks the teacher. The teacher carefully explains the matter, and Xiaohua finally understands.

## 6. Watch out

意思 (yìsi) can mean meaning or intention and can appear in 有意思 (yǒu yìsi); do not assume all these expressions are interchangeable.

**Pronunciation:** The final syllable of 意思 (yìsi) and 事情 (shìqing) is normally neutral in everyday conversation.
