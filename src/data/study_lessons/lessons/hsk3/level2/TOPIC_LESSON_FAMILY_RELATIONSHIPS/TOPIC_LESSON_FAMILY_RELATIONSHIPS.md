# Talking About Family Members

Talk about grandparents and spouses, and describe a family visit in ordinary Mandarin.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Name grandparents correctly.

- Use 妻子 (qīzi) and 丈夫 (zhàngfu) for spouses.

- Describe a family visit with familiar verbs.

## 1. Conversation — Talking About Family Members

Talk about grandparents and spouses, and describe a family visit in ordinary Mandarin.

**A:** 星期天你去哪儿？ (Xīngqītiān nǐ qù nǎr?) — Where are you going on Sunday?

**B:** 我要去看爷爷和奶奶。 (Wǒ yào qù kàn yéye hé nǎinai.) — I am going to visit my grandpa and grandma.

**A:** 他们住得远吗？ (Tāmen zhù de yuǎn ma?) — Do they live far away?

**B:** 不远。我的丈夫也想一起去。 (Bù yuǎn. Wǒ de zhàngfu yě xiǎng yìqǐ qù.) — Not far. My husband wants to go together too.

## 2. How the language works

### Introducing relatives

Place a possessive phrase before a family term: 我的 (wǒ de) + 爷爷 (yéye) or 奶奶 (nǎinai).

**Pattern:** Possessor + 的 + relative

**这是我爷爷，那是我奶奶。**
*Zhè shì wǒ yéye, nà shì wǒ nǎinai.*
This is my grandpa, and that is my grandma.

**我要去看爷爷和奶奶。**
*Wǒ yào qù kàn yéye hé nǎinai.*
I am going to visit my grandpa and grandma.

### Talking about a spouse

妻子 (qīzi) means wife and 丈夫 (zhàngfu) means husband. Use the ordinary verb phrase that follows to tell what they do.

**Pattern:** Person + 的 + 妻子/丈夫

**她的丈夫星期六要上班。**
*Tā de zhàngfu xīngqīliù yào shàngbān.*
Her husband has to work on Saturday.

**不远。我的丈夫也想一起去。**
*Bù yuǎn. Wǒ de zhàngfu yě xiǎng yìqǐ qù.*
Not far. My husband wants to go together too.

## 4. Read for meaning — Talking About Family Members — short reading

**王先生和妻子每个星期都去看爷爷奶奶。他们一起吃饭，也一起说话。**
*Wáng xiānsheng hé qīzi měi ge xīngqī dōu qù kàn yéye nǎinai. Tāmen yìqǐ chī fàn, yě yìqǐ shuōhuà.*
Mr Wang and his wife visit the grandparents every week. They eat and talk together.

**奶奶很高兴，因为大家今天都在家。**
*Nǎinai hěn gāoxìng, yīnwèi dàjiā jīntiān dōu zài jiā.*
Grandma is happy because everyone is at home today.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Mr Wang and his wife visit the grandparents every week. They eat and talk together.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Grandma is happy because everyone is at home today.

## 6. Watch out

Do not use 爷爷 (yéye) or 奶奶 (nǎinai) interchangeably; they refer to distinct grandparents in standard kinship terminology.

**Pronunciation:** The second syllables of 爷爷 (yéye) and 奶奶 (nǎinai) are neutral in everyday speech.
