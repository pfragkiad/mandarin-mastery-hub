# Words, Classes and Examinations

Talk about school, classrooms, word meanings, teachers, tests and stationery.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask about a word and recognize the reply.

- Identify classroom items such as 笔 (bǐ) and 本子 (běnzi).

- Discuss tests and the school year.

## 1. Conversation — Words, Classes and Examinations

Talk about school, classrooms, word meanings, teachers, tests and stationery.

**A:** 老师，这个词是什么意思？ (Lǎoshī, zhè ge cí shì shénme yìsi?) — Teacher, what does this word mean?

**B:** 你先看书；不懂的地方可以问我。 (Nǐ xiān kàn shū; bù dǒng de dìfang kěyǐ wèn wǒ.) — Read the book first; you can ask me about what you do not understand.

**A:** 我忘带笔和本子了。 (Wǒ wàng dài bǐ hé běnzi le.) — I forgot to bring a pen and notebook.

**B:** 没关系，我这儿有。考试前记得准备好！ (Méi guānxi, wǒ zhèr yǒu. Kǎoshì qián jìde zhǔnbèi hǎo!) — No problem, I have some here. Remember to prepare before the exam!

## 2. How the language works

### Learning a new word

Use 懂 (dǒng) to talk about understanding and 教 (jiāo) to say who teaches whom.

**Pattern**

人 + 教 + 学习者 + 教学内容  
*rén + jiāo + xuéxízhě + jiàoxué nèiróng*  
&lt;person&gt; + **教** + &lt;learner&gt; + &lt;what is taught&gt;

**我不懂这个词，请你教我。**
*Wǒ bù dǒng zhè ge cí, qǐng nǐ jiāo wǒ.*
I do not understand this word. Please teach me.

**你先看书；不懂的地方可以问我。**
*Nǐ xiān kàn shū; bù dǒng de dìfang kěyǐ wèn wǒ.*
Read the book first; you can ask me about what you do not understand.

### Talking about school tests

考试 (kǎoshì) is an examination; 考 (kǎo) can function as a verb, and 题 (tí) is a question or problem.

**Pattern**

时间 + 有 + 考试  
*shíjiān + yǒu + kǎoshì*  
&lt;time&gt; + **有** + **考试**

**我们班的考试下星期开始。**
*Wǒmen bān de kǎoshì xià xīngqī kāishǐ.*
Our class exams begin next week.

**没关系，我这儿有。考试前记得准备好！**
*Méi guānxi, wǒ zhèr yǒu. Kǎoshì qián jìde zhǔnbèi hǎo!*
No problem, I have some here. Remember to prepare before the exam!

## 4. Read for meaning — Words, Classes and Examinations — short reading

**高中开学了。新同学来到教室，老师教他们读新词。**
*Gāozhōng kāixué le. Xīn tóngxué lái dào jiàoshì, lǎoshī jiāo tāmen dú xīn cí.*
High school has started. New students come to the classroom, and the teacher teaches them to read new words.

**下周有考试，老师让全班认真做题。大家都想考得好。**
*Xià zhōu yǒu kǎoshì, lǎoshī ràng quán bān rènzhēn zuò tí. Dàjiā dōu xiǎng kǎo de hǎo.*
There is an exam next week, and the teacher asks the whole class to work carefully. Everyone wants to do well.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: High school has started. New students come to the classroom, and the teacher teaches them to read new words.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: There is an exam next week, and the teacher asks the whole class to work carefully. Everyone wants to do well.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Explain to a classmate: I do not understand this word. Please teach me.

*Possible response:* **我不懂这个词，请你教我。**
*Wǒ bù dǒng zhè ge cí, qǐng nǐ jiāo wǒ.*
I do not understand this word. Please teach me.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: Our class exams begin next week.

*Possible response:* **我们班的考试下星期开始。**
*Wǒmen bān de kǎoshì xià xīngqī kāishǐ.*
Our class exams begin next week.

## 6. Watch out

Do not treat 考 (kǎo) and 考试 (kǎoshì) as interchangeable in every construction; notice whether the sentence needs a verb or a noun.

**Pronunciation:** Distinguish 教 (jiāo), a first tone, from 考 (kǎo), a third tone; 本子 (běnzi) has a neutral second syllable.
