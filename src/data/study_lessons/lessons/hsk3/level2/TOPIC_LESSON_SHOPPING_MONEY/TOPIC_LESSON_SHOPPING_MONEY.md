# Buying Tickets and Shopping

Ask about tickets, discuss shopping at the mall and describe spending money.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Ask about the price of a 门票 (ménpiào).

- Talk about a shopping mall.

- Understand 花 (huā) when it means spend money.

## 1. Conversation — Buying Tickets and Shopping

Ask about tickets, discuss shopping at the mall and describe spending money.

**A:** 这家商场很大，我们进去看看吧。 (Zhè jiā shāngchǎng hěn dà, wǒmen jìnqù kànkan ba.) — This mall is big. Let’s go inside and look around.

**B:** 好。你今天想买什么？ (Hǎo. Nǐ jīntiān xiǎng mǎi shénme?) — Sure. What do you want to buy today?

**A:** 我想先问一下电影的门票多少钱。 (Wǒ xiǎng xiān wèn yíxià diànyǐng de ménpiào duōshao qián.) — First I’d like to ask how much the movie tickets cost.

**B:** 别花太多钱。我们还有别的事情要做。 (Bié huā tài duō qián. Wǒmen hái yǒu bié de shìqing yào zuò.) — Don’t spend too much. We still have other things to do.

## 2. How the language works

### Paying for an activity

In its spending-money sense, 花 (huā) is a verb; place the amount of money after it.

**Pattern**

人 + 花 + 金额 + 动词短语  
*rén + huā + jīn'é + dòngcí duǎnyǔ*  
&lt;person&gt; + **花** + &lt;money amount&gt; + &lt;verb phrase&gt;

**我在商场买了一件衣服。**
*Wǒ zài shāngchǎng mǎi le yí jiàn yīfu.*
I bought an item of clothing at the mall.

**好。你今天想买什么？**
*Hǎo. Nǐ jīntiān xiǎng mǎi shénme?*
Sure. What do you want to buy today?

### Asking about admission

门票 (ménpiào) is an admission ticket. Combine it with 多少钱 (duōshao qián) to ask the price.

**Pattern**

门票 + 多少钱？  
*ménpiào + duōshao qián?*  
**门票** + **多少钱**？

**你买门票花了多少钱？**
*Nǐ mǎi ménpiào huā le duōshao qián?*
How much did you spend buying admission tickets?

**别花太多钱。我们还有别的事情要做。**
*Bié huā tài duō qián. Wǒmen hái yǒu bié de shìqing yào zuò.*
Don’t spend too much. We still have other things to do.

## 4. Read for meaning — Buying Tickets and Shopping — short reading

**星期六，妈妈带孩子去商场。孩子想看电影，可是门票有点儿贵。**
*Xīngqīliù, māma dài háizi qù shāngchǎng. Háizi xiǎng kàn diànyǐng, kěshì ménpiào yǒudiǎnr guì.*
On Saturday, a mother takes her child to a mall. The child wants to see a movie, but tickets are a little expensive.

**妈妈说：“今天不花那么多钱，我们先去吃饭。”孩子同意了。**
*Māma shuō: “Jīntiān bù huā nàme duō qián, wǒmen xiān qù chī fàn.” Háizi tóngyì le.*
Mom says, “We won’t spend that much today; let’s eat first.” The child agrees.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: On Saturday, a mother takes her child to a mall. The child wants to see a movie, but tickets are a little expensive.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Mom says, “We won’t spend that much today; let’s eat first.” The child agrees.

## 6. Watch out

花 (huā) can mean spend or flower in other contexts; the presence of 钱 (qián) makes the spending meaning clear.

**Pronunciation:** Notice the fourth tone in 票 (piào) and the third tone in 场 (chǎng).
