# Using the Internet

**Subtopics:** digital-technology

Talk about going online and finding information on the internet in daily life.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Use 上网 (shàngwǎng) for going online.

- Recognize 网上 (wǎngshang) as a location or medium.

- Describe a simple online search with familiar verbs.

## 1. Conversation — Using the Internet

Talk about going online and finding information on the internet in daily life.

**A:** 你晚上经常做什么？ (Nǐ wǎnshang jīngcháng zuò shénme?) — What do you often do in the evening?

**B:** 我喜欢上网看新闻。 (Wǒ xǐhuan shàngwǎng kàn xīnwén.) — I like going online to read the news.

**A:** 明天的天气你知道吗？ (Míngtiān de tiānqì nǐ zhīdào ma?) — Do you know tomorrow’s weather?

**B:** 我不知道。我们一起在网上看看吧。 (Wǒ bù zhīdào. Wǒmen yìqǐ zài wǎngshang kànkan ba.) — I don’t know. Let’s check online together.

## 2. How the language works

### Going online

上网 (shàngwǎng) is a verb meaning go online or use the internet. Follow it with an optional purpose such as 学习 (xuéxí).

**Pattern:** Subject + 上网 + activity

**我每天上网学习。**
*Wǒ měitiān shàngwǎng xuéxí.*
I go online to study every day.

**我喜欢上网看新闻。**
*Wǒ xǐhuan shàngwǎng kàn xīnwén.*
I like going online to read the news.

### Doing something online

网上 (wǎngshang) is an online location or medium; use 在网上 (zài wǎngshang) before an activity.

**Pattern:** Subject + 在网上 + verb

**这本书可以在网上看。**
*Zhè běn shū kěyǐ zài wǎngshang kàn.*
You can read this book online.

**我不知道。我们一起在网上看看吧。**
*Wǒ bù zhīdào. Wǒmen yìqǐ zài wǎngshang kànkan ba.*
I don’t know. Let’s check online together.

## 4. Read for meaning — Using the Internet — short reading

**小明每天晚上上网学习汉语，有时还在网上看电影。**
*Xiǎomíng měitiān wǎnshang shàngwǎng xuéxí Hànyǔ, yǒushí hái zài wǎngshang kàn diànyǐng.*
Every evening Xiaoming goes online to study Mandarin; sometimes he also watches movies online.

**今天老师让学生在网上找一个中文故事。小明找到了以后，就把故事读给朋友听。**
*Jīntiān lǎoshī ràng xuéshēng zài wǎngshang zhǎo yí ge Zhōngwén gùshi. Xiǎomíng zhǎo dào le yǐhòu, jiù bǎ gùshi dú gěi péngyou tīng.*
Today the teacher asks students to find a Chinese story online. After finding one, Xiaoming reads it to a friend.

## 6. Watch out

上网 (shàngwǎng) is a verb phrase, while 网上 (wǎngshang) identifies the online setting; do not use them in identical sentence positions.

**Pronunciation:** Compare 上网 (shàngwǎng), both syllables fourth and third tones, with 网上 (wǎngshang), third tone followed by neutral tone.
