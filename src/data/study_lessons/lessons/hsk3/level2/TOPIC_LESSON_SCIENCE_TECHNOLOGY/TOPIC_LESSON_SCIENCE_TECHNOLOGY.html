1 · Conversation — Using the Internet

Talk about going online and finding information on the internet in daily life.

A

你晚上经常做什么？ (Nǐ wǎnshang jīngcháng zuò shénme?)

What do you often do in the evening?

B

我喜欢上网看新闻。 (Wǒ xǐhuan shàngwǎng kàn xīnwén.)

I like going online to read the news.

A

明天的天气你知道吗？ (Míngtiān de tiānqì nǐ zhīdào ma?)

Do you know tomorrow’s weather?

B

我不知道。我们一起在网上看看吧。 (Wǒ bù zhīdào. Wǒmen yìqǐ zài wǎngshang kànkan ba.)

I don’t know. Let’s check online together.

2 · How the language works

Going online

上网 (shàngwǎng) is a verb meaning go online or use the internet. Follow it with an optional purpose such as 学习 (xuéxí).

主语 + 上网 + (目的)
zhǔyǔ + shàngwǎng + (mùdì)
<subject> + 上网 + (<purpose>)

我每天上网学习。 (Wǒ měitiān shàngwǎng xuéxí.)

I go online to study every day.

我喜欢上网看新闻。 (Wǒ xǐhuan shàngwǎng kàn xīnwén.)

I like going online to read the news.

Doing something online

网上 (wǎngshang) is an online location or medium; use 在网上 (zài wǎngshang) before an activity.

主语 + 在网上 + 动词
zhǔyǔ + zài wǎngshàng + dòngcí
<subject> + 在网上 + <verb>

这本书可以在网上看。 (Zhè běn shū kěyǐ zài wǎngshang kàn.)

You can read this book online.

我不知道。我们一起在网上看看吧。 (Wǒ bù zhīdào. Wǒmen yìqǐ zài wǎngshang kànkan ba.)

I don’t know. Let’s check online together.

6 · Watch out

上网 (shàngwǎng) is a verb phrase, while 网上 (wǎngshang) identifies the online setting; do not use them in identical sentence positions.

Pronunciation clinic

Compare 上网 (shàngwǎng), both syllables fourth and third tones, with 网上 (wǎngshang), third tone followed by neutral tone.
