# Grammar & Function Words — Part 1: Linking Ideas in Conversation

Connect two thoughts naturally with because, so, but and choice questions.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Explain a reason with 因为 (yīnwèi) and a result with 所以 (suǒyǐ).

- Contrast statements with 但是 (dànshì).

- Ask about alternatives with 还是 (háishi).

## 1. Conversation — Linking Ideas in Conversation

Connect two thoughts naturally with because, so, but and choice questions.

**A:** 今天我们去公园还是去电影院？ (Jīntiān wǒmen qù gōngyuán háishi qù diànyǐngyuàn?) — Are we going to the park or to the cinema today?

**B:** 我想去公园，但是可能会下雨。 (Wǒ xiǎng qù gōngyuán, dànshì kěnéng huì xià yǔ.) — I want to go to the park, but it might rain.

**A:** 因为天气不好，所以我们去看电影吧。 (Yīnwèi tiānqì bù hǎo, suǒyǐ wǒmen qù kàn diànyǐng ba.) — Because the weather is bad, let’s go see a movie.

**B:** 好啊！我跟你一起去，现在就走。 (Hǎo a! Wǒ gēn nǐ yìqǐ qù, xiànzài jiù zǒu.) — Great! I’ll go with you; let’s leave right now.

## 2. How the language works

### A reason and its result

因为 (yīnwèi) introduces the reason; 所以 (suǒyǐ) introduces the consequence. One can also appear without the other when the relationship is clear.

**Pattern**

因为 + 原因， 所以 + 结果  
*yīnwèi + yuányīn,  suǒyǐ + jiéguǒ*  
**因为** + &lt;reason&gt;， **所以** + &lt;result&gt;

**我今天很累，所以想早点儿回家。**
*Wǒ jīntiān hěn lèi, suǒyǐ xiǎng zǎo diǎnr huí jiā.*
I am tired today, so I want to go home earlier.

**我想去公园，但是可能会下雨。**
*Wǒ xiǎng qù gōngyuán, dànshì kěnéng huì xià yǔ.*
I want to go to the park, but it might rain.

### Concession and contrast

虽然 (suīrán) starts a concession and 但是 (dànshì) can introduce a contrasting main clause.

**Pattern**

虽然 + 分句，但是 + 转折分句  
*suīrán + fēnjù, dànshì + zhuǎnzhé fēnjù*  
**虽然** + &lt;clause&gt;，**但是** + &lt;contrasting clause&gt;

**你喝茶还是喝水？**
*Nǐ hē chá háishi hē shuǐ?*
Do you drink tea or water?

**好啊！我跟你一起去，现在就走。**
*Hǎo a! Wǒ gēn nǐ yìqǐ qù, xiànzài jiù zǒu.*
Great! I’ll go with you; let’s leave right now.

## 4. Read for meaning — Linking Ideas in Conversation — short reading

**小李本来想在外面运动，但今天下雨了。他觉得可能不能去公园。**
*Xiǎo Lǐ běnlái xiǎng zài wàimiàn yùndòng, dàn jīntiān xià yǔ le. Tā juéde kěnéng bù néng qù gōngyuán.*
Xiao Li originally wanted to exercise outdoors, but it is raining. He thinks he may not be able to go to the park.

**因为天气不好，所以小李跟朋友去了电影院。虽然他喜欢运动，但他也喜欢看电影。**
*Yīnwèi tiānqì bù hǎo, suǒyǐ Xiǎo Lǐ gēn péngyou qù le diànyǐngyuàn. Suīrán tā xǐhuan yùndòng, dàn tā yě xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.*
Because the weather is bad, Xiao Li goes to the cinema with a friend. Although he likes sports, he also likes movies.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiao Li originally wanted to exercise outdoors, but it is raining. He thinks he may not be able to go to the park.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Because the weather is bad, Xiao Li goes to the cinema with a friend. Although he likes sports, he also likes movies.

## 6. Watch out

还是 (háishi) between alternatives commonly marks a choice question; 和 (hé) or 跟 (gēn) usually joins people or things rather than asking which one to choose.

**Pronunciation:** Practise the tone change in 但是 (dànshì) and the second-tone first syllable of 还是 (háishi).
