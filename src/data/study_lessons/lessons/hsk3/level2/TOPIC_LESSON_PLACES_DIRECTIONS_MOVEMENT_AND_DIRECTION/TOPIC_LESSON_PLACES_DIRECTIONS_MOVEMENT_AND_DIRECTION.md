# Places, Position & Direction — Part 2: Entering, Leaving and Returning

**Subtopics:** position-direction

Distinguish movement toward and away from the speaker when entering, exiting or returning.

> A teaching lesson: contextual communication, grammar, guided production, reading and speaking. Word-detail cards hold the original two examples per entry.

## Your goal

- Use 进来 (jìnlái) and 进去 (jìnqù) appropriately.

- Contrast 出来 (chūlái) with 出去 (chūqù).

- Talk about returning and moving up or down.

## 1. Conversation — Entering, Leaving and Returning

Distinguish movement toward and away from the speaker when entering, exiting or returning.

**A:** 你在里面吗？我可以进来吗？ (Nǐ zài lǐmiàn ma? Wǒ kěyǐ jìnlái ma?) — Are you inside? May I come in?

**B:** 可以，进来吧！我正要出去。 (Kěyǐ, jìnlái ba! Wǒ zhèng yào chūqù.) — Yes, come in! I’m just about to go out.

**A:** 你出去以后，还回来吗？ (Nǐ chūqù yǐhòu, hái huílái ma?) — Will you come back after you go out?

**B:** 我一会儿就回来。你先上去等我。 (Wǒ yíhuìr jiù huílái. Nǐ xiān shàngqù děng wǒ.) — I’ll be back soon. Go upstairs and wait for me.

## 2. How the language works

### Movement toward the reference point

来 (lái) signals movement toward the speaker or chosen reference point: 进来 (jìnlái), 出来 (chūlái), 回来 (huílái).

**Pattern:** Movement verb + 来

**请你过来帮我一下。**
*Qǐng nǐ guòlái bāng wǒ yíxià.*
Please come over and help me.

**可以，进来吧！我正要出去。**
*Kěyǐ, jìnlái ba! Wǒ zhèng yào chūqù.*
Yes, come in! I’m just about to go out.

### Movement away from the reference point

去 (qù) signals movement away from the speaker or chosen reference point: 进去 (jìnqù), 出去 (chūqù), 回去 (huíqù).

**Pattern:** Movement verb + 去

**老师走进教室，学生们都站起来。**
*Lǎoshī zǒu jìn jiàoshì, xuéshēngmen dōu zhàn qǐlái.*
The teacher walks into the classroom, and all the students stand up.

**我一会儿就回来。你先上去等我。**
*Wǒ yíhuìr jiù huílái. Nǐ xiān shàngqù děng wǒ.*
I’ll be back soon. Go upstairs and wait for me.

## 4. Read for meaning — Entering, Leaving and Returning — short reading

**小林走出教室，又发现自己的书包没拿。他跑回去，进去拿了书包。**
*Xiǎo Lín zǒu chū jiàoshì, yòu fāxiàn zìjǐ de shūbāo méi ná. Tā pǎo huíqù, jìnqù ná le shūbāo.*
Xiaolin leaves the classroom and notices he forgot his schoolbag. He runs back and enters to get it.

**朋友在楼下叫他：“快下来！”小林拿着包走下来，和朋友一起出门。**
*Péngyou zài lóu xià jiào tā: “Kuài xiàlái!” Xiǎo Lín názhe bāo zǒu xiàlái, hé péngyou yìqǐ chūmén.*
A friend calls from downstairs, “Come down quickly!” Xiaolin comes downstairs with his bag and leaves with the friend.

**Q1. What do we learn about the people or situation in the first part of the reading?** Suggested answer: Xiaolin leaves the classroom and notices he forgot his schoolbag. He runs back and enters to get it.

**Q2. What happens or is explained in the second part of the reading?** Suggested answer: A friend calls from downstairs, “Come down quickly!” Xiaolin comes downstairs with his bag and leaves with the friend.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

*Possible response:* **请你过来帮我一下。**
*Qǐng nǐ guòlái bāng wǒ yíxià.*
Please come over and help me.

**Speaking 2:** Explain to a classmate: The teacher walks into the classroom, and all the students stand up.

*Possible response:* **老师走进教室，学生们都站起来。**
*Lǎoshī zǒu jìn jiàoshì, xuéshēngmen dōu zhàn qǐlái.*
The teacher walks into the classroom, and all the students stand up.

## 6. Watch out

Do not choose 来 (lái) or 去 (qù) solely from English “come” and “go.” Consider the location from which the action is described.

**Pronunciation:** Practise the distinction between 出来 (chūlái) and 出去 (chūqù); pay attention to the directional final syllables.
