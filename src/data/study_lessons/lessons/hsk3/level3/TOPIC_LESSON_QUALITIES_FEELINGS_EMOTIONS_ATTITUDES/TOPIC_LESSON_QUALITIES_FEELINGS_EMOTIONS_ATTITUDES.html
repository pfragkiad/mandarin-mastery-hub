Feelings, Worries & Attitudes

Happiness, sadness, worry, enthusiasm, and willingness

qualities-feelings · #emotions · #feelings · #attitudes

Learning objectives

  • Express positive and negative emotions: 开心, 难过, 生气, 哭, 急, 着急, 害怕, and 怕.
  • Express attitudes and inner feelings: 感到, 放心, 满意, 热情, 喜爱, 愿意, 努力, and 心里.
  • Describe resemblance with 像.

1 · Cheering Up a Friend

Xiao Lin reassures her friend who is anxious before an exam.

A: 明天就要考试了，我心里有点儿着急，害怕考不好。 (Míngtiān jiù yào kǎoshì le, wǒ xīnlǐ yǒudiǎnr zháojí, hàipà kǎo bù hǎo.)
The exam is tomorrow—I feel anxious inside and am afraid I won’t do well.

B: 别怕！你这学期一直这么努力，肯定没问题，放心吧。 (Bié pà! Nǐ zhè xuéqī yìzhí zhème nǔlì, kěndìng méi wèntí, fàngxīn ba.)
Don’t be afraid! You’ve worked so hard all semester—there’s definitely no problem, rest assured.

A: 谢谢你这么热情地鼓励我，听你这么说我开心多了！ (Xièxie nǐ zhème rèqíng de gǔlì wǒ, tīng nǐ zhème shuō wǒ kāixīn duō le!)
Thank you for encouraging me so warmly—hearing you say that makes me feel much happier!

B: 如果你愿意，今晚咱们再一起复习半小时，保证让你感到满意！ (Rúguǒ nǐ yuànyì, jīnwǎn zánmen zài yìqǐ fùxí bàn xiǎoshí, bǎozhèng ràng nǐ gǎndào mǎnyì!)
If you’re willing, let’s review together for half an hour tonight—I guarantee you’ll feel satisfied!

2 · How the language works

Being satisfied or interested with 对……满意 / 感兴趣

<Subject> + 对 + <Target> + 很 + 满意 / 感兴趣 (<Subject> + duì + <Target> + hěn + mǎnyì / gǎn xìngqù)

<Subject> + toward + <Target> + very + satisfied / interested

Place 对 + target before 满意 (satisfied) or 感兴趣 (interested).

客人对这家宾馆的服务非常满意。 (Kèrén duì zhè jiā bīnguǎn de fúwù fēicháng mǎnyì.)

The guests are very satisfied with this hotel’s service.

弟弟从小就对历史故事很感兴趣。 (Dìdi cóngxiǎo jiù duì lìshǐ gùshi hěn gǎn xìngqù.)

My younger brother has been very interested in historical stories since childhood.

Being willing to do something with 愿意

<Subject> + 愿意 + <Verb Phrase> (<Subject> + yuànyì + <Verb Phrase>)

<Subject> + be willing to + <Verb Phrase>

Place 愿意 (yuànyì) before a verb phrase to say someone is happy or willing to do it.

他非常愿意帮助新来的同学。 (Tā fēicháng yuànyì bāngzhù xīn lái de tóngxué.)

He is very willing to help newly arrived classmates.

你愿意周末和我一块儿去看电影吗？ (Nǐ yuànyì zhōumò hé wǒ yíkuàir qù kàn diànyǐng ma?)

Are you willing to go see a movie with me this weekend?

3 · Build new sentences

Express emotions, reassurance, and effort.

Model

小女孩因为丢了玩具，难过得哭了起来。 (Xiǎo nǚhái yīnwèi diū le wánjù, nánguò de kū le qǐlái.)

Because she lost her toy, the little girl felt so sad she started crying.

1. Say don’t get angry; let’s talk it over calmly.

Show one natural answer

别生气了，咱们好好说。 (Bié shēngqì le, zánmen hǎohāo shuō.)

Don’t be angry anymore—let’s talk it over calmly.

2. Say everyone likes this warm-hearted teacher.

Show one natural answer

大家都很喜爱这位热情的老师。 (Dàjiā dōu hěn xǐ’ài zhè wèi rèqíng de lǎoshī.)

Everyone loves this warm-hearted teacher.

3. Say she looks very much like her mother.

Show one natural answer

她长得真像她妈妈。 (Tā zhǎng de zhēn xiàng tā māma.)

She really looks like her mother.

4 · Read in context: A Warm Welcome on the First Day

刚转学到新班级那天，小陈心里既紧张又有点儿害怕，担心自己交不到新朋友。 (Gāng zhuǎnxué dào xīn bānjí nà tiān, Xiǎo Chén xīnlǐ jì jǐnzhāng yòu yǒudiǎnr hàipà, dānxīn zìjǐ jiāo bú dào xīn péngyou.)
On the day he transferred to a new class, Xiao Chen felt both nervous and a bit afraid inside, worried he wouldn’t make new friends.

没想到班里的同学都非常热情，主动找他聊天儿，还愿意带他熟悉校园。他感到十分开心，再也不难过了。 (Méi xiǎngdào bān lǐ de tóngxué dōu fēicháng rèqíng, zhǔdòng zhǎo tā liáotiānr, hái yuànyì dài tā shúxī xiàoyuán. Tā gǎndào shífēn kāixīn, zài yě bù nánguò le.)
Unexpectedly, the classmates were all very warm, took the initiative to chat with him, and were willing to show him around campus. He felt very happy and wasn’t sad anymore.

Q1. Why was Xiao Chen afraid on his first day?
Show answer
He was worried he wouldn’t be able to make new friends.
Q2. How did his classmates make him feel better?
Show answer
They were warm, chatted with him, and showed him around campus.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Tell a friend not to worry using 放心 and 别着急.
Show one possible response

别着急，时间还够，你一定能做完，放心吧！ (Bié zháojí, shíjiān hái gòu, nǐ yídìng néng zuò wán, fàngxīn ba!)

Don’t worry—there’s still enough time, you’ll definitely finish, rest assured!

2. Say something you are interested in or satisfied with.
Show one possible response

我对中国音乐很感兴趣，最近学唱了两首新歌，自己挺满意的。 (Wǒ duì Zhōngguó yīnyuè hěn gǎn xìngqù, zuìjìn xué chàng le liǎng shǒu xīn gē, zìjǐ tǐng mǎnyì de.)

I’m very interested in Chinese music; recently I learned two new songs and I’m quite satisfied.

6 · Watch out

生气 is a separable verb ("get angry"): say 生谁的气 ("get angry at someone") or 对……生气, never *生气他.

Pronunciation clinic

着急 (zháojí) has two rising second tones; 满意 (mǎnyì) moves from low third tone to falling fourth tone.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

How did Xiao Chen’s feelings change by the end of the story?

Show answer
He felt very happy and was no longer sad or afraid.

The passage ends with 他感到十分开心，再也不难过了.

Production

Write two sentences using 放心 and 对……满意 or 愿意.

Show answer
请放心，我会努力把工作做好，让大家感到满意。

Use 放心 and 满意 naturally.

Review and next step

  • Express happiness, sadness, anger, and worry.
  • Reassure others with 放心 and 别着急.
  • Use 对……满意 / 感兴趣 and 愿意.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which word means "anxious / worried"?

a. 着急 (zháojí) — anxious / worried

b. 放心 (fàngxīn) — reassured

c. 满意 (mǎnyì) — satisfied

Show answer
a

着急 means anxious or worried.

Selection

Which verb means "to get angry"?

a. 害怕 (hàipà) — be afraid

b. 生气 (shēngqì) — get angry

c. 难过 (nánguò) — sad

Show answer
b

生气 means to get angry.

Which verb means "to be willing to"?

a. 愿意 (yuànyì) — be willing

b. 努力 (nǔlì) — work hard

c. 哭 (kū) — cry

Show answer
a

愿意 means to be willing to.
