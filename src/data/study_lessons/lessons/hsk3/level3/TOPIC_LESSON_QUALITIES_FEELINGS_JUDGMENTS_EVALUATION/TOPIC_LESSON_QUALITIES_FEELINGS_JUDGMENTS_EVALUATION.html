Describing Quality & Difficulty

Easy or hard, suitable, clear, strange, and important

qualities-feelings · #evaluation · #descriptions · #quality

Learning objectives

  • Evaluate difficulty and clarity: 简单, 容易, 难, 清楚, and 差.
  • Appraise suitability, value, and fame: 方便, 合适, 有名, 有用, 重要, 可爱, 难看, 难听, and 奇怪.
  • Use degree words and traits: 极, 特别, 挺, 聪明, and 认真.

1 · Choosing a Gift and a Course

Two friends evaluate a Chinese course and pick out a suitable gift.

A: 你觉得这门课难不难？我怕太难了学不会。 (Nǐ juéde zhè mén kè nán bu nán? Wǒ pà tài nán le xué bú huì.)
Do you think this course is hard? I’m afraid if it’s too hard I won’t learn it.

B: 其实挺简单的，老师讲得特别清楚，只要认真学就很容易。 (Qíshí tǐng jiǎndān de, lǎoshī jiǎng de tèbié qīngchu, zhǐyào rènzhēn xué jiù hěn róngyì.)
Actually it’s quite simple; the teacher explains especially clearly—as long as you study seriously, it’s very easy.

A: 那就好！对了，我想买那只可爱的小熊猫送给妹妹，你觉得合适吗？ (Nà jiù hǎo! Duìle, wǒ xiǎng mǎi nà zhī kě’ài de xiǎo xióngmāo sòng gěi mèimei, nǐ juéde héshì ma?)
Good! By the way, I want to buy that cute little panda toy for my younger sister—do you think it’s suitable?

B: 合适极了！她肯定喜欢。 (Héshì jíle! Tā kěndìng xǐhuan.)
Extremely suitable! She’ll definitely like it.

2 · How the language works

Saying "extremely" with ……极了

<Subject> + <Adjective> + 极了 (<Subject> + <Adjective> + jíle)

<Subject> + <Adjective> + extremely

Place 极了 (jíle) right after an adjective or mental verb to mean "extremely".

今天的天气好极了，不冷也不热。 (Jīntiān de tiānqì hǎo jíle, bù lěng yě bú rè.)

Today’s weather is wonderful—neither cold nor hot.

这本词典对初学者来说有用极了。 (Zhè běn cídiǎn duì chūxuézhě lái shuō yǒuyòng jíle.)

This dictionary is extremely useful for beginners.

Softened praise with 挺……的

<Subject> + 挺 + <Adjective> + 的 (<Subject> + tǐng + <Adjective> + de)

<Subject> + quite + <Adjective> + (marker)

Wrap an adjective in 挺……的 (tǐng … de) to mean "quite / rather".

这只小猫挺聪明的，一叫它就过来。 (Zhè zhī xiǎo māo tǐng cōngmíng de, yí jiào tā jiù guòlái.)

This kitten is quite clever—it comes right over as soon as you call it.

坐地铁去机场挺方便的。 (Zuò dìtiě qù jīchǎng tǐng fāngbiàn de.)

Taking the subway to the airport is quite convenient.

3 · Build new sentences

Evaluate clarity, quality, and strangeness.

Model

这个方法很重要，而且特别有用。 (Zhège fāngfǎ hěn zhòngyào, érqiě tèbié yǒuyòng.)

This method is very important, and especially useful.

1. Say that song sounds unpleasant, and that picture looks ugly.

Show one natural answer

那首歌有点儿难听，那幅画也不太好看，甚至有点儿难看。 (Nà shǒu gē yǒudiǎnr nántīng, nà fú huà yě bú tài hǎokàn, shènzhì yǒudiǎnr nánkàn.)

That song sounds a bit unpleasant, and that painting isn’t great—even a bit ugly.

2. Say it is strange that nobody is in the office.

Show one natural answer

真奇怪，办公室里一个人都没有。 (Zhēn qíguài, bàngōngshì lǐ yí ge rén dōu méiyǒu.)

How strange—there isn’t a single person in the office.

3. Say his Chinese pronunciation is not bad at all.

Show one natural answer

他的汉语发音一点儿也不差。 (Tā de Hànyǔ fāyīn yìdiǎnr yě bú chà.)

His Chinese pronunciation is not bad at all.

4 · Read in context: A Famous Small Restaurant

学校东门外有一家很有名的面馆。店面虽然不大，但味道好极了，价格也很合适。 (Xuéxiào dōngmén wài yǒu yì jiā hěn yǒumíng de miànguǎn. Diànmiàn suīrán bú dà, dàn wèidào hǎo jíle, jiàgé yě hěn héshì.)
Outside the east gate of the school there is a very famous noodle shop. Although the storefront isn’t big, the taste is terrific and the price is very reasonable.

老板做事特别认真，菜单写得清清楚楚。大家觉得来这里吃饭既方便又实惠，所以每天中午人都很多。 (Lǎobǎn zuòshì tèbié rènzhēn, càidān xiě de qīngqīngchǔchǔ. Dàjiā juéde lái zhèlǐ chīfàn jì fāngbiàn yòu shíhuì, suǒyǐ měitiān zhōngwǔ rén dōu hěn duō.)
The owner works especially conscientiously, and the menu is written crystal clear. Everyone feels eating here is both convenient and affordable, so it’s crowded every noon.

Q1. Where is the famous noodle shop located?
Show answer
Outside the east gate of the school.
Q2. Why is it crowded every noon?
Show answer
Because the food tastes great, the price is reasonable, the owner is conscientious, and eating there is convenient and affordable.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Recommend a book, app, or place using 有用, 方便, or 极了.
Show one possible response

这本词典有用极了，查生词特别方便。 (Zhè běn cídiǎn yǒuyòng jíle, chá shēngcí tèbié fāngbiàn.)

This dictionary is extremely useful—looking up new words is especially convenient.

2. Describe a friend or classmate using 聪明 and 认真.
Show one possible response

我同桌既聪明又认真，每次作业都写得特别好。 (Wǒ tóngzhuō jì cōngmíng yòu rènzhēn, měi cì zuòyè dōu xiě de tèbié hǎo.)

My deskmate is both clever and conscientious; every homework assignment is written especially well.

6 · Watch out

难看 ("ugly / unpleasant to look at") and 难听 ("unpleasant to hear") follow the pattern 难 + verb, just like 难吃 ("tastes bad") and 好吃 / 好看 / 好听.

Pronunciation clinic

清楚 (qīngchu) and 聪明 (cōngming) both often take a light neutral tone on the second syllable in connected speech.
