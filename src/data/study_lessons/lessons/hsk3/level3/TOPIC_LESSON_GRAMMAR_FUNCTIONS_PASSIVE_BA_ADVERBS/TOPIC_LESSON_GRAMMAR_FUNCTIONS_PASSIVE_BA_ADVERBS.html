把, 被, Perceptions & Adverbs

把, 被, parallel actions, perceptions, and key adverbs

grammar-functions · #ba-structure · #passive · #adverbs

Learning objectives

  • Use 把 (disposal) and 被 (passive) in everyday sentences.
  • Express parallel and increasing actions: 一边……一边, 越……越, 又, and 才.
  • Express exceptions, limits, and perceptions: 除了……以外, 只, 只能, 只是, 以为, 好像, 看来, and noun suffix 子.

1 · Where Did the Cake Go?

Two roommates talk about a missing cake and a busy afternoon.

A: 我放在冰箱里的蛋糕怎么不见了？是不是被你吃了？ (Wǒ fàng zài bīngxiāng lǐ de dàngāo zěnme bújiàn le? Shì bu shì bèi nǐ chī le?)
How did the cake I put in the fridge disappear? Was it eaten by you?

B: 没有啊，我以为那是你买给客人的，刚才一直没动。 (Méiyǒu a, wǒ yǐwéi nà shì nǐ mǎi gěi kèrén de, gāngcái yìzhí méi dòng.)
No! I mistakenly thought you bought it for guests, so I didn’t touch it.

A: 哦，看来是我记错了，我好像把它放到下一层了！ (Ò, kànlái shì wǒ jì cuò le, wǒ hǎoxiàng bǎ tā fàng dào xià yì céng le!)
Oh, it seems I remembered wrong—looks like I put it on the lower shelf!

B: 哈哈，除了那个蛋糕以外，冰箱里还有西瓜，咱们一边吃一边聊吧！ (Hāhā, chúle nàge dàngāo yǐwài, bīngxiāng lǐ hái yǒu xīguā, zánmen yìbiān chī yìbiān liáo ba!)
Haha, besides that cake, there’s watermelon in the fridge too—let’s eat while chatting!

2 · How the language works

把 (disposal) vs. 被 (passive)

<Agent> + 把 + <Object> + <Verb + Result> / <Object> + 被 + (<Agent>) + <Verb + Result> (<Agent> + bǎ + <Object> + <Verb + Result> / <Object> + bèi + (<Agent>) + <Verb + Result>)

<Agent> + take + <Object> + <Verb + Result> / <Object> + by + (<Agent>) + <Verb + Result>

把 moves a specific object before the verb to show how it is handled; 被 marks the doer in a passive sentence.

弟弟把杯子打碎了。 (Dìdi bǎ bēizi dǎsuì le.)

My younger brother broke the cup.

桌上的苹果被小猫碰掉在地上了。 (Zhuō shàng de píngguǒ bèi xiǎo māo pèng diào zài dì shàng le.)

The apple on the table was knocked onto the floor by the kitten.

"The more… the more…" with 越……越……

<Subject> + 越 + <Verb / Adj A> + 越 + <Verb / Adj B> (<Subject> + yuè + <Verb / Adj A> + yuè + <Verb / Adj B>)

<Subject> + more + <A> + more + <B>

Use 越 + verb/adj + 越 + verb/adj to show two qualities or actions increasing together.

雨越下越大，我们只能先回家了。 (Yǔ yuè xià yuè dà, wǒmen zhǐ néng xiān huí jiā le.)

The rain is falling heavier and heavier, so we can only go home first.

汉语越学越有意思。 (Hànyǔ yuè xué yuè yǒu yìsi.)

Chinese gets more and more interesting the more you study it.

3 · Build new sentences

Practice 除了……以外, 以为, 才, and 又.

Model

除了星期天以外，我每天都要上班。 (Chúle xīngqītiān yǐwài, wǒ měitiān dōu yào shàngbān.)

Except for Sunday, I have to go to work every day.

1. Say you mistakenly thought today was Saturday.

Show one natural answer

我还以为今天是星期六呢！ (Wǒ hái yǐwéi jīntiān shì xīngqīliù ne!)

I mistakenly thought today was Saturday!

2. Say it’s already nine o’clock, why did he only arrive now?

Show one natural answer

都九点了，他怎么现在才来？ (Dōu jiǔ diǎn le, tā zěnme xiànzài cái lái?)

It’s already nine—why did he only arrive now?

3. Say yesterday rained and today it is raining again.

Show one natural answer

昨天下雨了，今天又下雨了。 (Zuótiān xià yǔ le, jīntiān yòu xià yǔ le.)

It rained yesterday, and today it’s raining again.

4 · Read in context: A Rainy Evening at Home

下午五点多，天色看起来好像要下大雨。小李原本以为能赶在雨前到家，可是走到半路雨就越下越大。 (Xiàwǔ wǔ diǎn duō, tiānsè kàn qǐlái hǎoxiàng yào xià dà yǔ. Xiǎo Lǐ yuánběn yǐwéi néng gǎn zài yǔ qián dào jiā, kěshì zǒu dào bànlù yǔ jiù yuè xià yuè dà.)
Past 5 p.m., the sky looked as if a heavy rain was coming. Xiao Li originally thought he could make it home before the rain, but halfway there the rain got heavier and heavier.

因为他只带了一把小伞，裤子还是被雨水打湿了。回到屋子以后，他赶紧换了干衣服，一边喝热茶一边看书。 (Yīnwèi tā zhǐ dài le yì bǎ xiǎo sǎn, kùzi háishi bèi yǔshuǐ dǎshī le. Huí dào wūzi yǐhòu, tā gǎnjǐn huàn le gān yīfu, yìbiān hē rè chá yìbiān kànshū.)
Because he only brought a small umbrella, his pants still got soaked by the rain. After returning to his room, he quickly changed into dry clothes and read while drinking hot tea.

Q1. Why did Xiao Li’s pants still get wet?
Show answer
Because he only brought a small umbrella and the rain got heavier and heavier.
Q2. What did he do after changing into dry clothes?
Show answer
He read while drinking hot tea.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Describe two things you do at the same time using 一边……一边.
Show one possible response

周末我喜欢一边听音乐一边打扫屋子。 (Zhōumò wǒ xǐhuan yìbiān tīng yīnyuè yìbiān dǎsǎo wūzi.)

On weekends I like listening to music while cleaning my room.

2. Use 把 or 被 to describe something that happened today.
Show one possible response

我把借来的书还给图书馆了。 (Wǒ bǎ jiè lái de shū huán gěi túshūguǎn le.)

I returned the borrowed book to the library.

6 · Watch out

以为 (yǐwéi) almost always implies a mistaken belief ("I thought X, but actually Y"). For a neutral opinion, use 觉得 or 认为.

Pronunciation clinic

The noun suffix 子 (zi) is always neutral tone (桌子, 杯子, 盘子); 又 (yòu) is a sharp fourth tone for past/completed repetition.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

What did Xiao Li mistakenly think before walking home?

Show answer
He thought he could make it home before the rain started.

The passage says 小李原本以为能赶在雨前到家.

Production

Write two sentences: one using 把 or 被, and one using 越……越 or 一边……一边.

Show answer
我把作业写完以后，一边听音乐一边休息，觉得心情越来越好了。

Use 把/被 and 越……越 or 一边……一边.

Review and next step

  • Use 把 and 被 in everyday sentences.
  • Use 越……越, 一边……一边, 又, and 才.
  • Express exceptions and mistaken beliefs with 除了……以外 and 以为.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which verb means "to mistakenly think / suppose"?

a. 以为 (yǐwéi) — mistakenly think

b. 看来 (kànlái) — it seems

c. 好像 (hǎoxiàng) — seem

Show answer
a

以为 implies a belief that turned out to be wrong.

Selection

Which marker introduces the doer in a passive sentence?

a. 把 (bǎ) — disposal marker

b. 被 (bèi) — passive marker

c. 才 (cái) — only then

Show answer
b

被 marks the passive agent.

Selection

Which phrase means "can only / have no choice but"?

a. 只是 (zhǐshì) — merely

b. 只能 (zhǐ néng) — can only

c. 除了 (chúle) — besides

Show answer
b

只能 means can only / have no choice but.
