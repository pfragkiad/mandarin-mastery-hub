Seasons, Holidays & Days

Days before and after, seasons, holidays, and weekends

time-calendar · #calendar · #seasons · #holidays

Learning objectives

  • Name relative days and years: 前天, 后天, 前年, 后年, 工作日, 周末, and 新年.
  • Talk about seasons and breaks using 季, 季节, 假期, and 放假.
  • Express time lengths with 半天, 久, and 刻.

1 · Holiday Plans

Two friends discuss when school goes on break and how they will spend the holiday.

A: 后天就放假了，这个假期你有什么打算？ (Hòutiān jiù fàngjià le, zhège jiàqī nǐ yǒu shénme dǎsuàn?)
Break starts the day after tomorrow—what plans do you have for this holiday?

B: 我打算回老家过新年，前年我就在家过的。 (Wǒ dǎsuàn huí lǎojiā guò xīnnián, qiánnián wǒ jiù zài jiā guò de.)
I plan to go back to my hometown for New Year; the year before last I spent it at home.

A: 真好！不过老家的冬天是不是冷得久一点儿？ (Zhēn hǎo! Búguò lǎojiā de dōngtiān shì bu shì lěng de jiǔ yìdiǎnr?)
Nice! Though isn’t winter in your hometown cold for a bit longer?

B: 是啊，我最喜欢的季节还是春天。对了，周末三点一刻咱们先见个面吧？ (Shì a, wǒ zuì xǐhuan de jìjié háishi chūntiān. Duìle, zhōumò sān diǎn yí kè zánmen xiān jiàn ge miàn ba?)
Yes, my favorite season is still spring. By the way, shall we meet at 3:15 this weekend first?

2 · How the language works

Telling quarter-hours with 刻

<Hour> + 点 + 一刻 / 三刻 (<Hour> + diǎn + yí kè / sān kè)

<Hour> + o’clock + one quarter / three quarters

Use 一刻 (yí kè) for 15 minutes past the hour and 三刻 (sān kè) for 45 minutes past.

火车八点一刻出发。 (Huǒchē bā diǎn yí kè chūfā.)

The train departs at 8:15.

会议九点三刻才结束。 (Huìyì jiǔ diǎn sān kè cái jiéshù.)

The meeting didn’t end until 9:45.

Expressing a long while with 半天 / 久

<Verb> + 了 + 半天 (<Verb> + le + bàntiān)

<Verb> + (completed) + half a day / a long while

半天 (bàntiān) literally means half a day, and figuratively means "a long time" after a verb.

我在门口等了半天，他还没来。 (Wǒ zài ménkǒu děng le bàntiān, tā hái méi lái.)

I waited at the door for ages, and he still hasn’t come.

这本书我找了半天也没找到。 (Zhè běn shū wǒ zhǎo le bàntiān yě méi zhǎodào.)

I looked for this book for a long time and still didn’t find it.

3 · Build new sentences

Practice talking about relative days, seasons, and holidays.

Model

工作日我很忙，周末才有时间休息。 (Gōngzuòrì wǒ hěn máng, zhōumò cái yǒu shíjiān xiūxi.)

On weekdays I am busy; only on weekends do I have time to rest.

1. Say you met your friend the day before yesterday.

Show one natural answer

前天我和朋友见面了。 (Qiántiān wǒ hé péngyou jiànmiàn le.)

The day before yesterday I met with my friend.

2. Say you plan to travel abroad the year after next.

Show one natural answer

后年我打算去国外旅游。 (Hòunián wǒ dǎsuàn qù guówài lǚyóu.)

The year after next I plan to travel abroad.

3. Say there are four seasons in a year.

Show one natural answer

一年有四个季节，各有各的美。 (Yì nián yǒu sì ge jìjié, gè yǒu gè de měi.)

A year has four seasons, each with its own beauty.

4 · Read in context: Comparing Past and Present Holidays

过去，人们在工作日很少有时间出去玩儿，只有到新年或者长假期才能休息几天。 (Guòqù, rénmen zài gōngzuòrì hěn shǎo yǒu shíjiān chūqù wánr, zhǐyǒu dào xīnnián huòzhě cháng jiàqī cái néng xiūxi jǐ tiān.)
In the past, people rarely had time to go out on weekdays, and could only rest for a few days at New Year or during long holidays.

现在不一样了，每个周末大家都能放松一下，还可以根据不同的季节去附近旅游。 (Xiànzài bù yíyàng le, měi ge zhōumò dàjiā dōu néng fàngsōng yíxià, hái kěyǐ gēnjù bù tóng de jìjié qù fùjìn lǚyóu.)
Now it is different: every weekend everyone can relax a bit and travel nearby depending on the different seasons.

Q1. When could people rest in the past according to the passage?
Show answer
Only at New Year or during long holidays.
Q2. What can people do every weekend now?
Show answer
Relax and travel nearby depending on the season.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Say what you did 前天 and what you will do 后天.
Show one possible response

前天我去图书馆学习了，后天我要和家人吃饭。 (Qiántiān wǒ qù túshūguǎn xuéxí le, hòutiān wǒ yào hé jiārén chīfàn.)

The day before yesterday I studied at the library, and the day after tomorrow I’m having dinner with my family.

2. Describe your favorite season and what you do during holidays.
Show one possible response

我最喜欢的季节是秋天，放假的时候我常去爬山。 (Wǒ zuì xǐhuan de jìjié shì qiūtiān, fàngjià de shíhou wǒ cháng qù páshān.)

My favorite season is autumn; during holidays I often go hiking.

6 · Watch out

放假 is a verb ("to have a holiday / go on break"), whereas 假期 is a noun ("holiday / vacation"). Say 放假了, not *假期了.

Pronunciation clinic

季节 (jìjié) moves from fourth tone to second tone; 假期 (jiàqī) moves from fourth tone to first tone.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

How have weekend habits changed compared with the past in the reading?

Show answer
Now people can relax every weekend and travel nearby depending on the season.

The second paragraph contrasts modern weekends with the past.

Production

Write two sentences using 放假 and 后天 or 周末.

Show answer
后天我们学校就放假了，周末我打算回家。

Use 后天/周末 with 放假.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which word means "the day after tomorrow"?

a. 前天 (qiántiān) — day before yesterday

b. 后天 (hòutiān) — day after tomorrow

c. 后年 (hòunián) — year after next

Show answer
b

后天 means the day after tomorrow.

Selection

Which word means "quarter of an hour (15 minutes)"?

a. 刻 (kè) — quarter hour

b. 季 (jì) — season

c. 久 (jiǔ) — long time

Show answer
a

刻 means a 15-minute quarter hour.

Selection

Which word means "weekday / working day"?

a. 周末 (zhōumò) — weekend

b. 工作日 (gōngzuòrì) — weekday

c. 假期 (jiàqī) — holiday

Show answer
b

工作日 literally means working day.
