# Necessity, Advice & Certainty

Give advice, state obligations, express certainty or reality, and explain who or what an action is for.

## Learning objectives

- Express obligation and advice: 必须, 得 (děi), 该, 应该, 需要, 不用, and 最好.

- Express certainty, frequency, and reality: 一定, 当然, 其实, 一般, 几乎, 总, and 可.

- Express purpose and beneficiaries with 为 and 为了.

## 1. Preparing for a Trip Together

Two friends discuss what they must prepare and what they don’t need to bring.

**A:** 明天八点出发，咱们今天必须把行李收拾好，还得早点儿睡。 (Míngtiān bā diǎn chūfā, zánmen jīntiān bìxū bǎ xíngli shōushi hǎo, hái děi zǎodiǎnr shuì.) — We leave at eight tomorrow, so today we must pack our luggage and also have to sleep early.

**B:** 当然！不过我觉得不用带太多衣服，最好只带轻便的。 (Dāngrán! Búguò wǒ juéde búyòng dài tài duō yīfu, zuìhǎo zhǐ dài qīngbiàn de.) — Of course! Though I think we don’t need to bring too many clothes—it’s best to only bring light ones.

**A:** 其实那边的宾馆一般都有洗衣机，我们需要带的也就是牙刷和护照。 (Qíshí nàbiān de bīnguǎn yìbān dōu yǒu xǐyījī, wǒmen xūyào dài de yě jiùshì yáshuā hé hùzhào.) — Actually hotels there generally have washing machines, so what we need to bring is really just toothbrushes and passports.

**B:** 为了明天不迟到，咱们应该定两个闹钟，这样一定没问题！ (Wèile míngtiān bù chídào, zánmen yīnggāi dìng liǎng ge nàozhōng, zhèyàng yídìng méi wèntí!) — In order not to be late tomorrow, we should set two alarms—that way there will definitely be no problem!

## 2. How the language works

### Stating purpose with 为了

**Pattern:** 为了 + <Goal> + ， + <Subject> + <Action> (wèile + <Goal> + , + <Subject> + <Action>)

*in order to + <Goal> + , + <Subject> + <Action>*

Place 为了 (wèile) + goal/purpose at the start of a sentence before the action.

**为了提高汉语水平，她每天坚持读课文。**
*Wèile tígāo Hànyǔ shuǐpíng, tā měitiān jiānchí dú kèwén.*
In order to improve her Chinese level, she reads the lesson text every day.

**为了身体健康，我们应该多锻炼。**
*Wèile shēntǐ jiànkāng, wǒmen yīnggāi duō duànliàn.*
For physical health, we should exercise more.

### Giving strong advice with 最好

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 最好 + <Verb Phrase> (<Subject> + zuìhǎo + <Verb Phrase>)

*<Subject> + had better + <Verb Phrase>*

Place 最好 (zuìhǎo, "had better") before the verb phrase to give helpful advice.

**外面风很大，你最好穿件大衣。**
*Wàimiàn fēng hěn dà, nǐ zuìhǎo chuān jiàn dàyī.*
It’s very windy outside—you’d better put on a coat.

**发烧的时候最好去医院检查一下。**
*Fāshāo de shíhou zuìhǎo qù yīyuàn jiǎnchá yíxià.*
When you have a fever, you’d better go to the hospital for a checkup.

## 3. Build new sentences

Express obligation, lack of need, and general habits.

*Model:* **这件事你不用担心，我一定帮你办好。**
*Zhè jiàn shì nǐ búyòng dānxīn, wǒ yídìng bāng nǐ bàn hǎo.*
You don’t need to worry about this—I’ll definitely get it done for you.

**Cue 1:** Say it’s already 11 p.m. and we ought to sleep.

*Model:* **已经十一点了，咱们该睡觉了。**
*Yǐjīng shíyī diǎn le, zánmen gāi shuìjiào le.*
It’s already eleven o’clock; we ought to go to sleep.

**Cue 2:** Say he almost never drinks coffee at night.

*Model:* **他晚上几乎从不喝咖啡。**
*Tā wǎnshang jīhū cóngbù hē kāfēi.*
He almost never drinks coffee at night.

**Cue 3:** Say this gift was bought for Mom.

*Model:* **这件礼物是为妈妈买的。**
*Zhè jiàn lǐwù shì wèi māma mǎi de.*
This gift was bought for Mom.

## 4. Read in context: Good Habits for Busy Days

工作再忙，我们也必须照顾好自己的身体。其实，保持健康一般不需要太复杂的办法。 (Gōngzuò zài máng, wǒmen yě bìxū zhàogù hǎo zìjǐ de shēntǐ. Qíshí, bǎochí jiànkāng yìbān bù xūyào tài fùzá de bànfǎ.)

No matter how busy work is, we must take good care of our own health. Actually, staying healthy generally doesn’t require overly complicated methods.

每天最好早点儿休息，周末有空时多出去走走。为了让自己更有精神，平时也该多喝水，少吃太甜的东西。 (Měitiān zuìhǎo zǎodiǎnr xiūxi, zhōumò yǒukòng shí duō chūqù zǒuzou. Wèile ràng zìjǐ gèng yǒu jīngshen, píngshí yě gāi duō hē shuǐ, shǎo chī tài tián de dōngxi.)

Every day it’s best to rest a bit early, and on weekends go out for walks when free. In order to have more energy, you should normally drink more water and eat fewer overly sweet things.

**Q1. What does staying healthy generally NOT require according to the passage?** Suggested answer: Overly complicated methods.

**Q2. What should you do to have more energy?** Suggested answer: Drink more water and eat fewer overly sweet things.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Give advice to a friend who is tired using 最好, 应该, or 不用.

*Possible response:* **你今天太累了，晚上不用继续工作了，最好早点儿睡觉。**
*Nǐ jīntiān tài lèi le, wǎnshang búyòng jìxù gōngzuò le, zuìhǎo zǎodiǎnr shuìjiào.*
You’re too tired today—you don’t need to keep working tonight; you’d better go to sleep early.

**Speaking 2:** Explain a goal of yours using 为了.

*Possible response:* **为了学好中文，我每天坚持听录音和写汉字。**
*Wèile xuéhǎo Zhōngwén, wǒ měitiān jiānchí tīng lùyīn hé xiě Hànzì.*
In order to learn Chinese well, I stick to listening to recordings and writing characters every day.

## 6. Watch out

The negative of 必须 / 得 ("must") is 不用 ("need not / don’t have to"), NOT *不得 (*bù děi) or *不必须.

**Pronunciation:** 一定 (yídìng) changes 一 to rising yí before fourth-tone dìng; 得 meaning "have to" is read děi (third tone).

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** What two weekend or daily habits does the passage recommend for staying healthy?
**Answer:** Resting early each day and going out for walks on weekends when free.. The second paragraph says 每天最好早点儿休息，周末有空时多出去走走.

**Production:** Write two sentences using 为了 and 最好 or 必须.
**Answer:** 为了准时到机场，我们明天早上必须六点起床，最好提前叫车。. Use 为了 for purpose and 必须/最好 for necessity/advice.

## Review and next step

- Express obligation and advice with 必须, 得, 应该, and 最好.

- Use 不用 for "no need to".

- State purpose and beneficiaries with 为了 and 为.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which expression means "no need to / don’t have to"?
- a. 必须 (bìxū) — must
- b. 不用 (búyòng) — need not
- c. 一定 (yídìng) — certainly
**Answer:** b. 不用 means no need to.

**Selection:** Which word introduces a goal ("in order to")?
- a. 为了 (wèile) — in order to
- b. 其实 (qíshí) — actually
- c. 几乎 (jīhū) — almost
**Answer:** a. 为了 means in order to.

**Selection:** Which adverb means "actually / in fact"?
- a. 当然 (dāngrán) — of course
- b. 其实 (qíshí) — actually
- c. 一般 (yìbān) — generally
**Answer:** b. 其实 means actually or in fact.
