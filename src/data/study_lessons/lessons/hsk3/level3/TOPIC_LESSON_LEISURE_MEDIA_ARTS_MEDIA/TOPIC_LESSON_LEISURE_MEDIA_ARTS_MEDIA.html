Music, Festivals, News & Photos

Music, festivals, news, cameras, and photos

leisure-media · #media · #festivals · #photography

Learning objectives

  • Talk about performances and celebrations: 表演, 节目, 晚会, 音乐, 节, 节日, and 过节.
  • Discuss news, stories, and media: 报纸, 新闻, and 故事.
  • Talk about photography with 相机, 照相, 拍照, and 照片.

1 · At the Mid-Autumn Evening Party

Two friends talk about a campus festival show and taking photos.

A: 今晚学校有个中秋晚会，听说有很多精彩的节目！ (Jīnwǎn xuéxiào yǒu ge Zhōngqiū wǎnhuì, tīngshuō yǒu hěn duō jīngcǎi de jiémù!)
Tonight the school has a Mid-Autumn evening party—I heard there are lots of wonderful acts!

B: 对，我们班还要表演一段音乐节目呢。你带相机了吗？ (Duì, wǒmen bān hái yào biǎoyǎn yí duàn yīnyuè jiémù ne. Nǐ dài xiàngjī le ma?)
Yes, our class is even performing a music act. Did you bring your camera?

A: 带了！过节的时候大家一起拍照留念，多有意思。 (Dài le! Guòjié de shíhou dàjiā yìqǐ pāizhào liúniàn, duō yǒu yìsi.)
I did! Taking photos together during the festival as a keepsake is so meaningful.

B: 明天校报的新闻里说不定还会登我们的照片呢！ (Míngtiān xiàobào de xīnwén lǐ shuōbudìng hái huì dēng wǒmen de zhàopiàn ne!)
Maybe tomorrow’s school newspaper news will even print our photos!

2 · How the language works

Taking photos with 拍照 / 照相

给 + <Person> + 拍照 / 照相 (gěi + <Person> + pāizhào / zhàoxiàng)

for + <Person> + take photo

Both 拍照 (pāizhào) and 照相 (zhàoxiàng) mean "take a photo"; place 给 + person before them to say who you photograph.

能帮我们大家拍张照吗？ (Néng bāng wǒmen dàjiā pāi zhāng zhào ma?)

Could you help take a photo for all of us?

游客们喜欢在公园里照相。 (Yóukèmen xǐhuan zài gōngyuán lǐ zhàoxiàng.)

Tourists like taking photos in the park.

Celebrating a festival with 过节

<People> + 在一起 + 过节 (<People> + zài yìqǐ + guòjié)

<People> + together + celebrate festival

过节 (guòjié) means to celebrate or spend a holiday/festival.

每年春节，一家人都聚在一起过节。 (Měi nián Chūnjié, yì jiā rén dōu jù zài yìqǐ guòjié.)

Every Spring Festival, the whole family gathers together to celebrate.

你们国家有哪些重要的节日？ (Nǐmen guójiā yǒu nǎxiē zhòngyào de jiérì?)

What important festivals does your country have?

3 · Build new sentences

Talk about news, stories, music, and performances.

Model

爷爷每天早上都一边喝茶一边看报纸。 (Yéye měitiān zǎoshang dōu yìbiān hē chá yìbiān kàn bàozhǐ.)

Grandpa reads the newspaper while drinking tea every morning.

1. Say you like listening to quiet music.

Show one natural answer

我喜欢听安静的音乐。 (Wǒ xǐhuan tīng ānjìng de yīnyuè.)

I like listening to quiet music.

2. Say you read an interesting piece of news today.

Show one natural answer

今天我在网上看到一条有趣的新闻。 (Jīntiān wǒ zài wǎngshàng kàndào yì tiáo yǒuqù de xīnwén.)

Today I saw an interesting piece of news online.

3. Say the children performed a short story on stage.

Show one natural answer

孩子们在台上表演了一个小故事。 (Háizimen zài tái shàng biǎoyǎn le yí ge xiǎo gùshi.)

The children performed a short story on stage.

4 · Read in context: Remembering a Festival Evening

去年中秋节，社区举办了一场热闹的晚会。台上既有唱歌、跳舞等音乐节目，也有小朋友表演的有趣故事。 (Qùnián Zhōngqiūjié, shèqū jǔbàn le yì chǎng rènào de wǎnhuì. Táishàng jì yǒu chànggē, tiàowǔ děng yīnyuè jiémù, yě yǒu xiǎo péngyou biǎoyǎn de yǒuqù gùshi.)
Last Mid-Autumn Festival, the community held a lively evening party. On stage there were both music acts like singing and dancing, and fun stories performed by children.

张叔叔用他的新相机给大家拍了不少照片。第二天，当地报纸还发了一条关于这次过节活动的新闻。 (Zhāng shūshu yòng tā de xīn xiàngjī gěi dàjiā pāi le bùshǎo zhàopiàn. Dì-èr tiān, dāngdì bàozhǐ hái fā le yì tiáo guānyú zhè cì guòjié huódòng de xīnwén.)
Uncle Zhang used his new camera to take quite a few photos for everyone. The next day, the local newspaper even published a news piece about this festival event.

Q1. What kinds of performances were on stage at the evening party?
Show answer
Music acts like singing and dancing, and stories performed by children.
Q2. What appeared in the local newspaper the next day?
Show answer
A news piece about the festival event.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Talk about your favorite festival and how you celebrate it using 节日 and 过节.
Show one possible response

我最喜欢的节日是春节，过节时家人会一起吃饭、看晚会。 (Wǒ zuì xǐhuan de jiérì shì Chūnjié, guòjié shí jiārén huì yìqǐ chīfàn, kàn wǎnhuì.)

My favorite festival is Spring Festival; during the holiday my family eats together and watches the evening gala.

2. Say whether you read newspapers/news or like taking photos.
Show one possible response

我每天看新闻，出去玩儿时也喜欢用相机拍照。 (Wǒ měitiān kàn xīnwén, chūqù wánr shí yě xǐhuan yòng xiàngjī pāizhào.)

I read the news every day, and when I go out I also like taking photos with a camera.

6 · Watch out

报纸 is the physical newspaper ("a sheet of newspaper": 一份报纸), while 新闻 is the news content ("a news story": 一条新闻).

Pronunciation clinic

故事 (gùshi) and 报纸 (bàozhǐ) contrast neutral-tone shi with third-tone zhǐ; 拍照 (pāizhào) moves from flat pāi to falling zhào.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

What did Uncle Zhang use his new camera for at the Mid-Autumn party?

Show answer
He took many photos for everyone.

The second paragraph says 用他的新相机给大家拍了不少照片.

Production

Write two sentences using 节日 or 晚会 and 拍照 or 照片.

Show answer
过节的时候我们学校办了一场晚会，我用相机拍了很多照片。

Combine festival/show and photography terms.
