Banks & Payment Cards

Using banks, bank cards, and credit cards

shopping-money · #banking · #cards · #payments

Learning objectives

  • Refer to a bank with 银行.
  • Distinguish 银行卡 (bank card) and 信用卡 (credit card).
  • Ask whether card payment is accepted in everyday situations.

1 · At the Bank and Shop

Xiao Ming needs to go to the bank before buying a train ticket.

A: 请问，附近有银行吗？我要去办一张银行卡。 (Qǐngwèn, fùjìn yǒu yínháng ma? Wǒ yào qù bàn yì zhāng yínhángkǎ.)
Excuse me, is there a bank nearby? I need to get a bank card.

B: 往前走两百米就有一家银行，很方便。 (Wǎng qián zǒu liǎng bǎi mǐ jiù yǒu yì jiā yínháng, hěn fāngbiàn.)
Walk 200 meters ahead and there is a bank; it’s very convenient.

A: 谢谢！对了，买票的时候可以用信用卡吗？ (Xièxie! Duìle, mǎi piào de shíhou kěyǐ yòng xìnyòngkǎ ma?)
Thanks! By the way, can I use a credit card when buying tickets?

B: 可以，银行卡和信用卡都能用。 (Kěyǐ, yínhángkǎ hé xìnyòngkǎ dōu néng yòng.)
Yes, both bank cards and credit cards can be used.

2 · How the language works

Using a card to pay

用 + 银行卡 / 信用卡 + <Action> (yòng + yínhángkǎ / xìnyòngkǎ + <Action>)

using + bank card / credit card + <Action>

Use 用 (yòng) + card + verb (such as 付钱 or 买) to say how you pay.

我习惯用银行卡买东西。 (Wǒ xíguàn yòng yínhángkǎ mǎi dōngxi.)

I am used to using a bank card to buy things.

在酒店可以用信用卡付钱吗？ (Zài jiǔdiàn kěyǐ yòng xìnyòngkǎ fù qián ma?)

Can I pay with a credit card at the hotel?

Applying for or getting a card at the bank

去银行办 + 一张 + 银行卡 / 信用卡 (qù yínháng bàn + yì zhāng + yínhángkǎ / xìnyòngkǎ)

go to bank handle + one sheet + bank card / credit card

Use 办 (bàn) with 一张银行卡 / 信用卡 to mean applying for or setting up a card.

明天上午我要去银行办一张新卡。 (Míngtiān shàngwǔ wǒ yào qù yínháng bàn yì zhāng xīn kǎ.)

Tomorrow morning I need to go to the bank to get a new card.

这家银行办信用卡很快。 (Zhè jiā yínháng bàn xìnyòngkǎ hěn kuài.)

Applying for a credit card at this bank is very fast.

3 · Build new sentences

Ask about banks and card payments.

Model

我忘了带信用卡，可以用手机付钱吗？ (Wǒ wàng le dài xìnyòngkǎ, kěyǐ yòng shǒujī fù qián ma?)

I forgot to bring my credit card; can I pay with my phone?

1. Ask where the nearest bank is.

Show one natural answer

请问，最近的银行在哪里？ (Qǐngwèn, zuìjìn de yínháng zài nǎlǐ?)

Excuse me, where is the nearest bank?

2. Say you have two bank cards in your bag.

Show one natural answer

我包里有两张银行卡。 (Wǒ bāo lǐ yǒu liǎng zhāng yínhángkǎ.)

I have two bank cards in my bag.

3. Say you don’t use a credit card very often.

Show one natural answer

我平时很少用信用卡。 (Wǒ píngshí hěn shǎo yòng xìnyòngkǎ.)

I rarely use a credit card.

4 · Read in context: Opening a Bank Account

小陈刚来中国留学，他需要办一张本地的银行卡。今天早上，他带着护照去了学校附近的一家银行。 (Xiǎo Chén gāng lái Zhōngguó liúxué, tā xūyào bàn yì zhāng běndì de yínhángkǎ. Jīntiān zǎoshang, tā dài zhe hùzhào qù le xuéxiào fùjìn de yì jiā yínháng.)
Xiao Chen just came to China to study abroad and needs to get a local bank card. This morning, he brought his passport and went to a bank near his school.

银行的工作人员很热情，半个小时就帮他办好了。现在他买东西更方便了。 (Yínháng de gōngzuò rényuán hěn rèqíng, bàn ge xiǎoshí jiù bāng tā bàn hǎo le. Xiànzài tā mǎi dōngxi gèng fāngbiàn le.)
The bank staff were very helpful and got it done for him in half an hour. Now shopping is much more convenient for him.

Q1. What did Xiao Chen bring with him to the bank?
Show answer
His passport.
Q2. How long did it take to get his bank card?
Show answer
Half an hour.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Ask a clerk if you can pay with a bank card or credit card.
Show one possible response

你好，这里可以用信用卡或者银行卡吗？ (Nǐhǎo, zhèlǐ kěyǐ yòng xìnyòngkǎ huòzhě yínhángkǎ ma?)

Hello, can I use a credit card or bank card here?

2. Say why you are going to the bank today.
Show one possible response

我今天要去银行办一张新银行卡。 (Wǒ jīntiān yào qù yínháng bàn yì zhāng xīn yínhángkǎ.)

I need to go to the bank today to get a new bank card.

6 · Watch out

Use the classifier 张 (zhāng) for flat cards like 一张银行卡 and 家 (jiā) for banks like 一家银行.

Pronunciation clinic

银行 (yínháng) is second tone + second tone; 信用卡 (xìnyòngkǎ) is fourth + fourth + third tone.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which word means "bank"?

a. 银行 (yínháng) — bank

b. 银行卡 (yínhángkǎ) — bank card

c. 信用卡 (xìnyòngkǎ) — credit card

Show answer
a

银行 is the bank itself.

Selection

Which word means "credit card"?

a. 银行卡 (yínhángkǎ) — bank card

b. 信用卡 (xìnyòngkǎ) — credit card

c. 银行 (yínháng) — bank

Show answer
b

信用 means credit, so 信用卡 is a credit card.

Selection

Which classifier is used with 银行卡?

a. 张 (zhāng) — classifier for flat objects/cards

b. 本 (běn) — classifier for books

c. 辆 (liàng) — classifier for vehicles

Show answer
a

Cards take the classifier 张.
