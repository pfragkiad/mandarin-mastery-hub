# Letters, Emails & Paying Attention

Learn to send and receive messages, talk about sounds and hearsay, and give reminders.

## Learning objectives

- Send and receive messages using 发, 收到, 信, 邮件, and 邮箱.

- Report hearsay and trust with 听说 and 相信.

- Give reminders and confirm plans using 小心, 注意, and 行.

## 1. Checking Email

Two colleagues check whether an important email has arrived.

**A:** 我刚才给你发了一封邮件，你收到了吗？ (Wǒ gāngcái gěi nǐ fā le yì fēng yóujiàn, nǐ shōudào le ma?) — I just sent you an email—did you receive it?

**B:** 等一下，我看看邮箱……收到了！不过声音怎么这么小？ (Děng yíxià, wǒ kànkan yóuxiāng… shōudào le! Búguò shēngyīn zěnme zhème xiǎo?) — Wait a moment, let me check my inbox… Got it! Though why is the audio so quiet?

**A:** 听说这个文件有点儿问题，你注意看后面的说明。 (Tīngshuō zhège wénjiàn yǒudiǎnr wèntí, nǐ zhùyì kàn hòumiàn de shuōmíng.) — I heard there’s a slight issue with this file—pay attention to the instructions at the end.

**B:** 行，我相信很快就能弄好，你放心吧。 (Xíng, wǒ xiāngxìn hěn kuài jiù néng nòng hǎo, nǐ fàngxīn ba.) — Okay, I’m sure we can sort it out soon—don’t worry.

## 2. How the language works

### Sending and receiving messages

**Pattern:** 给 + <Recipient> + 发 + 邮件 / 信 (gěi + <Recipient> + fā + yóujiàn / xìn)

*to + <Recipient> + send + email / letter*

Use 给 + recipient + 发 + message to send, and 收到 + message to confirm receipt.

**请把你的邮箱地址发给我。**
*Qǐng bǎ nǐ de yóuxiāng dìzhǐ fā gěi wǒ.*
Please send your email address to me.

**昨天我收到了哥哥寄来的一封信。**
*Zuótiān wǒ shōudào le gēge jì lái de yì fēng xìn.*
Yesterday I received a letter sent by my older brother.

### Reporting what you heard with 听说

**Pattern:** 听说 + <Clause> (tīngshuō + <Clause>)

*heard that + <Clause>*

Place 听说 (tīngshuō) at the start of a clause to introduce something you heard from others.

**听说下周学校要放假两天。**
*Tīngshuō xià zhōu xuéxiào yào fàngjià liǎng tiān.*
I heard the school will have two days off next week.

**我听说那家店的蛋糕特别好吃。**
*Wǒ tīngshuō nà jiā diàn de dàngāo tèbié hǎochī.*
I heard that shop’s cake is especially delicious.

## 3. Build new sentences

Practice emails, warnings, and agreeing.

*Model:* **我已经把作业发到老师的邮箱里了。**
*Wǒ yǐjīng bǎ zuòyè fā dào lǎoshī de yóuxiāng lǐ le.*
I have already sent the homework to the teacher’s email inbox.

**Cue 1:** Remind someone to be careful on the stairs.

*Model:* **走楼梯的时候请小心。**
*Zǒu lóutī de shíhou qǐng xiǎoxīn.*
Please be careful when walking on the stairs.

**Cue 2:** Say you believe what your friend said.

*Model:* **我相信我朋友说的话。**
*Wǒ xiāngxìn wǒ péngyou shuō de huà.*
I believe what my friend said.

**Cue 3:** Say the sound outside is too loud.

*Model:* **外面的声音太大了，我听不见。**
*Wàimiàn de shēngyīn tài dà le, wǒ tīng bu jiàn.*
The noise outside is too loud; I can’t hear.

## 4. Read in context: A Letter from Afar

现在大家习惯用手机发邮件，很少写信了。不过上个月，小兰却收到了一封老同学写来的信。 (Xiànzài dàjiā xíguàn yòng shǒujī fā yóujiàn, hěn shǎo xiě xìn le. Búguò shàng ge yuè, Xiǎo Lán què shōudào le yì fēng lǎo tóngxué xiě lái de xìn.)

Nowadays everyone is used to sending emails by phone and rarely writes letters. However, last month Xiao Lan received a letter from an old classmate.

信里说她听说小兰换了新工作，提醒她工作再忙也要注意身体。小兰看完非常感动。 (Xìn lǐ shuō tā tīngshuō Xiǎo Lán huàn le xīn gōngzuò, tíxǐng tā gōngzuò zài máng yě yào zhùyì shēntǐ. Xiǎo Lán kàn wán fēicháng gǎndòng.)

The letter said she heard Xiao Lan changed to a new job, reminding her to take care of her health no matter how busy work gets. Xiao Lan was deeply moved after reading it.

**Q1. What did Xiao Lan receive last month?** Suggested answer: A letter written by an old classmate.

**Q2. What did the classmate remind Xiao Lan to pay attention to?** Suggested answer: Her health (注意身体), even when busy with her new job.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Confirm that you received an email and say it is okay.

*Possible response:* **你的邮件我收到了，时间安排很行，没问题。**
*Nǐ de yóujiàn wǒ shōudào le, shíjiān ānpái hěn xíng, méi wèntí.*
I received your email; the schedule works fine, no problem.

**Speaking 2:** Give a friend a reminder using 注意 or 小心.

*Possible response:* **刚下过雨，路很滑，走路要小心一点儿。**
*Gāng xià guò yǔ, lù hěn huá, zǒulù yào xiǎoxīn yìdiǎnr.*
It just rained and the road is slippery—be a little careful when walking.

## 6. Watch out

邮箱 is the email inbox or email address, while 邮件 is the email message itself. Say 查邮箱 ("check inbox") and 发邮件 ("send an email").

**Pronunciation:** 声音 (shēngyīn) has two high flat first tones; 注意 (zhùyì) has two sharp fourth tones.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** Why was Xiao Lan moved by the letter?
**Answer:** Her old classmate wrote to remind her to take care of her health at her new job.. The classmate heard she changed jobs and reminded her 要注意身体.

**Production:** Write two sentences: one saying you sent an email, and one using 听说.
**Answer:** 我上午给你发了一封邮件。听说你明天要出差，路上小心！. Use 发邮件 and 听说 naturally.

## Review and next step

- Send and receive letters or emails.

- Report hearsay with 听说.

- Give reminders with 小心 and 注意.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "email inbox / email address"?
- a. 邮件 (yóujiàn) — email message
- b. 邮箱 (yóuxiāng) — email inbox / address
- c. 信 (xìn) — letter
**Answer:** b. 邮箱 refers to the mailbox/inbox or email address.

**Selection:** Which verb means "to receive"?
- a. 收到 (shōudào) — receive
- b. 相信 (xiāngxìn) — believe
- c. 听说 (tīngshuō) — hear that
**Answer:** a. 收到 means to receive.

**Selection:** Which word means "to be careful"?
- a. 行 (xíng) — okay
- b. 小心 (xiǎoxīn) — be careful
- c. 声音 (shēngyīn) — sound / voice
**Answer:** b. 小心 means to be careful.
