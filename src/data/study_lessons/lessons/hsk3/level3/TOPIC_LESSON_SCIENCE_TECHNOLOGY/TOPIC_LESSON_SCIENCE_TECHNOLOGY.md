# Devices, Websites & E-Books

Talk about electricity, turning devices on and off, listening with headphones, reading e-books, and visiting websites.

## Learning objectives

- Talk about battery and electricity with 电.

- Turn devices on and off using 开机 and 关机.

- Discuss digital reading and browsing with 电子书, 耳机, and 网站.

## 1. Reading an E-Book on the Train

Linlin asks her friend about the headphones and e-book he is using.

**A:** 你戴着耳机在听什么呢？ (Nǐ dài zhe ěrjī zài tīng shénme ne?) — What are you listening to with your headphones?

**B:** 我在听一本电子书的录音，这个网站上的资源很多。 (Wǒ zài tīng yì běn diànzǐshū de lùyīn, zhège wǎngzhàn shàng de zīyuán hěn duō.) — I’m listening to the audio of an e-book; this website has lots of resources.

**A:** 真不错！我的手机刚才没电关机了，正准备充电呢。 (Zhēn búcuò! Wǒ de shǒujī gāngcái méi diàn guānjī le, zhèng zhǔnbèi chōngdiàn ne.) — Nice! My phone just ran out of battery and shut off, so I’m about to charge it.

**B:** 充一会儿再开机吧，我有充电宝。 (Chōng yíhuìr zài kāijī ba, wǒ yǒu chōngdiànbǎo.) — Charge it for a bit before turning it back on—I have a power bank.

## 2. How the language works

### Saying a device has no power

**Pattern:** <Device> + 没电了 (<Device> + méi diàn le)

*<Device> + has no electricity (now)*

Use 没电了 (méi diàn le) after a device name to say its battery is dead or there is no electricity.

**我的耳机没电了，听不见声音。**
*Wǒ de ěrjī méi diàn le, tīng bu jiàn shēngyīn.*
My headphones ran out of battery; I can’t hear any sound.

**电脑突然没电关机了。**
*Diànnǎo tūrán méi diàn guānjī le.*
The computer suddenly ran out of power and shut off.

### Turning a device on or off

**Pattern:** 把 + <Device> + 开机 / 关机 (bǎ + <Device> + kāijī / guānjī)

*take + <Device> + power on / power off*

开机 (kāijī) and 关机 (guānjī) mean powering a device on or off; they usually stand without a separate object.

**请把手机关机，电影马上开始了。**
*Qǐng bǎ shǒujī guānjī, diànyǐng mǎshàng kāishǐ le.*
Please turn off your phone; the movie is about to start.

**我早上到办公室以后才开机。**
*Wǒ zǎoshang dào bàngōngshì yǐhòu cái kāijī.*
I only turn on my computer after arriving at the office in the morning.

## 3. Build new sentences

Talk about digital reading, headphones, and websites.

*Model:* **我喜欢在这个网站上看电子书。**
*Wǒ xǐhuan zài zhège wǎngzhàn shàng kàn diànzǐshū.*
I like reading e-books on this website.

**Cue 1:** Say you wear headphones when studying in the library.

*Model:* **我在图书馆学习的时候会戴耳机。**
*Wǒ zài túshūguǎn xuéxí de shíhou huì dài ěrjī.*
I wear headphones when studying in the library.

**Cue 2:** Say the plane is about to take off, so please power off.

*Model:* **飞机要起飞了，请大家关机。**
*Fēijī yào qǐfēi le, qǐng dàjiā guānjī.*
The plane is about to take off; please power off your devices.

**Cue 3:** Say this website is very useful for learning Chinese.

*Model:* **这个网站对学汉语很有用。**
*Zhège wǎngzhàn duì xué Hànyǔ hěn yǒuyòng.*
This website is very useful for learning Chinese.

## 4. Read in context: Digital Study Habits

现在很多学生喜欢用电脑或手机看电子书，因为带起来很轻，找词也很快。 (Xiànzài hěn duō xuéshēng xǐhuan yòng diànnǎo huò shǒujī kàn diànzǐshū, yīnwèi dài qǐlái hěn qīng, zhǎo cí yě hěn kuài.)

Nowadays many students like using a computer or phone to read e-books, because they are light to carry and quick for looking up words.

不过晚上休息以前，最好早点儿把手机关机，这样睡得更好。 (Búguò wǎnshang xiūxi yǐqián, zuìhǎo zǎodiǎnr bǎ shǒujī guānjī, zhèyàng shuì de gèng hǎo.)

However, before resting at night, it is best to turn off your phone a little earlier so you sleep better.

**Q1. Why do many students like reading e-books?** Suggested answer: Because they are light to carry and quick for looking up words.

**Q2. What advice does the passage give before sleeping?** Suggested answer: Turn off your phone earlier at night.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Say whether you prefer paper books or 电子书, and why.

*Possible response:* **出门的时候我喜欢看电子书，因为很方便。**
*Chūmén de shíhou wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànzǐshū, yīnwèi hěn fāngbiàn.*
When going out I like reading e-books because it is very convenient.

**Speaking 2:** Explain when you use 耳机 or visit a useful 网站.

*Possible response:* **坐地铁的时候我常常戴耳机听音乐。**
*Zuò dìtiě de shíhou wǒ chángcháng dài ěrjī tīng yīnyuè.*
When taking the subway I often wear headphones to listen to music.

## 6. Watch out

Do not say *关机手机 (*guānjī shǒujī). Since 机 already means "device", say 把手机关机 or 关掉手机.

**Pronunciation:** 耳机 (ěrjī) starts with the retroflex third-tone ěr followed by high first-tone jī.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** According to the passage, why should you turn off your phone early at night?
**Answer:** So that you can sleep better.. The passage says 这样睡得更好.

**Production:** Write one sentence using 电子书 or 网站 and one using 关机 or 开机.
**Answer:** 我在这个网站下载了一本电子书，看完就关机休息。. Use 网站/电子书 for digital content and 关机/开机 for device power.

- Say a device is out of battery with 没电了.

- Use 开机 and 关机 naturally.

- Talk about e-books, headphones, and websites.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which verb means "to power off a device"?
- a. 开机 (kāijī) — turn on a device
- b. 关机 (guānjī) — turn off a device
- c. 电 (diàn) — electricity
**Answer:** b. 关机 means turning a device off.

**Selection:** Which word means "headphones"?
- a. 耳机 (ěrjī) — headphones
- b. 网站 (wǎngzhàn) — website
- c. 电子书 (diànzǐshū) — e-book
**Answer:** a. 耳机 means earphones or headphones.

**Selection:** Which word means "website"?
- a. 电子书 (diànzǐshū) — e-book
- b. 网站 (wǎngzhàn) — website
- c. 开机 (kāijī) — turn on
**Answer:** b. 网站 means website.
