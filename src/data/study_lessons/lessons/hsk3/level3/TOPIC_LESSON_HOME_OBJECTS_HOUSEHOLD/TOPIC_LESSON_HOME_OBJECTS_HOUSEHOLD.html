Moving House & Home Appliances

Moving house, rooms, appliances, and gardens

home-objects · #housing · #appliances · #moving

Learning objectives

  • Talk about homes and moving using 搬家, 房子, 屋子, and 花园.
  • Name major household appliances and furniture: 冰箱, 空调, 洗衣机, and 沙发.
  • Describe a home’s layout and comfort.

1 · Moving Into a New Apartment

Xiao Zhang shows his friend around his new house after moving.

A: 听说你上周搬家了，新房子怎么样？ (Tīngshuō nǐ shàng zhōu bānjiā le, xīn fángzi zěnmeyàng?)
I heard you moved last week—how is the new house?

B: 很不错！屋子很大，楼下还有一个小花园。 (Hěn búcuò! Wūzi hěn dà, lóuxià hái yǒu yí ge xiǎo huāyuán.)
Really nice! The rooms are big, and there’s a small garden downstairs.

A: 家具和电器都买齐了吗？ (Jiājù hé diànqì dōu mǎi qí le ma?)
Have you bought all the furniture and appliances yet?

B: 差不多了，沙发、冰箱、空调和洗衣机都已经放好了。 (Chàbuduō le, shāfā, bīngxiāng, kōngtiáo hé xǐyījī dōu yǐjīng fàng hǎo le.)
Almost—the sofa, fridge, air conditioner, and washing machine are all already in place.

2 · How the language works

Counting appliances with 台

<Number> + 台 + 冰箱 / 空调 / 洗衣机 (<Number> + tái + bīngxiāng / kōngtiáo / xǐyījī)

<Number> + unit + fridge / AC / washer

Use 台 (tái) as the classifier for electrical appliances and machines such as 冰箱, 空调, and 洗衣机.

新屋子里装了两台空调。 (Xīn wūzi lǐ zhuāng le liǎng tái kōngtiáo.)

Two air conditioners were installed in the new room.

我家的这台洗衣机用了五年了。 (Wǒ jiā de zhè tái xǐyījī yòng le wǔ nián le.)

This washing machine at our home has been used for five years.

Moving to a new place with 搬家

<Subject> + 搬家 + 到 + <Place> (<Subject> + bānjiā + dào + <Place>)

<Subject> + move home + to + <Place>

搬家 (bānjiā) means to move house; use 搬到 + place when specifying the new address.

下个月我们就要搬家到市中心了。 (Xià ge yuè wǒmen jiù yào bānjiā dào shìzhōngxīn le.)

Next month we are moving to the city center.

搬家那天，好几个朋友来帮忙。 (Bānjiā nà tiān, hǎo jǐ ge péngyou lái bāngmáng.)

On moving day, several friends came to help.

3 · Build new sentences

Describe rooms, furniture, and appliances.

Model

奶奶喜欢坐在花园里晒太阳。 (Nǎinai xǐhuan zuò zài huāyuán lǐ shài tàiyáng.)

Grandma likes sitting in the garden to enjoy the sun.

1. Say there is fresh fruit and milk in the refrigerator.

Show one natural answer

冰箱里有新鲜的水果和牛奶。 (Bīngxiāng lǐ yǒu xīnxiān de shuǐguǒ hé niúnǎi.)

There is fresh fruit and milk in the refrigerator.

2. Say the cat likes sleeping on the sofa.

Show one natural answer

小猫喜欢在沙发上睡觉。 (Xiǎo māo xǐhuan zài shāfā shàng shuìjiào.)

The kitten likes sleeping on the sofa.

3. Say this house is bright and clean.

Show one natural answer

这套房子又亮堂又干净。 (Zhè tào fángzi yòu liàngtang yòu gānjìng.)

This house is both bright and clean.

4 · Read in context: Cleaning Up the New Home

李叔叔一家刚搬进新房子。屋子虽然不算特别大，但是客厅里的沙发很舒服，窗外就能看到小区里的花园。 (Lǐ shūshu yì jiā gāng bān jìn xīn fángzi. Wūzi suīrán bú suàn tèbié dà, dànshì kètīng lǐ de shāfā hěn shūfu, chuāngwài jiù néng kàndào xiǎoqū lǐ de huāyuán.)
Uncle Li’s family just moved into a new house. Although the rooms aren’t huge, the sofa in the living room is very comfortable, and outside the window you can see the neighborhood garden.

夏天快到了，他们还提前检查了空调和冰箱，把屋子打扫得干干净净。 (Xiàtiān kuài dào le, tāmen hái tíqián jiǎnchá le kōngtiáo hé bīngxiāng, bǎ wūzi dǎsǎo de gāngānjìngjìng.)
Summer is almost here, so they checked the air conditioner and refrigerator in advance and cleaned the rooms spotless.

Q1. What can you see outside the window of Uncle Li’s new home?
Show answer
The neighborhood garden (小区里的花园).
Q2. Which appliances did they check before summer?
Show answer
The air conditioner and refrigerator.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Describe your home or room using 房子, 屋子, or 沙发.
Show one possible response

我的屋子不大，但放着一张床和一张小沙发，很舒服。 (Wǒ de wūzi bú dà, dàn fàng zhe yì zhāng chuáng hé yì zhāng xiǎo shāfā, hěn shūfu.)

My room isn’t big, but it has a bed and a small sofa—very comfortable.

2. Say which household appliance you use most often.
Show one possible response

夏天我每天都要开空调，冬天用得比较少。 (Xiàtiān wǒ měitiān dōu yào kāi kōngtiáo, dōngtiān yòng de bǐjiào shǎo.)

In summer I turn on the air conditioner every day, and use it less in winter.

6 · Watch out

房子 refers to a house/apartment building or unit as a whole, while 屋子 refers to a room (or the interior space of a home).

Pronunciation clinic

空调 (kōngtiáo) pairs flat kōng with rising tiáo; 洗衣机 (xǐyījī) has third-tone xǐ followed by two flat first tones.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which word means "washing machine"?

a. 冰箱 (bīngxiāng) — refrigerator

b. 洗衣机 (xǐyījī) — washing machine

c. 空调 (kōngtiáo) — air conditioner

Show answer
b

洗衣机 literally means clothes-washing machine.

b. 屋子 (wūzi) — room

c. 花园 (huāyuán) — garden

Show answer
a

沙发 is sofa.
