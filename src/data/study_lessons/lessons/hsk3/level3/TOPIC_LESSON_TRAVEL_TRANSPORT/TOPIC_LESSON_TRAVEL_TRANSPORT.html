Journeys, Tickets & Transit

High-speed trains, flights, tickets, and getting around

travel-transport · #travel · #transport · #tickets

Learning objectives

  • Name vehicles and travel essentials: 高铁, 汽车, 船, 自行车, 护照, 行李, 地图, 宾馆, and 游客.
  • Discuss departures, arrivals, and delays: 出发, 离开, 起飞, 检票, 接, 经过, and 晚点.
  • Navigate streets and traffic with 马路, 路口, 红绿灯, and 骑.

1 · Catching the High-Speed Train

Xiao Zhou and his friend arrive at the station to take a high-speed train.

A: 护照和车票都带了吗？前面路口有红绿灯，咱们过马路吧。 (Hùzhào hé chēpiào dōu dài le ma? Qiánmiàn lùkǒu yǒu hóng-lǜdēng, zánmen guò mǎlù ba.)
Did you bring your passport and ticket? There’s a traffic light at the intersection ahead—let’s cross the street.

B: 都带着呢，行李也不重。咱们几点出发去检票？ (Dōu dài zhe ne, xíngli yě bú zhòng. Zánmen jǐ diǎn chūfā qù jiǎnpiào?)
I have both, and my luggage isn’t heavy. What time do we head to ticket inspection?

A: 八点半开车，现在已经可以检票了。但愿这趟高铁别晚点。 (Bā diǎn bàn kāichē, xiànzài yǐjīng kěyǐ jiǎnpiào le. Dànyuàn zhè tàng gāotiě bié wǎndiǎn.)
It leaves at 8:30, so we can check tickets now. Hopefully this high-speed train isn’t delayed.

B: 到了以后我表哥会开车来接我们去宾馆。 (Dào le yǐhòu wǒ biǎogē huì kāichē lái jiē wǒmen qù bīnguǎn.)
After we arrive, my cousin will drive to pick us up and take us to the hotel.

2 · How the language works

Picking someone up with 接

去 / 来 + <Place> + 接 + <Person> (qù / lái + <Place> + jiē + <Person>)

go / come + <Place> + pick up + <Person>

Use 来 / 去 + 接 + person to mean going or coming to pick someone up at a station, airport, or school.

下午我去机场接一位外国游客。 (Xiàwǔ wǒ qù jīchǎng jiē yí wèi wàiguó yóukè.)

This afternoon I am going to the airport to pick up a foreign tourist.

爸爸每天五点开车去接妹妹放学。 (Bàba měitiān wǔ diǎn kāichē qù jiē mèimei fàngxué.)

Dad drives to pick up my younger sister from school every day at five.

Passing by or through with 经过

<Vehicle / Person> + 经过 + <Place> (<Vehicle / Person> + jīngguò + <Place>)

<Vehicle / Person> + pass through + <Place>

经过 (jīngguò) means to pass through a place on the way somewhere.

这路汽车经过图书馆门口。 (Zhè lù qìchē jīngguò túshūguǎn ménkǒu.)

This bus route passes by the library entrance.

回家的路上我们经过一个很大的路口。 (Huí jiā de lùshang wǒmen jīngguò yí ge hěn dà de lùkǒu.)

On the way home we passed through a very big intersection.

3 · Build new sentences

Talk about departures, riding bikes, and travel delays.

Model

飞机九点起飞，我们七点就得离开家。 (Fēijī jiǔ diǎn qǐfēi, wǒmen qī diǎn jiù děi líkāi jiā.)

The plane takes off at nine, so we have to leave home at seven.

1. Say you like riding a bicycle to work.

Show one natural answer

我喜欢骑自行车去上班。 (Wǒ xǐhuan qí zìxíngchē qù shàngbān.)

I like riding a bicycle to work.

2. Say the flight was delayed by half an hour.

Show one natural answer

今天的航班晚点了半个小时。 (Jīntiān de hángbān wǎndiǎn le bàn ge xiǎoshí.)

Today’s flight was delayed by half an hour.

3. Say you looked at the map and found the hotel.

Show one natural answer

我看了地图，很快就找到了宾馆。 (Wǒ kàn le dìtú, hěn kuài jiù zhǎodào le bīnguǎn.)

I looked at the map and quickly found the hotel.

4 · Read in context: A Smooth Trip by Train and Boat

上个月，小吴带着简单的行李坐高铁去南方旅游。高铁准时出发，没有晚点，三个小时就到了。 (Shàng ge yuè, Xiǎo Wú dài zhe jiǎndān de xíngli zuò gāotiě qù nánfāng lǚyóu. Gāotiě zhǔnshí chūfā, méiyǒu wǎndiǎn, sān ge xiǎoshí jiù dào le.)
Last month, Xiao Wu took light luggage and rode the high-speed train to travel in the south. The train departed on time without delay and arrived in three hours.

住进宾馆以后，他拿着地图去湖边坐船。湖面上风很凉快，船上还有不少拍照的游客。 (Zhù jìn bīnguǎn yǐhòu, tā ná zhe dìtú qù húbiān zuò chuán. Húmiàn shàng fēng hěn liángkuai, chuán shàng hái yǒu bùshǎo pāizhào de yóukè.)
After checking into the hotel, he took a map and went to the lake to ride a boat. The breeze on the lake was cool, and there were quite a few tourists taking photos on the boat.

Q1. How long did the high-speed train trip take?
Show answer
Three hours.
Q2. What did Xiao Wu do after checking into the hotel?
Show answer
He took a map and went to the lake to ride a boat.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Describe how you usually travel between cities and why.
Show one possible response

去附近的城市我喜欢坐高铁，又快又不容易晚点。 (Qù fùjìn de chéngshì wǒ xǐhuan zuò gāotiě, yòu kuài yòu bù róngyì wǎndiǎn.)

To nearby cities I like taking the high-speed train—it’s fast and not easily delayed.

2. Talk about a time you picked someone up or went to a hotel.
Show one possible response

朋友来玩儿时，我去火车站接他，然后一起去宾馆。 (Péngyou lái wánr shí, wǒ qù huǒchēzhàn jiē tā, ránhòu yìqǐ qù bīnguǎn.)

When my friend came to visit, I went to the train station to pick him up, then we went to the hotel together.

6 · Watch out

Use 骑 (qí) only for straddling vehicles/animals (骑自行车, 骑马); use 坐 for trains, planes, boats, and buses, and 开 for driving a car.

Pronunciation clinic

红绿灯 (hóng-lǜdēng) keeps the rounded vowel ü in lǜ; 高铁 (gāotiě) moves from flat gāo to low third-tone tiě.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

Did Xiao Wu’s high-speed train arrive on time?

Show answer
Yes, it departed on time without delay and arrived in three hours.

The passage states 高铁准时出发，没有晚点.

Production

Write two sentences about a trip using 出发, 宾馆, or 行李.

Show answer
我们早上八点出发，下午到达宾馆以后先把行李放好。

Use 出发, 宾馆, and 行李 naturally.

  • Give street directions with 马路, 路口, and 红绿灯.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which verb means "to be delayed / late (of transport)"?

a. 起飞 (qǐfēi) — take off

b. 晚点 (wǎndiǎn) — be delayed

c. 检票 (jiǎnpiào) — check tickets

Show answer
b

晚点 means a train, bus, or flight is delayed.

Selection

Which word means "passport"?

a. 护照 (hùzhào) — passport

b. 地图 (dìtú) — map

c. 行李 (xíngli) — luggage

Show answer
a

护照 means passport.

Selection

Which verb means "to pick up (someone)"?

a. 骑 (qí) — ride

b. 接 (jiē) — pick up

c. 离开 (líkāi) — leave

Show answer
b

接 means to meet/pick up someone.
