# Conditions, Reasons & Conjunctions

Connect complex clauses using 不但……而且, 如果……的话, 只要, 只有, 或者, 可是, 根据, and 比如.

## Learning objectives

- Link ideas with 不但……而且, 可是, 然后, and 直到.

- Express conditions with 如果……的话, 只要, and 只有.

- Give alternatives and examples with 或, 或者, 根据, and 比如.

## 1. Deciding on a Weekend Plan

Two friends weigh conditions and alternatives for a Saturday outing.

**A:** 如果明天不下雨的话，咱们去爬山吧，可是要是下雨怎么办？ (Rúguǒ míngtiān bú xià yǔ dehuà, zánmen qù páshān ba, kěshì yàoshi xià yǔ zěnme bàn?) — If it doesn’t rain tomorrow, let’s go hiking—but what if it rains?

**B:** 根据天气预报，明天是晴天。而且即使下雨，咱们也可以去博物馆或者看电影。 (Gēnjù tiānqì yùbào, míngtiān shì qíngtiān. Érqiě jíshǐ xià yǔ, zánmen yě kěyǐ qù bówùguǎn huòzhě kàn diànyǐng.) — According to the weather forecast, tomorrow is sunny. And even if it rains, we can go to a museum or watch a movie.

**A:** 山顶风景不但很美，而且空气也特别新鲜！ (Shāndǐng fēngjǐng búdàn hěn měi, érqiě kōngqì yě tèbié xīnxiān!) — The scenery at the mountaintop is not only beautiful, but the air is also especially fresh!

**B:** 对呀，只要明天早点儿出门，咱们就能在山顶多待一会儿，直到下午再下山。 (Duì ya, zhǐyào míngtiān zǎodiǎnr chūmén, zánmen jiù néng zài shāndǐng duō dāi yíhuìr, zhídào xiàwǔ zài xiàshān.) — Right—as long as we leave early tomorrow, we can stay longer at the top and not come down until afternoon.

## 2. How the language works

### Not only… but also with 不但……而且

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 不但 + <Clause A> + ，而且 + <Clause B> (<Subject> + búdàn + <Clause A> + , érqiě + <Clause B>)

*<Subject> + not only + <Clause A> + , but also + <Clause B>*

Use 不但……而且 (búdàn… érqiě) to add a stronger or second positive/negative point.

**这家餐厅不但菜好吃，而且服务也很热情。**
*Zhè jiā cāntīng búdàn cài hǎochī, érqiě fúwù yě hěn rèqíng.*
This restaurant not only has delicious food, but the service is also warm.

**他不但会说英语，而且汉语也说得很流利。**
*Tā búdàn huì shuō Yīngyǔ, érqiě Hànyǔ yě shuō de hěn liúlì.*
He can not only speak English, but also speaks Chinese very fluently.

### 只要……就 vs. 只有……才

**Pattern:** 只要 + <Condition> + ，就 + <Result>  /  只有 + <Condition> + ，才 + <Result> (zhǐyào + <Condition> + , jiù + <Result>  /  zhǐyǒu + <Condition> + , cái + <Result>)

*as long as + <Condition> + , then + <Result>  /  only if + <Condition> + , only then + <Result>*

只要……就 ("as long as…, then…") gives a sufficient condition; 只有……才 ("only if…, only then…") gives a necessary condition.

**只要每天练习，你的发音就会越来越好。**
*Zhǐyào měitiān liànxí, nǐ de fāyīn jiù huì yuèláiyuè hǎo.*
As long as you practice every day, your pronunciation will get better and better.

**只有认真复习，才能考出好成绩。**
*Zhǐyǒu rènzhēn fùxí, cái néng kǎochū hǎo chéngjì.*
Only if you review seriously can you get good exam grades.

## 3. Build new sentences

Connect clauses with conditions, examples, and time limits.

*Model:* **如果你有空的话，咱们先吃饭，然后再去看电影。**
*Rúguǒ nǐ yǒukòng dehuà, zánmen xiān chīfàn, ránhòu zài qù kàn diànyǐng.*
If you have time, let’s eat first and then go see a movie.

**Cue 1:** Give two examples of healthy fruit using 比如.

*Model:* **我喜欢吃很多水果，比如西瓜和香蕉。**
*Wǒ xǐhuan chī hěn duō shuǐguǒ, bǐrú xīguā hé xiāngjiāo.*
I like eating many fruits, such as watermelon and bananas.

**Cue 2:** Say he kept working until 9 p.m.

*Model:* **他一直工作到晚上九点，直到做完才回家。**
*Tā yìzhí gōngzuò dào wǎnshang jiǔ diǎn, zhídào zuò wán cái huí jiā.*
He kept working until 9 p.m. and didn’t go home until he finished.

**Cue 3:** Say you can choose tea or juice.

*Model:* **你可以喝茶或喝果汁。**
*Nǐ kěyǐ hē chá huò hē guǒzhī.*
You can drink tea or drink juice.

## 4. Read in context: Learning Through Hobbies

学习外语的方法有很多，比如看电影、听音乐或者读故事。根据很多老师的经验，兴趣是最好的老师。 (Xuéxí wàiyǔ de fāngfǎ yǒu hěn duō, bǐrú kàn diànyǐng, tīng yīnyuè huòzhě dú gùshi. Gēnjù hěn duō lǎoshī de jīngyàn, xìngqù shì zuì hǎo de lǎoshī.)

There are many methods for learning a foreign language, such as watching movies, listening to music, or reading stories. According to many teachers’ experience, interest is the best teacher.

如果只背课本的话，学习可能有点儿枯燥；可是只要把学习跟自己的爱好结合起来，学起来就会轻松得多。 (Rúguǒ zhǐ bèi kèběn dehuà, xuéxí kěnéng yǒudiǎnr kūzào; kěshì zhǐyào bǎ xuéxí gēn zìjǐ de àihào jiéhé qǐlái, xué qǐlái jiù huì qīngsōng de duō.)

If you only memorize the textbook, studying can be a bit dry; however, as long as you combine study with your own hobbies, learning becomes much easier.

**Q1. What examples of language-learning methods are given?** Suggested answer: Watching movies, listening to music, or reading stories.

**Q2. What makes studying much easier according to the passage?** Suggested answer: Combining study with your own hobbies.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Describe a city, restaurant, or friend using 不但……而且.

*Possible response:* **我的朋友不但聪明，而且非常愿意帮助别人。**
*Wǒ de péngyou búdàn cōngmíng, érqiě fēicháng yuànyì bāngzhù biérén.*
My friend is not only clever, but also very willing to help others.

**Speaking 2:** State a condition for success using 只要……就 or 只有……才.

*Possible response:* **只有每天坚持练习，才能把口语说流利。**
*Zhǐyǒu měitiān jiānchí liànxí, cái néng bǎ kǒuyǔ shuō liúlì.*
Only if you keep practicing every day can you speak fluently.

## 6. Watch out

Use 或者 (or 或) for "or" in statements ("tea or coffee"), and 还是 for "or" in questions ("tea or coffee?").

**Pronunciation:** 不但 (búdàn) changes 不 to rising bú before fourth-tone dàn; 而且 (érqiě) pairs rising ér with third-tone qiě.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** According to many teachers’ experience in the reading, what is the best teacher?
**Answer:** Interest (兴趣).. The passage states 根据很多老师的经验，兴趣是最好的老师.

**Production:** Write two sentences: one using 不但……而且 and one using 如果……的话 or 只要……就.
**Answer:** 这门课不但有用，而且老师讲得很清楚。只要认真复习，考试就一定能通过。. Use 不但……而且 and 只要……就.

## Review and next step

- Connect clauses with 不但……而且, 可是, and 然后.

- Express conditions with 如果……的话, 只要, and 只有.

- Use 或者, 比如, 根据, and 直到.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which conjunction pair means "only if…, only then…"?
- a. 只要……就 (zhǐyào…jiù) — as long as…then
- b. 只有……才 (zhǐyǒu…cái) — only if…only then
- c. 不但……而且 (búdàn…érqiě) — not only…but also
**Answer:** b. 只有……才 expresses a necessary condition.

**Selection:** Which word means "for example / such as"?
- a. 根据 (gēnjù) — according to
- b. 比如 (bǐrú) — for example
- c. 然后 (ránhòu) — then
**Answer:** b. 比如 introduces examples.

**Selection:** Which word means "or" in a statement?
- a. 或者 (huòzhě) — or (statement)
- b. 可是 (kěshì) — but
- c. 直到 (zhídào) — until
**Answer:** a. 或者 is used for "or" in statements.
