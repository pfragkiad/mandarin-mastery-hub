# People, Ages & Backgrounds

Learn to introduce people by background, age group, appearance, and occupation in natural Mandarin.

## Learning objectives

- Say where someone was born or comes from using 出生 and 来自.

- Refer to men, women, students, adults, and older people naturally.

- Describe a person’s hair or role such as 画家 or 运动员.

## 1. Meeting a New Classmate

Linlin tells her friend about a new classmate who just joined their art club.

**A:** 那个男生是谁？他来自哪里？ (Nàge nánshēng shì shéi? Tā láizì nǎlǐ?) — Who is that male student? Where is he from?

**B:** 他叫小陈，在广州出生，现在住在北京。 (Tā jiào Xiǎo Chén, zài Guǎngzhōu chūshēng, xiànzài zhù zài Běijīng.) — He is Xiao Chen; he was born in Guangzhou and lives in Beijing now.

**A:** 他的头发很长，看起来像一位年轻画家。 (Tā de tóufa hěn cháng, kàn qǐlái xiàng yí wèi niánqīng huàjiā.) — His hair is quite long; he looks like a young painter.

**B:** 没错，而且他以前还是学校的运动员呢。 (Méicuò, érqiě tā yǐqián hái shì xuéxiào de yùndòngyuán ne.) — That’s right, and he used to be a school athlete too.

## 2. How the language works

### Stating where someone is from

**Pattern:** <Person> + 来自 + <Place> (<Person> + láizì + <Place>)

*<Person> + comes from + <Place>*

Place 来自 (láizì) before a city or country to say where a person originates.

**那位女老师来自上海。**
*Nà wèi nǚ lǎoshī láizì Shànghǎi.*
That female teacher comes from Shanghai.

**我们班的留学生来自不同的国家。**
*Wǒmen bān de liúxuéshēng láizì bù tóng de guójiā.*
The international students in our class come from different countries.

### Talking about birthplace

**Pattern:** <Person> + 在 + <Place> + 出生 (<Person> + zài + <Place> + chūshēng)

*<Person> + at + <Place> + was born*

Put 在 + place before 出生 (chūshēng) to state where someone was born.

**我妹妹在成都出生。**
*Wǒ mèimei zài Chéngdū chūshēng.*
My younger sister was born in Chengdu.

**这位老人是在南京出生的。**
*Zhè wèi lǎorén shì zài Nánjīng chūshēng de.*
This elderly person was born in Nanjing.

## 3. Build new sentences

Introduce someone by swapping in a new role, birthplace, or origin.

*Model:* **我的朋友来自杭州，她是一位有名的画家。**
*Wǒ de péngyou láizì Hángzhōu, tā shì yí wèi yǒumíng de huàjiā.*
My friend comes from Hangzhou; she is a famous painter.

**Cue 1:** Say that the athlete was born in Qingdao.

*Model:* **那位运动员在青岛出生。**
*Nà wèi yùndòngyuán zài Qīngdǎo chūshēng.*
That athlete was born in Qingdao.

**Cue 2:** Say that female student has very short hair.

*Model:* **那个女生的头发很短。**
*Nàge nǚshēng de tóufa hěn duǎn.*
That female student has very short hair.

**Cue 3:** Say that adults should help elderly people.

*Model:* **大人应该多帮助老人。**
*Dàren yīnggāi duō bāngzhù lǎorén.*
Adults should help elderly people more.

## 4. Read in context: Our Community Center

我们社区的活动室每天都有很多人。早上，几位老人一起来这里喝茶、聊天。 (Wǒmen shèqū de huódòngshì měitiān dōu yǒu hěn duō rén. Zǎoshang, jǐ wèi lǎorén yìqǐ lái zhèlǐ hē chá, liáotiān.)

Our community activity room has many people every day. In the morning, several elderly people come here together to drink tea and chat.

周末的时候，很多男生和女生也来参加活动，还有一位来自西安的画家教大家画画儿。 (Zhōumò de shíhou, hěn duō nánshēng hé nǚshēng yě lái cānjiā huódòng, hái yǒu yí wèi láizì Xī’ān de huàjiā jiāo dàjiā huà huàr.)

On weekends, many boys and girls also come to join activities, and a painter from Xi’an teaches everyone to paint.

**Q1. Who comes to the community room in the morning?** Suggested answer: Several elderly people come to drink tea and chat.

**Q2. Where is the painter who teaches on weekends from?** Suggested answer: The painter is from Xi’an.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Introduce a friend: say where they are from and what they do.

*Possible response:* **我的朋友小王来自武汉，他现在是一名游泳运动员。**
*Wǒ de péngyou Xiǎo Wáng láizì Wǔhàn, tā xiànzài shì yì míng yóuyǒng yùndòngyuán.*
My friend Xiao Wang comes from Wuhan, and he is a swimming athlete now.

**Speaking 2:** Describe a family member or neighbor using 大人, 老人, or 头发.

*Possible response:* **我爷爷是一位老人，他的头发已经全白了。**
*Wǒ yéye shì yí wèi lǎorén, tā de tóufa yǐjīng quán bái le.*
My grandfather is an elderly person, and his hair is already completely white.

## 6. Watch out

Use 男生 / 女生 for students or young people, and 男人 / 女人 for adult men and women in general. Calling a classmate 男人 sounds unnatural.

**Pronunciation:** 头发 (tóufa) uses a neutral tone on the second syllable; 来自 (láizì) pairs a rising second tone with a falling fourth tone.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** In the reading passage, what happens in the community room on weekends?
**Answer:** Many boys and girls join activities, and a painter from Xi’an teaches everyone to paint.. The second paragraph describes weekend activities for students and the painter from Xi’an.

**Production:** Write two sentences introducing a person: where they were born and what their role or job is.
**Answer:** 她叫小李，在成都出生。她现在是一位有名的画家。. Use 在……出生 for birthplace and 是…… for occupation.

## Review and next step

- State where someone was born or comes from.

- Distinguish student and adult gender terms.

- Describe hair and roles like 画家 and 运动员.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "to come from"?
- a. 来自 (láizì) — come from
- b. 出生 (chūshēng) — be born
- c. 别人 (biérén) — other people
**Answer:** a. 来自 introduces a person’s place of origin.

**Selection:** Which word refers to a famous person?
- a. 大人 (dàren) — adult
- b. 名人 (míngrén) — famous person
- c. 老人 (lǎorén) — elderly person
**Answer:** b. 名人 literally means a well-known person.

**Selection:** Which word means "hair"?
- a. 头发 (tóufa) — hair
- b. 画家 (huàjiā) — painter
- c. 运动员 (yùndòngyuán) — athlete
**Answer:** a. 头发 refers to hair on the head.
