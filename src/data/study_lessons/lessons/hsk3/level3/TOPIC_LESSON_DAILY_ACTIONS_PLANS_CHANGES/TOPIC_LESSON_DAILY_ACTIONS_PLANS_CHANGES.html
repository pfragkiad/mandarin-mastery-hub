Plans, Changes & Keeping Going

Planning, discovering, exchanging, completing, and persisting

daily-actions · #plans · #changes · #goals

Learning objectives

  • Talk about intentions, persistence, and completion: 打算, 试, 选, 坚持, 完成, and 忘记.
  • Describe change and discovery: 变, 变成, 换, 加, 长, 不见, and 发现.
  • Talk about borrowing, receiving, and returning: 借, 还, 收, 得, 得到, 参加, and 照.

1 · Changing Plans and Keeping Going

Two friends talk about a photography contest and returning a borrowed camera.

A: 我把上次借你的相机带来还给你了！ (Wǒ bǎ shàng cì jiè nǐ de xiàngjī dài lái huán gěi nǐ le!)
I brought back the camera I borrowed from you last time to return it!

B: 谢谢！对了，你打算参加下个月的摄影比赛吗？ (Xièxie! Duìle, nǐ dǎsuàn cānjiā xià ge yuè de shèyǐng bǐsài ma?)
Thanks! By the way, do you plan to join next month’s photography contest?

A: 想试试！我选了几张照片，可总觉得还不够好，打算再换两张。 (Xiǎng shìshi! Wǒ xuǎn le jǐ zhāng zhàopiàn, kě zǒng juéde hái búgòu hǎo, dǎsuàn zài huàn liǎng zhāng.)
I want to try! I picked a few photos, but feel they’re not good enough yet, so I plan to swap two.

B: 只要坚持练习，你一定能完成目标，得到好成绩！ (Zhǐyào jiānchí liànxí, nǐ yídìng néng wánchéng mùbiāo, dédào hǎo chéngjì!)
As long as you keep practicing, you’ll definitely reach your goal and get a good result!

2 · How the language works

Turning into something with 变成

<Subject> + 变成 + <New State / Noun> (<Subject> + biànchéng + <New State / Noun>)

<Subject> + turn into + <New State / Noun>

Use 变成 (biànchéng) + noun to express changing from one state or thing into another.

几年没见，那个小男孩长成大高个儿了，家乡也变成了一座现代城市。 (Jǐ nián méi jiàn, nàge xiǎo nánhái zhǎng chéng dà gāogèr le, jiāxiāng yě biànchéng le yí zuò xiàndài chéngshì.)

Haven’t seen him in years—that little boy grew tall, and our hometown has turned into a modern city.

水在零度以下会变成冰。 (Shuǐ zài líng dù yǐxià huì biànchéng bīng.)

Water turns into ice below zero degrees.

Borrowing and returning with 借 / 还

向 + <Person> + 借 + <Item> / 把 + <Item> + 还给 + <Person> (xiàng + <Person> + jiè + <Item> / bǎ + <Item> + huán gěi + <Person>)

from + <Person> + borrow + <Item> / take + <Item> + return to + <Person>

借 (jiè) can mean borrow (向……借) or lend (借给……); 还 (huán) means return (还给……).

我向同学借了一本词典。 (Wǒ xiàng tóngxué jiè le yì běn cídiǎn.)

I borrowed a dictionary from my classmate.

看完以后，请按时把书还给图书馆。 (Kàn wán yǐhòu, qǐng ànshí bǎ shū huán gěi túshūguǎn.)

After reading, please return the book to the library on time.

3 · Build new sentences

Express plans, discoveries, and completing tasks.

Model

我发现这个办法能更快地完成任务。 (Wǒ fāxiàn zhège bànfǎ néng gèng kuài de wánchéng rènwu.)

I discovered this method can complete the task faster.

1. Say you plan to try a new sport this semester.

Show one natural answer

这学期我打算试着学一项新运动。 (Zhè xuéqī wǒ dǎsuàn shì zhe xué yí xiàng xīn yùndòng.)

This semester I plan to try learning a new sport.

2. Say you forgot where you put your umbrella.

Show one natural answer

我忘记把伞放在哪里了，伞不见了。 (Wǒ wàngjì bǎ sǎn fàng zài nǎlǐ le, sǎn bújiàn le.)

I forgot where I put my umbrella—it’s missing.

3. Say the weather has changed much cooler.

Show one natural answer

天气变凉快了，该加件衣服了。 (Tiānqì biàn liángkuai le, gāi jiā jiàn yīfu le.)

The weather has turned cooler; it’s time to add a layer of clothes.

4 · Read in context: Sticking With a Goal

去年秋天，小张打算每天坚持跑三公里。刚开始的时候，他觉得很累，好几次都想放弃，甚至差点儿忘记自己的计划。 (Qùnián qiūtiān, Xiǎo Zhāng dǎsuàn měitiān jiānchí pǎo sān gōnglǐ. Gāng kāishǐ de shíhou, tā juéde hěn lèi, hǎo jǐ cì dōu xiǎng fàngqì, shènzhì chàdiǎnr wàngjì zìjǐ de jìhuà.)
Last autumn, Xiao Zhang planned to stick to running three kilometers every day. At the start he felt very tired, wanted to quit several times, and even nearly forgot his plan.

后来他选了一条经过公园的路线，一边跑一边看风景。半年以后，他发现自己不仅完成了目标，身体也变得更健康了。 (Hòulái tā xuǎn le yì tiáo jīngguò gōngyuán de lùxiàn, yìbiān pǎo yìbiān kàn fēngjǐng. Bàn nián yǐhòu, tā fāxiàn zìjǐ bùjǐn wánchéng le mùbiāo, shēntǐ yě biàn de gèng jiànkāng le.)
Later he chose a route passing through a park, enjoying the scenery while running. Half a year later, he discovered he had not only completed his goal, but his body had also become healthier.

Q1. What was Xiao Zhang’s plan last autumn?
Show answer
To stick to running three kilometers every day.
Q2. What helped him keep going?
Show answer
He chose a route passing through a park so he could enjoy the scenery while running.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Talk about a plan you want to stick to using 打算, 坚持, and 完成.
Show one possible response

我打算每天复习十个生词，坚持一个月完成这本课本。 (Wǒ dǎsuàn měitiān fùxí shí ge shēngcí, jiānchí yí ge yuè wánchéng zhè běn kèběn.)

I plan to review ten new words every day and keep at it for a month to finish this textbook.

2. Describe a change you noticed recently using 发现 or 变.
Show one possible response

我发现公园里的树都长高了，草地也变绿了。 (Wǒ fāxiàn gōngyuán lǐ de shù dōu zhǎng gāo le, cǎodì yě biàn lǜ le.)

I noticed the trees in the park have grown taller and the lawn has turned green.

6 · Watch out

还 has two readings: hái ("still / also") and huán ("return / give back"). In 还书 ("return a book"), always read it huán.

Pronunciation clinic

发现 (fāxiàn) moves from flat fā to falling xiàn; 坚持 (jiānchí) moves from flat jiān to rising chí.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

What two results did Xiao Zhang discover after half a year of running?

Show answer
He completed his goal and became healthier.

The passage says 不仅完成了目标，身体也变得更健康了.

Production

Write two sentences using 打算 and 坚持 or 完成.

Show answer
我打算这周每天练习写汉字，坚持到周末完成全部作业。

Use 打算, 坚持, and 完成.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which verb means "to persist / keep going"?

a. 坚持 (jiānchí) — persist

b. 忘记 (wàngjì) — forget

c. 不见 (bújiàn) — be missing

Show answer
a

坚持 means to persist or stick with something.

Selection

Which verb means "to discover / notice"?

a. 完成 (wánchéng) — complete

b. 发现 (fāxiàn) — discover

c. 参加 (cānjiā) — participate

Show answer
b

发现 means to discover or notice.

Selection

How is 还 pronounced when it means "to return / give back"?

a. hái (still / also) — still

b. huán (return / give back) — return

c. huàn (change / exchange) — exchange

Show answer
b

还 meaning "return" is pronounced huán.
