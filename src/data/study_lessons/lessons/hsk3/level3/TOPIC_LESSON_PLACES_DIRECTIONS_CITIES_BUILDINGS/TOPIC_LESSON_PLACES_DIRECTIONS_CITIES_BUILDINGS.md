# Cities, Streets & Buildings

Talk about countries, cities, streets, neighborhoods, parks, and building features like elevators and stairs.

## Learning objectives

- Name geographic and urban places: 世界, 国家, 城市, 市, 外地, 小区, 街, 路边, and 公园.

- Distinguish 地点 (event location) and 地方 (general place/area), plus 地 (ground).

- Name building features: 电梯, 楼梯, 卫生间, 室, and 园.

## 1. Showing a Visitor Around

Xiao Chen shows an out-of-town friend around his residential neighborhood.

**A:** 欢迎来我们市玩儿！这是我住的小区。 (Huānyíng lái wǒmen shì wánr! Zhè shì wǒ zhù de xiǎoqū.) — Welcome to our city! This is the neighborhood where I live.

**B:** 你们这儿真安静，路边还有个漂亮的公园。 (Nǐmen zhèr zhēn ānjìng, lùbiān hái yǒu ge piàoliang de gōngyuán.) — It’s so quiet here, and there’s a pretty park by the roadside too.

**A:** 对，我们楼有电梯，不用爬楼梯。对了，你想去卫生间吗？ (Duì, wǒmen lóu yǒu diàntī, búyòng pá lóutī. Duìle, nǐ xiǎng qù wèishēngjiān ma?) — Yes, our building has an elevator, so no need to climb stairs. By the way, do you need the restroom?

**B:** 好啊，一会儿咱们再去外面那条街转转。 (Hǎo a, yíhuìr zánmen zài qù wàimiàn nà tiáo jiē zhuànzhuan.) — Sure, and later let’s stroll along that street outside.

## 2. How the language works

### Using 地方 vs. 地点

**Pattern:** 开会 / 集合 + 的 + 地点 + 是 + <Place> (kāihuì / jíhé + de + dìdiǎn + shì + <Place>)

*meeting / gathering + ’s + venue + is + <Place>*

地方 (dìfang) is any general place or region; 地点 (dìdiǎn) is the designated spot/venue for an event or meeting.

**明天集合的地点在公园门口。**
*Míngtiān jíhé de dìdiǎn zài gōngyuán ménkǒu.*
Tomorrow’s meeting location is at the park gate.

**我去了很多地方，最喜欢这个城市。**
*Wǒ qù le hěn duō dìfang, zuì xǐhuan zhège chéngshì.*
I’ve been to many places, and I like this city best.

### Taking the elevator vs. climbing stairs

**Pattern:** 坐电梯 / 走楼梯 + 上楼 / 下楼 (zuò diàntī / zǒu lóutī + shànglóu / xiàlóu)

*take elevator / walk stairs + go up / go down*

Use 坐电梯 (ride the elevator) and 爬楼梯 / 走楼梯 (climb/take the stairs).

**十层太高了，咱们坐电梯上去吧。**
*Shí céng tài gāo le, zánmen zuò diàntī shàngqù ba.*
The tenth floor is too high; let’s take the elevator up.

**二楼很近，我走楼梯就行。**
*Èr lóu hěn jìn, wǒ zǒu lóutī jiù xíng.*
The second floor is close; taking the stairs is fine for me.

## 3. Build new sentences

Talk about cities, countries, and public spaces.

*Model:* **世界各地的游客都喜欢来这座城市。**
*Shìjiè gèdì de yóukè dōu xǐhuan lái zhè zuò chéngshì.*
Tourists from all over the world like coming to this city.

**Cue 1:** Say his friend came from out of town.

*Model:* **他的朋友是从外地来的。**
*Tā de péngyou shì cóng wàidì lái de.*
His friend came from out of town.

**Cue 2:** Say don’t sit on the ground because it is dirty.

*Model:* **地上很脏，别坐在地上。**
*Dì shàng hěn zāng, bié zuò zài dì shàng.*
The ground is dirty; don’t sit on the ground.

**Cue 3:** Say there are many small shops along the street.

*Model:* **这条街的路边有很多小店。**
*Zhè tiáo jiē de lùbiān yǒu hěn duō xiǎo diàn.*
There are many small shops along the roadside of this street.

## 4. Read in context: A Friendly Neighborhood

我住在一个很大的城市里，虽然街道上车来车往，但是我们小区里面环境很好，也很安静。 (Wǒ zhù zài yí ge hěn dà de chéngshì lǐ, suīrán jiēdào shàng chē lái chē wǎng, dànshì wǒmen xiǎoqū lǐmiàn huánjìng hěn hǎo, yě hěn ānjìng.)

I live in a very big city; although the streets have heavy traffic, inside our residential neighborhood the environment is great and quiet.

小区北门外面就是一个公园，很多外地来的朋友都说这里是个适合生活的好地方。 (Xiǎoqū běimén wàimiàn jiùshì yí ge gōngyuán, hěn duō wàidì lái de péngyou dōu shuō zhèlǐ shì ge shìhé shēnghuó de hǎo dìfang.)

Right outside the north gate of the neighborhood is a park, and many friends from out of town say this is a great place to live.

**Q1. What is right outside the north gate of the neighborhood?** Suggested answer: A park.

**Q2. What do friends from out of town say about this neighborhood?** Suggested answer: That it is a good place to live.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Describe the neighborhood or city where you live.

*Possible response:* **我住的小区附近有一条热闹的街和一个公园，生活很方便。**
*Wǒ zhù de xiǎoqū fùjìn yǒu yì tiáo rènào de jiē hé yí ge gōngyuán, shēnghuó hěn fāngbiàn.*
Near my neighborhood there is a lively street and a park, so daily life is convenient.

**Speaking 2:** Ask where the restroom or elevator is in a building.

*Possible response:* **请问，这里的电梯和卫生间在几层？**
*Qǐngwèn, zhèlǐ de diàntī hé wèishēngjiān zài jǐ céng?*
Excuse me, which floor are the elevator and restroom on here?

## 6. Watch out

Use 条 for streets (一条街) and 座 for cities (一座城市). Also remember 卫生间 is the polite everyday word for restroom.

**Pronunciation:** 城市 (chéngshì) moves from rising chéng to falling shì; 电梯 (diàntī) moves from falling diàn to flat tī.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** How does the inside of the narrator’s neighborhood contrast with the streets outside?
**Answer:** The streets have heavy traffic, while the neighborhood inside is quiet with a good environment.. The first paragraph contrasts 街道上车来车往 with 小区里面环境很好，也很安静.

**Production:** Write two sentences using 城市 or 小区 and 电梯 or 公园.
**Answer:** 这个城市的公园很多，我们小区旁边就有一个。. Combine urban place nouns naturally.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "elevator"?
- a. 楼梯 (lóutī) — stairs
- b. 电梯 (diàntī) — elevator
- c. 卫生间 (wèishēngjiān) — restroom
**Answer:** b. 电梯 means elevator.

**Selection:** Which word means "residential neighborhood"?
- a. 小区 (xiǎoqū) — neighborhood
- b. 外地 (wàidì) — out of town
- c. 世界 (shìjiè) — world
**Answer:** a. 小区 is a residential community/neighborhood.

**Selection:** Which word means "specific venue / location for an event"?
- a. 地方 (dìfang) — general place
- b. 地点 (dìdiǎn) — event location
- c. 路边 (lùbiān) — roadside
**Answer:** b. 地点 refers to a specific venue or meeting point.
