# Mountains, Rivers & Nature

Describe natural landscapes, plants, the sun and moon, and caring for the environment.

## Learning objectives

- Name landforms and water: 山, 河, and 海.

- Name plants and celestial bodies: 草, 草地, 树, 开花, 太阳, and 月亮.

- Talk about nature conservation with 环境.

## 1. A View from the Mountain

Two friends hike up a hill and enjoy the scenery at sunset.

**A:** 你看山下那条河，一直流到东边的大海里。 (Nǐ kàn shānxià nà tiáo hé, yìzhí liú dào dōngbiān de dà hǎi lǐ.) — Look at that river below the mountain—it flows all the way into the sea in the east.

**B:** 这里的环境真好！山上有树，还有一大片绿色的草地。 (Zhèlǐ de huánjìng zhēn hǎo! Shānshàng yǒu shù, hái yǒu yí dà piàn lǜsè de cǎodì.) — The environment here is wonderful! There are trees on the mountain and a huge green lawn.

**A:** 春天的时候，路边的树还会开花呢。 (Chūntiān de shíhou, lùbiān de shù hái huì kāihuā ne.) — In spring, the trees along the road even bloom!

**B:** 太阳快下山了，一会儿月亮就要出来了。 (Tàiyáng kuài xiàshān le, yíhuìr yuèliang jiù yào chūlái le.) — The sun is about to set behind the mountains, and soon the moon will come out.

## 2. How the language works

### Protecting the environment

**Pattern:** <Place> + 的 + 环境 + 很 + 好 / 干净 (<Place> + de + huánjìng + hěn + hǎo / gānjìng)

*<Place> + ’s + environment + very + good / clean*

环境 (huánjìng) refers to both the natural environment and living surroundings; pair it with 保护 (protect) or 好.

**这片海边的环境非常干净。**
*Zhè piàn hǎibiān de huánjìng fēicháng gānjìng.*
The environment at this seaside is very clean.

**大家一起努力，这里的环境变得越来越好了。**
*Dàjiā yìqǐ nǔlì, zhèlǐ de huánjìng biàn de yuèláiyuè hǎo le.*
Everyone worked hard together, and the environment here became better and better.

### Counting trees, rivers, and grass

**Pattern:** 一 + 座山 / 条河 / 棵树 (yí + zuò shān / tiáo hé / kē shù)

*one + mountain / river / tree*

Use 棵 (kē) for trees, 条 (tiáo) for rivers, and 座 (zuò) for mountains.

**河边种着很多高大的树。**
*Hébiān zhòng zhe hěn duō gāodà de shù.*
Many tall trees are planted along the river.

**周末我们去爬那座山吧。**
*Zhōumò wǒmen qù pá nà zuò shān ba.*
Let’s go climb that mountain this weekend.

## 3. Build new sentences

Describe natural scenes with sun, moon, plants, and water.

*Model:* **太阳出来了，草地上的露水闪闪发光。**
*Tàiyáng chūlái le, cǎodì shàng de lùshuǐ shǎnshǎn fāguāng.*
The sun came out, and the dew on the grass sparkled.

**Cue 1:** Say tonight’s moon is very round and bright.

*Model:* **今晚的月亮又圆又亮。**
*Jīnwǎn de yuèliang yòu yuán yòu liàng.*
Tonight’s moon is both round and bright.

**Cue 2:** Say don’t step on the grass.

*Model:* **请爱护小草，不要踩草地。**
*Qǐng àihù xiǎocǎo, búyào cǎi cǎodì.*
Please care for the grass and don’t step on the lawn.

**Cue 3:** Say you want to go see the sea in summer.

*Model:* **夏天我想去海边看大海。**
*Xiàtiān wǒ xiǎng qù hǎibiān kàn dàhǎi.*
In summer I want to go to the beach to see the sea.

## 4. Read in context: Green Mountains and Clear Water

我的家乡有山有水，村子前面是一条清澈的小河，后面是一座绿油油的大山。 (Wǒ de jiāxiāng yǒu shān yǒu shuǐ, cūnzi qiánmiàn shì yì tiáo qīngchè de xiǎo hé, hòumiàn shì yí zuò lǜyóuyóu de dà shān.)

My hometown has mountains and water: in front of the village is a clear little river, and behind is a lush green mountain.

每年春天，树枝上长出新叶，草地上的野花也开花了。大家都很爱护这里的自然环境。 (Měi nián chūntiān, shùzhī shàng zhǎng chū xīn yè, cǎodì shàng de yěhuā yě kāihuā le. Dàjiā dōu hěn àihù zhèlǐ de zìrán huánjìng.)

Every spring, new leaves grow on the tree branches, and the wildflowers on the meadow bloom too. Everyone takes great care of the natural environment here.

**Q1. What is in front of and behind the village?** Suggested answer: A clear little river in front, and a green mountain behind.

**Q2. What happens in spring in the village?** Suggested answer: New leaves grow on the trees and wildflowers bloom on the meadow.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Describe a natural place you visited using 山, 河, 海, or 草地.

*Possible response:* **我去过一个海边城市，那里的海很蓝，环境也特别好。**
*Wǒ qù guò yí ge hǎibiān chéngshì, nàlǐ de hǎi hěn lán, huánjìng yě tèbié hǎo.*
I’ve been to a seaside city; the sea there is very blue and the environment is great.

**Speaking 2:** Say what you like about spring nature using 树 and 开花.

*Possible response:* **春天太阳暖洋洋的，很多树都开花了，非常好看。**
*Chūntiān tàiyáng nuǎnyángyáng de, hěn duō shù dōu kāihuā le, fēicháng hǎokàn.*
In spring the sun is warm and many trees bloom—it’s very pretty.

## 6. Watch out

开花 is a separable verb ("to bloom / blossom"): say 花开了 ("the flowers bloomed") or 树开花了 ("the trees bloomed"), not *开花花.

**Pronunciation:** 月亮 (yuèliang) ends with neutral-tone liang; 环境 (huánjìng) moves from second tone to fourth tone.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** How do the villagers treat their natural environment?
**Answer:** They all take great care of the natural environment.. The passage concludes with 大家都很爱护这里的自然环境.

**Production:** Write two sentences describing a mountain or seaside scene using 山/海 and 太阳 or 月亮.
**Answer:** 傍晚我们在海边散步，太阳慢慢落下，月亮升起来了。. Use nature nouns naturally.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "environment"?
- a. 草地 (cǎodì) — lawn
- b. 环境 (huánjìng) — environment
- c. 月亮 (yuèliang) — moon
**Answer:** b. 环境 means environment or surroundings.

**Selection:** Which verb means "to bloom / flower"?
- a. 开花 (kāihuā) — bloom
- b. 太阳 (tàiyáng) — sun
- c. 河 (hé) — river
**Answer:** a. 开花 means to bloom.

**Selection:** Which word means "sea / ocean"?
- a. 河 (hé) — river
- b. 山 (shān) — mountain
- c. 海 (hǎi) — sea
**Answer:** c. 海 means sea or ocean.
