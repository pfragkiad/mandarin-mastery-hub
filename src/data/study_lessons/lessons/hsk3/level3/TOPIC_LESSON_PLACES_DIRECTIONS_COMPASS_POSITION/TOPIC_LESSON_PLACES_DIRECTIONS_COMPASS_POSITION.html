Compass Directions & Positions

Compass directions, regions, and relative positions

places-directions · #directions · #compass · #positions

Learning objectives

  • Name the four cardinal and four intercardinal directions: 东, 南, 西, 北, 东北, 东南, 西北, 西南.
  • Talk about regions and orientation: 北方, 南方, 东方, 西方, and 方向.
  • Describe relative location with 中, 中间, 附近, 面前, 到处, and 向.

1 · Checking Directions on a Map

Two friends look at a map to plan a trip and find a nearby restaurant.

A: 你看地图，哈尔滨在中国的东北方向，冬天很冷。 (Nǐ kàn dìtú, Hā’ěrbīn zài Zhōngguó de dōngběi fāngxiàng, dōngtiān hěn lěng.)
Look at the map—Harbin is in the northeast direction of China, so winter is very cold.

B: 对，北方和南方的气候差别很大，西南那边冬天就暖和得多。 (Duì, běifāng hé nánfāng de qìhòu chābié hěn dà, xīnán nàbiān dōngtiān jiù nuǎnhuo de duō.)
Right, the climate in the north and south differs a lot; the southwest is much warmer in winter.

A: 咱们先别看远的地方了，附近哪儿有吃饭的地方？ (Zánmen xiān bié kàn yuǎn de dìfang le, fùjìn nǎr yǒu chīfàn de dìfang?)
Let’s not look at faraway places for now—where nearby is there a place to eat?

B: 向东走两百米，就在银行和书店的中间，面前有个小广场。 (Xiàng dōng zǒu liǎng bǎi mǐ, jiù zài yínháng hé shūdiàn de zhōngjiān, miànqián yǒu ge xiǎo guǎngchǎng.)
Walk east 200 meters; it’s right between the bank and the bookstore, with a small square in front.

2 · How the language works

Facing or moving toward a direction with 向

向 + <Direction> + <Verb> (xiàng + <Direction> + <Verb>)

toward + <Direction> + <Verb>

Place 向 (xiàng) + direction/person before the action verb.

出了地铁站以后，向南走五分钟就到了。 (Chū le dìtiězhàn yǐhòu, xiàng nán zǒu wǔ fēnzhōng jiù dào le.)

After exiting the subway station, walk south for five minutes and you’re there.

请大家向左边看。 (Qǐng dàjiā xiàng zuǒbiān kàn.)

Please everyone look toward the left.

Between two places with 在……中间

在 + <Place A> + 和 + <Place B> + 中间 (zài + <Place A> + hé + <Place B> + zhōngjiān)

at + <Place A> + and + <Place B> + middle

Use 在 + A + 和 + B + 中间 to locate something between two landmarks.

邮局在医院和学校中间。 (Yóujú zài yīyuàn hé xuéxiào zhōngjiān.)

The post office is between the hospital and the school.

他站在我们中间照了一张相。 (Tā zhàn zài wǒmen zhōngjiān zhào le yì zhāng xiàng.)

He stood in the middle of us and took a photo.

3 · Build new sentences

Describe compass directions, regions, and nearby places.

Model

春天的时候，公园里到处都是花。 (Chūntiān de shíhou, gōngyuán lǐ dàochù dōu shì huā.)

In spring, there are flowers everywhere in the park.

1. Say Beijing is in northern China.

Show one natural answer

北京在中国的北方。 (Běijīng zài Zhōngguó de běifāng.)

Beijing is in northern China.

2. Say there is a tall tree right in front of the house.

Show one natural answer

房子面前有一棵大树。 (Fángzi miànqián yǒu yì kē dà shù.)

There is a big tree in front of the house.

3. Say this city is in the southeast.

Show one natural answer

这座城市在国家的东南边。 (Zhè zuò chéngshì zài guójiā de dōngnán biān.)

This city is in the southeast of the country.

4 · Read in context: North and South

中国的北方和南方在饮食、天气上都有一些不同。北方人常吃面食，南方人更习惯吃米饭。 (Zhōngguó de běifāng hé nánfāng zài yǐnshí, tiānqì shàng dōu yǒu yìxiē bù tóng. Běifāng rén cháng chī miànshí, nánfāng rén gèng xíguàn chī mǐfàn.)
Northern and southern China have some differences in food and weather. Northerners often eat wheat-based foods, while southerners are more used to eating rice.

至于东方和西方文化，也有各自的特点。现在交通方便，到处都能尝到各地的美食。 (Zhìyú dōngfāng hé xīfāng wénhuà, yě yǒu gèzì de tèdiǎn. Xiànzài jiāotōng fāngbiàn, dàochù dōu néng chángdào gèdì de měishí.)
As for Eastern and Western cultures, each has its own features too. Now transportation is convenient, and you can taste regional delicacies everywhere.

Q1. What staple food difference is mentioned between northern and southern China?
Show answer
Northerners often eat wheat-based foods while southerners are more used to rice.
Q2. Why can you taste regional delicacies everywhere now?
Show answer
Because transportation is convenient.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Give simple compass directions from your home or school.
Show one possible response

从我家出门向西走，附近就有一个地铁站。 (Cóng wǒ jiā chūmén xiàng xī zǒu, fùjìn jiù yǒu yí ge dìtiězhàn.)

Walk west out of my home and there is a subway station nearby.

2. Say where two places are relative to each other using 中间 or 附近.
Show one possible response

图书馆在教学楼和体育馆中间，附近还有一家书店。 (Túshūguǎn zài jiàoxuélóu hé tǐyùguǎn zhōngjiān, fùjìn hái yǒu yì jiā shūdiàn.)

The library is between the classroom building and the gym, and there’s a bookstore nearby.

6 · Watch out

Chinese puts East/West first in intercardinal directions: 东北 (northeast), 东南 (southeast), 西北 (northwest), 西南 (southwest) — never *北东 or *南西.

Pronunciation clinic

方向 (fāngxiàng) pairs flat fāng with falling xiàng; 中间 (zhōngjiān) has two high flat first tones.

Comprehension

What two aspects are mentioned as different between northern and southern China?

Show answer
Food (diet) and weather.

The first sentence mentions 在饮食、天气上都有一些不同.

Production

Write two sentences giving directions using 向……走 and 附近 or 中间.

Show answer
向北走三分钟，你会看到一家银行，它就在超市和医院中间。

Use 向 + direction + verb and 在……中间.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which word means "northeast" in Mandarin?

a. 北东 (běidōng) — invalid order

b. 东北 (dōngběi) — northeast

c. 东南 (dōngnán) — southeast

Show answer
b

Chinese always orders 东/西 before 南/北: 东北.

Selection

Which word means "everywhere"?

a. 附近 (fùjìn) — nearby

b. 到处 (dàochù) — everywhere

c. 中间 (zhōngjiān) — middle

Show answer
b

到处 means everywhere.

Selection

Which word means "direction"?

a. 方向 (fāngxiàng) — direction

b. 面前 (miànqián) — in front of

c. 西方 (xīfāng) — the West

Show answer
a

方向 means direction.
