# Campus, Grades & Studying Abroad

Talk about school life, classes, grades, subjects like math and history, libraries, and studying abroad.

## Learning objectives

- Talk about school levels and organization: 初中, 年级, 班级, 校园, 校长, and 名单.

- Discuss subjects, grades, and places: 数学, 历史, 外语, 语言, 成绩, 黑板, and 图书馆.

- Talk about finishing school and studying abroad using 放学, 留学, and 留学生.

## 1. After Class in the Library

Two students talk about their semester grades and an international student in their class.

**A:** 放学以后，咱们去图书馆看看吧？ (Fàngxué yǐhòu, zánmen qù túshūguǎn kànkan ba?) — After school ends, let’s go check out the library?

**B:** 好啊，我还要查一下数学和历史的资料。 (Hǎo a, wǒ hái yào chá yíxià shùxué hé lìshǐ de zīliào.) — Sure, I also need to look up some math and history materials.

**A:** 对了，这学期我们班级新来了一位留学生。 (Duìle, zhè xuéqī wǒmen bānjí xīn lái le yí wèi liúxuéshēng.) — By the way, an international student newly joined our class this semester.

**B:** 我知道，他外语很好，而且上次考试成绩也很不错！ (Wǒ zhīdào, tā wàiyǔ hěn hǎo, érqiě shàng cì kǎoshì chéngjì yě hěn búcuò!) — I know—his foreign language is great, and his exam grades last time were really good too!

## 2. How the language works

### Talking about school grades and semesters

**Pattern:** 这 + 学期 / 年级 + 的 + 成绩 (zhè + xuéqī / niánjí + de + chéngjì)

*this + semester / grade + ’s + grades*

Combine 年级 (niánjí, grade level) or 学期 (xuéqī, semester) with 成绩 (chéngjì, grades/results).

**这学期我的数学成绩提高了很多。**
*Zhè xuéqī wǒ de shùxué chéngjì tígāo le hěn duō.*
My math grades improved a lot this semester.

**她现在上初中二年级。**
*Tā xiànzài shàng chūzhōng èr niánjí.*
She is in the second year of junior middle school now.

### Going abroad to study

**Pattern:** 去 + <Country> + 留学 (qù + <Country> + liúxué)

*go to + <Country> + study abroad*

Use 去 + country + 留学 (liúxué) for going abroad to study.

**明年他打算去中国留学。**
*Míngnián tā dǎsuàn qù Zhōngguó liúxué.*
Next year he plans to go to China to study abroad.

**校园里有很多来自各国的留学生。**
*Xiàoyuán lǐ yǒu hěn duō láizì gè guó de liúxuéshēng.*
On campus there are many international students from various countries.

## 3. Build new sentences

Talk about classes, teachers, and campus locations.

*Model:* **校长在校园里欢迎新来的同学。**
*Xiàozhǎng zài xiàoyuán lǐ huānyíng xīn lái de tóngxué.*
The principal welcomed the newly arrived students on campus.

**Cue 1:** Say the teacher wrote the name list on the blackboard.

*Model:* **老师把名单写在了黑板上。**
*Lǎoshī bǎ míngdān xiě zài le hēibǎn shàng.*
The teacher wrote the name list on the blackboard.

**Cue 2:** Say learning a foreign language is very interesting.

*Model:* **学习一门外语很有意思。**
*Xuéxí yì mén wàiyǔ hěn yǒu yìsi.*
Learning a foreign language is very interesting.

**Cue 3:** Say language is an important bridge for culture.

*Model:* **语言是了解文化的重要桥梁。**
*Yǔyán shì liǎojiě wénhuà de zhòngyào qiáoliáng.*
Language is an important bridge for understanding culture.

## 4. Read in context: New Semester on Campus

新学期开始了，校园里非常热闹。早上，校长在礼堂欢迎新同学，并公布了各年级的班级名单。 (Xīn xuéqī kāishǐ le, xiàoyuán lǐ fēicháng rènào. Zǎoshang, xiàozhǎng zài lǐtáng huānyíng xīn tóngxué, bìng gōngbù le gè niánjí de bānjí míngdān.)

The new semester has begun, and the campus is very lively. In the morning, the principal welcomed new students in the auditorium and announced the class rosters for each grade.

下午四点半放学以后，不少同学去图书馆看书，还有几位留学生在黑板前练习写汉字。 (Xiàwǔ sì diǎn bàn fàngxué yǐhòu, bùshǎo tóngxué qù túshūguǎn kànshū, hái yǒu jǐ wèi liúxuéshēng zài hēibǎn qián liànxí xiě Hànzì.)

After school ended at 4:30 p.m., quite a few students went to the library to read, and several international students practiced writing Chinese characters in front of the blackboard.

**Q1. What did the principal announce in the morning?** Suggested answer: The class rosters (名单) for each grade.

**Q2. What did the international students do after school?** Suggested answer: They practiced writing Chinese characters in front of the blackboard.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Say what subjects you like and what you do after school.

*Possible response:* **我喜欢历史和外语，放学后常去图书馆看书。**
*Wǒ xǐhuan lìshǐ hé wàiyǔ, fàngxué hòu cháng qù túshūguǎn kànshū.*
I like history and foreign languages, and after school I often go to the library to read.

**Speaking 2:** Talk about studying abroad or an international student you know.

*Possible response:* **我有一个朋友是留学生，他每天都很努力学习语言。**
*Wǒ yǒu yí ge péngyou shì liúxuéshēng, tā měitiān dōu hěn nǔlì xuéxí yǔyán.*
I have a friend who is an international student; he studies languages hard every day.

## 6. Watch out

放学 means finishing school for the day (like 下班 for work); finishing a single class is 下课.

**Pronunciation:** 图书馆 (túshūguǎn) has rising tú, flat shū, and low third-tone guǎn; 留学 (liúxué) is two rising second tones.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** Where did many students go after school ended at 4:30 p.m.?
**Answer:** They went to the library to read.. The passage states 放学以后，不少同学去图书馆看书.

**Production:** Write two sentences about your school or study experience using 学期, 成绩, or 图书馆.
**Answer:** 这学期我常去图书馆学习，数学成绩进步了不少。. Combine 学期, 图书馆, and 成绩 naturally.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "grades / academic results"?
- a. 成绩 (chéngjì) — grades
- b. 名单 (míngdān) — name list
- c. 年级 (niánjí) — grade level
**Answer:** a. 成绩 means grades or results.

**Selection:** Which word means "library"?
- a. 校园 (xiàoyuán) — campus
- b. 图书馆 (túshūguǎn) — library
- c. 黑板 (hēibǎn) — blackboard
**Answer:** b. 图书馆 is library.

**Selection:** Which verb means "finish school for the day"?
- a. 放学 (fàngxué) — finish school
- b. 留学 (liúxué) — study abroad
- c. 班级 (bānjí) — class
**Answer:** a. 放学 means finishing the school day.
