# Sports, Matches & Games

Talk about sports, matches, scoring points, gyms, tennis, badminton, games, zoos, and personal interests.

## Learning objectives

- Name sports and venues: 体育, 体育馆, 球场, 网球, 羽毛球, and 运动会.

- Talk about competitions and scoring: 比赛 and 得分.

- Discuss hobbies and recreation: 兴趣, 游戏, and 动物园.

## 1. After-Work Badminton

Two friends make plans to play badminton at the gym and talk about a school sports meet.

**A:** 下班以后咱们去体育馆打羽毛球吧？ (Xiàbān yǐhòu zánmen qù tǐyùguǎn dǎ yǔmáoqiú ba?) — After work shall we go to the gym to play badminton?

**B:** 好啊！不过我最近也对网球很有兴趣，球场那边空着吗？ (Hǎo a! Búguò wǒ zuìjìn yě duì wǎngqiú hěn yǒu xìngqù, qiúchǎng nàbiān kōng zhe ma?) — Sure! Though lately I’m also interested in tennis—is the court over there free?

**A:** 今天下午学校正在开运动会，球场可能有比赛。 (Jīntiān xiàwǔ xuéxiào zhèngzài kāi yùndònghuì, qiúchǎng kěnéng yǒu bǐsài.) — The school is holding a sports meet this afternoon, so there might be a match on the court.

**B:** 那咱们先去看比赛，看看哪个班得分最高！ (Nà zánmen xiān qù kàn bǐsài, kànkan nǎge bān défēn zuì gāo!) — Then let’s go watch the match first and see which class scores the highest!

## 2. How the language works

### Playing racquet and ball sports with 打

**Pattern:** 去 + <Venue> + 打 + 羽毛球 / 网球 (qù + <Venue> + dǎ + yǔmáoqiú / wǎngqiú)

*go + <Venue> + hit/play + badminton / tennis*

Use 打 (dǎ) for sports played with hands or racquets (打羽毛球, 打网球).

**我每个星期六都和爸爸去球场打网球。**
*Wǒ měi ge xīngqīliù dōu hé bàba qù qiúchǎng dǎ wǎngqiú.*
Every Saturday I go to the court with my dad to play tennis.

**她在大学时打羽毛球非常厉害。**
*Tā zài dàxué shí dǎ yǔmáoqiú fēicháng lìhai.*
She was very good at playing badminton in university.

### Expressing interest with 有兴趣

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 对 + <Topic> + 很 + 有兴趣 (<Subject> + duì + <Topic> + hěn + yǒu xìngqù)

*<Subject> + toward + <Topic> + very + have interest*

Say 对 + topic + 有兴趣 (or 没兴趣) to express what interests you.

**弟弟对体育比赛一直很有兴趣。**
*Dìdi duì tǐyù bǐsài yìzhí hěn yǒu xìngqù.*
My younger brother has always been very interested in sports matches.

**你对这个新游戏有兴趣吗？**
*Nǐ duì zhège xīn yóuxì yǒu xìngqù ma?*
Are you interested in this new game?

## 3. Build new sentences

Talk about sports meets, games, and weekend recreation.

*Model:* **运动会上，我们班的运动员得分最多。**
*Yùndònghuì shàng, wǒmen bān de yùndòngyuán défēn zuì duō.*
At the sports meet, our class’s athletes scored the most points.

**Cue 1:** Say you like playing fun games with friends.

*Model:* **周末我喜欢和朋友一起玩儿有趣的游戏。**
*Zhōumò wǒ xǐhuan hé péngyou yìqǐ wánr yǒuqù de yóuxì.*
On weekends I like playing fun games with friends.

**Cue 2:** Say the students went to the zoo on Friday.

*Model:* **星期五同学们一起去动物园玩儿了。**
*Xīngqīwǔ tóngxuémen yìqǐ qù dòngwùyuán wánr le.*
On Friday the classmates went to the zoo together.

**Cue 3:** Say there is a basketball match in the gym tonight.

*Model:* **今天晚上体育馆里有一场篮球比赛。**
*Jīntiān wǎnshang tǐyùguǎn lǐ yǒu yì chǎng lánqiú bǐsài.*
Tonight there is a basketball match in the gym.

## 4. Read in context: School Sports Meet

上星期五，我们学校举行了秋季运动会。早上八点，同学们都来到体育馆和旁边的球场。 (Shàng xīngqīwǔ, wǒmen xuéxiào jǔxíng le qiūjì yùndònghuì. Zǎoshang bā diǎn, tóngxuémen dōu láidào tǐyùguǎn hé pángbiān de qiúchǎng.)

Last Friday, our school held the autumn sports meet. At 8 a.m., the students all came to the gym and the court beside it.

上午有羽毛球和网球比赛，下午还有趣味游戏。我们班的同学配合得很好，最后总得分排在第一名！ (Shàngwǔ yǒu yǔmáoqiú hé wǎngqiú bǐsài, xiàwǔ hái yǒu qùwèi yóuxì. Wǒmen bān de tóngxué pèihé de hěn hǎo, zuìhòu zǒng défēn pái zài dì-yī míng!)

In the morning there were badminton and tennis matches, and in the afternoon fun games. Our classmates worked together well, and in the end our total score ranked first!

**Q1. What matches were held in the morning?** Suggested answer: Badminton and tennis matches.

**Q2. How did the narrator’s class place in the total score?** Suggested answer: First place.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Say what sport or game you have an interest in using 有兴趣.

*Possible response:* **我对打羽毛球很有兴趣，每周都会去体育馆打一次。**
*Wǒ duì dǎ yǔmáoqiú hěn yǒu xìngqù, měi zhōu dōu huì qù tǐyùguǎn dǎ yí cì.*
I’m very interested in playing badminton and go to the gym once a week.

**Speaking 2:** Talk about a match or sports event you watched or joined.

*Possible response:* **去年秋天我参加了学校的网球比赛，虽然没得第一，但是很开心。**
*Qùnián qiūtiān wǒ cānjiā le xuéxiào de wǎngqiú bǐsài, suīrán méi dé dì-yī, dànshì hěn kāixīn.*
Last autumn I joined the school tennis match; although I didn’t win first place, I was very happy.

## 6. Watch out

Say 对……有兴趣 (or 对……感兴趣), using the preposition 对 before the thing you are interested in.

**Pronunciation:** 羽毛球 (yǔmáoqiú) moves from third tone to second tone to second tone; 体育馆 (tǐyùguǎn) has third + fourth + third.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** What happened in the afternoon of the school sports meet?
**Answer:** There were fun games (趣味游戏).. The passage says 下午还有趣味游戏.

**Production:** Write two sentences about sports or hobbies using 羽毛球/网球 and 有兴趣.
**Answer:** 我对网球很有兴趣，周末常常去球场练习。. Use 对……有兴趣 and a sport/venue.

## Review and next step

- Name sports, courts, and gyms.

- Talk about matches, scores, and sports meets.

- Express personal interests with 有兴趣.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "badminton"?
- a. 网球 (wǎngqiú) — tennis
- b. 羽毛球 (yǔmáoqiú) — badminton
- c. 游戏 (yóuxì) — game
**Answer:** b. 羽毛球 literally means feather-ball (badminton).

**Selection:** Which word means "gymnasium / sports hall"?
- a. 体育馆 (tǐyùguǎn) — gymnasium
- b. 动物园 (dòngwùyuán) — zoo
- c. 球场 (qiúchǎng) — ball court
**Answer:** a. 体育馆 is a gymnasium or sports hall.

**Selection:** Which word means "score / points"?
- a. 比赛 (bǐsài) — match
- b. 兴趣 (xìngqù) — interest
- c. 得分 (défēn) — score
**Answer:** c. 得分 means score or points scored.
