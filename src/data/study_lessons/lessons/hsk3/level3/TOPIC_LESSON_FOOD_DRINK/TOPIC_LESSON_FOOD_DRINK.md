# Ordering, Snacks & Drinks

Order from a menu or takeout, talk about hunger, fullness, sweetness, freshness, fruit, drinks, and utensils.

## Learning objectives

- Order food and drinks using 菜单, 外卖, 方便面, 蛋糕, 冰激凌, 鸡, 矿泉水, 饮料, 酒, and 啤酒.

- Describe taste and appetite: 饿, 饱, 尝, 甜, 糖, 冰, and 新鲜.

- Name tableware and fruit: 碗, 筷子, 勺子, 西瓜, and 香蕉.

## 1. Ordering Lunch Together

Two roommates decide between cooking and ordering takeout.

**A:** 我好饿啊，今天不想吃方便面，咱们点外卖吧？ (Wǒ hǎo è a, jīntiān bù xiǎng chī fāngbiànmiàn, zánmen diǎn wàimài ba?) — I’m so hungry! I don’t want instant noodles today—shall we order takeout?

**B:** 好呀，你看看菜单，这家店的烤鸡很有名。 (Hǎo ya, nǐ kànkan càidān, zhè jiā diàn de kǎojī hěn yǒumíng.) — Sure, look at the menu—this place’s roast chicken is famous.

**A:** 那点一份烤鸡、两碗米饭，再来两瓶冰的矿泉水。 (Nà diǎn yí fèn kǎojī, liǎng wǎn mǐfàn, zài lái liǎng píng bīng de kuàngquánshuǐ.) — Then let’s order one roast chicken, two bowls of rice, and two bottles of cold mineral water.

**B:** 等外卖到了，别忘了拿筷子和勺子，饭后还可以吃点儿新鲜西瓜！ (Děng wàimài dào le, bié wàng le ná kuàizi hé sháozi, fànhòu hái kěyǐ chī diǎnr xīnxiān xīguā!) — When the delivery arrives, don’t forget chopsticks and spoons; after the meal we can eat some fresh watermelon!

## 2. How the language works

### Trying a taste with 尝

**Pattern:** 尝一尝 + <Food / Drink> (cháng yì cháng + <Food / Drink>)

*taste + <Food / Drink>*

Use 尝一尝 (cháng yì cháng) or 尝一下 to invite someone to taste food or drink.

**你尝一尝这块蛋糕，不会太甜。**
*Nǐ cháng yì cháng zhè kuài dàngāo, bú huì tài tián.*
Try a bite of this cake—it’s not too sweet.

**这串香蕉很新鲜，快来尝尝！**
*Zhè chuàn xiāngjiāo hěn xīnxiān, kuài lái chángchang!*
This bunch of bananas is very fresh—come taste some!

### Being hungry or full

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 饿了 / 吃饱了 (<Subject> + è le / chī bǎo le)

*<Subject> + is hungry / ate full*

饿 (è) and 饱 (bǎo) are stative verbs; use 吃饱了 to say you have eaten enough.

**我吃饱了，再来一杯饮料就够了。**
*Wǒ chī bǎo le, zài lái yì bēi yǐnliào jiù gòu le.*
I’m full; one more drink is enough.

**孩子玩儿了一上午，现在肯定饿了。**
*Háizi wánr le yí shàngwǔ, xiànzài kěndìng è le.*
The child played all morning and must be hungry now.

## 3. Build new sentences

Order drinks, snacks, and utensils.

*Model:* **请给我一碗热汤和一双筷子。**
*Qǐng gěi wǒ yì wǎn rè tāng hé yì shuāng kuàizi.*
Please give me a bowl of hot soup and a pair of chopsticks.

**Cue 1:** Say you don’t drink alcohol or beer.

*Model:* **我不喝酒，也不喝啤酒。**
*Wǒ bù hē jiǔ, yě bù hē píjiǔ.*
I don’t drink liquor, and I don’t drink beer either.

**Cue 2:** Say children like sweet ice cream.

*Model:* **小朋友很喜欢甜甜的冰激凌。**
*Xiǎo péngyou hěn xǐhuan tiántián de bīngjīlíng.*
Children really like sweet ice cream.

**Cue 3:** Say this tea has too much sugar.

*Model:* **这杯茶里的糖放得太多了。**
*Zhè bēi chá lǐ de táng fàng de tài duō le.*
Too much sugar was put in this cup of tea.

## 4. Read in context: Picnic by the River

星期天天气很好，我们带了蛋糕、香蕉、西瓜和几瓶饮料去河边野餐。 (Xīngqītiān tiānqì hěn hǎo, wǒmen dài le dàngāo, xiāngjiāo, xīguā hé jǐ píng yǐnliào qù hébiān yěcān.)

The weather on Sunday was great; we brought cake, bananas, watermelon, and several bottles of drinks for a picnic by the river.

大家坐在一起吃东西、聊天儿，还尝了朋友带来的新鲜水果。虽然没有买冰激凌，但每个人都吃得很饱、很开心。 (Dàjiā zuò zài yìqǐ chī dōngxi, liáotiānr, hái cháng le péngyou dài lái de xīnxiān shuǐguǒ. Suīrán méiyǒu mǎi bīngjīlíng, dàn měi ge rén dōu chī de hěn bǎo, hěn kāixīn.)

Everyone sat together eating and chatting, and tasted the fresh fruit our friend brought. Although we didn’t buy ice cream, everyone ate until full and felt very happy.

**Q1. What food and drinks did they bring to the picnic?** Suggested answer: Cake, bananas, watermelon, and several bottles of drinks.

**Q2. How did everyone feel at the end?** Suggested answer: Full and happy.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Order a meal and a drink from a menu or takeout.

*Possible response:* **我要点一份鸡肉面和一瓶矿泉水，谢谢。**
*Wǒ yào diǎn yí fèn jīròu miàn hé yì píng kuàngquánshuǐ, xièxie.*
I’d like to order one chicken noodles and a bottle of mineral water, thanks.

**Speaking 2:** Describe your favorite fruit or dessert using 新鲜 or 甜.

*Possible response:* **夏天我最喜欢吃冰西瓜，又新鲜又甜。**
*Xiàtiān wǒ zuì xǐhuan chī bīng xīguā, yòu xīnxiān yòu tián.*
In summer I like eating chilled watermelon best—both fresh and sweet.

## 6. Watch out

Chopsticks always come in pairs, so count them with 一双筷子 ("a pair of chopsticks"), not *一个筷子.

**Pronunciation:** 筷子 (kuàizi) and 勺子 (sháozi) both end in a neutral-tone 子; 冰激凌 (bīngjīlíng) has two flat first tones followed by a rising second tone.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** What did they NOT buy for the picnic in the reading?
**Answer:** Ice cream (冰激凌).. The passage says 虽然没有买冰激凌.

**Production:** Write two sentences about ordering food or eating fruit using 外卖, 尝, or 饱.
**Answer:** 我点了一份外卖，刚尝了两口就觉得很香，吃完以后很饱。. Use 外卖, 尝, and 饱 naturally.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "menu"?
- a. 菜单 (càidān) — menu
- b. 外卖 (wàimài) — takeout
- c. 蛋糕 (dàngāo) — cake
**Answer:** a. 菜单 is menu.

**Selection:** Which word means "chopsticks"?
- a. 勺子 (sháozi) — spoon
- b. 筷子 (kuàizi) — chopsticks
- c. 碗 (wǎn) — bowl
**Answer:** b. 筷子 means chopsticks.

**Selection:** Which word means "fresh"?
- a. 甜 (tián) — sweet
- b. 饱 (bǎo) — full
- c. 新鲜 (xīnxiān) — fresh
**Answer:** c. 新鲜 means fresh.
