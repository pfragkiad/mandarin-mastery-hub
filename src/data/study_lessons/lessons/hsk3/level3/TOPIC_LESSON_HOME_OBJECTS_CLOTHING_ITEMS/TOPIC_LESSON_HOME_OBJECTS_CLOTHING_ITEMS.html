Clothes & Everyday Belongings

Clothes, shoes, bags, and everyday personal items

home-objects · #clothing · #everyday-items · #belongings

Learning objectives

  • Name clothing and footwear: 衬衫, 大衣, 短裤, 裙子, 上衣, and 鞋.
  • Name everyday personal and household items: 灯, 盘子, 瓶子, 伞, 箱子, 牙刷, 雨衣, 纸, and 卡.
  • Pair clothing and objects with appropriate classifiers like 件, 条, 双, 把, and 张.

1 · Packing a Suitcase

Xiao Mei checks what clothes and daily items to put in her suitcase before a trip.

A: 你的箱子收拾好了吗？明天要早起呢。 (Nǐ de xiāngzi shōushi hǎo le ma? Míngtiān yào zǎoqǐ ne.)
Have you finished packing your suitcase? We have to get up early tomorrow.

B: 快了！我带了两件衬衫、一条裙子和一双舒服的鞋。 (Kuài le! Wǒ dài le liǎng jiàn chènshān, yì tiáo qúnzi hé yì shuāng shūfu de xié.)
Almost! I packed two shirts, a skirt, and a pair of comfortable shoes.

A: 听说那边会下雨，别忘了带伞或者雨衣，还有牙刷！ (Tīngshuō nàbiān huì xià yǔ, bié wàng le dài sǎn huòzhě yǔyī, hái yǒu yáshuā!)
I heard it might rain there—don’t forget an umbrella or raincoat, and your toothbrush!

B: 都放进去了，门卡也装在包里了，关灯睡觉吧！ (Dōu fàng jìnqù le, ménkǎ yě zhuāng zài bāo lǐ le, guān dēng shuìjiào ba!)
All packed, and the key card is in my bag too—let’s turn off the lamp and sleep!

2 · How the language works

Using the right classifier for clothes

一 + 件 / 条 / 双 + <Clothing> (yí + jiàn / yì + tiáo / yì + shuāng + <Clothing>)

one + piece / strip / pair + <Clothing>

Use 件 for tops/coats (衬衫, 大衣, 上衣), 条 for skirts/shorts (裙子, 短裤), and 双 for shoes (鞋).

她穿了一件白色衬衫和一条蓝裙子。 (Tā chuān le yí jiàn báisè chènshān hé yì tiáo lán qúnzi.)

She wore a white shirt and a blue skirt.

天气冷了，出门要穿大衣。 (Tiānqì lěng le, chūmén yào chuān dàyī.)

The weather got cold; you should wear a coat when going out.

Everyday containers and tools

一 + 把 / 张 / 个 + <Object> (yì + bǎ / yì + zhāng / yí + ge + <Object>)

one + handle / sheet / unit + <Object>

Use 把 for umbrellas (一把伞), 个 for plates/bottles/boxes, and 张 for paper and cards.

桌子上放着一个盘子和两个瓶子。 (Zhuōzi shàng fàng zhe yí ge pánzi hé liǎng ge píngzi.)

On the table are a plate and two bottles.

请给我一张纸，我要写个号码。 (Qǐng gěi wǒ yì zhāng zhǐ, wǒ yào xiě ge hàomǎ.)

Please give me a sheet of paper; I need to write a number.

3 · Build new sentences

Talk about clothes and personal items with matching classifiers.

Model

夏天大家常穿短裤和凉快的上衣。 (Xiàtiān dàjiā cháng chuān duǎnkù hé liángkuai de shàngyī.)

In summer people often wear shorts and cool tops.

1. Say you forgot to bring an umbrella when it started raining.

Show one natural answer

下雨了，我忘了带一把伞。 (Xià yǔ le, wǒ wàng le dài yì bǎ sǎn.)

It’s raining, and I forgot to bring an umbrella.

2. Say you turned on the desk lamp to read.

Show one natural answer

我打开台灯开始看书。 (Wǒ dǎkāi táidēng kāishǐ kànshū.)

I turned on the desk lamp and started reading.

3. Say you bought a new raincoat for cycling.

Show one natural answer

我买了一件新雨衣，骑车时用。 (Wǒ mǎi le yí jiàn xīn yǔyī, qí chē shí yòng.)

I bought a new raincoat to use when cycling.

4 · Read in context: Getting Ready for a Rainy Morning

早上起床以后，小王先刷牙洗脸，然后打开衣柜挑衣服。外面有点儿凉，他穿了一件长袖上衣和一条深色长裤。 (Zǎoshang qǐchuáng yǐhòu, Xiǎo Wáng xiān shuāyá xǐliǎn, ránhòu dǎkāi yīguì tiāo yīfu. Wàimiàn yǒudiǎnr liáng, tā chuān le yí jiàn chángxiù shàngyī hé yì tiáo shēnsè chángkù.)
After getting up in the morning, Xiao Wang brushed his teeth and washed his face first, then opened the closet to pick clothes. It was a bit cool outside, so he put on a long-sleeved top and dark trousers.

临出门前，他看见天空阴阴的，就顺手拿了一把伞，还把公交卡放进口袋，最后关上灯走了出去。 (Lín chūmén qián, tā kànjiàn tiānkōng yīnyīn de, jiù shùnshǒu ná le yì bǎ sǎn, hái bǎ gōngjiāokǎ fàng jìn kǒudài, zuìhòu guān shàng dēng zǒu le chūqù.)
Right before heading out, he saw the sky was overcast, so he grabbed an umbrella, put his transit card in his pocket, and finally turned off the light and walked out.

Q1. Why did Xiao Wang grab an umbrella before leaving?
Show answer
Because the sky looked overcast.
Q2. What did he put in his pocket?
Show answer
His transit card (公交卡).

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Describe what you are wearing today using 衬衫, 上衣, 裙子, 短裤, or 鞋.
Show one possible response

今天我穿了一件蓝衬衫和一双运动鞋。 (Jīntiān wǒ chuān le yí jiàn lán chènshān hé yì shuāng yùndòngxié.)

Today I am wearing a blue shirt and a pair of sneakers.

2. Name three everyday items in your room or bag.
Show one possible response

我的包里有一把伞、一张纸和一个水瓶子。 (Wǒ de bāo lǐ yǒu yì bǎ sǎn, yì zhāng zhǐ hé yí ge shuǐ píngzi.)

In my bag there is an umbrella, a sheet of paper, and a water bottle.

6 · Watch out

Use 件 for shirts/coats (衬衫, 大衣, 雨衣), 条 for skirts/pants (裙子, 短裤), and 把 for umbrellas (伞).

Pronunciation clinic

衬衫 (chènshān) moves from fourth tone to first tone; 牙刷 (yáshuā) moves from second tone to first tone.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

What did Xiao Wang do right before walking out the door?

Show answer
He grabbed an umbrella, put his transit card in his pocket, and turned off the light.

The second paragraph lists these three actions before he walked out.

Production

Write two sentences describing what you packed in your suitcase using 箱子 and two clothing items.

Show answer
我的箱子里装了两件衬衫、一件大衣和一双鞋。

Use 箱子 with clothing items and classifiers.

Review and next step

  • Name clothes and footwear with right classifiers.
  • Name everyday items like 伞, 箱子, 盘子, and 瓶子.
  • Talk about packing and getting ready.
