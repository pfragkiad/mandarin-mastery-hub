# Appearance, Colors & Comparisons

Describe physical appearance, size, colors, cleanliness, quietness, and compare similarities or differences.

## Learning objectives

- Describe appearance and physical properties: 矮, 短, 胖, 瘦, 老, 年轻, 旧, 大小, 干净, 脏, 蓝, and 黄色.

- Describe quietness and degree: 安静, 比较, and 更.

- Compare things using 一样, 不同, and 差不多.

## 1. Comparing Old and New Apartments

Two friends compare their old dorm room with a newly rented room.

**A:** 你新租的房间和以前那个大小差不多吗？ (Nǐ xīn zū de fángjiān hé yǐqián nàge dàxiǎo chàbuduō ma?) — Is the size of your newly rented room about the same as the old one?

**B:** 大小差不多，不过新房间更干净，也更安静。 (Dàxiǎo chàbuduō, búguò xīn fángjiān gèng gānjìng, yě gèng ānjìng.) — About the same size, though the new room is cleaner and quieter.

**A:** 墙是什么颜色的？还是蓝的吗？ (Qiáng shì shénme yánsè de? Háishi lán de ma?) — What color are the walls? Still blue?

**B:** 这次不同了，是淡黄色的，看起来比较亮堂。 (Zhè cì bù tóng le, shì dàn huángsè de, kàn qǐlái bǐjiào liàngtang.) — It’s different this time—light yellow, so it looks fairly bright.

## 2. How the language works

### Comparing sameness with 跟 / 和……一样

**Pattern:** <A> + 和 + <B> + 一样 + (<Adjective>) (<A> + hé + <B> + yíyàng + (<Adjective>))

*<A> + and + <B> + same + (<Adjective>)*

Use A + 跟 / 和 + B + 一样 (+ adj) to say two things are the same; negate with 不一样.

**这双鞋的颜色和那双不一样。**
*Zhè shuāng xié de yánsè hé nà shuāng bù yíyàng.*
The color of this pair of shoes is not the same as that pair.

**弟弟现在长得和哥哥一样高了。**
*Dìdi xiànzài zhǎng de hé gēge yíyàng gāo le.*
The younger brother has now grown as tall as his older brother.

### Intensifying with 比较 and 更

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 比较 / 更 + <Adjective> (<Subject> + bǐjiào / gèng + <Adjective>)

*<Subject> + relatively / even more + <Adjective>*

比较 (bǐjiào) means "fairly / relatively"; 更 (gèng) means "even more" (often in comparisons).

**她比去年瘦了一点儿，看起来更年轻了。**
*Tā bǐ qùnián shòu le yìdiǎnr, kàn qǐlái gèng niánqīng le.*
She is a bit slimmer than last year and looks even younger.

**这件旧外套比较短，换那件长的吧。**
*Zhè jiàn jiù wàitào bǐjiào duǎn, huàn nà jiàn cháng de ba.*
This old jacket is relatively short—switch to that long one.

## 3. Build new sentences

Describe physical traits, cleanliness, and colors.

*Model:* **那只小狗有点儿胖，腿也短短的。**
*Nà zhī xiǎo gǒu yǒudiǎnr pàng, tuǐ yě duǎnduǎn de.*
That little dog is a bit chubby, and its legs are short too.

**Cue 1:** Say the children’s clothes got dirty from playing.

*Model:* **孩子们玩儿得衣服都脏了。**
*Háizimen wánr de yīfu dōu zāng le.*
The children played until their clothes got dirty.

**Cue 2:** Say that tree isn’t tall—it is quite short.

*Model:* **那棵树不高，比较矮。**
*Nà kē shù bù gāo, bǐjiào ǎi.*
That tree isn’t tall—it’s fairly short.

**Cue 3:** Say this old book is different from the new edition.

*Model:* **这本旧书和新版有很多不同。**
*Zhè běn jiù shū hé xīn bǎn yǒu hěn duō bù tóng.*
This old book has many differences from the new edition.

## 4. Read in context: A Clean and Quiet Reading Room

学校新开了一间阅览室，里面非常安静，桌子和地板总是擦得很干净。墙上挂着几幅蓝天和黄色花朵的画。 (Xuéxiào xīn kāi le yì jiān yuèlǎnshì, lǐmiàn fēicháng ānjìng, zhuōzi hé dìbǎn zǒngshì cā de hěn gānjìng. Qiángshàng guà zhe jǐ fú lántiān hé huángsè huāduǒ de huà.)

The school newly opened a reading room; inside it is very quiet, and the tables and floor are always wiped clean. Paintings of blue skies and yellow flowers hang on the wall.

每天下课以后，不管年轻的学生还是老教师，都喜欢来这里看书。大家觉得这里比宿舍更适合专心学习。 (Měitiān xiàkè yǐhòu, bùguǎn niánqīng de xuéshēng háishi lǎo jiàoshī, dōu xǐhuan lái zhèlǐ kànshū. Dàjiā juéde zhèlǐ bǐ sùshè gèng shìhé zhuānxīn xuéxí.)

Every day after class, both young students and older teachers like coming here to read. Everyone feels it is even more suitable for focused study than the dorm.

**Q1. What paintings hang on the wall of the reading room?** Suggested answer: Paintings of blue skies and yellow flowers.

**Q2. Why do people prefer studying there over the dorm?** Suggested answer: Because it is quieter, clean, and better suited for focused study.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Compare two seasons, rooms, or cities using 一样, 不同, or 更.

*Possible response:* **上海和北京不一样，上海冬天更暖和，不过两个城市都很热闹。**
*Shànghǎi hé Běijīng bù yíyàng, Shànghǎi dōngtiān gèng nuǎnhuo, búguò liǎng ge chéngshì dōu hěn rènào.*
Shanghai and Beijing are not the same; Shanghai is warmer in winter, though both cities are lively.

**Speaking 2:** Describe a room or object using 干净, 大小, and a color.

*Possible response:* **我的房间大小刚刚好，被子是蓝色的，每天都收拾得很干净。**
*Wǒ de fángjiān dàxiǎo gānggāng hǎo, bèizi shì lán sè de, měitiān dōu shōushi de hěn gānjìng.*
My room is just the right size, my quilt is blue, and I tidy it clean every day.

## 6. Watch out

矮 (ǎi) is for height of people/trees/buildings; 短 (duǎn) is for length of objects, hair, or time. Never say *短人 for a short person.

**Pronunciation:** 安静 (ānjìng) moves from flat ān to falling jìng; 差不多 (chàbuduō) has fourth + neutral + first.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** Who likes coming to the new reading room after class?
**Answer:** Both young students and older teachers.. The passage says 不管年轻的学生还是老教师，都喜欢来这里看书.

**Production:** Write two sentences comparing two things using 一样 or 不同 and 更.
**Answer:** 这两件衣服的样式差不多，但蓝色的那件更干净好看。. Use 差不多/一样/不同 with 更.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which adjective means "short (in height)"?
- a. 短 (duǎn) — short in length
- b. 矮 (ǎi) — short in height
- c. 瘦 (shòu) — slim
**Answer:** b. 矮 describes vertical height.

**Selection:** Which adjective means "clean"?
- a. 干净 (gānjìng) — clean
- b. 脏 (zāng) — dirty
- c. 安静 (ānjìng) — quiet
**Answer:** a. 干净 means clean.

**Selection:** Which phrase means "almost / roughly the same"?
- a. 不同 (bù tóng) — different
- b. 差不多 (chàbuduō) — almost / about the same
- c. 一样 (yíyàng) — the same
**Answer:** b. 差不多 means almost or approximately the same.
