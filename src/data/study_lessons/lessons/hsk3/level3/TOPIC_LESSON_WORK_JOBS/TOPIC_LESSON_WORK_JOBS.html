At the Office & Taking Leave

Meetings, colleagues, managers, and taking leave

work-jobs · #office · #meetings · #workplace

Learning objectives

  • Name workplace people and places: 办公室, 同事, 经理, 司机, and the suffix 员.
  • Discuss meetings using 会, 会议, and 开会.
  • Ask for or take time off using 请假 and 休假.

1 · Before the Morning Meeting

Two colleagues talk in the office before a meeting with their manager.

A: 请问，李经理在办公室吗？ (Qǐngwèn, Lǐ jīnglǐ zài bàngōngshì ma?)
Excuse me, is Manager Li in the office?

B: 他去会议室开会了，一会儿才回来。 (Tā qù huìyìshì kāihuì le, yíhuìr cái huílai.)
He went to the meeting room for a meeting and won’t be back for a bit.

A: 那我等他开完会再找他，我想下周请假两天。 (Nà wǒ děng tā kāi wán huì zài zhǎo tā, wǒ xiǎng xià zhōu qǐngjià liǎng tiān.)
Then I’ll look for him after his meeting ends; I’d like to ask for two days off next week.

B: 没问题，不过记得提前告诉部门的同事。 (Méi wèntí, búguò jìde tíqián gàosu bùmén de tóngshì.)
No problem, just remember to tell your department colleagues in advance.

2 · How the language works

Holding or attending a meeting

开 + <Duration / Result> + 会 (kāi + <Duration / Result> + huì)

hold + <Duration / Result> + meeting

开会 (kāihuì) is a separable verb; insert duration or completion words like 完 between 开 and 会.

我们上午开了两个小时的会。 (Wǒmen shàngwǔ kāi le liǎng ge xiǎoshí de huì.)

We had a two-hour meeting this morning.

经理一开完会就给我打电话了。 (Jīnglǐ yì kāi wán huì jiù gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà le.)

As soon as the manager finished the meeting, he called me.

Requesting leave for a period

向 + <Person> + 请假 + <Duration> (xiàng + <Person> + qǐngjià + <Duration>)

to + <Person> + ask for leave + <Duration>

State the reason or length of time off with 请假 (qǐngjià); duration follows 请假.

小刘感冒了，今天向经理请假一天。 (Xiǎo Liú gǎnmào le, jīntiān xiàng jīnglǐ qǐngjià yì tiān.)

Xiao Liu caught a cold and asked the manager for one day of leave today.

下个月我想休假一个星期。 (Xià ge yuè wǒ xiǎng xiūjià yí ge xīngqī.)

Next month I want to take a one-week vacation.

3 · Build new sentences

Practice workplace requests and scheduling.

Model

我明天上午要和同事一起去开会。 (Wǒ míngtiān shàngwǔ yào hé tóngshì yìqǐ qù kāihuì.)

Tomorrow morning I need to go to a meeting with my colleagues.

1. Say the driver is waiting outside the office.

Show one natural answer

司机正在办公室外面等着呢。 (Sījī zhèngzài bàngōngshì wàimiàn děng zhe ne.)

The driver is waiting outside the office.

2. Say the service staff are very friendly.

Show one natural answer

这里的服务员非常热情。 (Zhèlǐ de fúwùyuán fēicháng rèqíng.)

The service staff here are very warm and friendly.

3. Say there is an important meeting this afternoon.

Show one natural answer

今天下午有一个重要的会议。 (Jīntiān xiàwǔ yǒu yí ge zhòngyào de huìyì.)

There is an important meeting this afternoon.

4 · Read in context: A Busy Friday

星期五上午，张经理在办公室里看了看今天的安排。九点半有一个重要的会议，所有同事都要参加。 (Xīngqīwǔ shàngwǔ, Zhāng jīnglǐ zài bàngōngshì lǐ kàn le kan jīntiān de ānpái. Jiǔ diǎn bàn yǒu yí ge zhòngyào de huìyì, suǒyǒu tóngshì dōu yào cānjiā.)
On Friday morning, Manager Zhang checked today’s schedule in his office. At 9:30 there is an important meeting that all colleagues must attend.

开完会以后，他还要见一位新来的司机，因为下周他要出差，之后才能休假。 (Kāi wán huì yǐhòu, tā hái yào jiàn yí wèi xīn lái de sījī, yīnwèi xià zhōu tā yào chūchāi, zhīhòu cái néng xiūjià.)
After the meeting, he also needs to meet a newly arrived driver, because next week he has a business trip and only afterward can he take leave.

Q1. When is the important meeting?
Show answer
At 9:30 on Friday morning.
Q2. Who does Manager Zhang need to meet after the meeting?
Show answer
A newly arrived driver.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Tell a colleague that you are going to a meeting or asking for leave.
Show one possible response

我下午两点去开会，四点回办公室。 (Wǒ xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn qù kāihuì, sì diǎn huí bàngōngshì.)

I am going to a meeting at 2 p.m. and returning to the office at 4 p.m.

2. Say what job someone has using 服务, 经理, or 司机.
Show one possible response

我哥哥是一家公司的经理，他每天工作很忙。 (Wǒ gēge shì yì jiā gōngsī de jīnglǐ, tā měitiān gōngzuò hěn máng.)

My older brother is a manager at a company; he is very busy at work every day.

6 · Watch out

请假 means "ask for leave" (from work or school), while 休假 means "be on leave / take vacation".

Pronunciation clinic

办公室 (bàngōngshì) ends with a falling fourth-tone shì; 会议 (huìyì) has two fourth tones in a row.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

When can Manager Zhang take leave according to the passage?

Show answer
Only after his business trip next week.

The passage states 下周他要出差，之后才能休假.

Production

Write a short message to your manager asking for one day of leave.

Show answer
王经理，我今天有点儿不舒服，想向您请假一天。

Address the manager politely and use 请假一天.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which verb means "to ask for leave"?

a. 开会 (kāihuì) — hold a meeting

b. 请假 (qǐngjià) — ask for leave

c. 服务 (fúwù) — serve

Show answer
b

请假 means requesting time off.

Selection

Which word means "colleague"?

a. 经理 (jīnglǐ) — manager

b. 司机 (sījī) — driver

c. 同事 (tóngshì) — colleague

Show answer
c

同事 refers to a coworker.

Selection

Which suffix forms words for staff or members (like 服务员)?

a. 员 (yuán) — member / staff

b. 会 (huì) — meeting

c. 室 (shì) — room

Show answer
a

员 is a common suffix for roles and members.
