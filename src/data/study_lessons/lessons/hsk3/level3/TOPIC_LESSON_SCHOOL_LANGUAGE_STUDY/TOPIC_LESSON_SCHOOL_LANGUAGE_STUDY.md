# Notes, Dictionaries & Practice

Talk about study tools, taking notes, reviewing lessons, solving hard problems, and raising your language level.

## Learning objectives

- Name study tools: 笔记, 笔记本, 词典, 字典, 课本, 课文, and 铅笔.

- Describe study actions: 记, 复习, 练, 练习, and doing 作业.

- Evaluate progress and difficulty with 常用, 常见, 句子, 难题, 水平, and 提高.

## 1. Preparing for Tomorrow’s Quiz

Two classmates review a lesson text and share study tips.

**A:** 今天的作业好多啊，你复习完第三课的课文了吗？ (Jīntiān de zuòyè hǎoduō a, nǐ fùxí wán dì-sān kè de kèwén le ma?) — There’s so much homework today—have you finished reviewing Lesson 3’s text?

**B:** 刚复习完。我用铅笔在课本上记了些常用的句子。 (Gāng fùxí wán. Wǒ yòng qiānbǐ zài kèběn shàng jì le xiē cháng yòng de jùzi.) — Just finished. I used a pencil to note down some commonly used sentences in the textbook.

**A:** 这个办法真好！遇到不认识的字怎么办？ (Zhège bànfǎ zhēn hǎo! Yùdào bú rènshi de zì zěnme bàn?) — That’s a great method! What do you do when you run into characters you don’t know?

**B:** 我会查字典或者词典，再多做练习，这样汉语水平提高得很快。 (Wǒ huì chá zìdiǎn huòzhě cídiǎn, zài duō zuò liànxí, zhèyàng Hànyǔ shuǐpíng tígāo de hěn kuài.) — I look them up in a character or word dictionary and do more exercises—that way my Chinese level improves fast.

## 2. How the language works

### Raising your level with 提高

**Pattern:** 提高 + <Skill> + 水平 (tígāo + <Skill> + shuǐpíng)

*raise + <Skill> + level*

Pair 提高 (tígāo) with 水平 (shuǐpíng) or 成绩 to talk about improving a skill or score.

**多听多说能提高口语水平。**
*Duō tīng duō shuō néng tígāo kǒuyǔ shuǐpíng.*
Listening and speaking more can improve your spoken language level.

**每天写日记帮助我提高了写作水平。**
*Měitiān xiě rìjì bāngzhù wǒ tígāo le xiězuò shuǐpíng.*
Writing a diary every day helped me raise my writing level.

### Noting things down in a notebook

**Pattern:** 把 + <Word / Sentence> + 记在 + 笔记本上 (bǎ + <Word / Sentence> + jì zài + bǐjìběn shàng)

*take + <Word / Sentence> + note on + notebook*

Use 把 + notes + 记在 + 笔记本上 or 记笔记 to talk about taking study notes.

**我把常见的难题都记在了笔记本上。**
*Wǒ bǎ cháng jiàn de nántí dōu jì zài le bǐjìběn shàng.*
I wrote down all the common difficult problems in my notebook.

**上课时他一边听一边认真记笔记。**
*Shàngkè shí tā yìbiān tīng yìbiān rènzhēn jì bǐjì.*
During class he listened while carefully taking notes.

## 3. Build new sentences

Talk about study tools, practice, and improvement.

*Model:* **每天晚上我都先做作业，再复习课文。**
*Měitiān wǎnshang wǒ dōu xiān zuò zuòyè, zài fùxí kèwén.*
Every evening I do my homework first, then review the lesson text.

**Cue 1:** Say you need to buy a new notebook and two pencils.

*Model:* **我要买一个新笔记本和两支铅笔。**
*Wǒ yào mǎi yí ge xīn bǐjìběn hé liǎng zhī qiānbǐ.*
I need to buy a new notebook and two pencils.

**Cue 2:** Say this is a commonly used Chinese sentence.

*Model:* **这是一个非常常用的汉语句子。**
*Zhè shì yí ge fēicháng cháng yòng de Hànyǔ jùzi.*
This is a very commonly used Chinese sentence.

**Cue 3:** Say you practice speaking Chinese every morning.

*Model:* **我每天早上都练说汉语。**
*Wǒ měitiān zǎoshang dōu liàn shuō Hànyǔ.*
I practice speaking Chinese every morning.

## 4. Read in context: How to Study Vocabulary Effectively

学外语的时候，光看课本是不够的。每学完一篇课文，最好用词典查一查生词的用法。 (Xué wàiyǔ de shíhou, guāng kàn kèběn shì búgòu de. Měi xué wán yì piān kèwén, zuìhǎo yòng cídiǎn chá yì chá shēngcí de yòngfǎ.)

When studying a foreign language, just reading the textbook is not enough. After finishing each lesson text, it is best to check the usage of new words in a dictionary.

然后把常用的句子记在笔记本里，周末再认真复习一遍。坚持练习，语言水平自然会慢慢提高。 (Ránhòu bǎ cháng yòng de jùzi jì zài bǐjìběn lǐ, zhōumò zài rènzhēn fùxí yí biàn. Jiānchí liànxí, yǔyán shuǐpíng zìrán huì mànmàn tígāo.)

Then write the commonly used sentences in a notebook, and review them carefully once more on the weekend. Keep practicing, and your language level will naturally rise.

**Q1. What should you do after finishing each lesson text?** Suggested answer: Check the usage of new words in a dictionary and write commonly used sentences in a notebook.

**Q2. What happens if you keep practicing and reviewing?** Suggested answer: Your language level will naturally improve.

**Speaking 2:** Explain how you solve a hard problem (难题) in your homework.

*Possible response:* **遇到难题时，我会先复习笔记，再请教老师。**
*Yùdào nántí shí, wǒ huì xiān fùxí bǐjì, zài qǐngjiào lǎoshī.*
When I run into a hard problem, I review my notes first and then ask the teacher.

## 6. Watch out

字典 is a character dictionary (by single 字), while 词典 is a word/phrase dictionary (by 词). Also, 笔记 means "notes", whereas 笔记本 means "notebook" (or laptop).

**Pronunciation:** 复习 (fùxí) moves from fourth tone to second tone; 练习 (liànxí) also moves from fourth tone to second tone.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "homework"?
- a. 作业 (zuòyè) — homework
- b. 课文 (kèwén) — lesson text
- c. 难题 (nántí) — hard problem
**Answer:** a. 作业 means homework or assignment.

**Selection:** Which word means "to review / revise"?
- a. 练习 (liànxí) — practice
- b. 复习 (fùxí) — review
- c. 提高 (tígāo) — improve
**Answer:** b. 复习 means to review previously learned material.

**Selection:** Which word means "notebook"?
- a. 笔记 (bǐjì) — notes
- b. 笔记本 (bǐjìběn) — notebook
- c. 词典 (cídiǎn) — dictionary
**Answer:** b. 笔记本 is the notebook itself.
