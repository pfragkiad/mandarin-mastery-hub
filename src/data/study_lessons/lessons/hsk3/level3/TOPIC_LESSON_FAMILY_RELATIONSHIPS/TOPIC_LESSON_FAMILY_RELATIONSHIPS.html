Family, Guests & Neighbors

Relatives, neighbors, guests, and caring for one another

family-relationships · #family · #guests · #relationships

Learning objectives

  • Refer to relatives and social circles such as 阿姨, 叔叔, 夫妻, 姐妹, and 邻居.
  • Talk about visiting, bringing gifts, and meeting with 见面, 做客, and 礼物.
  • Express care and encounters using 关心, 照顾, 遇到, and 遇见.

1 · Visiting the Neighbors

Xiao Lin and her husband prepare a small gift to visit their neighbors.

A: 今天晚上咱们去张叔叔和李阿姨家做客吧。 (Jīntiān wǎnshang zánmen qù Zhāng shūshu hé Lǐ āyí jiā zuòkè ba.)
Tonight let’s go visit Uncle Zhang and Auntie Li at their home.

B: 好啊，他们这对夫妻平时很关心咱们，咱们买点儿礼物带过去。 (Hǎo a, tāmen zhè duì fūqī píngshí hěn guānxīn zánmen, zánmen mǎi diǎnr lǐwù dài guòqù.)
Sure, that couple usually cares a lot about us—let’s buy a small gift to bring over.

A: 昨天我在楼下遇见李阿姨了，她说她妹妹也要一块儿来。 (Zuótiān wǒ zài lóuxià yùjiàn Lǐ āyí le, tā shuō tā mèimei yě yào yíkuàir lái.)
Yesterday I ran into Auntie Li downstairs, and she said her younger sister is coming too.

B: 太好了，她们姐妹好久没见面了，咱们早点儿去帮忙照顾客人吧。 (Tài hǎo le, tāmen jiěmèi hǎojiǔ méi jiànmiàn le, zánmen zǎodiǎnr qù bāngmáng zhàogù kèrén ba.)
Great! Those sisters haven’t seen each other in a long time—let’s go early to help look after the guests.

2 · How the language works

Meeting up or running into someone

<Person A> + 和 + <Person B> + 见面 (<Person A> + hé + <Person B> + jiànmiàn)

<Person A> + and + <Person B> + meet

见面 (jiànmiàn) is a separable verb for meeting up; 遇见 / 遇到 take an object directly for running into someone.

我明天上午和老朋友见面。 (Wǒ míngtiān shàngwǔ hé lǎo péngyou jiànmiàn.)

I am meeting an old friend tomorrow morning.

刚才在公园里，我遇见了我的邻居。 (Gāngcái zài gōngyuán lǐ, wǒ yùjiàn le wǒ de línjū.)

Just now in the park, I ran into my neighbor.

Doing something together with 一块儿

<Subject> + 一块儿 + <Verb Phrase> (<Subject> + yíkuàir + <Verb Phrase>)

<Subject> + together + <Verb Phrase>

Place 一块儿 (yíkuàir) before the verb phrase to mean doing something together (colloquial northern equivalent of 一起).

周末姐妹俩一块儿去买东西。 (Zhōumò jiěmèi liǎ yíkuàir qù mǎi dōngxi.)

On the weekend the two sisters go shopping together.

下班以后，我们一块儿去看望叔叔。 (Xiàbān yǐhòu, wǒmen yíkuàir qù kànwàng shūshu.)

After work, we’ll go visit Uncle together.

3 · Build new sentences

Talk about family, neighbors, and caring for guests.

Model

姐姐一直很照顾身边的人。 (Jiějie yìzhí hěn zhàogù shēnbiān de rén.)

My older sister always takes good care of the people around her.

1. Say your neighbor is getting married next month.

Show one natural answer

我的邻居下个月要结婚了。 (Wǒ de línjū xià ge yuè yào jiéhūn le.)

My neighbor is getting married next month.

2. Say you brought a gift when visiting Uncle’s home.

Show one natural answer

我去叔叔家做客的时候带了一件礼物。 (Wǒ qù shūshu jiā zuòkè de shíhou dài le yí jiàn lǐwù.)

When I visited Uncle’s home as a guest, I brought a gift.

3. Say parents care very much about their children’s health.

Show one natural answer

父母非常关心孩子的健康。 (Fùmǔ fēicháng guānxīn háizi de jiànkāng.)

Parents care very much about their children’s health.

4 · Read in context: Warm Neighbors

王叔叔和刘阿姨是一对热情的夫妻。他们搬来这个小区以后，总是很关心身边的邻居。 (Wáng shūshu hé Liú āyí shì yí duì rèqíng de fūqī. Tāmen bān lái zhège xiǎoqū yǐhòu, zǒngshì hěn guānxīn shēnbiān de línjū.)
Uncle Wang and Auntie Liu are a warm-hearted couple. After moving to this neighborhood, they have always cared about the neighbors around them.

今年春节，他们请附近的几家邻居到家里做客。大家一块儿包饺子、聊家常，像一家人一样。 (Jīnnián Chūnjié, tāmen qǐng fùjìn de jǐ jiā línjū dào jiālǐ zuòkè. Dàjiā yíkuàir bāo jiǎozi, liáo jiācháng, xiàng yì jiā rén yíyàng.)
This Spring Festival, they invited several nearby neighbors to visit their home. Everyone made dumplings and chatted together, just like one family.

Q1. How do Uncle Wang and Auntie Liu treat their neighbors?
Show answer
They are warm-hearted and always care about the neighbors around them.
Q2. What did everyone do together at their home during Spring Festival?
Show answer
They made dumplings and chatted together.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Talk about a neighbor or relative who looks after others.
Show one possible response

我的邻居张阿姨常常帮助老人，大家都很感谢她。 (Wǒ de línjū Zhāng āyí chángcháng bāngzhù lǎorén, dàjiā dōu hěn gǎnxiè tā.)

My neighbor Auntie Zhang often helps elderly people, and everyone is very grateful to her.

2. Describe what you bring when visiting someone as a guest.
Show one possible response

我去朋友家做客时，通常会带水果或者小礼物。 (Wǒ qù péngyou jiā zuòkè shí, tōngcháng huì dài shuǐguǒ huòzhě xiǎo lǐwù.)

When I visit a friend’s home, I usually bring fruit or a small gift.

6 · Watch out

见面 cannot take a direct object (*见面朋友 is wrong). Say 和朋友见面 ("meet with a friend") or 见朋友.

Pronunciation clinic

一块儿 (yíkuàir) has tone sandhi on 一 (changing to yí before fourth-tone 块) and an erhua ending.

7 · Check what you can do

Comprehension

Why did the neighbors gather at Uncle Wang and Auntie Liu’s home?

Show answer
They were invited as guests for Spring Festival.

The second paragraph says they invited nearby neighbors to visit for Spring Festival.

Production

Write a sentence using 和……见面 or 遇见 and 一块儿.

Show answer
周末我打算和姐妹见面，一块儿去吃饭。

Use 和……见面 for meeting and 一块儿 before the shared activity.

Review and next step

  • Name relatives, couples, guests, and neighbors.
  • Distinguish 见面 from 遇见 / 遇到.
  • Talk about visiting and caring for others.

Optional vocabulary recall quiz
Selection

Which word means "neighbor"?

a. 邻居 (línjū) — neighbor

b. 客人 (kèrén) — guest

c. 夫妻 (fūqī) — married couple

Show answer
a

邻居 refers to a neighbor.

Selection

Which verb means "to look after / care for"?

a. 结婚 (jiéhūn) — marry

b. 照顾 (zhàogù) — look after

c. 做客 (zuòkè) — visit as a guest

Show answer
b

照顾 means to look after or take care of someone.

Selection

Which word means "gift"?

a. 礼物 (lǐwù) — gift

b. 身边 (shēnbiān) — by one’s side

c. 爱人 (àiren) — spouse

Show answer
a

礼物 means a gift or present.
