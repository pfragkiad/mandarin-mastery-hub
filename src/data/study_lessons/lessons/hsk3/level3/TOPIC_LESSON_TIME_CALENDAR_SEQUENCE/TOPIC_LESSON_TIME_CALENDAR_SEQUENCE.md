# Sequence, Frequency & Duration

Express when events happen in sequence, how often they occur, and whether something happened just now or finally.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish 刚, 刚才, and 刚刚 for recent actions.

- Sequence events with 先, 后来, 以前, 以后, 最后, and 结束.

- Express frequency and continuity using 常, 常常, 平时, 一直, 总是, 突然, 马上, and 终于.

## 1. Catching Up After Class

Two classmates talk about why one arrived late and what happened.

**A:** 你刚才去哪儿了？上课的时候你一直没来。 (Nǐ gāngcái qù nǎr le? Shàngkè de shíhou nǐ yìzhí méi lái.) — Where did you go just now? You weren’t here the whole time during class.

**B:** 抱歉，我迟到了。平时我总是很早出门，但今天路上突然下大雨了。 (Bàoqiàn, wǒ chídào le. Píngshí wǒ zǒngshì hěn zǎo chūmén, dàn jīntiān lùshang tūrán xià dà yǔ le.) — Sorry, I was late. Usually I always leave early, but today it suddenly rained heavily on the way.

**A:** 没关系，老师刚才也刚讲完重点，一会儿我借你笔记。 (Méiguānxi, lǎoshī gāngcái yě gāng jiǎng wán zhòngdiǎn, yíhuìr wǒ jiè nǐ bǐjì.) — No worries—the teacher also just finished the key points; I’ll lend you my notes in a bit.

**B:** 太好了！咱们先去吃饭，吃完以后再看笔记吧。 (Tài hǎo le! Zánmen xiān qù chīfàn, chī wán yǐhòu zài kàn bǐjì ba.) — Great! Let’s eat first, and look at the notes after eating.

## 2. How the language works

### 刚 (adverb) vs. 刚才 (time noun)

**Pattern:** 刚才 + <Subject> + <Verb>  /  <Subject> + 刚 + <Verb> (gāngcái + <Subject> + <Verb>  /  <Subject> + gāng + <Verb>)

*just now + <Subject> + <Verb>  /  <Subject> + just + <Verb>*

刚才 (gāngcái) can precede or follow the subject and can be negated; 刚 (gāng) is an adverb that goes directly before the verb.

**刚才谁给你打电话了？**
*Gāngcái shéi gěi nǐ dǎ diànhuà le?*
Who called you just now?

**我刚到车站，车就开了。**
*Wǒ gāng dào chēzhàn, chē jiù kāi le.*
Just as I arrived at the station, the train left.

### Before and after with 以前 / 以后

**Pattern:** <Event / Time> + 以前 / 以后 (<Event / Time> + yǐqián / yǐhòu)

*<Event / Time> + before / after*

Place 以前 (before) or 以后 (after) right after a time word or verb phrase.

**来中国以前，他学了一年汉语。**
*Lái Zhōngguó yǐqián, tā xué le yì nián Hànyǔ.*
Before coming to China, he studied Chinese for one year.

**比赛结束以后，大家终于可以休息了。**
*Bǐsài jiéshù yǐhòu, dàjiā zhōngyú kěyǐ xiūxi le.*
After the match ended, everyone could finally rest.

## 3. Build new sentences

Narrate sequences and frequency using time adverbs.

*Model:* **最近我常常去体育馆锻炼。**
*Zuìjìn wǒ chángcháng qù tǐyùguǎn duànliàn.*
Recently I often go to the gym to work out.

**Cue 1:** Say you will reply right away.

*Model:* **我马上给你回信。**
*Wǒ mǎshàng gěi nǐ huíxìn.*
I will reply to you right away.

**Cue 2:** Say it hasn’t rained for a long time, and you hope it rains soon.

*Model:* **好久没下雨了，希望不久以后能下一场雨。**
*Hǎojiǔ méi xià yǔ le, xīwàng bùjiǔ yǐhòu néng xià yì chǎng yǔ.*
It hasn’t rained for a long time; I hope it rains soon.

**Cue 3:** Say at first he lived in Beijing, and later moved to Shanghai.

*Model:* **以前他住在北京，后来搬到了上海。**
*Yǐqián tā zhù zài Běijīng, hòulái bān dào le Shànghǎi.*
Previously he lived in Beijing, and later he moved to Shanghai.

## 4. Read in context: Finally Finding the Key

昨天下午，小李准备出门的时候，突然发现钥匙不见了。他平时总是把钥匙放在桌子上，今天怎么找不到了？ (Zuótiān xiàwǔ, Xiǎo Lǐ zhǔnbèi chūmén de shíhou, tūrán fāxiàn yàoshi bújiàn le. Tā píngshí zǒngshì bǎ yàoshi fàng zài zhuōzi shàng, jīntiān zěnme zhǎo bú dào le?)

Yesterday afternoon, when Xiao Li was about to go out, he suddenly discovered his key was missing. Usually he always puts his key on the table—why couldn’t he find it today?

他先在客厅找了一遍，后来又去卧室找，最后终于在衣服口袋里找到了。原来他刚才换大衣的时候放进去了！ (Tā xiān zài kètīng zhǎo le yí biàn, hòulái yòu qù wòshì zhǎo, zuìhòu zhōngyú zài yīfu kǒudài lǐ zhǎodào le. Yuánlái tā gāngcái huàn dàyī de shíhou fàng jìnqù le!)

He first searched the living room, later went to search the bedroom, and finally found it in his coat pocket. It turned out he had put it in when changing his coat just earlier!

**Q1. Where does Xiao Li usually put his key?** Suggested answer: On the table.

**Q2. Where did he finally find the key?** Suggested answer: In his coat pocket.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Describe your normal routine using 平时, 总是, and 最近.

*Possible response:* **平时我总是七点起床，不过最近睡得比较晚。**
*Píngshí wǒ zǒngshì qī diǎn qǐchuáng, búguò zuìjìn shuì de bǐjiào wǎn.*
Normally I always get up at seven, though recently I have been sleeping rather late.

**Speaking 2:** Tell a short 3-step story using 先, 后来, and 最后.

*Possible response:* **我先去了银行，后来去超市买菜，最后回家做饭。**
*Wǒ xiān qù le yínháng, hòulái qù chāoshì mǎi cài, zuìhòu huí jiā zuòfàn.*
First I went to the bank, later I went to the supermarket to buy groceries, and finally I went home to cook.

## 6. Watch out

后来 ("later, afterward") only describes past events that have already happened; use 以后 ("after / in the future") for future plans.

**Pronunciation:** 突然 (tūrán) moves from first tone to second tone; 终于 (zhōngyú) also moves from first tone to second tone.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** Why was Xiao Li’s key in his coat pocket?
**Answer:** He had put it there when changing his coat just earlier.. The passage ends with 原来他刚才换大衣的时候放进去了！

**Production:** Write a short sequence of two sentences using 刚才 or 刚 and 终于.
**Answer:** 我刚才找了半天，终于把笔记本找到了。. Use 刚才/刚 for the recent action and 终于 for the eventual result.

## Review and next step

- Contrast 刚, 刚才, and 刚刚.

- Sequence events with 先, 后来, 以前, 以后, and 最后.

- Use frequency and aspect adverbs like 一直, 总是, and 终于.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which word means "immediately / right away"?
- a. 马上 (mǎshàng) — right away
- b. 好久 (hǎojiǔ) — a long time
- c. 平时 (píngshí) — usually
**Answer:** a. 马上 means immediately.

**Selection:** Which word means "finally / at last"?
- a. 突然 (tūrán) — suddenly
- b. 终于 (zhōngyú) — finally
- c. 迟到 (chídào) — arrive late
**Answer:** b. 终于 expresses reaching a long-awaited result.

**Selection:** Which word means "to end / finish"?
- a. 结束 (jiéshù) — end / finish
- b. 一直 (yìzhí) — all along
- c. 不久 (bùjiǔ) — soon
**Answer:** a. 结束 means to end or conclude.
