Body, Illness & Staying Healthy

Body parts, catching a cold, hospital stays, and exercise

health-wellbeing · #health · #body · #hospital

Learning objectives

  • Name body parts: 脸, 耳朵, 嘴, 牙, 腿, 脚, and 身高.
  • Talk about illness and medical care: 病人, 感冒, 发烧, 渴, 检查, 住院, 出院, and 担心.
  • Talk about staying fit with 锻炼 and 健康.

1 · Visiting a Friend After the Hospital

Linlin checks on her friend who caught a cold and just left the hospital.

A: 听说你前几天感冒发烧了，我很担心，现在好点儿了吗？ (Tīngshuō nǐ qián jǐ tiān gǎnmào fāshāo le, wǒ hěn dānxīn, xiànzài hǎo diǎnr le ma?)
I heard you caught a cold and had a fever a few days ago—I was worried! Are you feeling better now?

B: 好多了，昨天做完检查就出院了，没住院太久。 (Hǎo duō le, zuótiān zuò wán jiǎnchá jiù chūyuàn le, méi zhùyuàn tài jiǔ.)
Much better; yesterday after finishing the checkup I was discharged and didn’t stay in the hospital too long.

A: 那就好！多喝水，别觉得渴了才喝。 (Nà jiù hǎo! Duō hē shuǐ, bié juéde kě le cái hē.)
That’s good! Drink plenty of water—don’t wait until you feel thirsty to drink.

B: 谢谢关心，医生也让我以后多锻炼，保持健康。 (Xièxie guānxīn, yīshēng yě ràng wǒ yǐhòu duō duànliàn, bǎochí jiànkāng.)
Thanks for caring; the doctor also told me to exercise more from now on to stay healthy.

2 · How the language works

Hospital admission and discharge

<Patient> + 住院 / 出院 + 了 (<Patient> + zhùyuàn / chūyuàn + le)

<Patient> + entered hospital / left hospital + (change)

住院 (zhùyuàn) means to be hospitalized; 出院 (chūyuàn) means to be discharged from the hospital.

爷爷昨天住院了，我们要去看看他。 (Yéye zuótiān zhùyuàn le, wǒmen yào qù kànkan tā.)

Grandpa was hospitalized yesterday; we need to go visit him.

这位病人下星期一就可以出院了。 (Zhè wèi bìngrén xià xīngqīyī jiù kěyǐ chūyuàn le.)

This patient can be discharged next Monday.

Worrying about someone with 担心

<Subject> + 担心 + <Person / Situation> (<Subject> + dānxīn + <Person / Situation>)

<Subject> + worry about + <Person / Situation>

Place the person or situation you worry about directly after 担心 (dānxīn).

妈妈很担心孩子的健康。 (Māma hěn dānxīn háizi de jiànkāng.)

Mom worries a lot about her child’s health.

别担心，医生检查以后说没什么大问题。 (Bié dānxīn, yīshēng jiǎnchá yǐhòu shuō méi shénme dà wèntí.)

Don’t worry; after checking, the doctor said there’s no big problem.

3 · Build new sentences

Describe body parts, symptoms, and healthy habits.

Model

他每天跑步锻炼，腿和脚都很有力量。 (Tā měitiān pǎobù duànliàn, tuǐ hé jiǎo dōu hěn yǒu lìliàng.)

He runs to exercise every day, and both his legs and feet are very strong.

1. Say you have a toothache and need a checkup.

Show one natural answer

我的牙有点儿疼，想去医院检查一下。 (Wǒ de yá yǒudiǎnr téng, xiǎng qù yīyuàn jiǎnchá yíxià.)

My tooth hurts a bit; I want to go to the hospital for a checkup.

2. Say his height is one meter eighty.

Show one natural answer

他的身高是一米八。 (Tā de shēngāo shì yì mǐ bā.)

His height is 1.8 meters.

3. Say when you’re cold, your face and ears turn red.

Show one natural answer

外面很冷，她的脸和耳朵都冻红了。 (Wàimiàn hěn lěng, tā de liǎn hé ěrduo dōu dòng hóng le.)

It’s very cold outside; her face and ears both got red from the cold.

4 · Read in context: Staying Healthy in Winter

冬天天气冷，人很容易感冒。如果不小心发烧了，应该及时去医院请医生检查，不要拖着不管。 (Dōngtiān tiānqì lěng, rén hěn róngyì gǎnmào. Rúguǒ bù xiǎoxīn fāshāo le, yīnggāi jíshí qù yīyuàn qǐng yīshēng jiǎnchá, búyào tuō zhe bù guǎn.)
Winter weather is cold, so people easily catch a cold. If you accidentally get a fever, you should go to the hospital promptly to have a doctor check you, rather than putting it off.

平时也要注意多锻炼，早睡早起，保护好牙齿和身体，这样才能少生病，让家人不用担心。 (Píngshí yě yào zhùyì duō duànliàn, zǎo shuì zǎo qǐ, bǎohù hǎo yáchǐ hé shēntǐ, zhèyàng cái néng shǎo shēngbìng, ràng jiārén búyòng dānxīn.)
Normally you should also pay attention to exercising more, sleeping and waking early, and taking good care of your teeth and body—that way you get sick less and your family won’t need to worry.

Q1. What should you do if you get a fever in winter?
Show answer
Go to the hospital promptly to have a doctor check you.
Q2. What daily habits help you get sick less?
Show answer
Exercising more, sleeping and waking early, and taking care of your teeth and body.

5 · Speak and personalize

Answer aloud before opening the model. Adapt each reply to your own life.

1. Say how you stay healthy using 锻炼 and 健康.
Show one possible response

我每天晚上散步半小时，多锻炼身体才会更健康。 (Wǒ měitiān wǎnshang sànbù bàn xiǎoshí, duō duànliàn shēntǐ cái huì gèng jiànkāng.)

I walk for half an hour every evening; exercising more keeps your body healthier.

2. Describe a time you had a cold or went for a checkup.
Show one possible response

上个月我感冒了，有点儿发烧，去医院检查后很快就好了。 (Shàng ge yuè wǒ gǎnmào le, yǒudiǎnr fāshāo, qù yīyuàn jiǎnchá hòu hěn kuài jiù hǎo le.)

Last month I caught a cold and had a slight fever; after a hospital checkup I recovered quickly.

6 · Watch out

住院 means "be admitted to / stay in the hospital", not "live near a hospital". Use 住 + hospital name only if you are an inpatient.

Pronunciation clinic

耳朵 (ěrduo) has a neutral tone on duo; 锻炼 (duànliàn) has two consecutive fourth tones.
