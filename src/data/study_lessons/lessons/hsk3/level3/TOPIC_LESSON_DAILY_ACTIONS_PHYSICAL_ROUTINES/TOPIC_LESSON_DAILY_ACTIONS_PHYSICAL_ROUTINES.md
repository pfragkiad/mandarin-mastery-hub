# Everyday Actions & Chores

Describe everyday physical actions, household chores, routines, and using or sharing objects.

## Learning objectives

- Describe chores and hygiene: 打扫, 扫, 刷, 洗澡, 起, and 习惯.

- Talk about handling objects: 搬, 带, 放, 关, 丢, 查, 分开, 养, and 用.

- Describe physical movements: 爬, 跳, and 站.

## 1. Weekend Housework

Two roommates divide up chores before guests arrive.

**A:** 客人下午就到了，咱们先把屋子打扫一下吧！ (Kèrén xiàwǔ jiù dào le, zánmen xiān bǎ wūzi dǎsǎo yíxià ba!) — The guests arrive this afternoon—let’s clean the room first!

**B:** 没问题，我负责扫地，你把桌子上的书搬到书架上放好。 (Méi wèntí, wǒ fùzé sǎodì, nǐ bǎ zhuōzi shàng de shū bān dào shūjià shàng fàng hǎo.) — No problem, I’ll sweep the floor, and you move the books on the table onto the bookshelf.

**A:** 好，别忘了把旧报纸和纸盒分开，等会儿拿去楼下丢掉。 (Hǎo, bié wàng le bǎ jiù bàozhǐ hé zhǐhé fēnkāi, děnghuìr ná qù lóuxià diū diào.) — Okay, don’t forget to separate the old newspapers and cardboard boxes to throw away downstairs in a bit.

**B:** 打扫完我再去洗个澡，然后咱们就准备做饭！ (Dǎsǎo wán wǒ zài qù xǐ ge zǎo, ránhòu zánmen jiù zhǔnbèi zuòfàn!) — After cleaning I’ll go take a shower, and then we’ll get ready to cook!

## 2. How the language works

### Being used to something with 习惯

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 习惯 + <Verb / Noun> (<Subject> + xíguàn + <Verb / Noun>)

*<Subject> + is used to + <Verb / Noun>*

Use 习惯 (xíguàn) before a verb or noun to mean "be accustomed to", or as a noun for "habit".

**我已经习惯每天早上七点起床了。**
*Wǒ yǐjīng xíguàn měitiān zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng le.*
I am already used to getting up at 7 a.m. every day.

**晚上刷牙是一个好习惯。**
*Wǎnshang shuāyá shì yí ge hǎo xíguàn.*
Brushing your teeth at night is a good habit.

### Putting or moving something somewhere

**Pattern:** 把 + <Object> + 放在 / 搬到 + <Location> (bǎ + <Object> + fàng zài / bān dào + <Location>)

*take + <Object> + put at / move to + <Location>*

Combine 把 + object + 放 / 搬 + 在 / 到 + location.

**请把雨伞放在门旁边。**
*Qǐng bǎ yǔsǎn fàng zài mén pángbiān.*
Please put the umbrella beside the door.

**我们一起把那张桌子搬到客厅里。**
*Wǒmen yìqǐ bǎ nà zhāng zhuōzi bān dào kètīng lǐ.*
Let’s move that table into the living room together.

## 3. Build new sentences

Describe everyday actions, chores, and physical movements.

*Model:* **出门以前，别忘了关灯和关门。**
*Chūmén yǐqián, bié wàng le guān dēng hé guān mén.*
Before going out, don’t forget to turn off the light and close the door.

**Cue 1:** Say you raised a cute puppy at home.

*Model:* **我在家里养了一只可爱的小狗。**
*Wǒ zài jiālǐ yǎng le yì zhī kě’ài de xiǎo gǒu.*
I raised a cute puppy at home.

**Cue 2:** Say you need to look up a word in the dictionary.

*Model:* **我用词典查了一下那个词。**
*Wǒ yòng cídiǎn chá le yíxià nàge cí.*
I looked up that word using the dictionary.

**Cue 3:** Say the children are jumping and climbing on the lawn.

*Model:* **孩子们在草地上跑来跑去，又爬又跳。**
*Háizimen zài cǎodì shàng pǎo lái pǎo qù, yòu pá yòu tiào.*
The children ran around on the lawn, climbing and jumping.

## 4. Read in context: Grandpa’s Morning Routine

爷爷今年七十多岁了，但是身体非常健康。他每天早上六点半准时起来，先在阳台站一会儿，再刷牙洗脸。 (Yéye jīnnián qīshí duō suì le, dànshì shēntǐ fēicháng jiànkāng. Tā měitiān zǎoshang liù diǎn bàn zhǔnshí qǐlái, xiān zài yángtái zhàn yíhuìr, zài shuāyá xǐliǎn.)

Grandpa is over seventy this year, yet his body is very healthy. Every morning at 6:30 sharp he gets up, stands on the balcony for a moment, and then brushes his teeth and washes his face.

早饭以后，他习惯带着小狗去附近散步，有时候还会爬爬小山，或者帮家里打扫院子。 (Zǎofàn yǐhòu, tā xíguàn dài zhe xiǎo gǒu qù fùjìn sànbù, yǒu shíhou hái huì pápa xiǎoshān, huòzhě bāng jiālǐ dǎsǎo yuànzi.)

After breakfast, he is used to taking his dog for a walk nearby, and sometimes climbs the small hill or helps sweep the courtyard at home.

**Q1. What time does Grandpa get up every morning?** Suggested answer: At 6:30 sharp.

**Q2. What does he do after breakfast?** Suggested answer: He takes his dog for a walk nearby, and sometimes climbs a small hill or helps clean the courtyard.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Describe one good daily habit you have using 习惯.

*Possible response:* **我习惯每天晚上睡前看半个小时书。**
*Wǒ xíguàn měitiān wǎnshang shuìqián kàn bàn ge xiǎoshí shū.*
I am used to reading for half an hour before bed every night.

**Speaking 2:** Say what chores you do at home using 打扫, 扫, or 搬.

*Possible response:* **周末我会打扫自己的屋子，还会把垃圾拿出去丢掉。**
*Zhōumò wǒ huì dǎsǎo zìjǐ de wūzi, hái huì bǎ lājī ná chūqù diū diào.*
On weekends I clean my own room and take the trash out to throw away.

## 6. Watch out

丢 (diū) means both "lose / misplace" (丢了钥匙) and "throw away" (丢垃圾). Context makes the meaning clear.

**Pronunciation:** 打扫 (dǎsǎo) has two consecutive third tones, so 打 shifts to a rising second tone (dásǎo) in speech; 洗澡 (xǐzǎo) likewise shifts to xízǎo.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** What does Grandpa do right after getting up at 6:30?
**Answer:** He stands on the balcony for a bit, then brushes his teeth and washes his face.. The passage says 先在阳台站一会儿，再刷牙洗脸.

**Production:** Write two sentences about your daily routine or chores using 习惯 and 打扫 or 带.
**Answer:** 我习惯周末打扫屋子，出门时也会带着水瓶子。. Use 习惯 and 打扫/带 naturally.

## Review and next step

- Describe chores like 打扫, 扫, and 搬.

- Talk about habits and routines with 习惯 and 洗澡.

- Use action verbs like 爬, 跳, 站, 放, and 关.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which verb means "to sweep / clean"?
- a. 打扫 (dǎsǎo) — clean / sweep
- b. 洗澡 (xǐzǎo) — take a shower
- c. 分开 (fēnkāi) — separate
**Answer:** a. 打扫 means to clean or sweep.

**Selection:** Which verb means "to raise / keep (pets)"?
- a. 爬 (pá) — climb
- b. 养 (yǎng) — raise / keep
- c. 搬 (bān) — move / carry
**Answer:** b. 养 means to raise or keep animals/plants.

**Selection:** Which verb means "to lose / throw away"?
- a. 丢 (diū) — lose / throw away
- b. 关 (guān) — close
- c. 站 (zhàn) — stand
**Answer:** a. 丢 means lose or throw away.
