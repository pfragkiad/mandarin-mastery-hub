# Measure Words, Weights & Totals

Use everyday classifiers, weights, lengths, coin units, approximations, and totals accurately.

## Learning objectives

- Use action and text classifiers: 遍, 段, 句, 页, 张, 封, and 种.

- Use physical and money units: 公斤, 斤, 米, 层, 辆, 双, 角, and 毛.

- Express totals and ranges with 一共, 大概, 好多, 以上, and 以下.

## 1. Buying Fruit and Shoes

Xiao Fang buys apples at a market stall and asks about the total price.

**A:** 请问，这种苹果多少钱一斤？ (Qǐngwèn, zhè zhǒng píngguǒ duōshao qián yì jīn?) — Excuse me, how much per half-kilogram is this kind of apple?

**B:** 五块五毛一斤，买两公斤以上还可以便宜一点儿。 (Wǔ kuài wǔ máo yì jīn, mǎi liǎng gōngjīn yǐshàng hái kěyǐ piányi yìdiǎnr.) — Five yuan fifty jiao per jin; if you buy two kilograms or more, it’s a bit cheaper.

**A:** 那给我来两公斤吧。一共多少钱？ (Nà gěi wǒ lái liǎng gōngjīn ba. Yígòng duōshao qián?) — Then give me two kilograms. How much is it in total?

**B:** 两公斤就是四斤，一共二十块钱！ (Liǎng gōngjīn jiùshì sì jīn, yígòng èrshí kuài qián!) — Two kilograms is four jin, so twenty yuan altogether!

## 2. How the language works

### Ranges with 以上 and 以下

**Pattern:** <Number + Unit> + 以上 / 以下 (<Number + Unit> + yǐshàng / yǐxià)

*<Number + Unit> + above / below*

Place 以上 (or more / above) or 以下 (or less / below) directly after a number + unit.

**一米二以下的小朋友不用买票。**
*Yì mǐ èr yǐxià de xiǎo péngyou búyòng mǎi piào.*
Children 1.2 meters and under do not need to buy a ticket.

**每天看三十页以上的书是个好习惯。**
*Měitiān kàn sānshí yè yǐshàng de shū shì ge hǎo xíguàn.*
Reading 30 or more pages of a book every day is a good habit.

### Complete repetitions with 遍

**Pattern:** <Verb> + 了 + <Number> + 遍 (<Verb> + le + <Number> + biàn)

*<Verb> + (completed) + <Number> + complete times*

Use 遍 (biàn) after a verb to count reading, watching, or doing something from start to finish.

**这段话我读了三遍才明白。**
*Zhè duàn huà wǒ dú le sān biàn cái míngbai.*
I read this passage three times before understanding it.

**那句话他重复了两遍。**
*Nà jù huà tā chóngfù le liǎng biàn.*
He repeated that sentence twice.

## 3. Build new sentences

Use the right classifier or unit for each noun.

*Model:* **我住在这栋楼的五层，楼下停着一辆自行车。**
*Wǒ zhù zài zhè dòng lóu de wǔ céng, lóuxià tíng zhe yí liàng zìxíngchē.*
I live on the fifth floor of this building, and a bicycle is parked downstairs.

**Cue 1:** Say you bought a new pair of shoes.

*Model:* **我买了一双新鞋。**
*Wǒ mǎi le yì shuāng xīn xié.*
I bought a new pair of shoes.

**Cue 2:** Ask for someone’s phone number.

*Model:* **请问，你的电话号码是多少？**
*Qǐngwèn, nǐ de diànhuà hàomǎ shì duōshao?*
Excuse me, what is your phone number?

**Cue 3:** Say there are roughly thirty students in the room.

*Model:* **教室里大概有三十个学生。**
*Jiàoshì lǐ dàgài yǒu sānshí ge xuéshēng.*
There are roughly thirty students in the classroom.

## 4. Read in context: Packing for a Move

周末搬家的时候，家里有好多东西要整理。我们一共装了十几个大箱子，还寄出了两封信和几张照片。 (Zhōumò bānjiā de shíhou, jiālǐ yǒu hǎoduō dōngxi yào zhěnglǐ. Wǒmen yígòng zhuāng le shí jǐ ge dà xiāngzi, hái jì chū le liǎng fēng xìn hé jǐ zhāng zhàopiàn.)

When moving house on the weekend, there were lots of things at home to organize. Altogether we packed over ten big boxes, and also mailed out two letters and several photos.

新家在四层，没有电梯，所以搬一辆自行车和几双鞋上楼大概花了半个小时。 (Xīn jiā zài sì céng, méiyǒu diàntī, suǒyǐ bān yí liàng zìxíngchē hé jǐ shuāng xié shànglóu dàgài huā le bàn ge xiǎoshí.)

The new home is on the fourth floor without an elevator, so carrying a bicycle and several pairs of shoes upstairs took roughly half an hour.

**Q1. How many big boxes did they pack altogether?** Suggested answer: Over ten big boxes (十几个大箱子).

**Q2. Which floor is the new home on?** Suggested answer: The fourth floor (四层).

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Ask the price per jin or kilogram and ask for the total.

*Possible response:* **这种香蕉多少钱一斤？我要两斤，一共多少钱？**
*Zhè zhǒng xiāngjiāo duōshao qián yì jīn? Wǒ yào liǎng jīn, yígòng duōshao qián?*
How much per jin is this kind of banana? I want two jin—how much altogether?

**Speaking 2:** Use 遍, 句, or 页 to talk about reading.

*Possible response:* **今天我看了二十页书，把重点句子读了两遍。**
*Jīntiān wǒ kàn le èrshí yè shū, bǎ zhòngdiǎn jùzi dú le liǎng biàn.*
Today I read 20 pages of a book and read the key sentences twice.

## 6. Watch out

1 公斤 = 2 斤 (500 grams per 斤). In speech, 毛 is common for 0.1 yuan (五毛), while 角 is used on price tags and formal receipts.

**Pronunciation:** 一共 (yígòng) changes 一 to rising yí before fourth-tone gòng; 好多 (hǎoduō) keeps a low third tone on hǎo.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** Why did carrying the bicycle and shoes upstairs take about half an hour?
**Answer:** Because the new home is on the fourth floor and has no elevator.. The passage notes 新家在四层，没有电梯.

**Production:** Write one sentence using 遍 and one using 一共 or 大概.
**Answer:** 这篇课文我读了三遍，大概用了二十分钟。. Use 遍 for complete readings and 大概/一共 for estimates or totals.

## Review and next step

- Use classifiers like 辆, 双, 张, 封, 段, 句, 页, and 遍.

- Compare 公斤 and 斤, 角 and 毛.

- Express totals and bounds with 一共, 大概, 以上, and 以下.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which classifier is used for vehicles like cars or bicycles?
- a. 辆 (liàng) — classifier for vehicles
- b. 双 (shuāng) — pair
- c. 封 (fēng) — classifier for letters
**Answer:** a. 辆 is the classifier for wheeled vehicles.

**Selection:** Which word means "kilogram"?
- a. 斤 (jīn) — 500 grams
- b. 公斤 (gōngjīn) — kilogram
- c. 米 (mǐ) — meter
**Answer:** b. 公斤 is 1 kilogram (2 斤).

**Selection:** Which word means "altogether / in total"?
- a. 大概 (dàgài) — roughly
- b. 一共 (yígòng) — altogether
- c. 好多 (hǎoduō) — many
**Answer:** b. 一共 gives the total.
