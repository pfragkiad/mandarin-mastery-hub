# Asking How & Talking About Others

Learn to include the listener with 咱们, refer to others with 别的 or 其他, and ask what to do.

## Learning objectives

- Include the listener when suggesting plans with 咱们.

- Distinguish 别的 and 其他 when referring to other items or people.

- Ask how to solve a problem with 怎么办 and 怎样.

## 1. Missed the Last Bus

Two friends realize the last bus has already left and decide what to do next.

**A:** 糟糕，最后一班车刚开走，咱们怎么办？ (Zāogāo, zuìhòu yì bān chē gāng kāizǒu, zánmen zěnme bàn?) — Oh no, the last bus just left—what should we do?

**B:** 别急，咱们看看附近还有没有别的车。 (Bié jí, zánmen kànkan fùjìn hái yǒu méiyǒu biéde chē.) — Don’t worry, let’s check if there are any other buses nearby.

**A:** 如果没有其他办法，咱们就打车回去吧。 (Rúguǒ méiyǒu qítā bànfǎ, zánmen jiù dǎchē huíqù ba.) — If there is no other way, let’s take a taxi back.

**B:** 好主意，你觉得打车怎么样，方便吗？ (Hǎo zhǔyi, nǐ juéde dǎchē zěnmeyàng, fāngbiàn ma?) — Good idea—how do you feel about taking a taxi, is it convenient?

## 2. How the language works

### Asking what to do

**Pattern:** <Situation> + 怎么办？ (<Situation> + zěnme bàn?)

*<Situation> + what to do?*

Add 怎么办 (zěnme bàn) after stating a problem to ask how to deal with it.

**下雨了，我没带伞，怎么办？**
*Xià yǔ le, wǒ méi dài sǎn, zěnme bàn?*
It’s raining and I didn’t bring an umbrella—what should I do?

**手机没电了，咱们现在怎么办？**
*Shǒujī méi diàn le, zánmen xiànzài zěnme bàn?*
My phone ran out of battery—what do we do now?

### Referring to other things with 别的 / 其他

**Pattern:** 别的 / 其他 + <Noun> (biéde / qítā + <Noun>)

*other + <Noun>*

Place 别的 or 其他 before a noun to mean "other"; 别的 can also stand alone as "something else".

**你还需要买别的东西吗？**
*Nǐ hái xūyào mǎi biéde dōngxi ma?*
Do you still need to buy anything else?

**其他同学都已经到教室了。**
*Qítā tóngxué dōu yǐjīng dào jiàoshì le.*
The other classmates have all already arrived at the classroom.

## 3. Build new sentences

Practice suggesting plans with 咱们 and asking about alternatives.

*Model:* **咱们去看看其他书店吧。**
*Zánmen qù kànkan qítā shūdiàn ba.*
Let’s go check out other bookstores.

**Cue 1:** Ask what to do when you forgot your homework.

*Model:* **我忘记带作业了，怎么办？**
*Wǒ wàngjì dài zuòyè le, zěnme bàn?*
I forgot to bring my homework—what should I do?

**Cue 2:** Ask if there are other colors.

*Model:* **这件衣服还有别的颜色吗？**
*Zhè jiàn yīfu hái yǒu biéde yánsè ma?*
Does this piece of clothing come in other colors?

**Cue 3:** Ask how to get to the train station in the best way.

*Model:* **请问，去火车站怎样走最方便？**
*Qǐngwèn, qù huǒchēzhàn zěnyàng zǒu zuì fāngbiàn?*
Excuse me, how is the most convenient way to go to the train station?

## 4. Read in context: Planning a Weekend Trip

小周对朋友说：“周末咱们去爬山吧，你们觉得怎样？” (Xiǎo Zhōu duì péngyou shuō: “Zhōumò zánmen qù páshān ba, nǐmen juéde zěnyàng?”)

Xiao Zhou said to his friends: “Let’s go hiking this weekend—how does that sound to you?”

大家都很高兴，不过小王问：“要是周末下雨怎么办？”小周笑着说：“那咱们就改去博物馆，或者去别的公园。” (Dàjiā dōu hěn gāoxìng, búguò Xiǎo Wáng wèn: “Yàoshi zhōumò xià yǔ zěnme bàn?” Xiǎo Zhōu xiào zhe shuō: “Nà zánmen jiù gǎi qù bówùguǎn, huòzhě qù biéde gōngyuán.”)

Everyone was happy, though Xiao Wang asked: “What if it rains on the weekend?” Xiao Zhou smiled and said: “Then we’ll go to a museum instead, or another park.”

**Q1. What does Xiao Zhou suggest doing on the weekend?** Suggested answer: Going hiking together.

**Q2. What backup plan does Xiao Zhou offer if it rains?** Suggested answer: Going to a museum or another park instead.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Invite a friend to do something together using 咱们 and ask what they think.

*Possible response:* **咱们下午去图书馆复习吧，你觉得怎样？**
*Zánmen xiàwǔ qù túshūguǎn fùxí ba, nǐ juéde zěnyàng?*
Let’s go to the library to review this afternoon—how does that sound?

**Speaking 2:** Describe a small problem and ask 怎么办.

*Possible response:* **我的电脑打不开了，今天还有作业要交，怎么办？**
*Wǒ de diànnǎo dǎ bu kāi le, jīntiān hái yǒu zuòyè yào jiāo, zěnme bàn?*
My computer won’t turn on, and I still have homework due today—what should I do?

## 6. Watch out

咱们 always includes the listener ("we = you and I"), whereas 我们 can exclude the listener ("we = my group, not you").

**Pronunciation:** 咱们 (zánmen) has a rising second tone followed by a light neutral tone; 怎样 (zěnyàng) moves from a low third tone to a falling fourth tone.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** In the reading passage, why does Xiao Wang ask 怎么办?
**Answer:** He wants to know what they will do if it rains on the weekend.. Xiao Wang asks 要是周末下雨怎么办？

**Production:** Write one suggestion using 咱们 and one question using 别的 or 其他.
**Answer:** 咱们换一家饭馆吧，这家没有别的菜了吗？. Combine 咱们 for a shared suggestion with 别的/其他 for alternatives.

## Review and next step

- Use 咱们 to include the listener.

- Contrast 别的 and 其他.

- Ask 怎么办 and 怎样 in practical situations.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which pronoun specifically includes the listener?
- a. 咱们 (zánmen) — we (inclusive)
- b. 别的 (biéde) — other
- c. 其他 (qítā) — others
**Answer:** a. 咱们 includes both the speaker and the listener.

**Selection:** Which expression asks "what should we do?"
- a. 怎样 (zěnyàng) — how
- b. 怎么办 (zěnme bàn) — what to do
- c. 别的 (biéde) — other
**Answer:** b. 怎么办 asks for a course of action.

**Selection:** Which word is a slightly more formal way to say "other(s)"?
- a. 其他 (qítā) — other / others
- b. 咱们 (zánmen) — we
- c. 怎么办 (zěnme bàn) — what to do
**Answer:** a. 其他 means other or others.
