# Staying Safe in Daily Life

Learn to use 安全 to describe safe places, safe travel, and everyday safety.

## Learning objectives

- Use 安全 as a stative verb/adjective to describe safe situations.

- Use 安全 as a noun in phrases like 注意安全 ("pay attention to safety").

- Wish someone a safe trip or confirm that someone arrived safely.

## 1. Heading Home Late

Two friends say goodbye after dinner and remind each other to get home safely.

**A:** 天黑了，你一个人骑车回家安全吗？ (Tiān hēi le, nǐ yí ge rén qí chē huí jiā ānquán ma?) — It’s dark—is it safe for you to ride your bike home alone?

**B:** 放心吧，这条路晚上灯很亮，非常安全。 (Fàngxīn ba, zhè tiáo lù wǎnshang dēng hěn liàng, fēicháng ānquán.) — Don’t worry, the lights on this road are bright at night—it’s very safe.

**A:** 那就好，过马路的时候还是要注意安全。 (Nà jiù hǎo, guò mǎlù de shíhou háishi yào zhùyì ānquán.) — That’s good; still, be careful about safety when crossing the street.

**B:** 好的，我安全到家以后给你发消息。 (Hǎode, wǒ ānquán dào jiā yǐhòu gěi nǐ fā xiāoxi.) — Okay, I’ll send you a message after I get home safely.

## 2. How the language works

### Reminding someone to stay safe

**Pattern:** <Activity> + 要注意安全 (<Activity> + yào zhùyì ānquán)

*<Activity> + need pay attention to safety*

注意安全 (zhùyì ānquán) is the standard everyday expression for "be careful / stay safe".

**下雨天开车，大家一定要注意安全。**
*Xià yǔ tiān kāichē, dàjiā yídìng yào zhùyì ānquán.*
When driving on rainy days, everyone must pay attention to safety.

**孩子在公园玩儿时，家长要注意安全。**
*Háizi zài gōngyuán wánr shí, jiāzhǎng yào zhùyì ānquán.*
When children play in the park, parents should watch out for safety.

### Doing something safely

**Pattern:** <Subject> + 安全 + <Verb> (<Subject> + ānquán + <Verb>)

*<Subject> + safely + <Verb>*

Place 安全 before a verb like 到家 or 到达 to mean arriving safely.

**飞机已经安全降落了。**
*Fēijī yǐjīng ānquán jiàngluò le.*
The plane has already landed safely.

**坐地铁上班既快又安全。**
*Zuò dìtiě shàngbān jì kuài yòu ānquán.*
Taking the subway to work is both fast and safe.

## 3. Build new sentences

Use 安全 as a noun, adjective, and adverb.

*Model:* **这个小区晚上很安全。**
*Zhège xiǎoqū wǎnshang hěn ānquán.*
This neighborhood is very safe at night.

**Cue 1:** Remind a friend who is traveling to stay safe.

*Model:* **你路上一定要注意安全！**
*Nǐ lùshang yídìng yào zhùyì ānquán!*
Be sure to stay safe on the road!

**Cue 2:** Say everyone arrived home safely.

*Model:* **大家都安全到家了。**
*Dàjiā dōu ānquán dào jiā le.*
Everyone arrived home safely.

**Cue 3:** Say swimming in a deep river is not safe.

*Model:* **在很深的河里游泳不安全。**
*Zài hěn shēn de hé lǐ yóuyǒng bù ānquán.*
Swimming in a very deep river is not safe.

## 4. Read in context: School Safety Day

今天是学校的“安全日”，老师给大家讲了交通安全和网络安全。 (Jīntiān shì xuéxiào de “Ānquán Rì”, lǎoshī gěi dàjiā jiǎng le jiāotōng ānquán hé wǎngluò ānquán.)

Today is the school’s “Safety Day,” and the teacher talked to everyone about traffic safety and internet safety.

同学们学到：过马路要看红绿灯，上网不要随便告诉别人自己的信息，这样才更安全。 (Tóngxuémen xuédào: guò mǎlù yào kàn hóng-lǜdēng, shàngwǎng búyào suíbiàn gàosu biérén zìjǐ de xìnxī, zhèyàng cái gèng ānquán.)

The students learned: watch the traffic lights when crossing the street, and don’t casually tell others your personal information online—that way you’re safer.

**Q1. What two kinds of safety did the teacher talk about?** Suggested answer: Traffic safety and internet safety.

**Q2. What should students avoid doing online?** Suggested answer: Casually telling others their personal information.

## 5. Speak and personalize

Try to answer aloud before checking the sample; adapt it to your own situation.

**Speaking 1:** Say goodbye to a friend who is about to ride a bike or drive.

*Possible response:* **路上慢点儿，注意安全，到了告诉我一声。**
*Lùshang màn diǎnr, zhùyì ānquán, dào le gàosu wǒ yì shēng.*
Take it slow on the road, stay safe, and let me know when you arrive.

**Speaking 2:** Say one place or habit that you think is very safe.

*Possible response:* **坐高铁去别的城市既舒服又安全。**
*Zuò gāotiě qù biéde chéngshì jì shūfu yòu ānquán.*
Taking the high-speed train to another city is both comfortable and safe.

## 6. Watch out

Use 很安全 to describe something that is safe; do not say *是安全 without 的.

**Pronunciation:** 安全 (ānquán) pairs a flat first tone ān with a rising second tone quán.

## 7. Check what you can do

**Comprehension:** What did the students learn about crossing the street on Safety Day?
**Answer:** They should watch the traffic lights when crossing the street.. The passage says 过马路要看红绿灯.

**Production:** Write a short reminder to a friend using 注意安全.
**Answer:** 晚上骑车回家一定要注意安全！. Use 注意安全 to remind someone to be careful.

## Optional vocabulary recall quiz

**Selection:** Which phrase means "pay attention to safety"?
- a. 注意安全 (zhùyì ānquán) — pay attention to safety
- b. 很安全 (hěn ānquán) — very safe
- c. 安全日 (ānquán rì) — safety day
**Answer:** a. 注意安全 is the standard reminder to stay safe.

**Selection:** What tone pattern does 安全 have?
- a. 1st + 2nd (ānquán) — first tone + second tone
- b. 4th + 2nd (ànquán) — fourth + second
- c. 1st + 4th (ānquàn) — first + fourth
**Answer:** a. 安 is first tone (ān) and 全 is second tone (quán).
