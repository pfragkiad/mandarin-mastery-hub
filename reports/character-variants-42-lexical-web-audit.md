## Clarifications for external review

- **了→瞭:** Say 了解 may also be written 瞭解 in Taiwan; do not imply mandatory replacement. Particle 了 remains 了 in 吃了. Cite Taiwan dictionary evidence separately from the base OpenCC 喫了 record.
- **面→麵:** Label 麵條 as Taiwan-supported spelling or cite Taiwan MOE directly. If citing base STPhrases, accurately report 麪條 and the separate TWVariants 麪→麵 record.
- **只→衹:** Retain 衹要 as an attested literary alternative, with an actual text citation; present 只要 as the ordinary default in the checked conversion dictionary. Keep readings and other senses source-specific.
- **饥→飢:** Both 飢餓 and 饑餓 are attested. Separate the two dictionary attestations from the OpenCC phrase policy, which records 飢餓; do not describe the slash as two OpenCC outputs or an exclusive Taiwan/Hong Kong rule.

The Taiwan/Hong Kong distinction can also affect 台 examples: OpenCC HKVariants records 檯→枱 and 臺→台. The checked base phrase rows for 櫃檯 and 臺灣 remain valid evidence for the selected forms; regional defaults should be labeled separately.

Three whole expressions are absent as exact STPhrases keys: 脏衣服, 一斗米, and 一只猫. The JSON identifies dictionary attestations or explicit composition instead of claiming exact phrase-table matches.
