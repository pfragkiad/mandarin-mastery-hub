# Etymology association review

The machine-readable [audit](etymology-association-audit.json) is generated by
`python scripts/import-etymology.py --write`. Its current counts are authoritative;
this document explains how to review the results. Neither file is fetched by the
application. The frontend consumes the separate
[runtime handoff](../public/data/etymology/README.md).

## What has been verified operationally

- Source downloads match their recorded original-byte hashes and upstream pins.
- Howell explanations and Shuowen headword explanations retain original text,
  entry locators and independent source IDs. Shuowen duplicate headwords survive.
- Separate English AI drafts bind to exact original Shuowen text hashes and
  individual IDs, including duplicate headwords. `shuowenEnglishDrafts` and
  `uncertainShuowenEnglishDrafts` count coverage and flagged passages. Completeness
  and checksum checks establish attachment integrity, not translation accuracy;
  all English remains unreviewed. See the
  [translation guide](../importer/data/etymology/translations/README.md).
- Application record IDs and family-qualified forms come from current shared
  adapters. All associations use exact written forms, without normalization or
  dictionary/index/shape-based substitutions.
- BabelStone references use direct-G alternatives and the shared IDS parser.
  Stale tree bindings and unsupported notation fail closed. No stroke mappings,
  outlines, phonetic roles or historical meanings are inferred.
- Shared headwords indicate comparison coverage only; the report intentionally
  records `historicalInterpretationsVerified: 0`.

## Initial review queues

The initial export has 6,376 Howell entries and 9,833 Shuowen records, with 4,401
shared headwords and 3,798 shared Characters records. At least one exact source
exists for 6,004 Characters records. Component/radical matches are review targets,
not approved claims about their role in containing characters.

| Audit field | Review action |
| --- | --- |
| `unmatchedSourceHeadwords` | Check source-only/historical/Japanese forms separately. Do not add catalogue records or normalize forms merely to increase coverage. |
| `duplicateShuowenHeadwords` | Preserve original entry IDs and inspect distinct original entries; never choose the first automatically. |
| `contextReviewTargets` | Establish whether standalone headword history is applicable to the selected component/radical role, using explicit evidence. Family-form statuses are separately available in the runtime index. |
| `blockedContextualForms` | Respect existing reviewed contextual identities. The initial 王-shaped jade form blocks inheritance of the standalone king interpretation. |
| `staleBabelstoneBindings` | Review the existing sidecar against current catalogue trees. The initial exclusions are 丁, 乡, 孓 and 由. The etymology importer must not repair those trees or silently bypass bindings. |
| `unsupportedBabelstoneExpressions` | Preserve raw expression and regional qualifiers; do not build a plausible partial tree. |
| `numberedTokens` | Retain BabelStone identities and use the established numbered-component presentation. No lexical/headword substitution is authorized. |

## Comparison fixtures for a reviewer

- 林: BabelStone records two 木 occurrences. Both sources explicitly discuss two
  trees; this is a useful candidate for reviewed compatible accounts. The dataset
  still labels the comparison unreviewed.
- 休: graphical 亻 must remain distinct from the 人 mentioned by sources. Howell
  also discusses a possible phonetic history. Establish form relationships and
  historical roles separately rather than automatically assigning a semantic role.
- 古: BabelStone records 十 above 口; Shuowen names those parts, while Howell
  offers a different historical explanation. Graphical agreement does not settle
  historical interpretation.
- 发: no exact headword entry. Existing written-form associations suggest 發 and
  髮 separately; neither narrative belongs automatically to 发.
- 王: standalone character notes remain available while family 96 / form 96-1
  keeps the reviewed jade contextual identity.

Future editorial judgments should be recorded in an explicit review input with
source locators, precise claim/form scope and reviewer evidence, then regenerated
through the importer. No such judgments have been invented for this handoff.
